## Executive Summary

Authored by Onevest/CoFoundersLab co-founder and serial startup advisor **Alejandro Cremades**—with a brisk Foreword by real-estate mogul and *Shark Tank* investor **Barbara Corcoran** (pp. 15–18)—*The Art of Startup Fundraising* (Wiley, 2016, copyright p. 6) is a clear, step-by-step tactical manual covering the full arc of startup capital formation. Starting from the sobering baseline that "investors don't want to invest" until de-risked by proof, momentum, and trust (Ch. 3, p. 47), Cremades walks founders methodically through storytelling, pitch-deck architecture, due-diligence data rooms, capital sources, PR buzz-building, and round-by-round financing mechanics.

Written specifically to level the informational playing field for first-time founders, the book demystifies every fundraising tier—from bootstrapping, friends & family, equity crowdfunding, and super-angels through Seed, Series A–D, venture debt, and IPO (Ch. 6–8, pp. 87–124).

## Operational Fundraising Blueprint

1. **The 18-to-24-Month Milestone Runway (Ch. 2–3)**: Frames each capital raise around hitting concrete value-inflection milestones that justify a step-up valuation in the subsequent round while preparing clean legal incorporation and cap tables upfront.
2. **Pitch Crafting & Due-Diligence Readiness (Ch. 4–5, pp. 59–86)**: Contrasts outdated static business plans with crisp elevator pitches, concise 10–15 slide pitch decks, one-pagers, and an exhaustive pre-assembled due-diligence checklist (IP, financials, cap table, references).
3. **Capital Spectrum & VC Mechanics (Ch. 6–8, pp. 87–124)**: Clarifies partner hierarchies inside VC firms, how VCs monetize, how VC differs from private equity, and exactly what metrics unlock Micro-Seed, Seed, and Series A institutional checks.
4. **Momentum, PR & Investor Selection (Ch. 9–11, pp. 125–155)**: Shows how to calibrate asking amounts, generate earned media ("The Rainmaker Code"), and vet prospective investors on three core filters: **domain expertise**, **network connectivity**, and **follow-on financial strength**.
