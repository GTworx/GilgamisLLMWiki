## Analytical Overview
### A History of Covert Subversion and Regional Resistance

*Targeting the Islamic Republic: Covert Operations, Regime Change Agendas, and Iranian Geopolitical Resilience* compiles three investigative dossiers detailing the turbulent history of Western intelligence operations directed against Iran.

### Key Investigations Covered

The compendium examines:
- **The Historical Shadow of Operation Ajax (1953):** How the CIA-MI6 coup overthrowing democratically elected Prime Minister Mohammad Mossadegh permanently radicalized Iranian politics, culminating in the 1979 Islamic Revolution.
- **Leaked Preemptive War Goals (The New York Times Investigation):** Declassified reporting exposing contingency planning for surgical decapitation strikes aimed at provoking internal regime chaos.
- **Asymmetric Resilience:** Evaluating how the Islamic Republic survives maximum-pressure sanctions through sanctions-evading oil exports to China and the 'Axis of Resistance' deterrence network.
