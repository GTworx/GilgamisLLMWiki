## Executive Summary

*The Math-Hacker Book: Shortcut Your Way to Maths Success* (2015, 742 pp., *Maths in a Minute* series) by British mathematics educator [[paul-carson]] demystifies core numeracy and secondary-school algebra by stripping away rote textbook jargon in favor of intuitive mental shortcuts, visual meaning, and real-world financial and scientific applications (source: BOOKS/Fun & Puzzles_/The Math-Hacker Book...pdf, Contents & Copyright, pp. 2–8).

Progressing systematically from foundational multiplication, multi-digit mental arithmetic, division, fractions, decimals ("Key Change Effect"), percentages, and compound interest/depreciation all the way through negative numbers, area/volume, indices, surds, standard form, logarithms, sequences, linear gradients, simultaneous equations, and quadratics, Carson turns math anxiety into rapid pattern fluency (pp. 2–4, 14–742).

---

## Key Arguments & Structural Breakdown

### Mental Arithmetic & Financial Numeracy (pp. 14–209)
- **Intuitive Shortcuts**: Reinvents times tables, 2x2 and 3x3 mental multiplication, long division, ultra-fast fraction arithmetic, decimal scaling, mental percentage multipliers, reverse percentages, and fast compound-interest and depreciation calculations (pp. 2–3).

### Powers, Logarithms & Algebraic Architecture (pp. 210–742)
- **Unlocking Secondary Math**: Demystifies squaring/cubing, geometric areas and engine volumes, the seven rules of indices, surd rationalization, scientific standard form, everyday logarithms, linear and non-linear sequences (invoking young Gauss's summation insight), straight-line gradients, simultaneous equations, and quadratic factoring (pp. 3–4).
