## Summary
Official biannual industry audit of advertising spending across TV, digital, print, and out-of-home media in Turkey for H1 2024, charting the decisive surge of programmatic and video ad spend.

## Analytical Overview
### The Financial Flows of Turkish Advertising

The *Türkiye Medya Yatırımları Raporu: 2024 İlk Yarı Özeti*, prepared jointly by the Advertisers Association (Reklamcılar Derneği), Advertising Agencies Association (RVD), and Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB Türkiye), provides the authoritative statistical record of ad spending across all communication channels in Turkey during the first half of 2024.

### Digital Dominance and Channel Redistribution

The report charts the ongoing structural transformation of media budgets:
- **Digital Media:** Commands over 70% of total national advertising investments, driven overwhelmingly by mobile display, programmatic video, and search performance marketing.
- **Television:** Remains resilient as the primary mass-reach vehicle for FMCG and retail giants, capturing roughly 25% of expenditure.
- **Print and Radio:** Continue their precipitous structural decline, capturing minor fractions of overall national spend.

### Inflationary Nominal Growth vs. Real Volume

The analysis disentangles nominal hyper-growth (driven by lira depreciation and rate-card inflation) from real advertising volume, providing agencies and brand CMOs with essential data for annual budget planning.
