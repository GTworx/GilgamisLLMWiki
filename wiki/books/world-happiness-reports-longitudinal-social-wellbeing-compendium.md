## Summary
Monumental 426-page two-volume global benchmark (2023 and 2025 editions) evaluating subjective life evaluations, pro-social behavior, social trust, and institutional benevolence across 140+ countries.

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes Gallup World Poll datasets to decompose national life satisfaction across six structural factors: GDP per capita, healthy life expectancy, social support, freedom to make life choices, generosity, and perceived corruption. Highlights the resilience of global benevolence during crises, the growing happiness divide among youth in North America and Western Europe, and the paramount role of social connections.
