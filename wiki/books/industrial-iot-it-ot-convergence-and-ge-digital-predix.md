## Summary
Definitive industrial engineering and business strategy compendium on the convergence of Information Technology (IT) and Operational Technology (OT), analyzing real-time SCADA integration, GE Digital's historic quest to scale the Predix industrial OS, Schneider Electric’s EcoStruxure, and Ericsson cellular IoT data.

## Analytical Overview
For decades, Information Technology (IT)—governing enterprise databases, ERPs, and corporate networks—and Operational Technology (OT)—governing physical programmable logic controllers (PLCs), SCADA systems, factory robotics, and electric turbines—operated in completely segregated universes with conflicting priorities: IT prioritized data confidentiality and integrity, while OT prioritized human life safety, deterministic real-time latency, and continuous 99.999% uptime. This work documents the epochal technological and organizational convergence of these two domains.

The volume opens with Paul Healey’s definitive industrial guide *What is IT/OT Convergence: Everything You Need to Know*, establishing the reference architectural frameworks (Purdue Model / ISA-95) that bridge plant-floor sensors to enterprise cloud analytics through edge gateways, industrial protocol translation (Modbus/OPC UA to MQTT), and strict DMZ network segmentation. This is paired with an intimate corporate strategic case study by Pierre-Yves Dolbec on General Electric’s multi-billion-dollar effort to build 'GE Digital' and scale its industrial IoT operating system, **Predix**.

Dolbec’s case study and the GE Digital Glossary dissect the cultural and technical hurdles that challenged Predix: underestimating the bespoke complexity of diverse industrial assets (jet engines vs. wind turbines vs. locomotives) and trying to build a centralized cloud software business on top of fragmented physical machinery. The compilation is enriched by Schneider Electric’s corporate strategy and annual reporting on EcoStruxure IoT architectures, Verdantix market overviews on systems integrators for smart commercial facilities, and Ericsson’s Mobility Report charting the exponential growth of massive and broadband cellular IoT connections.
