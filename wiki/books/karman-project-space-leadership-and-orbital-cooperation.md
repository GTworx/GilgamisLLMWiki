## Executive Summary
This 28-page strategic presentation deck details the mission, governance, and diplomatic initiatives of The Karman Project, the premier independent global foundation dedicated to fostering trust, sustainability, and international cooperation in space exploration. Named after aerospace pioneer Theodore von Kármán, the fellowship convenes astronauts, agency heads (NASA, ESA, JAXA, ISRO), private space CEOs, and geopolitical strategists to build consensus on orbital debris mitigation (the Kessler Syndrome), lunar resource property rights under the Artemis Accords, space environmental protection, and the peaceful preservation of outer space.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Space Sustainability & Debris Mitigation**: Addresses the escalating hazard of orbital debris, advocating for binding international protocols on active debris removal (ADR) and post-mission disposal.
- **The Artemis Era Governance**: Analyzes the legal frameworks governing lunar extraction, cis-lunar navigation, and space resource utilization beyond the 1967 Outer Space Treaty.
- **Cross-Sector Space Diplomacy**: Unites legacy national space agencies with commercial new-space entrepreneurs to prevent orbital conflict and foster shared scientific data reserves.
