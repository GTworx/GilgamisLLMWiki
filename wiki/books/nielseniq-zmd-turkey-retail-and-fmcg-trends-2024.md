## Summary
Authoritative scanner-data industry presentation by NielsenIQ and ZMD mapping FMCG volume trends, inflation-driven basket composition changes, and discount retailer dominance in Turkey.

## Analytical Overview
### Retail Dynamics in an Inflationary Emerging Market

*NielsenIQ & ZMD Türkiye Perakende ve Hızlı Tüketim Trendleri 2024* provides an empirical, scanner-data analysis of how Turkish households adapted their purchasing habits in response to persistent double-digit food inflation. Presented jointly by NielsenIQ and the Chain Stores Association (ZMD), the report captures granular point-of-sale data across tens of thousands of modern and traditional retail outlets.

### The Rise of Hard Discounters and Private Labels

The research charts significant structural shifts in consumer retail dynamics:
- The unprecedented expansion of hard-discount grocers (BİM, A101, Şok), which now capture over 60% of organized FMCG retail volume.
- Aggressive consumer migration toward private-label (store brand) goods as brand premiums become unsustainable for middle-class budgets.
- Basket down-sizing, promotional sensitivity, and frequency-of-visit increases as consumers manage liquidity on weekly cycles.

### Category Resilience and Operational Challenges

The study breaks down performance across food, beverage, personal care, and home cleaning sectors, providing vital pricing elasticity benchmarks for brand manufacturers and retail executives.
