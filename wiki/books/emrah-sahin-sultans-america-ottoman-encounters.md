## Summary
Deep archival investigation into late Ottoman perceptions of the United States, analyzing Sultan Abdülhamid II's intelligence gathering, missionary diplomacy, the 1893 Chicago World's Fair, and early transatlantic statecraft.

## Analytical Overview
Utilizes Prime Ministry Ottoman Archives (BOA) dossiers to examine how Ottoman statesmen viewed the rise of the United States as an emerging industrial and naval power. Investigates American Protestant missionary activities in Anatolia, Ottoman diplomatic protests over anti-Turkish press depictions, and reciprocal diplomatic visits that laid the groundwork for modern US-Turkish relations.
