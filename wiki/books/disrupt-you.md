## Executive Summary

Written by serial digital-media entrepreneur and former Sony/EMI/Deloitte innovation executive **Jay Samit** with a Foreword by LinkedIn co-founder **Reid Hoffman** (2015, Flatiron Books, pp. 298–300), *Disrupt You!* argues that technological disruption is not an external threat reserved for Silicon Valley unicorns—it is a learnable personal mindset for turning everyday friction, industry bottlenecks, and career obsolescence into entrepreneurial leverage.

---

## Practical Disruption Playbook (Contents, pp. 298–299)

- **Self-Disruption First (Chapters 1–4)**: Challenges fixed career scripts, teaches readers to spot unmet consumer pain points (keeping a daily problem log), maps transferable skills, and builds a resilient "Brand of One."
- **Value-Chain Disruption (Chapters 5–13)**: Walks step by step through intrapreneurship inside legacy corporations, design-driven differentiation, creative repurposing of existing production infrastructure, problem-first marketing, and unlocking trapped distribution bottlenecks.
