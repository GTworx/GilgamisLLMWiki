## Summary
Monumental 270-page macroeconomic flagship publication from the OECD providing country-by-country forecasts across 38 member states and major emerging economies, analyzing disinflation and fiscal consolidation pressures.

## Analytical Overview
Details macroeconomic projections for real GDP growth, labor markets, sovereign borrowing requirements, and current account balances through 2026. Emphasizes the imperative for decisive fiscal consolidation to restore budgetary buffers depleted by the pandemic and energy crises, while advocating structural labor and educational reforms to boost stagnant total factor productivity.
