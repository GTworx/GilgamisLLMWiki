## Executive Summary

Following extensive on-the-ground field trip meetings in Dallas, Texas with leading datacenter developers, independent power producers (IPPs), and ERCOT grid operators, Goldman Sachs Americas Energy Equity Research delivers a bullish assessment of the structural power demand inflection driven by data center construction. The report evaluates ERCOT interconnection queues, gas turbine procurement, behind-the-meter generation strategies, and power purchase agreement (PPA) pricing dynamics. Goldman Sachs details how large hyperscale campuses requiring 500 MW to 1 GW+ are bypassing traditional utility queues by contracting directly with natural gas combined-cycle plants and nuclear operators, permanently altering utility rate base expansion and electricity market fundamentals.

---

## Key Strategic, Scientific & Engineering Takeaways

- **ERCOT Grid Interconnection Dynamics**: Texas has emerged as the epicentre of data center development due to abundant land, business-friendly permitting, and the deregulated ERCOT market, but interconnection queues for transmission-level connections now face multi-year delays.
- **Behind-the-Meter Power Solutions**: Developers are increasingly co-locating gigawatt-scale data center campuses behind the meter with existing natural gas generation or dedicated nuclear assets to bypass transmission queue congestion.
- **Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) Price Re-acceleration**: Corporate clean energy buyers are accepting substantial premiums and multi-decade commitments to secure firm, 24/7 carbon-free energy (CFE), driving demand for nuclear power uprates, small modular reactors (SMRs), and firm geothermal power.
- **Grid Reliability & Peaking Capacity**: The influx of non-dispatchable renewable energy combined with flat, baseload data center demand requires substantial additions of quick-start natural gas peaking turbines and utility-scale battery storage.
- **Capital Expenditure Supercycle**: Utilities and independent power producers are embarking on multi-decade transmission and generation investment programs, creating a robust capex supercycle across electric infrastructure equities.
