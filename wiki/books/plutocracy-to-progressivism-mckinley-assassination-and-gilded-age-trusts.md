## Summary
Deep 47-page historical and political economy study analyzing the 1901 assassination of President William McKinley by Leon Czolgosz as the decisive institutional turning point from Gilded Age plutocracy to Theodore Roosevelt's trust-busting Progressivism.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the political and financial domination of Mark Hanna's corporate coalition and J.P. Morgan's trusts (Northern Securities, Standard Oil). Analyzes how McKinley's sudden death elevated Vice President Theodore Roosevelt to the presidency, inaugurating the modern regulatory state, the enforcement of the Sherman Antitrust Act, Hepburn Act railroad regulation, and the Progressive reassertion of public authority over private monopolies.
