## Overview
This comprehensive epigraphic and historical monograph investigates the origins, decipherment, and paleographic evolution of the Old Turkic runic script (Orkhon-Yenisey inscriptions, Tonyukuk, Kül Tigin, Bilge Qaghan). The text traces the debate over its origins—comparing derivation from Aramaic/Sogdian alphabets with indigenous nomadic tamga (clan brand) pictographic evolution.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Examines Vilhelm Thomsen's 1893 decipherment of the Orkhon inscriptions and the structural phonetics of Turkic runic letters.
- **Key Finding**: Details vowel harmony reflection and front/back consonantal pairing unique to the Orkhon script.
- **Key Finding**: Surveys hundreds of rock inscriptions and tamga petroglyphs across Mongolia, Tuva, Khakassia, and the Altai mountains.
