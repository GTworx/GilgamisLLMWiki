## Summary
Historic-to-modern 5-volume compendium linking Afet İnan's landmark 1937 nationwide anthropometric survey of 64,000 individuals with state-of-the-art whole-genome sequencing (Kars et al., PNAS) and Cypriot Y-chromosomal population genomics.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the evolution of population science in Turkey from Republican physical anthropometry to high-coverage whole-genome sequencing. Reveals that the contemporary Turkish population forms an intricate genetic bridge exhibiting high genomic variation shaped by Neolithic Anatolian ancestry, Mediterranean and Balkan interactions, and substantial (~10–15%) medieval Central Asian Turkic admixture.
