## Overview
This monumental 284-page visual art auction and curatorial catalog from Henry Butcher Art Auctioneers documents a premier Singapore/Kuala Lumpur sale of Southeast Asian modern and contemporary masterpieces. Featuring extensive provenance records, technical medium analyses, and art historical essays on seminal Malaysian, Indonesian, Singaporean, and Vietnamese artists (including Abdul Latiff Mohidin's Gelombang series), the catalog provides invaluable empirical data on the Southeast Asian fine art market.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Documents market valuations, historical auction records, and collector provenance for post-war Southeast Asian modernists.
- **Key Finding**: Provides in-depth curatorial essays contextualizing regional avant-garde movements against decolonization and rapid urban growth.
- **Key Finding**: Illustrates museum-grade conservation standards, physical condition reporting, and authentication documentation.
