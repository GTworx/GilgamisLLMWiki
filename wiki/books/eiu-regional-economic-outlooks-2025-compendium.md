## Summary
Comprehensive 6-volume continental survey from the Economist Intelligence Unit detailing macroeconomic growth, inflation, currency risks, and geopolitical flashpoints across all major world regions for 2025.

## Analytical Overview
Provides granular, comparative regional analysis: North American fiscal policy shifts; Europe's sluggish industrial output; Asia's regional trade pacts and supply chain relocations; Latin America's fiscal deficits and critical mineral investments; Middle Eastern energy diversification and conflict spillovers; and Africa's debt restructuring and sovereign debt distress.
