## Analytical Overview
### The Resurgence of European Democratic Participation

*The 2019 Post-Electoral Survey: Have European Elections Entered a New Dimension?*, published by the Directorate-General for Communication of the European Parliament (118 pp.), delivers an exhaustive demographic and political audit of the landmark 2019 European Parliament elections.

### Reversing a 40-Year Decline in Voter Turnout

The 2019 elections marked a historic inflection point in European integration:
- Turnout surged to **50.66%**, the highest participation rate in a quarter-century, reversing a four-decade trend of declining voter participation since direct elections began in 1979.
- The surge was overwhelmingly propelled by young voters (<25 years: +14 percentage points; 25–39 years: +12 points), catalyzed by Fridays for Future climate demonstrations and acute anxieties over Brexit.
- The report documents the breakdown of the historic grand coalition between the center-right EPP and center-left S&D, resulting in a fragmented, multi-polar parliament with Greens and Liberals (Renew Europe) holding kingmaker balances.

### Comprehensive Civic Benchmark

The survey provides cross-tabulated polling data across all 28 member states, evaluating citizen trust in European institutions.
