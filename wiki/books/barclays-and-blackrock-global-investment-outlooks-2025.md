## Summary
Three-volume institutional strategy compendium synthesizing BlackRock's 'mega-forces' framework (AI, demographic divergence, geopolitical fragmentation) with Barclays' granular earnings analysis across S&P 500 sectors.

## Analytical Overview
Details how structural macro shifts have replaced the stable low-inflation Great Moderation with persistent regime volatility. Evaluates corporate profit margins facing wage stickiness, capital expenditures required for AI datacenter buildouts, and tactical positioning across private markets and public credit.
