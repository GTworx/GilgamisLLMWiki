## Summary
G20 pilot benchmark report assessing national entrepreneurial performance, regulatory friction, and venture capital liquidity across the world's 20 largest economies.

## Analytical Overview
### Comparative Entrepreneurial Policy Across the G20

The *APEXE Nations Report 2024: G20 Pilot* provides a comprehensive policy and macroeconomic diagnostic comparing the entrepreneurial vitality of the world's twenty largest economies. Moving beyond aggregate GDP figures, the report evaluates each nation's structural capacity to cultivate, finance, and scale high-impact technology ventures.

### Analytical Pillars of National Performance

The benchmark assesses national ecosystems across four foundational criteria:
- **Capital Liquidity & Depth:** The availability of domestic risk capital across the entire venture funding lifecycle from seed to pre-IPO growth rounds.
- **Regulatory Gating & Friction:** Corporate incorporation speed, tax incentives for angel investors, IP enforcement efficiency, and non-punitive bankruptcy frameworks.
- **Talent Density & High-Skill Migration:** Quality of technical engineering universities, vocational education alignment, and fast-track tech visa availability.
- **Market Integration & Cross-Border Scaling:** The domestic market size and trade agreements enabling local startups to expand internationally.

### Policy Recommendations for Governments

The report offers tailored policy toolkits for emerging market G20 members seeking to modernize their regulatory environments.
