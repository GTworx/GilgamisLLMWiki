## Analytical Overview
### Extending Maslow to Enterprise Commerce

In *The B2B Elements of Value: What Business Buyers Really Care About*, published in *Harvard Business Review* (March–April 2018), Bain & Company partners Eric Almquist, Jamie Cleghorn, and Lori Sherer overturn the traditional economic assumption that business-to-business (B2B) purchasing is purely rational, price-sensitive, and commodity-driven.

### The 40-Element B2B Value Pyramid

Drawing on quantitative research across thousands of enterprise procurement decisions, the authors structure 40 discrete elements of value into a five-tier hierarchy:
1. **Table Stakes (Base Tier):** Meeting specifications, acceptable price, regulatory compliance, and ethical standards.
2. **Functional Value:** Cost reduction, scalability, product quality, innovation, and operational performance.
3. **Ease of Doing Business:** Productivity, operational integration, responsiveness, transparency, and risk mitigation.
4. **Individual Value (Personal & Career Drivers):** Career enhancement, reputational security, network expansion, and anxiety reduction for the procurement executive.
5. **Inspirational Value (Top Tier):** Vision, social responsibility, hope, and purpose.

### The Commercial Dividend of Subjective Value

The empirical analysis proves that B2B vendors who score high on ease of doing business and individual subjective elements achieve significantly higher Net Promoter Scores (NPS) and pricing power than competitors who compete solely on product specifications.
