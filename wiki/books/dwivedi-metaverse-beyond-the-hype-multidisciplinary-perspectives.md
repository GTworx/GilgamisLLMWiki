## Overview
Published in the International Journal of Information Management (Vol. 66), this monumental 55-page treatise brings together 79 leading global scholars across business, sociology, law, computer science, and public policy led by Yogesh K. Dwivedi. The monograph deconstructs the metaverse beyond commercial hype, providing an exhaustive multi-sectoral research agenda encompassing digital marketing, workplace transformation, mental health, legal jurisdiction, and environmental sustainability.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Synthesizes expert perspectives across 15 distinct functional domains of business, society, and governance.
- **Key Finding**: Identifies critical operational tensions between centralized corporate platforms and decentralized Web3 ecosystems.
- **Key Finding**: Formulates dozens of testable research propositions guiding information systems scholarship through 2030.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The metaverse is poised to redefine human interaction, commerce, and work, but its realization demands rigorous multidisciplinary scrutiny to prevent amplifying preexisting socioeconomic inequities."
