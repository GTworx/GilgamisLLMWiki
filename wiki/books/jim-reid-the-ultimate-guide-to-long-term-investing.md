## Summary
Epic 172-page long-term asset management compendium led by Deutsche Bank's Jim Reid, evaluating multi-century returns across equities, bonds, gold, and commodities from the 18th century to 2025.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes the real inflation-adjusted compounding of major asset classes across monetary regimes (classical gold standard, Bretton Woods, post-1971 fiat). Evaluates gold as the premier structural store of purchasing power during sovereign currency debasement and stagflationary shocks, integrating ING's comprehensive 2025 global commodity supply-demand balances.
