## Analytical Overview
### The Forensic 10-Minute Founder Interrogation

*Y Combinator Founder Evaluation Framework* details the concise, razor-sharp interrogation methodology utilized by Y Combinator partners during the legendary 10-minute partner interview that determines admission into the world's most prestigious startup accelerator.

### The Core Diagnostic Categories

The framework strips away pitch-deck theater to evaluate five core operational truths:
1. **Clarity of the Problem:** Can the founder explain what they are building and who needs it in a single, jargon-free sentence?
2. **Direct User Insight:** Has the founder talked directly to dozens of users? What is a non-obvious truth they discovered about customer behavior that competitors miss?
3. **Execution Velocity:** How much progress (code shipped, users onboarded, revenue generated) has the team achieved relative to the time they have been working together?
4. **Distribution & Growth Mechanics:** How will the company acquire its first 1,000 customers without paid advertising?
5. **Founder Dynamics & Grit:** How long have the co-founders known each other, how do they resolve conflict, and will they persevere when the startup faces existential crisis?

### Masterclass in Entrepreneurial Focus

The framework serves as an indispensable self-diagnostic tool for any early-stage entrepreneur preparing to launch a venture.
