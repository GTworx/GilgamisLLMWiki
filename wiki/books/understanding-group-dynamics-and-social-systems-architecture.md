## Summary
Comprehensive academic treatise on the psychological and structural mechanics of small group dynamics, covert role assignments, socio-technical systemic feedback, and team performance.

## Analytical Overview
Explores how unexamined group anxieties trigger dysfunctional defensive postures (fight/flight, pairing, dependency). Outlines practical frameworks for diagnostic systems consulting, group boundary management, psychological safety, and resolving covert interpersonal conflicts in complex organizations.
