## Analytical Overview
### Educational Equity and Non-Formal Learning in Turkey

*Türkiye Eğitim Gönüllüleri Vakfı 2024 Entegre Faaliyet Raporu* documents the nationwide operations, pedagogical architectures, and civil society impact of TEGV—Turkey's flagship educational non-governmental organization. Spanning Education Parks, Learning Units, and Mobile 'Ateşböceği' (Firefly) learning vans, the report details how the foundation reached over 150,000 children across disadvantaged socio-economic geographies during the 2023-2024 operating year.

### Integrated Reporting and Sustainable Value Creation

Following International Integrated Reporting Framework (IIRC) standards, the report assesses TEGV's capital allocations across Financial, Human, Social, Intellectual, and Natural capitals. Key strategic vectors detailed include:
- Algorithmic thinking, robotic coding, and STEM literacy curricula.
- Post-earthquake psychosocial and educational rehabilitation across southeastern Anatolia.
- Volunteer mobilization and professional development architectures involving thousands of university students.

### Metrics of Institutional Governance

The report demonstrates how civil society organizations can achieve corporate-grade transparency, robust monitoring and evaluation metrics, and enduring social return on investment (SROI).
