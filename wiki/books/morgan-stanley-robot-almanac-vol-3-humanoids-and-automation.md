## Executive Summary
Authored by Adam Jonas and Morgan Stanley's global equity research team, this comprehensive 96-page investment treatise analyzes the commercial inflection of general-purpose humanoid robots (Tesla Optimus, Figure, Boston Dynamics Atlas, Agility Robotics Digit). The report audits hardware bills of materials (BOM), rotary and linear actuator costs, harmonic drives, battery densities, and multi-modal embodied AI foundation models. Morgan Stanley models a multi-trillion-dollar total addressable market (TAM), projecting that humanoid robots will reach economic parity with human factory labor ($3–$5 per operating hour) by 2028–2030, permanently transforming industrial manufacturing, automotive assembly, logistics, and eldercare.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Humanoid BOM Cost Deflation**: The manufacturing bill of materials for humanoid robots is dropping from $150,000+ to under $30,000 at 100,000-unit scale, driven by automotive supply-chain efficiencies.
- **Actuator & Joint Bottlenecks**: High-torque-density actuators, planetary rollers, and dexterous 16-DoF robotic hands represent over 60% of current hardware costs.
- **Embodied AI Foundation Models**: The convergence of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models allows robots to generalize manipulation skills across novel environments without manual coding.
- **Macroeconomic Labor Displacement**: Identifies manufacturing, warehouse palletizing, and hazardous material handling as the initial insertion points for commercial deployment.
