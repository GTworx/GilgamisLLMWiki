## Executive Summary
This authoritative 88-page six-monograph clinical research compendium represents the definitive international standard on cardiovascular computed tomography, anchored by the two-part official consensus guideline of the European Association of Cardiovascular Imaging (EACVI) and landmark clinical trial publications by Dr. Gianluca Pontone and collaborators across the European Heart Journal and JACC: Cardiovascular Imaging.

The compendium establishes rigorous clinical pathways for deploying Coronary Computed Tomography Angiography (CCTA), Fractional Flow Reserve derived from CT (FFR-CT), and dynamic stress Computed Tomography Perfusion (CTP) with whole-heart coverage scanners. It systematically compares quantitative versus qualitative myocardial blood flow (MBF) algorithms against invasive fractional flow reserve (FFR), demonstrating that dynamic stress CTP achieves superior diagnostic specificity and positive predictive value in detecting hemodynamically significant multi-vessel coronary artery disease.

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **EACVI Consensus Recommendations**: Codifies evidence-based indications for CCTA in chronic coronary syndromes, pre-procedural planning for transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI), and complex congenital heart defects.
- **Dynamic Stress CTP Superiority**: Demonstrates that whole-heart coverage dynamic stress CTP yields absolute myocardial blood flow (MBF in mL/100g/min) quantification, eliminating beam-hardening artifacts inherent to static CTP.
- **FFR-CT Computational Hemodynamics**: Explores non-invasive fluid dynamic simulations modeling hyperemic pressure drops across coronary stenoses without administering pharmacological adenosine.
- **Plaque Characterization and Vulnerability**: Evaluates high-risk plaque features—low-attenuation plaque (<30 HU), positive remodeling, napkin-ring sign, and spotty calcification—as independent predictors of acute coronary syndromes.
