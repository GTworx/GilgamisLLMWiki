## Overview
Published in December 2024 by SNB Capital Equity Research, this 17-page economic briefing examines the macroeconomic and financial impact of Saudi Arabia hosting the FIFA World Cup 2034. The analysts evaluate multi-billion-dollar stadium construction, metro and rail expansion, hospitality hospitality capacity surges, and the broader integration into Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 economic diversification strategy.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Quantifies the infrastructure expenditure required: 15 world-class stadiums across five host cities (Riyadh, Jeddah, Al Khobar, Abha, NEOM).
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes non-oil GDP multiplier effects across tourism, construction, telecommunications, and aviation.
- **Key Finding**: Examines the geopolitical soft-power strategy: leveraging global sports mega-events to rebrand international standing and attract foreign direct investment.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Hosting the FIFA World Cup 2034 is the ultimate centerpiece of Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030, catalyzing hundreds of billions in infrastructure that accelerates non-oil economic diversification."
