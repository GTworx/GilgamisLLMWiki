## Analytical Overview
### Post-Brexit Transatlantic Grand Strategy

*Defending Europe: Global Britain, the Future of European Geopolitics, and the Gaza Crisis*, published by the Henry Jackson Society (2025), outlines the strategic defense doctrine of the United Kingdom in an era marked by simultaneous security crises on Europe's eastern and southern frontiers.

### Strategic Pillars Evaluated

The briefing investigates:
- **The Global Britain Defense Footprint:** The Royal Navy's deployment in the Red Sea combating Houthi anti-ship missile attacks and securing maritime commercial freedom of navigation through the Bab el-Mandeb strait.
- **European Rearmament and British Deterrence:** British military contributions to NATO's Enhanced Forward Presence in Estonia and bilateral security pacts with Ukraine.
- **Humanitarian Coordination in Urban Warfare:** Analyzing operational requirements for delivering civilian aid into conflict zones without Hamas diversion.
