## Analytical Overview
### The Digital Revolution in Financial Services

*The Global Fintech Ecosystem Report 2020* provides an exhaustive quantitative diagnostic of the international financial technology landscape. Capturing data across dozens of global innovation hubs, the report charts the dramatic unbundling of traditional commercial banking services by agile software challengers.

### Sub-Sector Dynamics Across Global Hubs

The report tracks key fintech verticals:
- **Digital Payments and Cross-Border Transfers:** The rapid expansion of mobile wallets (Stripe, Adyen, Ant Group) compressing transaction fee structures.
- **Neobanks and Challenger Banking:** Retail banking customer acquisition by mobile-first platforms (Revolut, Chime, Nubank) challenging brick-and-mortar incumbents.
- **Open Banking and API Architecture:** The regulatory impact of Europe's PSD2 directive forcing legacy banks to open customer financial data to third-party developers.
- **InsurTech and RegTech:** Algorithmic underwriting and automated compliance monitoring.

### Ecosystem Rankings and Policy Blueprints

The report benchmarks top fintech cities (London, New York, Singapore, São Paulo, Tel Aviv), outlining essential public policy frameworks: the creation of regulatory sandboxes, open-banking mandates, and venture capital tax incentives.
