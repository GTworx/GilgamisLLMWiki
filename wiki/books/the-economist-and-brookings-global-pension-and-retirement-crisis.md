## Summary
Dual-volume retirement policy analysis from The Economist and the Brookings Institution evaluating global public pension insolvency, the death of defined-benefit plans, and legislative reforms under the US SECURE 2.0 Act.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes how longer life expectancies combined with sub-replacement fertility rates have placed defined-benefit state pension systems under existential strain. Details the provisions of SECURE 2.0, including mandatory automatic retirement plan enrollment, emergency savings sidecars, higher catch-up contributions, and expanded incentives for low-to-moderate earners.
