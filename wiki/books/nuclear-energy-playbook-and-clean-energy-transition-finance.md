## Summary
Authoritative 76-page energy finance playbook analyzing the nuclear power renaissance driven by datacenter AI power demands, small modular reactor (SMR) commercialization, and US clean energy executive orders.

## Analytical Overview
Details the physical and financial necessity of zero-carbon baseload electricity to support 24/7 hyperscale AI compute clusters. Evaluates uranium fuel cycle economics, lifetime extension economics for existing nuclear fleets, federal loan guarantee programs, and valuation comp sheets across publicly traded AI and energy infrastructure vendors.
