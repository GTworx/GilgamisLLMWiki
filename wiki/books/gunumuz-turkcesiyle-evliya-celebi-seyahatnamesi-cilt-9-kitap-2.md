## Executive Summary

Transcribed into fluent contemporary Turkish by eminent Ottomanists **Seyit Ali Kahraman** and **Yücel Dağlı** (under the scholarly oversight of Robert Dankoff, YKY 2011, 472 pp.), Volume 9 (Book 2) of the *Seyahatnâme* chronicles Evliyâ Çelebi's great 1671–1672 pilgrimage journey (*Hac Seferi*) through Southwestern Anatolia, the Levant, and the Hejaz: traversing Kütahya, Manisa, İzmir, Aydın, Muğla, Antalya, Alanya, Karaman, Adana, Aleppo (Halep), Damascus (Şam), Jerusalem (Kudüs), Mecca, and Medina.

|---|---|

| **Filistin & Kudüs** | Detailed topographic survey of Jerusalem (Mescid-i Aksa, Kubbetü's-Sahra, Holy Sepulchre), Bethlehem, and Hebron (Halilürrahman) |
| **Hicaz: Mekke & Medine** | Desert caravan logistics; arrival at Medina and Rawdah of Prophet Muhammad; Hajj rites in Mecca, Kaaba rituals, and Mount Arafat |

---

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **Micro-Historical Urban Surveys**: Provides exact architectural measurements, mosque inscriptions, waqf endowments, prices, and guild censuses across 17th-century Aegean, Syrian, and Palestinian cities.
2. **Pluralistic Sacred Geography**: Records shared inter-confessional pilgrimage sites in Jerusalem, Hebron, and Damascus, describing Christian, Jewish, and Muslim communal interactions with remarkable curiosity.
3. **Caravan Administration and Security**: Details the immense logistical orchestration of the Imperial Surre Caravan (*Surre Alayı*), Bedouin escort diplomacy, water reservoir upkeep, and desert survival.
