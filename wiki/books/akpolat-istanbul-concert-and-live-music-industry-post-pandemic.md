## Overview
Published in Kapsül in May 2024 by prominent Turkish music journalist Barış Akpolat, this investigative cultural report analyzes the economic and political survival of Istanbul's live concert sector following severe pandemic lockdowns, runaway inflation, and local municipal festival bans. Akpolat interviews promoters, venue owners, and artists, detailing soaring production costs, currency volatility, and audience resilience.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Documents how triple-digit inflation and currency devaluation devastated foreign artist bookings in Turkey.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes the cultural resilience of independent local venues fighting municipal censorship and conservative cancellations.
- **Key Finding**: Highlights innovative ticketing models and collaborative grassroots festivals sustaining Istanbul's musical ecosystem.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Despite crippling economic crises and political festival bans, Istanbul's music scene refuses to extinguish; it reinvents itself in underground basements, alternative rooftops, and defiant community solidarity."
