## Executive Summary
This technical hardware dossier unites confidential investor presentations and enterprise deployment roadmaps from pioneer spatial computing companies, most notably Vrvana (the Montreal mixed-reality startup whose Totem headset architecture was acquired by Apple for ~$30M to form the foundation of Apple Vision Pro). The collection details the engineering trade-offs of pass-through video mixed reality versus optical see-through waveguides, low-latency camera sensor fusion, inside-out 6DoF positional tracking, and enterprise industrial inspection platforms deployed across General Electric (GE) manufacturing plants (Project Magellan).

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Pass-Through Mixed Reality Architecture**: Vrvana Totem established the industry benchmark for video pass-through MR, eliminating optical occlusion boundaries and enabling seamless transition between VR and AR.
- **Apple Acquisition Lineage**: The Totem's dual OLED micro-displays and ultra-low-latency 120Hz camera pass-through pipelines served as the direct technological precursor to Apple Vision Pro.
- **Industrial Inspection at Scale**: Project Magellan demonstrates how GE and industrial sponsors achieved 30% inspection time reductions by projecting step-by-step CAD blueprints directly onto turbine components.

## Key Data Points & Excerpts
