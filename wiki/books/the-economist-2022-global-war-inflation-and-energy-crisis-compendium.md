## Summary
Monumental 30-issue archival compendium of The Economist from 2022 documenting the Russian invasion of Ukraine, Western economic sanctions, European natural gas shortages, 40-year-high inflation, Federal Reserve rate hikes, and the UK's 'Truss deficit' crisis.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the pivotal geopolitical and financial turning point of 2022. Provides exhaustive coverage of: the weaponization of SWIFT and Russian sovereign asset freezes; the European energy transition following the sabotage of Nord Stream pipelines; the death of zero-interest-rate policy (ZIRP); the short-lived premiership of Liz Truss; China's property market implosion; and the emergence of structural inflation across global supply chains.
