## Summary
Definitive industrial compendium on the global electric vehicle battery market, featuring BCC Research's 337-page global market study (FCB063A), peer-reviewed evaluations of critical mineral supply chains (lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite), high-precision automated electrode manufacturing, and the SBMC swappable battery standards.

## Analytical Overview
This volume provides an in-depth technological and geopolitical dissection of the critical core of vehicle electrification: the high-voltage battery pack and its upstream mineral supply chain. Anchoring the collection is BCC Research's authoritative 337-page market study *Global Electric Vehicle Battery Market* (Report Code FCB063A), authored by Satyabrata Sahu. The report evaluates the commercial trajectory of dominant lithium-ion chemistries—lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxide (NMC 622 and 811), lithium nickel cobalt aluminum oxide (NCA), and lithium iron phosphate (LFP)—forecasting massive shifts toward cobalt-free LFP chemistry for mass-market vehicles due to lower thermal runaway risk and lower raw material costs.

This commercial forecast is coupled with Pranjal Barman, Lachit Dutta, and Brian Azzopardi’s comprehensive peer-reviewed survey in *Energies* dissecting the acute vulnerability of critical raw material supply chains. The authors map the geographical chokepoints of lithium mining in the Lithium Triangle (Chile, Argentina, Bolivia) and Australia, cobalt extraction in the Democratic Republic of Congo, and nickel processing in Indonesia, alongside China’s overwhelming 70–85% market share in synthetic graphite refining, cathode precursor synthesis, and chemical processing.

The work also provides detailed industrial engineering manuals on automated gigafactory production—highlighting automated gravimetric weighing, continuous slurry mixing, roll-to-roll electrode calendering, pouch and prismatic cell winding, and formation cycling. It concludes with the foundational position paper of the Swappable Batteries Motorcycle Consortium (SBMC), detailing universal geometric, electrical, and communication standards for swappable light EV batteries to eliminate consumer range anxiety and lower upfront EV capital costs.
