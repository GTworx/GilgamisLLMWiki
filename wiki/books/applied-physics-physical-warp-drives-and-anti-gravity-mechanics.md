## Executive Summary
Authored by Gianni Martire, Alexey Bobrick, and the Applied Physics Think Tank, this 12-page executive summary and technical monograph establishes the first generalized constant-velocity model for physical warp drives operating strictly within classical General Relativity. Overcoming the historic limitation of Miguel Alcubierre's 1994 warp metric—which required unphysical 'negative energy' or exotic matter—the Applied Physics team introduces positive-energy stress-energy tensors enclosed within passenger shells of regular matter. The paper formalizes 'metric engineering', demonstrating that subluminal warp bubbles are theoretically consistent with known laws of physics, paving the way for advanced gravitational propulsion research without violating Einstein's field equations.

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Elimination of Negative Energy**: Proves mathematically that physical subluminal warp bubbles can be constructed using positive mass-energy configurations, removing the primary theoretical objection to warp propulsion.
- **Metric Engineering Paradigm**: Re-conceptualizes space propulsion from expelling mass (chemical rocketry) to modifying the local spacetime metric curvature around the craft.
- **Relativistic Energy Densities**: Shows that while positive-energy warp drives are physically admissible, creating measurable spacetime curvature still requires planetary-scale mass-energy compression.
- **Anti-Gravity and Gravitational Shielding**: Investigates how gravitational gradients can be tailored to eliminate tidal g-forces inside the passenger cabin during extreme accelerations.
