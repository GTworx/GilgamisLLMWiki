## Analytical Overview
### The Voice of Turkish Big Business on Entrepreneurship

The *TÜSİAD Türkiye Girişimcilik Raporu 2024*, published by the Turkish Industry and Business Association (TÜSİAD), establishes an ambitious strategic agenda positioning technology entrepreneurship as the central pillar of Turkey's national economic growth and international competitiveness.

### Structural Diagnostic and Scale-Up Dilemmas

The report diagnoses Turkey's core entrepreneurial challenge: while Turkey excels at creating early-stage startups (ranking among the highest in Europe for seed volume), it faces a severe **Scale-Up Gap**:
- Startups struggle to transition from Series A to Series B/C growth rounds due to the shortage of domestic institutional growth equity funds.
- Macroeconomic volatility and high local interest rates induce corporate conglomerates to hoard cash rather than deploy aggressive risk capital.
- The report outlines the urgent necessity of deepening Corporate Venture Capital (CVC) engagement, opening corporate procurement to local startups, and expanding employee stock option (ESOP) tax incentives under Turkish commercial law.

### National Action Agenda

TÜSİAD outlines concrete policy proposals to elevate Turkey into a top-10 global entrepreneurial nation.
