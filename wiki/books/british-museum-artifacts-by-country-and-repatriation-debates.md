## Summary
Comprehensive catalog and provenance survey detailing the geographic origins, acquisition circumstances, and international legal restitution claims for the British Museum's 8 million artifacts.

## Analytical Overview
Documents major collections across Greece (Parthenon Sculptures), Egypt (Rosetta Stone), Nigeria (Benin Bronzes), Turkey/Ottoman Empire (Mausoleum at Halicarnassus, Temple of Artemis, Knidos Lion), Iraq (Assyrian palace reliefs), and China. Evaluates international legal frameworks (1970 UNESCO Convention) and museum deaccession laws.
