## Summary
Methodological and archival compendium formulating econometric equations to calculate net colonial surplus extraction and compound interest debt owed by colonial powers, alongside documentation of French colonial war crimes.

## Analytical Overview
Formulates rigorous fiscal formulas to compute imperial net drain (unrequited exports, forced labor, captive trade price depressions) compounded to present value. Contrasts global settler-colonial regimes (North America, Australia, Algeria, South Africa) with documentation of French colonial atrocities and indemnities in Haiti, Algeria, Madagascar, and Indochina.
