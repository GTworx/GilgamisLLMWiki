## Summary
Comparative 4-part legal and historical investigation evaluating successful vs. failed state reparations frameworks over the last century, alongside indigenous genocide complicity and the foundational Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes the legal conditions that enabled successful historical reparations (German Wiedergutmachung to Holocaust survivors, Japanese-American internment redress) versus stalled or rejected claims (U.S. slavery reparations, Herero-Namaqua claims against Germany, British colonial Kenya). Examines academic and institutional misrepresentation as a form of continuing complicity in the destruction of Indigenous North American societies.
