## Analytical Overview
### Mapping the Capital Geography of the Generative AI Boom

*Dealroom & Accel GenAI Report 2024* provides an exhaustive quantitative diagnostic of the monumental wave of venture capital poured into the generative artificial intelligence ecosystem following the release of ChatGPT. Produced collaboratively by data intelligence firm Dealroom.co and elite venture firm Accel, the report analyzes over $50 billion in global venture allocations.

### The Three-Layer AI Stack Dynamics

The report categorizes the generative AI landscape into three distinct structural layers:
1. **Foundation Models & Compute Infrastructure:** Dominated by a handful of mega-funded titans (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral) commanding multi-billion-dollar rounds driven by GPU cluster capex costs.
2. **Developer Tooling & Infrastructure Orchestration:** Vector databases, fine-tuning frameworks, synthetic data generation, and LLM evaluation platforms capturing high-margin developer loyalty.
3. **Verticalized Enterprise Applications:** The battleground where enterprise software incumbents (Salesforce, Microsoft, Adobe) collide with venture-backed AI-native challengers in legal tech, code generation, and customer operations.

### Cross-Regional Capital Flows

The study highlights the geographic concentration of AI financing in the San Francisco Bay Area, alongside the vibrant emergence of European foundational hubs in Paris and London.
