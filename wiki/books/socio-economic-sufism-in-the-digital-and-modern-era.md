## Overview
Published in Al-Kawakib (2023), this sociological and theological study investigates 'Social and Economic Sufism' in the digital era. The authors analyze how modern Sufi brotherhoods (tariqas) move beyond passive monastic seclusion (*khalwa*) to build ethical business enterprises, microfinance cooperatives, digital wellness platforms, and spiritual social safety nets.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Defines Neo-Sufism: reconciling inward contemplation with active economic productivity and civic philanthropy.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes Islamic spiritual capital as a buffer against modern consumer alienation and predatory economic practices.
- **Key Finding**: Documents the utilization of digital networks and online zawiyas for global transnational spiritual community building.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Modern Sufism proves that mysticism is not an escape from society; it is the spiritual recalibration of commerce and technology to serve human dignity rather than unchecked greed."
