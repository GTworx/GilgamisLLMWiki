## Summary
Authoritative 102-page comprehensive retrospective of every FIFA World Cup tournament from Uruguay 1930 through Qatar 2022, detailing tactical revolutions, iconic athletes, and geopolitical controversies.

## Analytical Overview
Examines how the FIFA World Cup evolved into the planet's preeminent mega-event and geopolitical mirror. Chronicles the interwar tournaments under Italian fascism, the 'Miracle of Bern' and German post-war rehabilitation (1954), Pelé's Brazilian sambafield dominance, the tactical shock of Dutch 'Total Football' (1974), Maradona's 1986 Falklands-tinged masterpiece, and the sportswashing disputes of contemporary host nations.
