## Executive Summary

Reported by Pulitzer-finalist *New York Times* technology journalists **Sheera Frenkel** and **Cecilia Kang** from over 1,000 hours of interviews with more than 400 current and former executives, engineers, policy staff, and advisors (p. 7), *An Ugly Truth* (2021) is an unsparing inside chronicle of how **Mark Zuckerberg** and **Sheryl Sandberg** prioritized relentless user growth and advertising engagement above democratic integrity, user privacy, and internal security warnings between 2016 and the January 6, 2021 U.S. Capitol attack.

---

## Narrative Arc of Platform Power & Crisis (Contents, p. 5)

- **Warning Signs Ignored (Chapters 1–5)**: Tracks Facebook's internal security leadership (including Alex Stamos) discovering Russian Internet Research Agency election interference in 2016 and encountering intense executive resistance to public disclosure lest it trigger regulatory backlash or spook advertisers.
- **Cambridge Analytica & Damage Control (Chapters 6–9)**: Details the explosive 2018 data-harvesting scandal, the `#DeleteFacebook` movement, PR counter-campaigns (including hiring Definers Public Affairs), and the widening strategic and personal rift between Zuckerberg's uncompromising control and Sandberg's defensive crisis management.
- **Global Harm, Polarization & Reckoning (Final Chapters)**: Documents how engagement-maximizing News Feed algorithms amplified hate speech in Myanmar, COVID-19 medical misinformation, and election-denial mobilization—exposing the structural tension between ad-funded attention monopolies and civic accountability.
