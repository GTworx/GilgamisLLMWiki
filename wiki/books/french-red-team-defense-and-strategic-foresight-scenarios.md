## Executive Summary
This strategic defense compendium brings together the French Ministry of the Armed Forces' Red Team Defense foresight scenarios (*Ces guerres qui nous attendent: 2030–2060*) with technical ballistic analysis of novel military munitions (Türkiye's Gazap bomb). The French Red Team—a unique institutional collaboration uniting science fiction authors, defense analysts, and military scientists—constructs disruptive military scenarios spanning neuromorphic cognitive hacking, climate-induced piracy and hyper-fortified eco-cities, autonomous weapon swarms, and space-based kinetic strikes. The technical munition appendix examines the physics and doctrine of hybrid fragmentation-thermobaric weapons in modern urban combat.
