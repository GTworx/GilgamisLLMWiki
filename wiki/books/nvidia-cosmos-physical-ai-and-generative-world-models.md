## Summary
Frontier physical artificial intelligence and generative simulation compendium. Focuses on Nvidia's groundbreaking Cosmos World Foundation Model platform, evaluating how generative video foundation models learn intuitive physical principles (kinematics, gravity, friction, rigid body dynamics) directly from internet video to simulate physical worlds for robotics training.

## Analytical Overview
This volume examines the emergence of **Physical AI**—the transition from text-only language models to models that comprehend, predict, and manipulate physical 3D space.

Key scientific frameworks:
1. **Nvidia Cosmos World Foundation Platform**: Dissects Nvidia’s 2025 *Cosmos* platform, detailing world foundation models designed to generate photorealistic, physically compliant synthetic environments for training autonomous vehicles, humanoid robots, and industrial automation.
2. **Do Generative Video Models Learn Physics?**: Features empirical research evaluating whether video models (Sora, Cosmos, Runway) construct latent internal representations of Newtonian physics or merely memorize optical surface transformations, demonstrating that deep generative video models develop emergent understandings of occlusion, momentum, and collision mechanics.
