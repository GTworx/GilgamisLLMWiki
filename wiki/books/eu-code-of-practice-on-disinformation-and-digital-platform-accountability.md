## Executive Summary
Published in September 2023 by the European Commission Directorate-General for Communications Networks, Content and Technology (DG CONNECT), this 96-page official regulatory framework establishes the strengthened *Code of Practice on Disinformation*. Signed by major digital platforms (Google, Meta, Microsoft, TikTok), the Code introduces 44 binding commitments and 128 qualitative and quantitative performance indicators under the Digital Services Act (DSA) co-regulatory umbrella. The framework addresses the demonetization of disinformation purveyors, transparency of political advertising, algorithmic botnet detection, independent researcher data access, and automated detection and labeling of AI-generated synthetic media and deepfakes during democratic elections.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Co-Regulatory Architecture Under the DSA**: Connects self-regulatory platform pledges to enforceable legal sanctions under the Digital Services Act, where non-compliance can trigger fines up to 6% of global annual turnover.
- **Demonetizing Disinformation**: Mandates that platforms cut off programmatic advertising revenues to websites and channels systematically spreading verified medical or political disinformation.
- **Synthetic Media & Generative AI Labeling**: Requires platforms to deploy technological tools to detect, watermark, and prominently label AI-generated images, audio, and video deepfakes.
- **Empowering Independent Fact-Checkers & Researchers**: Establishes standardized API access for academic researchers to investigate algorithmic recommendation amplification, hate speech cascades, and state-sponsored information manipulation.
