## Summary
Exhaustive physical review and experimental compilation on quantum sensing technologies, featuring Degen's Reviews of Modern Physics survey, cold-atom quantum gravimeters mapping subterranean urban infrastructure in Nature, NV-center diamond probes for cellular biomedicine, and deterministic non-neighbouring quantum network teleportation at QuTech.

## Analytical Overview
While universal fault-tolerant quantum computing remains on a multi-year horizon, quantum sensing has achieved immediate, transformative real-world utility. By exploiting the exquisite sensitivity of coherent quantum superpositions and entanglement to external perturbations, quantum sensors surpass the standard quantum limit (SQL) in measuring magnetic fields, electric fields, temperature, and gravitational acceleration. This volume compiles the definitive experimental treatises across the field, anchored by Christian Degen and Reinhard Reinhard’s landmark 46-page *Reviews of Modern Physics* review (*Quantum sensing*).

In geophysics and civil engineering, Ben Stray and the UK Quantum Technology Hub team report a historic breakthrough in *Nature* (2022): the successful field deployment of an hourglass-configuration cold-atom quantum gravity gradiometer capable of cartographic sub-surface mapping. Utilizing laser-cooled rubidium atom clouds falling under gravity, the sensor detected an underground tunnel buried one meter beneath a road with millimeter spatial resolution, establishing a new paradigm for non-destructive geophysical excavation, sinkhole detection, and subterranean defense surveillance.

In life sciences, Nabeel Aslam and co-authors provide a comprehensive *Nature Reviews* roadmap on nitrogen-vacancy (NV) color centers in diamond. Biocompatible nanodiamonds functionalized inside living cells enable nanoscale nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR), measuring single-protein spin structures, intracellular temperature variations of millikelvins, and real-time neuronal action potentials. The volume concludes with Sophie Hermans and Ronald Hanson's landmark QuTech experiment achieving deterministic qubit state teleportation between non-adjacent nodes via diamond NV electron-spin entanglement, laying the physical backbone for the global Quantum Internet.
