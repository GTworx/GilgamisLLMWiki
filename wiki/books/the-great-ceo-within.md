## Executive Summary

Written by Silicon Valley CEO coach **Matt Mochary**—mentor to the founders of OpenAI, Coinbase, Reddit, Plaid, Brex, Rippling, Robinhood, Grammarly, and AngelList (Introduction, p. 4; ©2021, p. 2; 135 pp.)—*The Great CEO Within* is a brutally concise, copy-and-paste operational manual designed specifically for time-starved technical founders scaling hypergrowth companies. Deliberately released as a free living handbook, the text translates recurring founder bottlenecks into step-by-step protocols and verbatim scripts across eight modular sections.

Spanning **Individual Habits** (Part II: GTD, Inbox Zero, Top Goal, Energy Audit / Zone of Genius), **Group Habits** (Part III: RAPID Decision-Making, Impeccable Agreements, Radical Transparency, Conscious Leadership, Conflict Resolution), **Infrastructure** (Part IV: Company Wiki, OKRs, Areas of Responsibility [AORs], KPIs), **Collaboration** (Part V), and **Department Playbooks** (Parts VI–VII: Fundraising, Recruiting, Sales, Marketing, Product, Engineering, HR, Finance, Legal), Mochary distills modern startup scaling into executable systems.

---

## Core Frameworks & Insights

1. **Individual CEO Operating System (Part II, Ch. 3–10)**: Combines David Allen's GTD and Inbox Zero with a protected two-hour daily "Top Goal" block, "When You Say It Twice, Write It Down," and regular Energy Audits to keep the CEO operating inside their *Zone of Genius*.
2. **Group Habits, Conscious Leadership & Conflict Protocols (Part III, Ch. 10–17)**: Eliminates decision paralysis through single-DRI ownership, counters "Loudest Voice in the Room" with written pre-reads, replaces blame with "Impeccable Agreements," and embeds Jim Dethmer's *Above the Line / Below the Line* Conscious Leadership framework (Appendix A).
3. **Organizational Infrastructure & AOR Architecture (Parts IV–V, Ch. 18–25)**: Mandates a single-source-of-truth Company Wiki, crystal-clear **Areas of Responsibility (AORs)** to eliminate overlap and single points of failure, structured weekly executive meetings, and direct issue-clearing feedback loops.
