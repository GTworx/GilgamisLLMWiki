## Executive Summary
Published in 2025 by a pioneering nanobiotechnology collaborative, this landmark 33-page paper demonstrates the realization of full-color, spatiotemporally resolved near-infrared (NIR) vision in humans without bulky night-vision goggles or power supplies. The researchers developed biocompatible, flexible contact lenses embedded with core-shell lanthanide-doped upconversion nanoparticles (UCNPs). The nanoparticles absorb invisible 800–1,000 nm NIR photons and convert them via multi-photon upconversion into visible green and red light photons directly on the human cornea, allowing human photoreceptors (cones and rods) to perceive infrared patterns, lasers, and thermal targets in real time while maintaining normal daylight visible vision.

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Sub-Visible Upconversion Optics**: Core-shell NaYF4:Yb/Er/Tm nanoparticles absorb sub-visible NIR wavelengths and emit higher-energy visible photons directly into the ocular visual axis with sub-millisecond response latency.
- **Simultaneous Day and Night Vision**: Unlike electronic night-vision goggles that block natural visible sight and require battery packs, passive contact lenses allow wearers to perceive visible light and infrared light concurrently.
- **High Spatiotemporal Resolution**: Human clinical trials demonstrate the ability to read NIR barcode displays, detect covert infrared signaling beams, and distinguish moving targets in pitch darkness.
- **Defense and Clinical Applications**: Unlocks unprecedented tactical situational awareness for defense personnel while providing visual aid therapies for patients suffering from severe red-green color blindness.
