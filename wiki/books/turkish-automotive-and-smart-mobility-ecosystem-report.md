## Executive Summary
This 128-page industrial and innovation dossier integrates the Turkish Automotive Manufacturers Association (OSD) *Otomotiv Sektör Raporu* with GOOINN's comprehensive *Mobilite Raporu 2024*. The compendium provides a detailed audit of Türkiye's automotive manufacturing capacity ($30B+ export footprint), domestic supplier ecosystems, and the structural pivot toward electric vehicle (EV) battery platforms, charging infrastructure, connected software, autonomous commercial transit, and micromobility startups. The authors evaluate national industrial competitiveness amid the European Green Deal, carbon border adjustments, and the global software-defined vehicle (SDV) transition.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Manufacturing Core Competence**: Türkiye represents Europe's top commercial vehicle manufacturing hub, producing over 1.4 million vehicles annually with high domestic value-add.
- **The SDV Transition**: Highlights the urgent need for domestic automotive suppliers to transition from mechanical stamping and powertrain parts toward software-defined architectures and battery cells.
- **Micromobility Expansion**: Examines how urban congestion in Istanbul and Ankara drives rapid adoption of shared electric kick-scooters, e-bikes, and integrated MaaS (Mobility-as-a-Service) apps.
- **Charging Infrastructure Corridors**: Tracks the nationwide rollout of ultra-fast DC charging networks essential for passenger EV penetration.
