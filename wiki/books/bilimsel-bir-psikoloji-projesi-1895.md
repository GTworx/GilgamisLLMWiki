## Executive Summary

Drafted feverishly in autumn 1895 for his confidant Wilhelm Fliess and unpublished during Freud's lifetime (first appearing in German as *Entwurf einer Psychologie* in 1950 and in James Strachey's *Standard Edition Vol. I*; translated into Turkish with German terminological footnotes and Appendix B by **İbrahim Şahin Ateş**, June 2022, 80 pp., pp. 2–4), Freud's *Project for a Scientific Psychology* represents his breathtaking attempt to construct a **quantitative, neurobiological theory of the human mind**.

Before pivoting decisively toward clinical metapsychology in *The Interpretation of Dreams*, Freud modeled the nervous system as an energetic network of three specialized neuron classes—permeable perceptual neurons ($\phi$), memory-retaining impermeable neurons ($\psi$), and qualitative consciousness neurons ($\omega$)—separated by **"contact-barriers" (*Kontaktschranken*)**, remarkably anticipating Sherrington's discovery of the synapse and Donald Hebb's associative neural learning by decades.

---

## Key Proto-Neuroscience & Metapsychological Concepts

1. **Neuronal Inertia & Constancy**: Postulates that the psychic apparatus seeks to discharge excessive excitation ($Q$) while preserving minimal energy reserves necessary for adaptive action against internal drives (*Not des Lebens*).
2. **Contact-Barriers & Facilitation (*Bahnung*)**: Explains memory as differential resistance across synaptic junctions altered permanently by repeated excitation pathways.
3. **The Ego as Inhibitory Network**: Defines the nascent **Ego (*Ich*)** as a laterally cathexed neural organization that inhibits hallucinatory wish-fulfillment so the organism can test external reality—plus Part II's early theory of hysterical **deferred action (*Nachträglichkeit*)** (pp. 48–77).
