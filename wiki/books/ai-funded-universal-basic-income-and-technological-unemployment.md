## Summary
Analytical synthesis evaluating tech leadership proposals for funding Universal Basic Income (UBI) through automated compute productivity dividends and national AI corporate equity funds, contrasted with classic shareholder wealth maximization theory.

## Analytical Overview
Examines Sam Altman's 'Moore's Law for Everything' and Elon Musk's autonomous vehicle wealth abundance projections. Evaluates whether taxing autonomous compute or equity stakes can realistically offset mass white-collar wage displacement, contrasting these utopian redistribution models with Milton Friedman and Michael Jensen's corporate governance principles regarding fiduciary duty.
