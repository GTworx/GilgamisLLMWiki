## Overview
Published by the Reuters Institute for the Study of Journalism at Oxford University, this monumental 160-page global study surveys over 93,000 news consumers across 46 markets. The report documents historic shifts in news media: plunging trust in traditional journalism, rising active news avoidance (36%), and the rapid migration of youth audiences from Facebook and news sites toward TikTok, YouTube, and creator-led journalism.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Tracks global news trust falling to 40%, with widespread public fatigue driving intentional news avoidance.
- **Key Finding**: Documents the generational shift toward video-first platforms (TikTok, Instagram) as primary news discovery hubs.
- **Key Finding**: Examines publisher subscription revenue models struggling under cost-of-living pressures and platform algorithm shifts.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Younger generations are actively turning away from traditional news broadcasts, choosing instead to consume current events filtered through charismatic creators on video-first networks."
