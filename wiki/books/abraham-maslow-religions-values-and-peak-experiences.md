## Summary
Classic 122-page monograph by Abraham Maslow arguing that peak experiences, transcendental values, and spiritual illumination are intrinsic psychological capacities of human nature rather than the exclusive monopoly of institutional religions.

## Analytical Overview
Bridges the false dichotomy between mechanistic positivistic science and dogmatic theological orthodoxy. Maslow posits that 'Being-values' (truth, beauty, goodness, wholeness) arise naturally in self-actualizing individuals during peak experiences. Warns against both anti-rational mysticism and sterile scientism that excludes human values and transcendent experiences from psychological inquiry.
