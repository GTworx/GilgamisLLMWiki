## Summary
Four-dossier defense intelligence compendium mapping active global territorial disputes, comparative military personnel and nuclear capabilities across regional blocs (NATO, SCO, CSTO), and the theoretical erosion of the international rule of law.

## Analytical Overview
### The Re-Militarization of the International Order

*Global Disputed Territories and Military Blocs Compendium 2025* compiles four strategic datasets and legal analyses detailing the fracturing of the post-1945 international order into competing military blocs and contested borderlands.

### Granular Quantitative Intelligence

The compendium delivers extensive empirical data:
- **Global Disputed Territories Ledger (2025):** Exhaustive catalog of active territorial flashpoints across Asia (Kashmir, Taiwan Strait, South China Sea, Kuril Islands), the Middle East (Golan, Shebaa Farms), and the Post-Soviet space (Donbas, Crimea, Transnistria, Abkhazia).
- **Comparative Military Bloc Analytics:** Cross-tabulating active military personnel (millions), nuclear warheads, carrier strike groups, and defense budgets across major regional pacts: NATO, Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO), CSTO, and AUKUS.
- **NATO vs. SCO Historical Evolution:** Comparing NATO's early Cold War institutional challenges (1949–1960s) with the SCO's contemporary internal frictions (India-China border clashes within the same security bloc).
- **Geopolitics and the Death of International Law:** A theoretical treatise examining how great-power unilateralism has dismantled the Westphalian rule-of-law framework in favor of raw coercive power.
