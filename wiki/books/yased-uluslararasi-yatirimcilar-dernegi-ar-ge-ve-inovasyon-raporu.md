## Analytical Overview
### Multinational Corporate R&D in Turkey

*YASED Ar-Ge ve İnovasyon Çalışma Grubu Raporu*, published by the International Investors Association of Turkey (YASED), provides an insider executive diagnostic of multinational corporate R&D operations, design centers, and foreign direct investment in Turkey's innovation economy.

### Attracting High-Value Multinational R&D

The working group report examines the competitive policy levers required to convince global corporate headquarters (in Germany, the US, France, Japan) to locate regional R&D engineering centers in Turkey:
- **Evaluating R&D Law No. 5746:** The efficacy of state corporate tax credits, stamp duty exemptions, and researcher payroll subsidies in incentivizing multinational engineering labs.
- **Intellectual Property Protection & Legal Predictability:** Addressing corporate anxieties regarding IP ownership and specialized commercial patent courts.
- **University Talent Pipeline:** Partnering with premier engineering faculties (ODTÜ, İTÜ, Boğaziçi) to design custom Master's and PhD programs that supply specialized talent to multinational research facilities.

### Essential Policy Dialogue

The document captures the crucial institutional dialogue between global capital and Turkish state policymakers.
