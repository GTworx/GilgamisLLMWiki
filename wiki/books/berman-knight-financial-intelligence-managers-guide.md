## Summary
Masterwork of practical executive financial literacy published by Harvard Business Review Press, teaching non-financial managers how to decode income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow dynamics, and recognize executive accounting estimates and biases.

## Analytical Overview
Demystifies corporate accounting by showing that financial reporting involves art as well as science—relying on management assumptions, accruals, and revenue recognition choices. Details key working capital ratios, operating leverage, return on invested capital (ROIC), and cash vs. accrual discrepancies to empower managers with analytical tools to assess corporate health.
