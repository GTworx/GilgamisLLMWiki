## Executive Summary

Co-authored by entrepreneur **David S. Kidder** and writer-producer **Noah D. Oppenheim** and translated into Turkish by **Egemen Yılgür** (Maya Kitap, 1st ed. March 2013, p. 9; 1,036 pp.), *Entelektüelin Kutsal Kitabı: Biyografiler* (*The Intellectual Devotional: Biographies*) organizes **365 concise daily biographical readings**—one for every day of the year—rotating across seven weekly domains (rulers, thinkers, scientists, artists, rebels, spiritual figures, and cultural pioneers) from ancient Egypt to the modern era.

Spanning figures from **Khufu**, **Thales**, **Imhotep**, **Homer**, **Sappho**, **Socrates**, **Plato**, **Aristotle**, **Lao Tzu**, **Confucius**, **Hypatia**, **Murasaki Shikibu**, **Al-Khwarizmi**, **Ibn Rushd**, **Ibn al-Haytham**, **Al-Ghazali**, **Omar Khayyam**, **Christine de Pizan**, and **Leonardo da Vinci** through **Marquis de Sade** (p. 519) and modern revolutionaries (TOC, pp. 19–340+), the volume serves as a structured daily curriculum for broad humanist literacy.

---

## Core Design & Intellectual Value

1. **Micro-Habit Polymathy**: Applies daily devotional structure to secular liberal-arts education—transforming 5-minute daily reading into a panoramic mental timeline of world history.
2. **Cross-Domain Pattern Recognition**: Juxtaposes monarchs, philosophers, inventors, and iconoclasts week by week, helping readers spot recurring patterns of patronage, dissent, and paradigm shifts across centuries.
3. **Accessible Reference Gateway**: Pairs each concise sketch with "Additional Facts" pointers that act as springboards into deeper primary biographies.
