## Summary
Master pedagogical and financial engineering curriculum on private equity and venture capital. Compiles Stefano Caselli's acclaimed Bocconi University course modules (Modules 1, 2, and 3), covering European fund structures (FCPR, SICAV, limited partnerships), company valuation methodologies, debt covenants, and complete financial modeling templates for Leveraged Buyouts (LBOs).

## Analytical Overview
This volume provides a rigorous, textbook-level operational foundation in the corporate finance, legal mechanisms, and quantitative modeling of private equity and venture capital.

Key pedagogical modules:
1. **Fund Structuring & Regulatory Architectures**: Details Caselli’s Module 1 curriculum, contrasting Anglo-Saxon limited partnership agreements (LPs/GPs, 2-and-20 fee structures, hurdle rates, catch-ups, and European waterfall vs. American deal-by-deal carry distributions) with continental European closed-end vehicle regulations.
2. **Venture Capital Investment & Deal Valuation**: Covers Caselli’s Module 2, exploring seed, early-stage, and expansion financing, post-money valuation mechanics, convertible notes, anti-dilution protections (full ratchet vs. weighted average), and liquidation preferences.
3. **Leveraged Buyout (LBO) Architecture & Modeling**: Features Caselli’s Module 3 and accompanying financial spreadsheet models, deconstructing senior secured bank debt, mezzanine tranches, high-yield bonds, debt service coverage ratios (DSCR), and value creation drivers (EBITDA margin expansion, multiple arbitrage, debt paydown).
