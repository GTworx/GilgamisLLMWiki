## Analytical Overview
### The Maritime Inflection Point in Turkish Foreign Policy

*The Mavi Marmara Flotilla Raid: Maritime Law, Siege Warfare, and the Rupture of Turkish-Israeli Strategic Ties* examines the May 31, 2010 assault by Israeli naval commandos (Shayetet 13) on the Turkish civilian vessel *Mavi Marmara* in international waters, resulting in the deaths of ten Turkish humanitarian activists.

### Legal and Strategic Reverberations

The dossier analyzes:
- **International Humanitarian Law:** Reviewing the legality of the Israeli naval blockade of Gaza under the San Remo Manual on Armed Conflicts at Sea, contrasting the findings of the UN Human Rights Council with the Palmer Commission Report.
- **The Collapse of Strategic Alignment:** How the incident permanently dismantled the 1990s Turkish-Israeli military and intelligence alliance, propelling Turkey into its leadership role advocating for Palestinian sovereignty across the Global South.
- **Diplomatic Resolution Mechanics:** Analyzing the 2013 Obama-brokered apology by Netanyahu and the subsequent 2016 compensation agreement.
