# Canaanite, Israelite, and Levantine Population Demography: Genetic Continuity, Philistine Assimilation, and Forced Dispersions

## Summary
Demographic and archaeogenetic synthesis examining Bronze Age Canaanite genetic continuity among modern Jews and Palestinians, post-Iron Age Philistine genetic assimilation, and comparative historical tables of forced population dispersions.

## Analytical Overview
Combines whole-genome ancient DNA evidence demonstrating shared Canaanite roots between contemporary Levantine Jewish and Palestinian populations with demographic models of Philistine urban decline and assimilation. Features comparative structural tables cross-referencing Assyrian, Babylonian, Roman, and Ottoman forced population transfers.
