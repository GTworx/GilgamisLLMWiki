## Summary
Monumental 6-volume, 212-page economic history compendium charting thirty years of macroeconomic transformation across the fifteen post-Soviet republics, from 1990s shock therapy and Russia's 1998 sovereign debt default to Central Asian energy divergence.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the transition from command economies to market systems: the voucher privatization chaotic transfer of state assets to oligarchs, the collapse of interstate trade, the 1998 GKO treasury bill default, and subsequent fiscal recovery. Contrasts institutional trajectories in the Baltic states with resource-dependent Central Asian petrostates and Belarus.
