## Analytical Overview
### The Anatomy of the First High-Speed Social Media Bank Run

The March 13, 2023 edition of *Bloomberg Businessweek* captures the ground-shaking collapse of **Silicon Valley Bank (SVB)**—the 16th largest bank in the United States and the financial heartbeat of the global venture capital ecosystem. Unfolding over mere 48 hours, the collapse marked the first hyper-accelerated digital bank run in financial history.

### Duration Mismatch and Unhedged Long-Term Treasuries

Businessweek delivers a forensic breakdown of SVB's fatal balance sheet mechanics:
- Inundated with $130 billion in zero-interest tech deposits during the 2020-2021 pandemic boom, SVB locked hundreds of billions into long-dated fixed-rate mortgage-backed securities and US Treasuries.
- When the Federal Reserve hiked interest rates by 500 basis points to fight inflation, SVB's hold-to-maturity bond portfolio sustained over $15 billion in unhedged unrealized losses, wiping out its equity capital.
- A sudden deposit run orchestrated over Twitter and private venture Slack channels drained $42 billion in 10 hours, forcing the FDIC to seize the institution.

### Systemic Aftermath and Contagion Containment

The issue reports on the emergency joint intervention by the Treasury, Fed, and FDIC guaranteeing all uninsured depositors, while detailing the ensuing panic that swallowed Signature Bank and forced the shotgun wedding of Credit Suisse and UBS in Switzerland.
