## Executive Summary

Promulgated by the High Council of Planning and coordinated by AFAD with Türkiye's premier Earthquake Advisory Board, this 84-page official document (UDSEP 2012–2023) established the nation's multi-decade strategic vision for earthquake risk mitigation following the 2011 Van earthquakes. The plan was formulated around three overarching strategic axes: Learning About Earthquakes (seismological research), Safe Living and Earthquake-Resilient Settlements (urban planning and structural engineering), and Coping with the Consequences of Earthquakes (emergency response and recovery).

UDSEP-2023 codified 13 strategic goals, 29 objectives, and 87 detailed action items, creating the institutional mandate for updating the national building seismic code (which culminated in TBDY 2018), expanding the national seismic monitoring network to over 1,000 stations, enforcing compulsory earthquake insurance (DASK), and mandating seismic vulnerability screenings across public schools and hospitals.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Three Strategic Pillars**: Balances scientific investigation of seismotectonics with urban planning enforcement and catastrophe financing mechanisms.
- **Seismic Code and Inspection Upgrades**: Directly mandated the revision of the 2007 earthquake code, the modernization of Law No. 4708 on building inspection, and the certification of civil engineers.
- **National Seismic Network Densification**: Programmed the expansion of strong-motion and weak-motion recording stations across the North Anatolian and East Anatolian fault systems.
- **Historical Assessment and Accountability**: Established an annual auditing framework to evaluate municipal and ministerial progress in inventorying vulnerable building stocks.
