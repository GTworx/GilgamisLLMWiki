## Summary
Two-volume macro rates and market structure study evaluating yield curve steepening/flattening trades (Twist and Shout) and the IMF special analysis on the disconnect between soaring financial asset prices and lagging real GDP during emergency liquidity injections.

## Analytical Overview
Examines fixed-income trading strategies navigating shifts in Federal Reserve policy rates, quantitative tightening, and inflation expectations. Details how historic central bank asset purchases during the 2020 pandemic compressed credit spreads and generated unprecedented asset price inflation while small business insolvency risks escalated across the real economy.
