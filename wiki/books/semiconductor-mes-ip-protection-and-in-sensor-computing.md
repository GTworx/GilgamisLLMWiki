## Summary
Technical and architectural compendium on advanced semiconductor manufacturing execution systems (MES), silicon design intellectual property protection against reverse engineering and hardware trojans, and breakthrough phononic metamaterials performing passive, zero-power speech classification directly inside sensors.

## Analytical Overview
This work covers the intersection of smart gigafab operations, microelectronic intellectual property defense, and next-generation neuromorphic edge sensing. The first section examines the digital nervous system of leading-edge semiconductor fabrication: Advanced Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES). In 300mm wafer fabs processing thousands of steps across photolithography, atomic layer deposition (ALD), chemical-mechanical planarization (CMP), and dry plasma etching, modern MES platforms leverage real-time edge telemetry, automated recipe management, and predictive fault detection to maximize overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) and accelerate defect-learning cycles.

The second section addresses the existential threat of hardware counterfeiting, reverse engineering, and IP piracy in a fragmented global fabless-foundry ecosystem. Authors Johann Knechtel and Satwik Patnaik provide a definitive overview of hardware security primitives: logic locking (inserting key-gates into combinational logic), IC camouflaging (manufacturing layout cells that appear identical under SEM delayering), and split manufacturing (fabricating sensitive front-end-of-line transistor layers in an untrusted commercial foundry while routing back-end metal layers in a secure domestic facility).

The volume culminates in a groundbreaking physical demonstration published in *Advanced Functional Materials* by Dubček and colleagues: in-sensor passive speech classification enabled by phononic metamaterials. By engineering acoustic resonant cavities and mechanical metamaterial filters directly onto silicon micro-electro-mechanical systems (MEMS), incoming spoken acoustic phonemes are mechanically decomposed and classified prior to any analog-to-digital conversion (ADC), eliminating active DSP power consumption and achieving near-zero-energy acoustic wake-word detection for ultra-low-power IoT nodes.
