## Executive Summary

Authored by Seraf co-founders and veteran angel/venture investors **Hambleton Lord** and **Christopher Mirabile** (2018, Seraf LLC, pp. 2–5), *Venture Capital: A Practical Guide* is a hands-on operational manual for emerging venture-capital general partners (GPs), fund managers, and institutional limited partners (LPs). Moving past Silicon Valley folklore into the actual plumbing of fund management, the guide covers thesis design, LP fundraising, GP time allocation, legal/accounting structures, fee and carry mechanics, and transparent LP reporting—capped by practical capitalization-table waterfall and fund-modeling appendices (p. 4).

---

## Seven-Chapter Operational Playbook (Contents, p. 4)

1. **Building a Winning VC Fund Strategy (p. 5)**: Defines stage focus, check size, reserve ratios for follow-on pro-rata rounds, and portfolio-construction math driven by power-law returns.
2. **How to Raise a VC Fund (p. 18)**: Maps LP segmentation (family offices, endowments, funds-of-funds), pitch-deck diligence, anchor-investor strategy, and closing timelines.
3. **Time Management, Legal/Accounting & Team Skills (Chapters 3–5, pp. 41–81)**: Details the relentless balancing act between deal sourcing, board governance, and LP relations; reviews LPAs, 409A/ASC 820 fair-value marks, and fund-administration tooling.
4. **Venture Fund Economics & Reporting (Chapters 6–7 & Appendices, pp. 82–174)**: Explains management fees, recycling capital, J-curve dynamics, TVPI/DPI/IRR metrics, carried-interest waterfalls, and cap-table dilution modeling.
