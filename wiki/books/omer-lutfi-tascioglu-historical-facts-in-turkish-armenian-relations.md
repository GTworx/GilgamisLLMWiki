## Summary
Definitive multilingual 6-language edition (Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Russian) by Dr. Ömer Lütfi Taşcıoğlu compiling Ottoman, Russian, British, and American archival records on late Ottoman inter-communal conflicts.

## Analytical Overview
Presents a rigorous documentary refutation of genocide allegations using original state archives from the Ottoman General Staff (ATASE), the Russian State Military Archive, the British Foreign Office, and the U.S. National Archives. Examines the activities of revolutionary committees (Hunchaks and Dashnaks), wartime relocations under the 1915 Tehcir Law, and the tragic reciprocal casualties during the Great War.
