## Overview
This empirical survey and computational social science study analyzes public sentiment, expectations, and deep anxieties regarding the emergence of the metaverse across diverse demographic cohorts. The researchers evaluate willingness to adopt VR hardware, moral concerns over digital addiction, and public skepticism regarding corporate ownership of virtual public squares.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Reveals widespread public ambivalence: high fascination with entertainment immersion paired with severe distrust of corporate data practices.
- **Key Finding**: Identifies generational divides in avatar identification and willingness to spend fiat currency on virtual fashion.
- **Key Finding**: Highlights cultural fears of social atomization and physical community abandonment in favor of synthetic digital spaces.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The general public views the metaverse through a dual lens of cinematic awe and dystopian foreboding, fearing that physical isolation will be commodified as synthetic digital intimacy."
