## Summary
In-depth historiographical analysis of the emergence of Russian ethnic identity, tracing the synthesis of East Slavic tribes, Scandinavian Varangians, Finno-Ugric substrate, and Golden Horde institutional influences.

## Analytical Overview
Reconstructs the multi-phase ethnogenesis of Russians from the 9th-century Kievan Rus' confederation through the Mongol conquest of Vladimir-Suzdal and the rise of the Muscovite Tsardom. Deconstructs Normanist vs. anti-Normanist controversies, Byzantine Christianization, and the imperial myth of 'triune Russian nationality' (Great, Little, and White Russians).
