## Summary

## Analytical Overview
### Forensic Anatomy of the 2024 American Presidential Election

*US Presidential Election 2024: Swing State Dynamics, Candidate Attributes, and the Conservative Coalition* compiles four comprehensive polling dossiers and analytical treatises capturing the voter psychology, economic grievances, and swing-state alignments that shaped the 2024 presidential race between Donald Trump and Kamala Harris.

### Key Polling and Sociological Dimensions

The compendium evaluates the decisive factors:
- **AtlasIntel Swing-State Final Polls:** Granular, cross-tabulated sampling across Pennsylvania, Michigan, Wisconsin, Georgia, North Carolina, Arizona, and Nevada, accurately capturing the rightward shift among working-class, Latino, and non-college-educated men.
- **YouGov Presidential Qualities Index:** Measuring voter perceptions of economic competence, leadership strength, cognitive stamina, and authenticity.
- **Why Half of America Votes for Trump:** An unsentimental sociological analysis dissecting inflation fatigue, southern border security anxieties, cultural resentment against progressive elite credentialism, and working-class disillusionment with foreign military interventions.
