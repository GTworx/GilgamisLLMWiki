## Executive Summary
Published by the World Health Organization Regulation and Prequalification Department, this monumental 350-page regulatory governance manual represents the primary global instrument for evaluating the overarching maturity and effectiveness of National Regulatory Authorities (NRAs) governing medicines, vaccines, and medical products.

The Global Benchmarking Tool (GBT Revision VI) establishes standardized objective indicators across nine core regulatory functions: National Regulatory System governance, Registration and Marketing Authorization, Vigilance (Pharmacovigilance), Market Surveillance and Control, Licensing Establishments, Regulatory Inspection, Laboratory Testing, Clinical Trials Oversight, and NRA Lot Release. It defines a four-tier Maturity Level classification (ML 1 to ML 4), distinguishing between fragmented agencies and fully functioning, advanced regulatory systems capable of operating as regional and global reference benchmarks.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Four-Tier Maturity Framework**: Defines Maturity Level 1 (ad hoc operational elements), Level 2 (evolving regulatory systems), Level 3 (stable, well-functioning regulatory systems), and Level 4 (operating at advanced international standards with continuous improvement).
- **Institutional Accountability and Integrity**: Mandates transparent public disclosure of regulatory approval rationales, strict conflict-of-interest policies, and legal protection against regulatory capture.
- **Risk-Based Inspection and Market Surveillance**: Articulates protocols for prioritizing factory inspections (GMP), post-market quality testing, and the rapid recall of substandard and falsified (SF) medical products.
- **Regulatory Reliance and Convergence**: Formulates frameworks allowing resource-constrained national regulators to rely upon evaluations, inspections, and lot-release certifications from WHO-listed authorities (WLAs).
