## Summary
Davos 2024 flagship investigation revealing that the world's five richest men doubled their fortunes since 2020 while five billion people grew poorer, analyzing corporate monopoly concentration and defining absolute vs. relative poverty.

## Analytical Overview
Details how monopoly power across pharmaceuticals, Big Tech, food, and energy transfers wealth from workers and consumers to ultra-wealthy shareholders through share buybacks, aggressive tax avoidance, and union-busting. Proposes a comprehensive public power agenda: break up monopolies, implement permanent wealth and windfall profits taxes, and expand public ownership of essential services.
