## Summary
Engineering and industrial hardware compendium examining the physical devices and optics underpinning the spatial computing revolution. Synthesizes key reports across augmented reality (AR) industrial smartglasses, virtual reality (VR) standalone headsets, inside-out SLAM tracking, waveguide optics, micro-OLED displays, and the consumer and enterprise adoption cycles across healthcare, field service, and simulation.

## Analytical Overview
This volume provides a thorough technical and market survey of the physical hardware enabling the Spatial Web: optical engines, tracking sensors, display silicon, and enterprise form factors. Synthesizing four foundational industry intelligence reports (preserved across multiple library archival editions):
1. **Emerging Applications in Augmented Reality**: Details optical waveguide architectures (diffractive, reflective, holographic), micro-LED efficiency curves, and field-of-view (FOV) engineering trade-offs required for day-long outdoor smartglasses versus indoor heads-up displays (HUDs).
2. **Future Applications of Immersive Computing**: Analyzes sensor fusion pipelines integrating 6-degree-of-freedom (6DoF) inside-out visual inertial odometry (VIO), high-refresh-rate eye tracking for foveated rendering, and low-latency wireless transmission protocols (Wi-Fi 6E/7 and 5G mmWave).
3. **Headset Growth & Killer Applications**: Documents the hardware cycle following the launch of first-generation commercial VR headsets, mapping out enterprise ROI across surgical training, aerospace maintenance, remote collaborative CAD design, and military tactical simulation.
