## Analytical Overview
### International Law and the Corporate Infrastructure of Occupation

*From Economy of Occupation to Economy of Genocide: Corporate Complicity, Academic Ties, and Legal Accountability*, published by the United Nations Human Rights Council (Report A/HRC/59/23, 2025), delivers an exhaustive forensic investigation into the economic, technological, and legal structures sustaining the Israeli occupation and military operations in Gaza.

### Core Findings of UN Report A/HRC/59/23

The report, spearheaded by UN Special Rapporteur Francesca Albanese, provides documented evidence across three domains:
- **Corporate Arms Transfers & Dual-Use Technology:** Documenting multinational defense contractors, surveillance software vendors, and cloud computing providers providing military hardware and AI targeting telemetry utilized in residential urban warfare.
- **Corporate Extraction & Real Estate Complicity:** Identifying global financial institutions, heavy machinery manufacturers (Caterpillar, Hyundai), and tourist booking portals profiting from illegal settlements in the West Bank and East Jerusalem.
- **Legal Liability of Third-State Officials:** Analyzing the legal accountability of American and European government officials under the 1948 Genocide Convention and the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court (ICC) for aiding and abetting war crimes through continuous weapons transfers.

### Definitive Legal Reference

The dossier serves as an essential primary document for international criminal tribunals, divestment campaigns, and universal jurisdiction cases.
