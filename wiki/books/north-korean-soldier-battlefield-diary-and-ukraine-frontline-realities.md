## Executive Summary
Published in 2025 by *The Wall Street Journal*, this poignant investigative dispatch reconstructs the battlefield diary recovered from the body of a fallen North Korean soldier deployed to the Russian frontline in the Kursk border offensive. The diary documents the brutal tactical realities of modern high-intensity attrition warfare: language barriers between Russian commanders and Korean infantry, total absence of air support, continuous First-Person-View (FPV) kamikaze drone surveillance, heavy artillery fragmentation, and catastrophic casualty rates. The analysis explores the geopolitical alliance between Pyongyang and Moscow exchanging cannon-fodder troops for Russian missile, nuclear, and satellite technology.
