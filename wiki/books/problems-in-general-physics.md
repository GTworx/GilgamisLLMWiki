## Executive Summary

*Problems in General Physics* (published in English by Mir Publishers, Moscow, 1981, translated from the 1979 Russian *Nauka* edition by Yuri Atanov; 402 pp., ISBN `5-03-000800-4`) by Soviet physicist and Moscow Engineering Physics Institute (MEPhI) professor [[igor-yevgenyevich-irodov]] is one of the most revered and demanding problem compendiums in the global history of physics education (source: BOOKS/Physics/1981 Irodov-Problems_in_General_Physics.pdf, pp. 8–11). Used for decades by International Physics Olympiad contenders, IIT-JEE aspirants, and university physics honors students worldwide, the book contains nearly **1,900 concise, conceptually penetrating problems** spanning the complete architecture of undergraduate general physics—complete with Mendeleev's Periodic Table, physical constants, concise formula summaries, and compact analytical answers (source: BOOKS/Physics/1981 Irodov-Problems_in_General_Physics.pdf, pp. 6–11, 51).

Unlike plug-and-chug textbook drill sets, Irodov's problems across **six canonical parts**—(1) *Physical Fundamentals of Mechanics* (kinematics, dynamics, conservation laws, celestial mechanics, rigid bodies, relativistic mechanics), (2) *Thermodynamics and Molecular Physics*, (3) *Electrodynamics*, (4) *Oscillations and Waves*, (5) *Optics*, and (6) *Atomic and Nuclear Physics*—strip away verbal scaffolding to force students to derive differential equations, exploit conservation symmetries, and apply calculus fluently to genuine physical systems (source: BOOKS/Physics/1981 Irodov-Problems_in_General_Physics.pdf, pp. 10–11, 51).

## Key Arguments / Chapter Breakdown

* **Parts 1–2: Mechanics, Special Relativity & Thermodynamics (pp. 12–130)**:
  * *First-Principles Derivation*: Tests vector kinematics, non-inertial frames, orbital mechanics (e.g., Problem 1.200 deriving planetary revolution period from heliocentric velocity), rigid-body dynamics, relativistic collisions, kinetic theory, and entropy laws (source: BOOKS/Physics/1981 Irodov-Problems_in_General_Physics.pdf, p. 51).

* **Parts 3–5: Electrodynamics, Oscillations, Waves & Optics (pp. 131–285)**:
  * *Fields & Wave Interference*: Challenges problem-solvers with electrostatic symmetry, Maxwell's equations, electromagnetic induction, coupled/damped oscillators, acoustics, wave interference, diffraction, and polarization (source: BOOKS/Physics/1981 Irodov-Problems_in_General_Physics.pdf, pp. 10–11).

* **Part 6 & Reference Tables: Atomic, Quantum & Nuclear Physics (pp. 286–402)**:
  * *Microscopic Physics*: Transitions into blackbody radiation, photoelectric/Compton scattering, Bohr–de Broglie quantization, Schrödinger wave mechanics, radioactive decay, and nuclear reactions, supported by Mendeleev's periodic table and analytical answer keys (source: BOOKS/Physics/1981 Irodov-Problems_in_General_Physics.pdf, pp. 6–7, 10–11).
