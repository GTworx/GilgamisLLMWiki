## Executive Summary

Curated from Harvard Business Review's acclaimed *Women at Work* podcast (introduced by cohosts Emily Caulfield and Amy Gallo, pp. 5–9) alongside research essays by scholars including **Amy C. Edmondson**, **Dorie Clark**, **Laura Morgan Roberts**, and **Amy Jen Su**, this three-book omnibus (2022) equips women professionals and organizational allies with evidence-based strategies for navigating workplace bias, cultivating sponsorship, and exercising inclusive leadership.

---

## Three-Book Structure (Contents, p. 5)

1. ***Making Real Connections***: Reframes networking around trust, **hybrid psychological safety** (Amy C. Edmondson), inclusion, repairing broken trust, overcoming networking friction for women and people of color, and distinguishing active **sponsorship** (advocating in rooms where decisions happen) from passive mentoring (TOC).
2. ***Speak Up, Speak Out***: Addresses voice, credit-claiming, navigating double binds (assertiveness vs. likability), confronting microaggressions, and advocating effectively in high-stakes meetings.
3. ***You, the Leader***: Guides transitions into senior authority, executive presence without conformity, setting sustainable boundaries, and leading structural equity across teams.
