## Executive Summary
This monumental 89-page compensation and corporate governance report provides the definitive empirical benchmarking survey of executive compensation, base salaries, annual bonuses, equity ownership, and talent retention across the global digital healthcare and HealthTech startup sector.

Surveying hundreds of digital health companies across Seed, Series A through D, and growth stages, the study analyzes compensation trends across C-suite roles (CEO, CMO, CTO, CFO, Chief Commercial Officer). It documents the post-ZIRP recalibration of equity grants, the rise of performance-vesting equity, severance standards, and the intense market competition for Chief Medical Officers (CMOs) possessing both clinical regulatory credibility and enterprise software sales acumen.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **C-Suite Cash vs. Equity Trade-Offs**: Benchmarks cash compensation and equity grant percentages across venture funding rounds, demonstrating equity dilution dynamics from founder-led early stages to late-stage growth rounds.
- **The Critical Role of the Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**: Details how the CMO role transitioned from an advisory figurehead into a critical executive driving clinical evidence validation, FDA/HIPAA compliance, and enterprise provider sales.
- **Post-2022 Market Correction Impact**: Analyzes down-rounds, recaps, and option repricing strategies deployed by HealthTech boards to retain mission-critical engineering and clinical talent.
- **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Metrics**: Tracks executive gender pay gaps and minority board representation, noting progressive improvements in female C-suite recruitment across digital health compared to traditional biotech.
