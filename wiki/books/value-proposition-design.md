## Executive Summary

Created by Strategyzer co-founders **Alexander Osterwalder** and **Alan Smith**, University of Lausanne professor **Yves Pigneur**, and **Greg Bernarda** (with visual architecture by Trish Papadakos, copyright pp. 4–5, Core Team p. 306), *Value Proposition Design* is the hands-on sequel to *Business Model Generation*. Zooming into the two most critical blocks of the **Business Model Canvas**—**Customer Segments** and **Value Propositions** (p. 18)—the book equips product teams, founders, and corporate innovators with a shared visual grammar to eliminate the #1 cause of innovation waste: building products nobody wants.

Structured around four deeply visual, workshop-ready chapters—**1. Canvas** (pp. 30–93), **2. Design** (pp. 94–201), **3. Test** (pp. 202–283), and **4. Evolve** (pp. 284–303)—the book transforms customer empathy and Lean experimentation into repeatable tools such as the **Value Proposition Canvas**, **Test Cards**, **Learning Cards**, and the **Progress Board**.

## Core Visual Frameworks & Testing System

1. **The Value Proposition Canvas (Ch. 1, pp. 30–93)**: Aligns the **Customer Profile** (*Customer Jobs* [functional, social, emotional], *Pains*, and *Gains*) against the **Value Map** (*Products & Services*, *Pain Relievers*, and *Gain Creators*) to engineer three progressive tiers of fit: **Problem–Solution Fit**, **Product–Market Fit**, and **Business Model Fit** (pp. 68–86).
2. **Prototyping & Customer Discovery (Ch. 2, pp. 94–201)**: Combines napkin sketching, six customer-insight modes (Data Detective, Journalist, Anthropologist, Impersonator, Co-creator, Scientist), B2B stakeholder mapping, and structured critique (de Bono's Six Thinking Hats, Dotmocracy).
3. **Systematic Evidence Generation (Ch. 3, pp. 202–283)**: Extracts critical "kill-switch" hypotheses and validates them through an **Experiment Library** (ad tracking, landing pages, MVP catalogs, split tests, Innovation Games, mock sales, presales) tracked via standardized **Test Cards** and **Learning Cards** (pp. 232–265).
4. **Continuous Reinvention (Ch. 4, pp. 284–303)**: Demonstrates through case studies (Azuri Solar, Owlet, Taobao) how established firms continuously refresh value propositions before market disruption erodes them.
