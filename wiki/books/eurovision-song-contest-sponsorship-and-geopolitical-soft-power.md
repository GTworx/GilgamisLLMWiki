## Overview
This investigative media and corporate finance report audits commercial sponsorship networks, television advertising contracts, and geopolitical friction surrounding the Eurovision Song Contest. The study analyzes the intersection of corporate brand marketing (e.g., Moroccanoil), state soft power broadcasting, and civil society boycotts in polarized international cultural events.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Maps the financial dependency of the European Broadcasting Union (EBU) on corporate title sponsorships.
- **Key Finding**: Examines how international cultural pageants become battlegrounds for geopolitical legitimacy and narrative warfare.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes brand vulnerability when commercial sponsors are targeted by international consumer boycotts.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Eurovision claims to be non-political, but its corporate sponsorship contracts and tele-voting mechanisms make it one of the most politically charged stages on earth."
