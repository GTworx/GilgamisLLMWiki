## Overview
This investigative legal and technological report analyzes the transition from web-based targeted advertising to immersive surveillance capitalism in social VR. The authors expose how eye-tracking, pupillometry, vestibular kinetics, and room-scale LiDAR spatial scans create inescapable behavioral micro-targeting matrices.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Documents the biometric telemetry captured by commercial VR headsets: 200+ distinct movement data points every minute.
- **Key Finding**: Demonstrates that unconscious involuntary reactions (pupil dilation, emotional micro-expressions) can be inferred in real-time.
- **Key Finding**: Calls for strict legal firewalls separating spatial telemetry from ad-auction algorithmic pipelines.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "In 2D surveillance capitalism, platforms tracked what you clicked; in the social metaverse, platforms record how your nervous system reacts before you are even conscious of the thought."
