## Executive Summary
Authored by Andres Perez and PitchBook Emerging Tech Research, this 32-page market intelligence compendium analyzes the economics and venture investment dynamics of commercial spaceflight and defense technology entering late 2024. The research audits the radical collapse in launch costs per kilogram into Low Earth Orbit (LEO) spearheaded by SpaceX's reusable Falcon 9 and Starship architectures. The briefings detail the proliferation of satellite mega-constellations (Starlink, Kuiper), commercial space stations, optical laser space relays, and orbital manufacturing, while mapping surging private capital flows into defense-tech startups (Anduril, Shield AI) modernizing sovereign defense procurement.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **The Starship Cost Collapse**: Fully reusable heavy launch vehicles are projected to lower LEO launch costs from $2,500/kg (Falcon 9) to under $200/kg, fundamentally altering the economics of orbital industrialization.
- **LEO Mega-Constellations**: Satellite broadband constellations transform space into a critical global telecommunications layer, while raising orbital debris and space traffic management challenges.
- **Defense Tech Venture Inflection**: Venture capital investment in defense hardware and dual-use space technologies has surged 3x since 2021, breaking the legacy prime contractor oligopoly.
- **Orbital Sovereign Domain**: Low-Earth orbit is recognized as an active domain of geopolitical competition, driving demand for responsive space launch and on-orbit servicing.
