## Overview
This specialized applied linguistic and lexicographical dossier analyzes the syntactic structure and semantic productivity of Persian compound verbs (fe'l-e morakkab), pairing auxiliary verbs (kardan, shodan, dāshtan) with nominal bases. Accompanying transliterated colloquial idiom datasets illustrate the romantic, poetic, and intimate expressive registers of contemporary Persian speech.
