## Analytical Overview
### The Diagnostic Discipline of Top-Performing CEOs

In *CEO Excellence: How Exceptional Leaders Assess and Elevate Their Own Performance*, McKinsey Senior Partners Carolyn Dewar, Scott Keller, and Vikram Malhotra synthesize their extensive empirical study of the 2,400 most successful global public company chief executives of the 21st century. The authors investigate how elite leaders evaluate their personal performance in a role notoriously isolated from honest upward feedback.

### The Six Responsibilities of the Chief Executive

The study maps how top CEOs structure their cognitive bandwidth across six core dimensions:
1. **Setting the Direction:** Re-framing vision around bold, asymmetric strategic bets rather than incremental annual plans.
2. **Aligning the Organization:** Managing organizational health with the same rigorous metric discipline applied to financial balance sheets.
3. **Mobilizing Through Leaders:** Assembling and coaching high-trust, unified executive teams.
4. **Engaging the Board:** Transforming the board of directors from an adversarial audit tribunal into a strategic sounding board.
5. **Connecting with Stakeholders:** Navigating corporate purpose and public scrutiny with authenticity.
6. **Managing Personal Effectiveness:** Ruthlessly guarding personal time, sleep, and physical energy to preserve high-stakes decision quality.

### Actionable Benchmarks for Corporate Leaders

The monograph provides self-assessment diagnostic rubrics enabling leaders to identify operational blind spots and elevate enterprise governance.
