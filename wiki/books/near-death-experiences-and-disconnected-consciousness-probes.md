## Overview
This two-study neuroscientific dossier (spanning 2020 and 2023) by Charlotte Martial, Steven Laureys, and the Coma Science Group investigates Near-Death Experiences (NDEs) as unique clinical probes for 'disconnected consciousness'—complex subjective awareness occurring in the absence of behavioral responsiveness during cardiac arrest and deep coma.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes core phenomenological features: out-of-body perceptions, tunnel experiences, life reviews, and profound peace.
- **Key Finding**: Evaluates neurochemical hypotheses: endogenous DMT release, temporal lobe seizures, hypercapnia, and cortical disinhibition.
- **Key Finding**: Establishes standardized clinical assessment tools (Greyson NDE Scale) separating authentic NDEs from drug delirium.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Near-death experiences challenge classical neuroscientific models of mind-brain identity, demonstrating that vivid, highly structured conscious experience can persist under clinical conditions of profound cerebral ischemia."
