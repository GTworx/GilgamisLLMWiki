## Summary
Advanced economic history study analyzing the institutional, fiscal, and scientific causes of the Great Divergence between 1000 and 1700, alongside econometric modeling of immigrant scholar flows in European knowledge clusters.

## Analytical Overview
Investigates why Western European states pulled ahead economically and technologically from the Qing Empire, the Ottoman Empire, and Mughal India. Models the role of university self-governance, polycentric legal fragmentation, secure property rights, and high-skilled religious refugee movements (e.g. Huguenots) in accelerating knowledge agglomerations.
