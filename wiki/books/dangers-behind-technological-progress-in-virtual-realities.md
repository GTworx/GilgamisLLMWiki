## Overview
This 2012 philosophical and sociological critique warns of the hidden cognitive and societal hazards accompanying rapid immersion into persistent virtual realities. Analyzing sensory disconnection, perceptual manipulation, and the atrophy of somatic presence, the monograph cautions against uncritically trading physical reality for algorithmic simulations.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Identifies cognitive dissociation risks arising from prolonged immersive spatial embodiment.
- **Key Finding**: Critiques the commercial capture of human attention through gamified virtual reality feedback loops.
- **Key Finding**: Advocates for philosophical friction and somatic grounding in the engineering of virtual environments.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "As simulations grow indistinguishable from somatic reality, the danger lies not in machine malfunction, but in human willing abandonment of the messy, uncurated physical world."
