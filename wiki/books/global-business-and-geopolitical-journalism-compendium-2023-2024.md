## Summary
Four-issue journalistic compendium investigating major corporate and geopolitical shifts: Barron's fixed-income strategies, Bloomberg Businessweek's deep dive into Mexican industrial nearshoring boom, and Guardian Weekly's global conflict analysis.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the economic realignment of North American supply chains as multinational manufacturers relocate production from China to northern Mexico under the USMCA. Analyzes Wall Street bond market re-pricing amidst higher yields, corporate debt refinancing hurdles, and international diplomatic responses to regional conflicts.
