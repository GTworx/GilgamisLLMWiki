## Summary
Historic 1923 self-critical manifesto delivered by the first Prime Minister of the Independent Armenian Republic to the Dashnagtsutyun General Congress, condemning the party's disastrous illusions regarding Western imperialist support.

## Analytical Overview
Katchaznouni presents an unsparing historical indictment of his own party's strategic errors, acknowledging that the Dashnaks blindly aligned with the Allied Entente powers, overestimated foreign military assistance, provoked Ottoman state security retaliation, and brought devastation upon their people. Calls for the voluntary dissolution of the party.
