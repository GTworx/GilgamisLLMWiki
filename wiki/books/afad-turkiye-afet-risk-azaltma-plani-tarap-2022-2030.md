## Executive Summary

The Disaster and Emergency Management Presidency (AFAD) published this 104-page master strategic framework (TARAP 2022–2030) as Türkiye's principal multi-hazard disaster risk reduction roadmap, fully aligned with the United Nations Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction (2015–2030). TARAP codifies a nationwide shift from reactive crisis response and post-disaster compensation to proactive, pre-disaster risk mitigation across all levels of public administration and the private sector.

The plan articulates 4 strategic goals, 17 objectives, and 227 concrete action items covering seismic hazards, floods, landslides, avalanches, industrial accidents, and climate-induced meteorological extremes. It bridges national governance with provincial execution through the Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction Plans (İRAP), establishing rigorous monitoring indicators, inter-ministerial accountability protocols, and spatial planning integrations.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Proactive Risk Reduction Paradigm**: Mandates institutional reorientation toward proactive mitigation, establishing that each unit of currency invested in risk reduction saves four to seven units in post-disaster reconstruction.
- **Inter-Agency Governance Matrix**: Assigns primary and support responsibilities across ministries, municipal administrations, universities, and civil society organizations with strict delivery milestones through 2030.
- **Provincial Alignment via İRAP**: Synchronizes 81 provincial action plans (İRAP) into the national TARAP framework, ensuring uniform risk assessment methodologies and vulnerability remediation priorities.
- **Critical Infrastructure Hardening**: Prioritizes seismic and hydraulic retrofitting of schools, hospitals, bridges, energy pipelines, and historic cultural monuments.
