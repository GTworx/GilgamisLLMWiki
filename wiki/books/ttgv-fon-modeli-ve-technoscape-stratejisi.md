## Summary
Dual-volume strategic policy and market architecture compendium by the Technology Development Foundation of Turkey (TTGV), establishing a novel public-private Fund of Funds (Üst Fon) model and the Technoscape foresight methodology.

## Analytical Overview
### Sovereign Risk-Capital Engineering and Technology Foresight

*TTGV Fon Modeli ve Technoscape Stratejisi*, published by the Technology Development Foundation of Turkey (TTGV), presents two foundational policy frameworks designed to propel Turkish industry into global technological leadership.

### The TTGV Public-Private Fund of Funds (Üst Fon) Model

The first treatise addresses the chronic market failure in Turkish venture capital: the severe shortage of institutional Limited Partners (LPs) capable of writing $10M+ checks into venture funds:
- TTGV formulates a structural **Fund-of-Funds (Üst Fon)** architecture where public funds partner with private industrial conglomerates to anchor emerging venture capital managers.
- By leveraging risk-sharing guarantees and disciplined corporate governance, the model crowds in private institutional capital while aligning investment mandates with national technology priorities.

### The Technoscape Foresight Methodology

The second treatise establishes **Technoscape**—a systematic technological foresight framework that maps emergent global technologies (advanced materials, climate engineering, industrial sensors) directly to the competitive capabilities of Turkish manufacturing industries.
