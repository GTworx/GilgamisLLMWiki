## Summary
Pioneering spatial criminology and GIS spatial analysis studying the geographical distribution, urban density correlations, and socioeconomic determinants of public order crimes across 81 provinces in Turkey.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes municipal police and gendarmerie statistical datasets to evaluate property crime vs. violent assault rates across urban Turkey. Examines how rapid internal migration, unplanned urbanization, demographic youth bulges, and regional economic disparities shape localized spatial clusters of crime in metropolitan centers.
