## Analytical Overview
### Global Youth Psychology and Political Values

*Generation Z: Global Citizenship and Youth Wellbeing*, compiled by the Varkey Foundation (121 pp.), presents one of the largest cross-cultural sociological surveys of young people (aged 15 to 21) ever conducted across twenty major developed and emerging nations.

### Universal Values vs. Regional Anxieties

The report captures the complex worldview of the first truly digitally native generation:
- **Global Identity vs. National Roots:** Gen Z expresses unprecedented support for human rights, gender equality, and climate action, while simultaneously experiencing profound psychological anxieties regarding economic security, housing affordability, and algorithmic social isolation.
- **The Dedicated Turkey Dossier:** Measures Turkish youth wellbeing (Warwick-Edinburgh score 51.52), documenting elevated concerns regarding youth unemployment, educational access, and political freedom, alongside strong familial solidarity.

### Sociological Diagnostic

The volume provides policymakers and sociologists with empirical data mapping how the emerging generation will reshape democratic elections and labor markets.
