## Summary
Authoritative 107-page independent review by former Fed Chair Ben Bernanke for the Court of Directors of the Bank of England, diagnosing post-COVID forecasting failures and designing new scenario-based monetary frameworks.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes why central bank forecasting models (COMPASS) failed to anticipate the 2021–2023 global inflation surge triggered by supply chain bottlenecks and energy price shocks. Bernanke recommends retiring the central bank fan chart in favor of publishing explicit alternative scenarios, upgrading software infrastructure, and reforming how monetary policy committees communicate economic uncertainty.
