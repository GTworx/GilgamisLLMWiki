## Summary
An exhaustive national intelligence and public health compendium compiling the official *Turkish Drug Report* (TUBIM / Department of Anti-Narcotics Crimes, 140 pp.), the UNODC National Assessment on Drug Abuse (Kamran Niaz), Mahmut Cengiz's Brookings analysis of Turkish narcotics transit routes, and European Monitoring Centre for Drugs and Drug Addiction (EMCDDA) country profiles.

## Analytical Overview
### Historical Context & Institutional Setting
Situated at the geographic crossroads between Afghan opiate production centers in Central Asia and lucrative consumer markets in Western Europe, Türkiye occupies the central axis of the historic 'Balkan Route'. Over the past two decades, the Turkish National Police (EGM) and its specialized monitoring center TUBIM developed one of the most sophisticated interdiction and addiction surveillance frameworks in Eurasia.

### Core Theoretical Foundations & Methodological Architecture
The compendium analyzes the dual challenge confronting Turkish drug policy: 1) Transnational Supply Interdiction (policing borders against high-tonnage opiate shipments, Captagon flows from Syria, and maritime cocaine pipelines); and 2) Domestic Demand Reduction (managing domestic addiction prevention, AMATEM treatment centers, and psychiatric rehabilitation). Mahmut Cengiz examines how regional geopolitical upheavals (the Syrian civil war, state fragmentation in Iraq and Afghanistan) transformed traditional trafficking routes.

### Key Empirical Findings, Case Studies & Frameworks
The 140-page TUBIM report documents unprecedented seizure volumes: Turkish law enforcement routinely seizes more illicit heroin and methamphetamine annually than all 27 European Union member states combined. The report details the lethal rise of synthetic cannabinoids (Bonzai) and methamphetamine, tracking mortality data, prison demographics, and AMATEM treatment capacity. UNODC field assessments in major cities provide sociological surveys of adolescent vulnerability and socio-economic deprivation.

### Critical Assessment, Methodological Rigor & Strategic Implications
This compendium is the definitive institutional reference on narcotics policy and trafficking geopolitics in the Eastern Mediterranean. Its detailed seizure statistics, demographic risk profiles, and historical route mappings provide indispensable visibility into the frontline of global drug interdiction.
