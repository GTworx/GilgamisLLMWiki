## Analytical Overview
### Mapping the Global Polycrisis Landscape

The *Global Risks Report 2024*, published by the World Economic Forum in collaboration with Marsh McLennan and Zurich Insurance Group, presents the findings of the Global Risks Perception Survey (GRPS) tracking the assessments of over 1,500 global experts, corporate leaders, and policymakers. The 2024 report outlines an increasingly fractured global landscape characterized by systemic risk compounding.

### The Short-Term vs. Long-Term Risk Horizon

The report articulates a striking divergence between immediate and decadal perils:
- **Top 2-Year Risk:** **AI-Generated Misinformation and Disinformation**, fueled by generative AI tools, threatening democratic electoral stability across dozens of major elections worldwide in 2024.
- **Top 10-Year Risks:** Dominated overwhelmingly by environmental collapse: **Extreme Weather Events, Critical Change to Earth Systems, and Biodiversity Loss**.
- **Geoeconomic Warfare:** Societal polarization, interstate armed conflict, and economic precarity compounding systemic friction.

### Frameworks for Multilateral Resilience

The report urges public and private leadership to move past reactive crisis management, outlining actionable governance architectures for boundary-spanning technological safety and planetary climate adaptation.
