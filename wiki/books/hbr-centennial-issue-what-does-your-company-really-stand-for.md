## Analytical Overview
### A Century of Managerial Capitalism

Marking the 100th anniversary of its founding in 1922, the November-December 2022 issue of *Harvard Business Review* (Vol. 100, No. 6) interrogates the central philosophical and operational question of 21st-century capitalism: **What Does Your Company Really Stand For?** Moving beyond Milton Friedman's pure shareholder primacy doctrine, the issue examines how corporate purpose, stakeholder capitalism, and moral responsibility integrate with long-term competitive advantage.

### Perspectives from Global Corporate Titans

The issue features original essays from eight world-leading CEOs (including Satya Nadella of Microsoft, Julie Sweet of Accenture, and Ajay Banga):
- Defining authentic corporate purpose that guides tough trade-offs during economic downturns rather than serving as performative marketing PR.
- Case study of Formula 1's Mercedes-AMG Petronas engineering dominance under Toto Wolff on high-performance organizational culture.
- Practical guidance on executive one-on-one coaching, talent retention, and cross-functional collaboration in hybrid work cultures.

### The Next Century of Management

The centennial edition synthesizes a century of management evolution, demonstrating that enduring market capitalization is ultimately sustained by organizational integrity and cultural trust.
