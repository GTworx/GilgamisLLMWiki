## Summary
Influential 65-page foresight report tracking five macro cultural trends: customer obsession decay, the generative AI talent divide, the homogenization of mass culture, and changing consumer priorities.

## Analytical Overview
Details how cost-cutting and automated customer service algorithms have alienated consumers ('the customer de-centered'). Analyzes the creative stagnation caused by algorithmic optimization across entertainment and interior design, the growing consumer desire for tactile authenticity, and corporate strategies to rebuild customer trust.
