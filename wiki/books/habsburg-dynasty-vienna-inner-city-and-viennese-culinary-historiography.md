## Summary
Exhaustive 9-source architectural, political, and culinary compendium surveying the Habsburg dynasty (1278–1918), complete urban walking tours of Vienna's Innere Stadt, and the Ottoman-Armenian origins of Viennese cafe culture.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the six-century rise and collapse of the House of Habsburg through the urban fabric of Vienna: St. Stephen's Cathedral, the Hofburg Palace, the Ringstraße, and historic defenses against Ottoman sieges (1529, 1683). Investigates culinary and mercantile myths, revealing how Armenian coffee merchant Johannes Diodato established Vienna's first licensed coffeehouse in 1685 and deconstructing the defense-myth origin of the croissant.
