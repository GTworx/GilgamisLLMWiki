## Summary
Specialized human capital, organizational design, and technological transformation compendium on private equity operating groups. Features Heidrick & Struggles' North American PE Operating Professional Compensation Survey, comprehensive playbooks on the role of the Operating Partner, and an executive guide to deploying Generative AI across portfolio company operations.

## Analytical Overview
This volume examines the operational arm of modern private equity: the operating partners and portfolio operations teams responsible for driving organic EBITDA expansion.

Key analytical components:
1. **The Operating Partner Compensation Architecture**: Features Heidrick & Struggles’ definitive survey benchmarking base salary, annual cash bonuses, and carried interest allocations for operating professionals across fund size tiers, contrasting dedicated functional specialists (sales, supply chain, digital) with generalist former CEOs.
2. **Portfolio Operations Governance Models**: Analyzes *The Operating Partner* playbook, contrasting structural engagement archetypes: internal advisory teams, external executive networks, and shared-services platforms, detailing the 100-day plan execution and board governance dynamics.
3. **Generative AI as a Value Creation Lever**: Integrates McKinsey and industry guides on deploying Generative AI across portfolio companies, evaluating immediate ROI in software development productivity, customer support automation, marketing personalization, and automated financial reporting.
