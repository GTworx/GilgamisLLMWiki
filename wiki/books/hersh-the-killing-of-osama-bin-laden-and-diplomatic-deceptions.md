## Analytical Overview
### Investigative Scrutiny of the 2011 Abbottabad Operation

*The Killing of Osama bin Laden: Intelligence Deceptions, Pakistani Complicity, and Sovereign Intrusion in Abbottabad* reviews and contextualizes Seymour M. Hersh's landmark investigative report published in the *London Review of Books* (2015).

### Challenging the Official White House Narrative

Hersh's investigation systematically challenged the official account of Operation Neptune Spear:
- **The Walk-In Informant vs. Courier Tracking:** Presenting evidence that bin Laden was not found through NSA electronic surveillance or tracking a courier, but was betrayed by a senior Pakistani intelligence officer (a "walk-in") seeking the $25 million CIA reward.
- **Pakistani Military Complicity:** Alleging that Pakistan's military leadership (General Kayani and General Pasha) held bin Laden under hostage detention in Abbottabad and secretly cleared the U.S. Navy SEAL helicopter raid in exchange for continued military aid.
- **The Politics of Declassified Heroic Narratives:** Analyzing how executive administrations construct cinematic raid narratives for domestic electoral consumption.
