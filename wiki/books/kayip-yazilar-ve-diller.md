## Executive Summary

Written by eminent German Hittitologist and ancient-Near-Eastern linguist **Johannes Friedrich** (1893–1972) and translated into Turkish with a scholarly preface by epigrapher **Recai Tekoğlu** (Arkeoloji ve Sanat Yayınları, İstanbul, 2000, pp. 2–6; 233 pp.), *Kayıp Yazılar ve Diller* (*Extinct Languages*) is the classic systematic history and methodological handbook of how modern philologists cracked the forgotten scripts and dead languages of antiquity.

Structured into two major sections (Contents, pp. 4–5)—**Part I: Three Great Decipherments Illuminating the Ancient Near East** (**Egyptian Hieroglyphs** via Champollion and the Rosetta Stone, **Mesopotamian Cuneiform** from Grotefend and Rawlinson's Behistun inscription to Sumerian/Akkadian/Hittite, and **Anatolian/Hittite Hieroglyphic [Luwian]** decipherment, pp. 14–128) and **Part II: Other Decipherments & Interpretations in the Ancient World** (**Lycian, Lydian, Sidetic, Numidian, Cypriot syllabary, Ugaritic alphabet, Cretan Linear B**, and methodological principles for evaluating undeciphered scripts like Etruscan and Indus Valley, pp. 129–233)—the work serves as an enduring primer in rigorous epigraphic method.

---

## Core Scientific & Methodological Insights

1. **Combinatorial & Bilingual Decipherment Logic**: Explains step by step how decipherers use proper names in bilinguals (Rosetta Stone, Karatepe Luwian-Phoenician bilingual), statistical sign-frequency analysis (distinguishing alphabets, syllabaries, and logograms), and grammatical paradigm mapping.
2. **Anatolia's Epigraphic Wealth**: Gives rich, authoritative attention to Anatolian scripts—Hittite cuneiform, Luwian hieroglyphs, Lycian, Lydian, and Sidetic—directly illuminating Türkiye's pre-classical linguistic heritage.
3. **Epistemic Guardrails Against Pseudo-Decipherment**: Establishes strict falsifiability standards showing why genuine decipherments yield verifiable, internally consistent grammar across *new* texts—contrasting rigorous philology with speculative etymological guesswork.
