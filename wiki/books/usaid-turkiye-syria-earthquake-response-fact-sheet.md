## Executive Summary

Published on March 2, 2023, by the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) Bureau for Humanitarian Assistance (BHA), this 10-page official operational fact sheet (Fact Sheet #9) summarizes the United States Government's comprehensive humanitarian, search-and-rescue, and logistical response to the February 6 earthquakes in southern Türkiye and northern Syria.

The report details the deployment of USAID's Disaster Assistance Response Team (DART) and urban search-and-rescue teams (Virginia Task Force 1 and Los Angeles County Fire Department), encompassing over 160 specialized personnel, rescue canines, and 170,000 pounds of specialized heavy rescue gear deployed to Adıyaman. It outlines the allocation of $185 million in USG emergency assistance, tracking cross-border humanitarian corridors into northwest Syria through Bab al-Hawa, Bab al-Salam, and Al-Rai.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **DART and USAR Deployment Mechanics**: Details the operational footprint of heavy international urban search and rescue (USAR) task forces equipped with seismic listening devices, search cameras, and specialized hydraulic breaching tools.
- **Cross-Border Humanitarian Logistics**: Documents the delicate diplomatic and logistical orchestration of multi-million dollar aid consignments traversing conflict lines into non-regime-controlled northwest Syria.
- **Water, Sanitation, and Shelter Operations**: Audits funding distribution across emergency shelter materials, water purification kits, winterization equipment, and clinical trauma response through partner UN agencies and NGOs.
- **Coordination with AFAD and UNDAC**: Highlights the liaison mechanisms linking US emergency responders with Turkish AFAD commanders and the UN Disaster Assessment and Coordination (UNDAC) on-site operations center.
