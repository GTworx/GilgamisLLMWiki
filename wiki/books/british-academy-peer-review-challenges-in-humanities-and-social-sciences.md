## Summary
Authoritative 61-page report evaluating the efficacy, fairness, cognitive biases, and systemic burdens of peer review in academic publishing and research grant allocation across the humanities and social sciences.

## Analytical Overview
Examines double-blind versus open peer review, interdisciplinary assessment hurdles, publication bias, reviewer fatigue, and journal citation metric distortion. Proposes systemic institutional reforms to preserve rigorous scholarly evaluation while preventing metric-driven conformity in qualitative and historical research disciplines.
