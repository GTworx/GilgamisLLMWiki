## Summary
Official Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences Nobel monograph celebrating Claudia Goldin's comprehensive 200-year historical dataset tracking female labor force participation, career-family trade-offs, and the parenthood penalty.

## Analytical Overview
Examines Goldin's discovery of the U-shaped curve of female labor participation during the transition from agrarian to industrial society. Explores how the contraceptive pill transformed educational expectations, why occupational 'greedy work' and temporal inflexibility sustain the modern gender earnings gap following childbirth, and structural solutions for workplace equity.
