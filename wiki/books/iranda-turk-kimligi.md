## Executive Summary

Published by the Center for Iranian Studies (İRAM) in Ankara (July 2026, 37 pp.), *İran'da Türk Kimliği* (*Turkish Identity in Iran*) is a cutting-edge demographic, sociolinguistic, and geopolitical analysis by researcher **Hamid Şehanegi** (edited by Merve Dilek Dağdelen and Büşra Yılmaz) investigating the contemporary status of Iran's substantial Turkic-speaking population (Azerbaijanis, Qashqais, Turkmens, Khalaj, and Afshars) comprising an estimated 30–35% of the country's population.

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **Millennium of Shared Sovereignty**: Reminds readers that Turks ruled Iran continuously from the tenth century until the fall of the Qajar dynasty in 1925—making Iranian history inextricably Turkic.
2. **Mother-Tongue Education as Civil Imperative**: Documents the growing peaceful civil demand for official mother-tongue schooling in Azerbaijani Turkish, challenging state Persianization policies.
3. **The Geopolitical Hinge**: Analyzes South Azerbaijan and Iran's Turkic communities as a crucial demographic factor shaping future Iranian internal stability and South Caucasus geopolitics.
