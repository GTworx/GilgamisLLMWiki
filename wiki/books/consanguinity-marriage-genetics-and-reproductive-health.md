## Summary
Authoritative two-volume epidemiological synthesis and WHO Geneva Workshop report detailing the genetic, reproductive, and socioeconomic dimensions of first-cousin and endogamous marriages.

## Analytical Overview
Reviews biological and clinical evidence from populations with high rates of consanguinity (Middle East, North Africa, South Asia). Quantifies the excess risk for autosomal recessive congenital disorders, infant mortality, and multifactorial diseases while analyzing social, financial, and family stability factors that sustain endogamy, proposing effective community genetic counseling models.
