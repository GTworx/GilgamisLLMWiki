## Summary
Critical historiographical investigation into the medieval and early modern intellectual tradition tracing the genealogical origin of the Turks back to the ancient Trojans (Teucri) and its ideological use in Ottoman and European statecraft.

## Analytical Overview
Traces how Frankish and Byzantine chroniclers (Fredegar, Vincent of Beauvais, Laonikos Chalkokondyles) linked both Western Europeans and Ottoman Turks to survivors of the Trojan War. Analyzes how Sultan Mehmed II utilized the 'revenge of Troy' narrative upon conquering Constantinople and visiting the ruins of Ilium in 1462.
