## Summary
Applied sector-by-sector exploration of spatial computing, digital twins, and immersive platforms across education, cultural heritage, and enterprise architecture. Highlights Christopoulos' ethical framework for augmented reality learning, museum deployment plans for interactive metaverse exhibitions, KPMG's strategic roadmap for enterprise XR, and early corporate blueprints following Facebook's corporate rebrand to Meta.

## Analytical Overview
This volume gathers applied research on the pragmatic implementation of spatial computing, IoT-connected digital twins, and immersive interfaces across civic, educational, and cultural institutions.

Key domains investigated:
1. **Ethical AR Learning Frameworks**: Analyzes Christopoulos et al.’s *AR Learning Ethical Framework* published in *Computers*, establishing design principles to prevent cognitive overload, biometric data extraction, and commercial persuasion in primary and secondary classrooms.
2. **Museum Informatics and Cultural Heritage Digital Twins**: Features technical deployment architectures for IoT-enabled metaverse museum exhibitions, exploring how photogrammetric 3D asset scanning, spatial audio, and interactive avatars allow global audiences to interact with fragile antiquities without physical contact.
3. **Corporate Pivot and Enterprise XR Strategy**: Compiles KPMG's *Future of XR* white paper alongside internal and public strategy dossiers following Facebook’s rebranding to Meta, dissecting the capital expenditure dynamics of building persistent enterprise collaboration environments and spatial productivity suites.
