## Summary
Two-volume sociological and legal investigation exploring how digital networks, algorithmic radicalization, and cultural narratives legitimize extremist political violence, while analyzing Section 230 platform liabilities.

## Analytical Overview
Deconstructs the shifting boundary between celebrated revolutionary heroism and vilified terrorism using popular culture case studies (such as Alan Moore's V for Vendetta). Examines the legal mechanisms of the Material Support to Foreign Terrorist Organizations statutes and Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act in confronting digital recruiting pipelines.
