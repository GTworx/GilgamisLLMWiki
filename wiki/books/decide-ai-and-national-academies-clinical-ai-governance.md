## Summary
Authoritative medical, regulatory, and algorithmic governance compendium spanning 50 specialized clinical AI papers. Centers around the international consensus DECIDE-AI reporting guidelines for early clinical evaluation of diagnostic systems, PNAS National Academy of Medicine foundational treatises by Dzau and Bertagnolli, AI deployment in radiation oncology, and hospital clinical decision support validation.

## Analytical Overview
This volume establishes the rigorous clinical, ethical, and methodological standards required before machine learning algorithms can be safely deployed at the patient bedside.

Key clinical standards:
1. **The DECIDE-AI Reporting Consensus**: Features the landmark consensus statement published in *Nature Medicine* and *The BMJ*, establishing a 17-item guideline for reporting the early-stage live clinical evaluation of AI decision support systems, mandating rigorous documentation of human-AI interaction, algorithmic error modes, safety events, and pilot clinical utility.
2. **National Academy of Medicine Strategic Vision**: Compiles Victor Dzau and Monica Bertagnolli’s *PNAS* treatises establishing the core institutional requirements for AI in health and medicine: eliminating algorithmic racial and socio-economic biases, securing multi-center validation cohorts, and ensuring multidisciplinary collaboration across computer scientists, clinicians, and ethicists.
3. **Radiation Oncology & Diagnostic Decision Support**: Examines specialized technical papers on automating radiation contouring, dosimetric optimization, and algorithmic auditing pipelines in live hospital workflows.
