## Summary
Definitive autonomous vehicles, corporate accountability, and legal forensics treatise. Pairs Waymo's technical breakthroughs in end-to-end multimodal foundation models for autonomous driving (EMMA) with the complete 195-page independent investigative report by Quinn Emanuel into the October 2023 Cruise robotaxi pedestrian accident in San Francisco, documenting technical sensor failures, corporate governance breakdowns, and regulatory deception.

## Analytical Overview
This volume provides an unprecedented dual perspective on the technical zenith and regulatory catastrophe of commercial autonomous driving.

Key technical and investigative dimensions:
1. **End-to-End Multimodal Driving (Waymo EMMA)**: Evaluates Waymo's *EMMA (End-to-End Multimodal Model for Autonomous Driving)*, which replaces traditional siloed pipelines (perception, tracking, prediction, motion planning) with unified vision-language-action models processing raw camera inputs and sensor telemetry directly into trajectory planning.
2. **The Quinn Emanuel Cruise Investigation**: Analyzes the monumental 195-page independent report commissioned by GM after a Cruise robotaxi dragged a pedestrian 20 feet following an initial impact by a human driver. Documents critical perception sensor blind spots, algorithmic misclassification of the pedestrian as a side-impact obstacle, corporate leadership's failure to disclose video footage to the California DMV, and the resulting nationwide grounding of Cruise's autonomous fleet.
