## Summary
Applied legal, operational, and strategic handbook for venture financing. Compiles HSBC Innovation Banking's authoritative 2025 VC Term Sheet Guide, Seraf Compass's empirical 'Angel Investing by the Numbers', EY Turkey's 2024 Corporate Venture Capital Strategic Management Report, and Stanford University research on government-backed venture investing in China.

## Analytical Overview
This volume provides a pragmatic, deal-level guide for founders, angel investors, and corporate venture capitalists navigating negotiations and fund architecture.

Key operational guides:
1. **HSBC Innovation Banking 2025 VC Term Sheet Guide**: Offers clause-by-clause legal analysis of standard venture agreements, detailing liquidation preferences (participating vs. non-participating, caps), protective provisions, board governance thresholds, drag-along rights, and founder vesting.
2. **Seraf Compass: Angel Investing by the Numbers**: Evaluates quantitative portfolio construction for angel investors, proving that positive returns require maintaining a diversified portfolio of 25–40+ investments and reserving 50–70% of total capital for follow-on rounds to defend against dilution.
3. **Corporate Venture Capital (CVC) & Sovereign Venture**: Incorporates EY Turkey’s *Kurumsal Girişim Sermayesi Stratejik Yönetimi Raporu* on structuring CVC funds for strategic synergy versus financial return, paired with Stanford research analyzing government guidance funds (*Government Guiding Funds*) and state capitalism in Chinese venture ecosystems.
