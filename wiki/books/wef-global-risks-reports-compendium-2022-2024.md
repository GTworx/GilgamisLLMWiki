## Summary
Comprehensive 241-page dual-volume risk assessment benchmark from the World Economic Forum charting short-term technological threats (AI disinformation, cyber warfare) against long-term planetary tipping points (extreme weather, biodiversity collapse).

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes data from the Global Risks Perception Survey to construct risk interconnection maps. Highlights AI-generated disinformation and societal polarization as the top short-term global risks facing the 2024 super-election cycle, while identifying environmental cataclysms, debt crises, and geoeconomic confrontation as the defining structural threats of the next decade.
