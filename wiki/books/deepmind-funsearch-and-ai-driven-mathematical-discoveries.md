## Summary
Breakthrough computational mathematics and algorithmic discovery compendium. Centers around DeepMind's landmark 2024 Nature paper 'Mathematical discoveries from program search with large language models' (FunSearch), which paired LLMs with automated evaluators to make genuine new discoveries in extremal combinatorics (the cap set problem and bin packing), alongside OpenAI's 'Ten Advances in Mathematics' and Claude Cycles.

## Analytical Overview
This volume documents the historic milestone where artificial intelligence produced genuine, verifiable mathematical discoveries that had eluded human mathematicians.

Key mathematical breakthroughs:
1. **The FunSearch Evolutionary Engine**: Features Romera-Paredes and Fawzi’s *Nature* 2024 paper introducing **FunSearch** (searching in function space). By pairing a code-generating language model with an automated programmatic evaluator, FunSearch iteratively evolves human-readable computer programs. Unlike black-box neural networks, FunSearch outputs explicit algorithms that mathematicians can inspect, verify, and understand.
2. **Breaking Extremal Combinatorics Bounds**: Details FunSearch’s discovery of the largest known cap sets in high-dimensional affine spaces ($n=8$), solving an open mathematical problem in extremal combinatorics that stood for over twenty years, alongside discovering superior heuristic algorithms for the classical online bin-packing problem.
3. **Formal Verification & Mathematical Frontier**: Incorporates OpenAI and Anthropic research into automated formal theorem proving in Lean and Isabelle, analyzing how self-correcting reasoning loops (*Claude Cycles*) facilitate automated lemma generation and mathematical research.
