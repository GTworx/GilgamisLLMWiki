## Executive Summary

This 36-page flagship research report by global telecommunications equipment leader Ericsson delivers authoritative forecasts on global mobile data traffic, 5G subscription uptake, and the explosive growth of cellular Internet of Things (Cellular IoT). Analyzing data across global telecom operator networks, Ericsson details the technological split in cellular IoT between Massive IoT technologies (Narrowband IoT / NB-IoT and LTE-M for low-power, long-range utility and asset monitoring) and Critical IoT technologies (5G ultra-reliable low-latency communications / URLLC for autonomous vehicles, industrial robotics, and smart grid automation).

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Explosive Cellular IoT Connection Growth**: Forecasts global cellular IoT connections to surpass 4 billion, driven by massive adoption of NB-IoT and Cat-M1 in smart metering, fleet logistics, and connected healthcare.
- **Massive IoT vs. Critical IoT Segments**: Distinguishes between Massive IoT (characterized by low cost, low bandwidth, and 10-year battery life) and Critical IoT (characterized by sub-millisecond latency, extreme throughput, and ultra-high reliability).
- **5G Network Rollout Trajectory**: Tracks commercial 5G spectrum deployments across North America, Northeast Asia, and Europe, projecting 5G subscriptions to reach 1.9 billion within five years.
- **Mobile Data Traffic Growth Drivers**: Analyzes the exponential surge in global monthly mobile data traffic (exceeding 38% CAGR), propelled by video streaming, high-resolution sensor feeds, and cloud mobile computing.
- **Private 5G Networks in Industry**: Evaluates the deployment of dedicated private cellular networks inside automotive factories, open-pit mines, and container ports, replacing unreliable Wi-Fi with deterministic wireless control.
