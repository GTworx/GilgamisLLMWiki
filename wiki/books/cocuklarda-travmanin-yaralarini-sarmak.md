## Executive Summary

Created by **Kateřina Honzíková** and edited by child-trauma therapist **Maggie Kline** under the *Syrian/Refugee Children Trauma Protection Project* (`Češi pro Sýrii, z.s.`, illustrated by Zhyldyz Shaiymbet, translated into Turkish by the **Somatik Deneyimleme Türkiye** team, April 2023, 44 pp., pp. 1–5), this open-access illustrated caregiver manual adapts **Peter A. Levine** and **Maggie Kline**'s **Somatic Experiencing®** program (*Trauma-Proofing Your Kids*, 2008) for parents, teachers, and humanitarian workers supporting children displaced by war, earthquakes, and acute disaster.

Grounded in evolutionary neurobiology of the autonomic nervous system, the guidebook explains gently and visually why children who are physically safe can still suffer nightmares, somatic aches, rage, or withdrawal: **trauma occurs when an overwhelming threat arrives "too fast, too soon, or too intensely"** (p. 9), locking survival energy (*fight, flight, or freeze*) inside the brainstem until safely discharged.

---

## Core Somatic Caregiver Principles

1. **Neurobiology of Survival States (pp. 6–10)**: Demystifies fight-flight-freeze responses so caregivers recognize aggressive outbursts or numbness as nervous-system protection rather than misbehavior.
2. **Co-Regulation & Safe Grounding**: Trains parents first to stabilize their own breathing and bodily grounding so children can borrow adult calm through relational co-regulation.
3. **Gentle Titration & Playful Discharge**: Offers simple body-based sensory exercises, imaginative play, and safe emotional check-ins that help children complete thwarted defensive responses and restore resilience without re-traumatization.
