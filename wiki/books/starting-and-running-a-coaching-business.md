## Executive Summary

Authored by British Chartered Psychologist and executive coach **Aryanne Oade** (How To Books Ltd., Oxford, 1st ed. 2009, ISBN 978-1-84803-315-3, pp. 5, 14; 145 pp.), *Starting and Running a Coaching Business* bridges the practical gap between being trained *to coach* and actually building a profitable, ethical, self-sustaining independent coaching practice.

Across ten structured, hands-on chapters (Contents, pp. 6–9), Oade walks prospective solo practitioners through self-employment readiness (Ch. 1), productizing one's coaching offer and niche (Ch. 2), solo time/energy management (Ch. 3), non-sleazy consultative selling, pricing, and referral marketing (Ch. 4), client trust and boundaries (Ch. 5), clinical/professional supervision (Ch. 6), CPD and peer-coach ethics (Ch. 7–8), confidentiality and data protection (Ch. 9), and UK small-business financial administration—sole trader vs. limited company, VAT, insurance, and invoicing (Ch. 10).

---

## Core Practice-Building Frameworks

1. **"You Are Your Own Product" & Niche Positioning (Ch. 2 & 4, pp. 27–37, 50–81)**: Translates intangible coaching value into clear programme outlines, client-style-adapted sales conversations, transparent fee structures, and systematic client references.
2. **Solo Practitioner Resilience (Ch. 3, pp. 38–49)**: Addresses the distinct psychological realities of working alone from home—combating overwhelm, managing energy peaks, and separating business-development blocks from client delivery.
3. **Supervision, Boundaries & Ethical Governance (Ch. 5–9, pp. 82–123)**: Elevates coaching from ad-hoc advice to a disciplined profession anchored in formal supervision, peer-coach boundary hygiene, written confidentiality agreements, and continuous client feedback audits.
