## Summary
Exhaustive 52-page empirical benchmark report by Carta analyzing real capitalization tables, down rounds, SAFE note conversion dynamics, and employee stock option exercising trends across 40,000 venture-backed companies.

## Analytical Overview
### Cap Table Ground Truth in the Post-ZIRP Economy

*Carta State of US Startups 2023* provides an unvarnished, empirical examination of the private venture capitalization ecosystem. Drawing on real-time transaction data across tens of thousands of venture-backed startups on Carta's platform, the report chronicles the severe valuation adjustments and structural recapitalizations defining the 2023 funding landscape.

### Key Structural Findings

The report delivers granular benchmarks:
- **The Explosion of Down Rounds and Flat Rounds:** Tracking the dramatic surge in down rounds (>20% of all venture rounds in 2023) and the widespread use of structured pay-to-play provisions and senior liquidation preferences.
- **SAFE Notes vs. Priced Equity:** Documenting the overwhelming preference for post-money SAFE notes at pre-seed and seed stages, while analyzing the hidden cap table dilution risks when multiple stacked SAFEs convert simultaneously at Series A.
- **Employee Stock Options & Forfeiture:** Revealing that over 75% of vested employee stock options were left unexercised upon departure due to prohibitively high strike prices and tax liabilities.

### Practical Compendium for Founders

The report serves as an essential tactical guide for founders and venture directors navigating distressed cap table restructuring.
