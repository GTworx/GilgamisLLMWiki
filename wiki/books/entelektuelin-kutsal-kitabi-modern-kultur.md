## Executive Summary

Translating **David S. Kidder** and **Noah D. Oppenheim**'s 2008 cultural compendium into Turkish via **Egemen Yılgür** (Maya Kitap, 1st ed. March 2014, ISBN 978-605-5675-85-1, p. 3; 3,774 mobile-reflowed PDF pages), *Entelektüelin Kutsal Kitabı: Modern Kültür* delivers **365 daily cultural essays** mapping the twentieth century's defining revolutions in cinema, literature, music, sports, visual art, science, ideology, and mass entertainment.

Advancing chronologically from the fin-de-siècle through the Cold War and digital era, its entries synthesize touchstones including **Sigmund Freud** (p. 22), **Lumière Brothers**, **Albert Einstein**, **Picasso**, **Charlie Chaplin**, **Gandhi**, **Henry Ford's Model T**, **Modernism**, **James Joyce**, **Billie Holiday**, **Oppenheimer**, **Jackie Robinson**, **Orson Welles's *War of the Worlds***, **Penicillin**, **Fidel Castro**, and **Bertolt Brecht** (TOC, pp. 22–1259+).

---

## Core Cultural Themes

1. **Interplay of High Modernism & Mass Culture**: Treats Schoenberg, T. S. Eliot, Virginia Woolf, and Jackson Pollock alongside jazz, Hollywood, comics, baseball, and television—showing how elite avant-garde ideas and mass media continuously reshaped one another.
2. **Ideology, Science & Everyday Objects**: Places political movements (Communism, Fascism, Zionism, Anti-colonialism) alongside breakthroughs like penicillin, plastics, and the Model T to map how ideas and artifacts jointly defined modern consciousness.
3. **Structured Cultural Literacy**: Provides an encyclopedic daily mental map of twentieth-century references essential for cross-disciplinary leaders, writers, and strategists.
