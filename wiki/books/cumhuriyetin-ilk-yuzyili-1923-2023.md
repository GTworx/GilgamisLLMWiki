## Executive Summary

Conducted in a rigorous, thematic dialogue format between **İlber Ortaylı** and journalist **İsmail Küçükkaya**, *Cumhuriyet'in İlk Yüzyılı (1923–2023)* delivers a sweeping centennial assessment of the Turkish Republic. Ortaylı surveys the founding architectural principles of the Kemalist revolution, the geopolitical genius of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk and İsmet İnönü, the transition to multi-party democracy (1946–1950), military coups (1960, 1971, 1980), demographic urbanization, educational triumphs and systemic failures, and Turkey's position entering its second century.

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **The Republic as the Logical Apex of Modernization**: Rejects the idea of an artificial rupture, arguing that the Republic was the culmination of two centuries of Ottoman modernization (*Tanzimat*, *Meşrutiyet*).
2. **The Primacy of Legal Secularism**: Identifies the 1926 Civil Code and legal secularism as the single most revolutionary leap in Turkish history, emancipating women and institutionalizing modern citizenship.
3. **The Importance of Institutional Memory**: Warns against eroding the historical traditions of the Turkish Foreign Ministry, military staff colleges, and high-caliber state schools (*Mülkiye*, *Galatasaray*).
