## Summary
Master commercial, financial, and engineering compendium on the global data center real estate market, featuring Morgan Stanley's 135-page report projecting a 6x capacity surge to 2030, CBRE and Cushman & Wakefield global market rankings, JLL outlooks, McKinsey investment theses, and complete facility construction guides.

## Analytical Overview
This volume provides a panoramic, data-rich analysis of the global data center real estate asset class, examining the unprecedented convergence of institutional capital, hyperscale tenant leasing, and complex physical engineering. Anchoring the collection is Morgan Stanley’s monumental 135-page institutional research report (*Global Data Center Capacity Growth to Increase 6x by 2030*), which models the expansion of worldwide operational data center capacity from ~40 gigawatts (GW) in 2023 to nearly 250 GW by the decade's end, requiring upwards of $1.5 trillion in cumulative infrastructure investment.

This macroeconomic expansion is paired with granular market metrics from top-tier commercial real estate advisory firms: CBRE’s *Global Data Center Trends 2023*, Cushman & Wakefield’s annual *Data Center Global Rankings* (evaluating top markets: Northern Virginia, Dallas, Silicon Valley, London, Frankfurt, Singapore), and JLL’s North American and Global Data Centre Outlooks. These studies document record-low vacancy rates (often sub-2%), surging wholesale colocation rental rates, and the structural pivot toward build-to-suit pre-leasing where hyperscalers contract entire campuses years prior to groundbreaking.

To ground financial valuations in physical reality, the volume incorporates comprehensive facility design engineering manuals by Paul Healey and Allison Allocca (*How to Design and Build a Data Center* and *Guide to Colocation Provider Selection*). The manuals detail Uptime Institute Tier classifications (Tier I to IV), dual-path electrical architectures (2N redundancy, automated transfer switches, lithium-ion UPS systems), structural floor loading requirements, physical perimeter security, and cooling topologies (chilled water loops, adiabatic cooling), establishing a full-lifecycle manual for modern digital real estate.
