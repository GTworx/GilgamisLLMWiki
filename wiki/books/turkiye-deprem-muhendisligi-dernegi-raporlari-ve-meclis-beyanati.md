## Executive Summary

This expert dossier compiles the authoritative technical statements and legislative submissions issued by the Turkish Earthquake Engineering Association (Türkiye Deprem Mühendisliği Derneği - TDMD), including its formal testimony to the Turkish Grand National Assembly (TBMM) Earthquake Research Commission (December 22, 2020) and its immediate engineering emergency assessment published on February 7, 2023, hours after the Kahramanmaraş catastrophe.

TDMD's expert manifestos represent the unvarnished consensus of Türkiye's leading academic structural and geotechnical engineering community. The Association vehemently condemns the recurring political practice of 'zoning amnesties' (imar barışı / imar affı), exposing how granting legal occupancy permits to uninspected, substandard buildings directly institutionalizes mass manslaughter in future earthquakes. It outlines uncompromising technical reforms for professional engineering licensing, mandatory construction supervision, and the criminalization of non-code-compliant structural modifications.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Condemnation of Zoning Amnesties**: Formulates an unequivocal scientific critique demonstrating that periodic political amnesties for illegal construction invalidate building codes and sentence citizens to death.
- **Professional Civil Engineering Licensing**: Demands the immediate introduction of a rigorous, tiered professional engineering (Yetkin Mühendislik) licensing and examination system similar to global PE standards.
- **Separation of Inspection from Contractors**: Demands that structural inspection firms be assigned through an automated national lottery and paid via a blind state escrow fund, completely severing financial ties between builders and inspectors.
- **Ground Shaking Characteristics of Feb 6**: Provides immediate preliminary spectral analysis of the Kahramanmaraş ground motions, noting spectral acceleration peaks exceeding design code values in near-fault zones.
