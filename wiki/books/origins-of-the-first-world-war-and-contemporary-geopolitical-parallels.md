## Summary
Deep structural analysis of the diplomatic crises, alliance entanglements, and military mobilization timetables that triggered the outbreak of World War I in 1914, drawing rigorous structural parallels to contemporary geopolitical rivalries.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes the July Crisis of 1914, the Austro-Hungarian ultimatum to Serbia, Russian mobilization dilemmas, and the Schlieffen Plan through structural balance-of-power theories. Contrasts early 20th-century Anglo-German naval arms races with contemporary US-China naval competition in the Indo-Pacific, warning of systemic sleepwalking toward systemic conflict.
