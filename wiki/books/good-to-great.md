## Executive Summary

Serving as the empirical prequel to *Built to Last*, **Jim Collins's** *Good to Great* (2001, pp. 2–5) investigates how eleven historically mediocre Fortune 500 companies (including Abbott, Circuit City, Fannie Mae, Gillette, Kimberly-Clark, Kroger, Nucor, Philip Morris, Pitney Bowes, Walgreens, and Wells Fargo) broke through to sustained fifteen-year stock-market outperformance of at least 3x the general market—contrasted against matched comparison peers that failed to leap.

Opening with the maxim **"Good is the enemy of great"** (Chapter 1, p. 5), Collins distills a disciplined three-stage buildup-to-breakthrough framework—**Disciplined People, Disciplined Thought, and Disciplined Action**—powered by cumulative momentum rather than dramatic restructurings, celebrity CEOs, or flashy technology fads.

---

## Core Framework (Contents, p. 5)

1. **Level 5 Leadership (Chapter 2)**: Paradoxical fusion of deep personal humility and fierce professional will—leaders who look out the window to credit others in success and in the mirror to accept blame in failure.
2. **First Who, Then What (Chapter 3)**: Get the right people on the bus (and the wrong people off) *before* deciding where to drive; great vision with the wrong team fails.
3. **Confront the Brutal Facts — The Stockdale Paradox (Chapter 4)**: Maintain unwavering faith that you will prevail in the end *while* confronting the most brutal facts of current reality without illusion.
4. **The Hedgehog Concept (Chapter 5)**: Radical simplicity at the intersection of three circles: what you can be the best in the world at, what drives your economic engine (profit per *X*), and what you are deeply passionate about.
5. **Culture of Discipline, Technology Accelerators & The Flywheel (Chapters 6–8)**: Disciplined action within clear frameworks replaces bureaucracy; technology accelerates existing momentum rather than creating it; sustainable breakthroughs come from relentless cumulative turns of the **Flywheel**, never a single "Doom Loop" silver bullet.
