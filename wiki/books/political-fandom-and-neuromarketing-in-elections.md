## Summary
Three-volume psychometric and cognitive science compendium analyzing how political party identification has morphed into tribal 'fandom', and how neuromarketing 2.0 micro-targeting exploits cognitive biases to sway democratic elections.

## Analytical Overview
### From Civic Deliberation to Sports-Team Tribal Fandom

*Political Fandom and Neuromarketing* unites three groundbreaking academic studies exploring the psychological transformation of modern electoral politics. Traditional democratic theory assumes that voters evaluate candidates like rational consumers assessing competing policy proposals. This compendium proves that contemporary voters operate identically to fervent sports and pop-culture **fans**.

### Measuring the Political Fandom Scale

The research establishes standardized psychometric scales measuring "political fandom":
- **Fanship vs. Fandom:** Distinguishing individual psychological devotion to a political leader from social participation in an aggressive in-group fan community.
- **Neuromarketing 2.0 in the 2016 Trump Campaign:** Analyzing how Cambridge Analytica and digital campaign squads combined Facebook psychographic OCEAN profiles with galvanic skin response and facial coding to deliver individualized affective stimuli that bypass conscious cognitive deliberation.
- **Affective Polarization:** When politics becomes fandom, policy debate ceases; the opposing party is viewed not as a political adversary with different ideas, but as an existential cultural rival whose defeat is paramount.
