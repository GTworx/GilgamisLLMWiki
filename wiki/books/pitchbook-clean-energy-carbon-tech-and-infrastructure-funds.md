## Executive Summary
Authored by PitchBook's emerging technology research division, this 92-page institutional market intelligence compendium analyzes private equity, venture capital, and infrastructure fund allocations driving the global energy transition. The research evaluates deal counts, median valuations, and exit horizons across solar/wind platforms, grid battery storage, point-source carbon capture, direct air capture (DAC), and hydrogen tech. The reports highlight how mega-cap private infrastructure funds (Brookfield, BlackRock, EQT) have raised hundreds of billions in dedicated energy transition dry powder, underwriting gigawatt-scale renewable assets with contracted cash flows.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Infrastructure Fund Mega-Cap Rotation**: Infrastructure private equity has surpassed early-stage venture capital as the primary financing engine of the energy transition, funding utility-scale battery storage and transmission assets.
- **Carbon Tech Valuation Maturation**: Point-source carbon capture achieves commercial project financeability under enhanced 45Q tax credits ($85/ton), while Direct Air Capture (DAC) remains pre-commercial, awaiting technology cost declines toward $100/ton.
- **Grid Storage Capital Intensity**: Venture investments in advanced battery chemistries (sodium-ion, iron-air) surged to address supply chain concentration in lithium and cobalt.
