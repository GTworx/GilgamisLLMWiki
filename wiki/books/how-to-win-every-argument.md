## Executive Summary

Authored by British philosopher and Adam Smith Institute co-founder **Madsen Pirie** (2006, Continuum), *How to Win Every Argument* is a razor-sharp, alphabetically organized field guide to nearly eighty classical and modern **logical fallacies**—combining rigorous informal logic with wickedly dry wit. For every fallacy (*ad hominem*, *straw man*, *post hoc ergo propter hoc*, *begging the question*, *slippery slope*, *false dilemma*, *tu quoque*, *undistributed middle*, *special pleading*, *poisoning the well*), Pirie defines the formal error, demonstrates how skilled debaters sneak it past unsuspecting audiences, and equips readers to dismantle it cleanly in real time.

---

## Key Practical Value

- **Cognitive Self-Defense**: Exposes how political speeches, media framing, and corporate pitches routinely substitute emotional sleight-of-hand for valid inference.
- **Logical Hygiene**: Training yourself to spot fallacies in adversaries simultaneously sharpens the rigor and intellectual honesty of your own reasoning.
