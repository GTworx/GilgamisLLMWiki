## Executive Summary

Curated by entrepreneur and writer **Eric Jorgenson** from a decade of essays, podcasts, and tweetstorms by AngelList co-founder and early-stage investor **Naval Ravikant** (Foreword by Tim Ferriss, pp. 13–15), *The Almanack of Naval Ravikant* synthesizes a unified operating philosophy across two halves: **Part I: Wealth** (pp. 29–125) and **Part II: Happiness** (pp. 127–205). Our library holds both Jorgenson's authentic free public-service PDF (`Final.pdf`, 242 pp., copyright p. 4) and a companion Bookey structured chapter summary (399 pp.).

Ravikant's central financial thesis separates **wealth** (equity and assets that earn while you sleep) from **money** (a ledger for time-trading) and **status** (a zero-sum hierarchy game, pp. 30–39). Durable wealth creation rests on combining **specific knowledge** (untrainable skills pursued through authentic curiosity), **accountability** (taking public risk under one's own name), and **permissionless leverage**—specifically **code and media**, which replicate at near-zero marginal cost unlike older labor or capital leverage (pp. 40–66).

---

## Core Frameworks & Philosophical Pillars

1. **Productize Yourself**: Compound unique specific knowledge with scalable leverage ("specific knowledge + accountability + leverage") and own equity rather than renting out time (pp. 40–55).
2. **Judgment Over Effort**: In an age of infinite leverage, high-leverage decisions matter far more than raw hours worked; clear thinking requires shedding rigid identity and collecting cross-disciplinary mental models (pp. 67–113).
3. **Long-Term Games with Long-Term People**: All returns in life—wealth, relationships, and knowledge—arise from compounding within high-trust, repeated-game networks (pp. 46–49).
4. **Happiness as a Trainable Skill**: Drawing on Stoicism and "rational Buddhism," Ravikant frames desire as a self-chosen contract to be unhappy until an external condition is met, grounding peace in presence, acceptance, health habits, and freedom from envy (pp. 128–204).
