## Overview
This specialized dialectological and lexicographical dictionary documents 'Çavuşca'—a distinctive local trade slang, occupational argot, and Anatolian dialectal lexicon. The authors compile etymologies, phonological shifts, and semantic usages preserving endangered oral linguistic heritage.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Compiles hundreds of unique dialectal headwords, occupational idioms, and localized vernacular expressions.
- **Key Finding**: Documents morphological adaptations and phonetic mutations characteristic of localized Anatolian trade communities.
- **Key Finding**: Preserves fragile oral linguistic patrimony against homogenization by standard broadcast media.
