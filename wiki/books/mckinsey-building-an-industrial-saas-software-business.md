## Executive Summary

Authored by McKinsey & Company's Advanced Industries Practice, this strategic case-study report details the operational, cultural, and organizational transformation required when a traditional industrial equipment manufacturer builds an independent B2B industrial SaaS software business. Addressing the classic corporate failure modes of treating software as a mere hardware accessory, McKinsey outlines how to establish an autonomous software business unit, recruit top digital product talent, transition from transactional capex sales to recurring ARR subscription licensing, and monetize IoT equipment telemetry through predictive maintenance and asset optimization applications.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **The Industrial Software Dilemma**: Traditional industrial OEMs excel at linear supply chains and physical engineering but struggle with rapid iterative software release cycles and subscription billing economics.
- **Autonomous Business Unit Structure**: Recommends isolating the emerging software entity with independent P&L accountability, distinct compensation structures, and direct board oversight to shield it from legacy corporate bureaucracy.
- **Transitioning from Capex to ARR**: Navigates the painful revenue dip (the 'fish model') that occurs when transitioning multi-million-dollar upfront hardware/software licenses to multi-year subscription contracts.
- **Monetizing IoT Machine Telemetry**: Outlines how industrial equipment data (vibration, temperature, pressure) is converted into high-margin predictive maintenance and operational efficiency SaaS modules.
- **Ecosystem and Channel Conflict**: Details strategies for managing friction between legacy industrial sales reps accustomed to hardware commissions and specialized software GTM account executives.
