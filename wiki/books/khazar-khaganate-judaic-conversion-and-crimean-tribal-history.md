## Summary
Comprehensive 3-source compendium investigating the political institutions, royal Judaic conversion, Silk Road trade dominance, and Crimean cultural-tribal history of the medieval Khazar Empire.

## Analytical Overview
Integrates Arabic, Hebrew (Khazar Correspondence, Schechter Letter), and Byzantine sources to analyze the 8th–9th century adoption of Rabbinic/Karaite Judaism by the Khazar royal court. Examines the geopolitical buffer role of Khazaria between the Abbasid Caliphate and Byzantine Empire, the ethnogenesis of Crimean Karaites and Krymchaks, and the long-term impact on East Slavic statecraft.
