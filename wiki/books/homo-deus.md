## Executive Summary

Dedicated to his Vipassana teacher S. N. Goenka (p. 3), **Yuval Noah Harari**'s *Homo Deus* (2016/2017; 358 pp.) begins with an extraordinary empirical observation: for the first time in human history, famine, plague, and war have shifted from uncontrollable divine curses into manageable technical problems—more people today die from overeating than starvation, and from old age than infectious disease (Ch. 1, pp. 6–7). With survival secured, humanity's **New Human Agenda** pivots toward three godlike ambitions: **immortality (radical longevity), engineered bliss, and bio-digital divinity (*Homo deus*)**.

Across three bold parts—**Part I: Homo Sapiens Conquers the World** (The Anthropocene & Human Spark), **Part II: Homo Sapiens Gives Meaning to the World** (The Modern Covenant & Humanist Revolution), and **Part III: Homo Sapiens Loses Control** (The Time Bomb in the Laboratory, The Great Decoupling, The Ocean of Consciousness, and **The Data Religion**, Contents p. 4)—Harari explores how the very life sciences and computer algorithms born of humanism now threaten to make liberal humanism obsolete.

---

## Core Speculative & Analytical Frameworks

1. **The Modern Deal & Humanism's Triumph (Part II, Ch. 4–7)**: Frames modernity as a grand bargain trading cosmic *meaning* for *power* (economic growth), elevating human feelings, free will, and subjective experience into supreme moral authority.
2. **The Great Decoupling of Intelligence from Consciousness (Part III, Ch. 8–10)**: Argues that modern neuroscience undermines free will ("organisms are algorithms"), while AI decouples high-level problem-solving intelligence from subjective consciousness—potentially rendering most humans economically redundant while upgrading an elite caste.
3. **Dataism — The Emerging Techno-Religion (Ch. 11)**: Defines **Dataism** as a worldview declaring the universe to consist of data flows, where the value of any entity or institution is measured solely by its contribution to global data processing—threatening to hand authority from human voters to the Internet-of-All-Things.
