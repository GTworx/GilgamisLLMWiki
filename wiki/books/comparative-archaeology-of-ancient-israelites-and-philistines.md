## Summary
Exhaustive comparative synthesis benchmarking archaeological visibility, ceramic chronologies, monumental architecture, state formation, and geopolitical exilic vulnerabilities between Iron Age Israelites and Sea Peoples (Philistines).

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes archaeological stratigraphy across Tel Miqne (Ekron), Ashdod, Megiddo, and Jerusalem to contrast Philistine urban pentapolis culture (Aegean-derived bichrome pottery, hearths, metallurgy) with rural proto-Israelite highland settlements (four-room houses, collared-rim jars). Examines historical hypotheses for repeated Levantine exiles under Neo-Assyrian and Neo-Babylonian imperial policies.
