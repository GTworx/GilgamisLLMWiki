## Executive Summary

This multi-part multimedia dossier compiles the operational reports and technical slide decks of prominent grassroots diaspora and technology industry relief initiatives deployed after the 2023 Kahramanmaraş and Hatay earthquakes. It includes the *Bridge to Türkiye Fund (BTF) Earthquake Relief Fund 1-Year Impact Report* (31 pp.), the *Hatay'a Hayat Ver* international recovery initiative presentation, and the *TÜBİSAD Technological Education Container Project Presentation* (14 pp.).

The dossier documents how civil society and technology industry associations mobilized tens of millions of dollars in rapid-response micro-grants and targeted infrastructure. It details the deployment of solar-powered, high-speed internet-connected container classrooms (TÜBİSAD Teknoloji Konteynerleri) across Hatay, Adıyaman, and Kahramanmaraş to prevent learning loss among displaced children, provide STEM education, and distribute thousands of academic scholarships, prosthetic support grants, and psychological rehabilitation programs.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Diaspora Philanthropic Mobilization**: Details BTF's decentralized fundraising mechanisms across the North American Turkish diaspora, mobilizing over $12 million deployed through verified local grassroots NGOs.
- **Technological Education Containers (TEK)**: Outlines the engineering, hardware specifications, and educational curriculum of containerized digital classrooms providing computer literacy, coding, and remote school access.
- **Hatay Reconstruction and Cultural Preservation**: Analyzes the multi-stakeholder framework of *Hatay'a Hayat Ver*, uniting urban planners, architects, and sociologists to preserve the unique multi-cultural heritage of ancient Antioch.
- **Targeted Grants for Vulnerable Children**: Details specialized funding streams for amputee children (prosthetics and physical therapy), orphaned students, and regional university scholars.
