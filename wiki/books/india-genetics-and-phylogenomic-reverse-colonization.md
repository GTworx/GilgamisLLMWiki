## Summary
Two-part high-resolution paleogenomic investigation analyzing human dispersal through the Indian corridor and proving prehistoric reverse gene flow from South Asia back into Western Eurasia.

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes whole-genome sequencing from modern and ancient skeletal remains across the subcontinent. Reconstructs the complex admixture of ancient Indigenous Hunter-Gatherers, Iranian-related agriculturalists, and Steppe pastoralists, while documenting post-Neolithic back-migrations into the Iranian plateau and Central Asia.
