## Overview
This 2020 prospective media study interrogates the trajectory of social networks as they evolve from text and video feeds toward immersive spatial environments. The authors dissect algorithmic echo chambers, affective polarization, and predictive profiling, mapping out alternative developmental pathways for public digital spaces.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Charts the evolutionary arc from early bulletin boards to engagement-maximizing feed algorithms and persistent 3D spaces.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes how immersive presence intensifies psychological vulnerabilities to radicalization and cognitive distortion.
- **Key Finding**: Proposes decentralized, public-interest governance protocols to counter corporate walled-garden monopolies.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The pathologies of 2D social media will not vanish in the metaverse; without fundamental architectural reform, they will acquire spatial, sensory, and affective permanence."
