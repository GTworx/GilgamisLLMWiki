## Summary
Exhaustive 235-page monograph analyzing all references, ethnonyms, and diplomatic treaties concerning Turkic peoples (Huns, Avars, Göktürks, Khazars, Bulgars) in the Chronographia of Theophanes Confessor.

## Analytical Overview
Provides systematic Greek philological translation and historical commentary on the 8th–9th century chronicle of Theophanes Confessor. Explores Byzantine imperial diplomacy toward Eurasian steppe confederations, military alliances against the Sasanian Empire under Emperor Heraclius, and the symbolic representation of steppe nomadic warriors in imperial Constantinople.
