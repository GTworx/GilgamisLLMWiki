## Summary
Ten-issue longitudinal compendium covering the 2024–2025 global 'super-election year' (US, EU, UK, India, Taiwan), expanding Western tariffs on Chinese clean-tech exports, and shifting macroeconomic fiscal regimes.

## Analytical Overview
Details the geopolitical and commercial stakes of democratic elections spanning over half the world's population. Investigates the escalating trade friction surrounding Chinese electric vehicle and solar panel exports, the European Union's economic competitiveness dilemma, US industrial subsidies, the assassination attempt on Donald Trump, and shifting global sovereign debt dynamics.
