## Executive Summary

Written by New York School of Finance founder and former Morgan Stanley/UBS investment banker **Paul Pignataro** (Wiley Finance, 2013, copyright pp. 8–9), *Financial Modeling and Valuation* is an exhaustive, line-by-line Wall Street workbook that teaches three-statement financial modeling and institutional corporate valuation through a single continuous real-world case study: **Walmart** (with **Costco** as primary comparable, Preface p. 11; TOC pp. 2–5).

Unlike abstract corporate-finance textbooks, Pignataro builds every schedule from scratch exactly as an investment banking analyst or private-equity associate would in Excel—forcing the reader to reconcile historical SEC filings, project forward drivers, handle iterative debt/interest circularities, and triangulate enterprise value across all three core Wall Street valuation methodologies.

## Two-Part Institutional Modeling Curriculum

1. **Part One — Integrated Three-Statement Modeling (Ch. 1–6, pp. 14–318)**:
   - **Income Statement & Cash Flow Statement (Ch. 1–2)**: Builds historical line items (revenue, COGS, SG&A, D&A, taxes, non-recurring items) and links operating, investing, and financing cash flows.
   - **Supporting Schedules (Ch. 3–4)**: Constructs straight-line vs. accelerated **Depreciation & Deferred Tax** schedules and **Operating Working Capital** (AR, inventory, AP) cash bridges.
   - **Balance Sheet & Debt Schedule (Ch. 5–6)**: Balances the projected balance sheet automatically, builds revolver/term-debt sweep schedules, manages Excel **circular references** (cash ↔ interest income ↔ net income), and embeds scenario switches.
2. **Part Two — Institutional Valuation (Ch. 7–11, pp. 319–432)**:
   - **Value Foundations (Ch. 7)**: Clarifies Book Value, Equity Market Value, **Enterprise Value (EV)**, and trading multiples.
   - **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF, Ch. 8)**: Computes Unlevered Free Cash Flow (UFCF), WACC, levered/unlevered Beta, mid-year discounting, and Terminal Value (EBITDA multiple vs. perpetuity growth).
   - **Comparable Companies & Precedent Transactions (Ch. 9–11)**: Covers LTM adjustments, calendarization (Costco), acquisition control premiums, and football-field valuation synthesis (plus Excel hotkey/flow appendices, pp. 433–440).
