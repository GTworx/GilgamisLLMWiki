## Summary
Four-volume longitudinal research compendium by the Turkish Exporters Assembly (TİM), detailing the structural growth of Turkish exports from $30B to $150B+ between 2001 and 2016, alongside the TİM Entrepreneurship Working Group strategy for high-value-added innovation exports.

## Analytical Overview
### The Structural Transformation of Turkish Export Power

*TİM Girişimcilik ve İhracat Dinamikleri: İhracatın Gelişimi (2001–2016) ve İnovasyon Odaklı Girişimcilik Stratejisi* compiles four landmark research dossiers published by the Turkish Exporters Assembly (TİM). Chronicling fifteen years of dramatic trade expansion, the compendium analyzes how Turkish exports expanded from $31 billion in 2001 to over $150 billion, transforming Turkey into the industrial manufacturing powerhouse of the Customs Union.

### The Value-Per-Kilogram Dilemma

Despite monumental volume growth, TİM identifies a fundamental strategic challenge: Turkey's average export value per kilogram hovered around $1.40 to $1.50, heavily dominated by automotive assembly, textiles, and basic steel:
- To escape the middle-income trap and reach high-income prosperity ($200B+ target), Turkey must dramatically increase its share of high-technology exports ($50+ per kilogram).
- The TİM Entrepreneurship Working Group formulates actionable policies: establishing the **TİM-TEB Girişim Evi** incubator network across 10 provinces to accelerate export-oriented tech scaleups.
- Creating corporate-startup matchmaking pipelines so traditional industrial exporters can integrate local robotics, IoT, and AI solutions directly into export manufacturing lines.

### Indispensable National Archive

The compendium preserves the macroeconomic datasets and executive strategies that reshaped modern Turkish industrial exports.
