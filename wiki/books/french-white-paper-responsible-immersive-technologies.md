## Overview
This exhaustive 96-page national French white paper (Livre Blanc) synthesizes recommendations from over 100 industry leaders, sociologists, neuroscientists, and policymakers on building responsible immersive technologies. The report champions a distinct European vision of spatial computing—prioritizing ecological sustainability, public cultural heritage, cognitive safety for minors, and digital sovereignty against Silicon Valley hegemony.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Establishes a rigorous methodology for measuring the full life-cycle carbon and material footprint of VR/AR hardware.
- **Key Finding**: Proposes strict age-gated developmental thresholds and usage time safeguards to prevent adolescent neurological strain.
- **Key Finding**: Advocates for European sovereign open-source 3D rendering engines and public cultural metaverse archives.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "L'immersion ne doit pas être synonyme d'aliénation. Nous devons concevoir des technologies immersives sobres, éthiques et émancipatrices, fondées sur les valeurs européennes de liberté et de protection des citoyens."
