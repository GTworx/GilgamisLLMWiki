## Overview
Published in Buildings (MDPI 2022) by Ji-Hyun Lee and Sung-Ah Park, this extensive bibliometric study maps decades of multidisciplinary research on 'Embodied Experience' within virtual architecture and immersive spatial environments. Utilizing co-citation clustering and keyword co-occurrence networks across thousands of studies, the authors chart the evolution from visual realism to multisensory somatic presence.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Maps the intellectual structure of spatial presence, identifying key thematic clusters: avatar ownership, proprioception, and spatial navigation.
- **Key Finding**: Demonstrates the central role of spatial cognition and architectural proportions in eliciting genuine emotional immersion.
- **Key Finding**: Provides an extensive longitudinal visualization of citations from 1990 to 2022 guiding future architectural spatial computing.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Embodied experience in virtual environments relies not simply on graphical fidelity, but on the sensorimotor coherence between physical bodily intention and virtual responsiveness."
