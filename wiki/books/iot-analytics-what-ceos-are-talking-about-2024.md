## Summary
Applies natural language processing across 8,000 corporate earnings calls from 4,000 global public companies, tracking the explosive rise of generative AI, nearshoring, and macroeconomic headwinds.

## Analytical Overview
### Macro Trends Through the Lens of Executive Discourse

*What CEOs Are Talking About Going into 2024*, authored by IoT Analytics, employs large-scale natural language processing (NLP) to parse and quantify keyword frequency, sentiment, and strategic priority shifts across roughly 8,000 earnings conference calls held by 4,000 global listed corporations. The methodology provides an objective, unvarnished pulse of global corporate leadership focus.

### The Generative AI Ingestion Wave

The data reveals the most rapid linguistic takeover in earnings call history:
- Mentions of **'Generative AI', 'Large Language Models', and 'Copilots'** skyrocketed by over 1,000% year-over-year, moving from theoretical tech hype into operational cost-efficiency and product monetization discussions.
- In contrast, mentions of 'Metaverse', 'NFTs', and 'Blockchain' experienced near-total collapse from executive vocabularies.

### Supply Chains, Geopolitics, and Inflation

Beyond AI, the report details how executives navigated the transition from hyper-globalized supply chains to 'nearshoring' and 'friendshoring' amidst US-China tensions, alongside strategies for protecting operating margins amid high interest rates and sticky wage inflation.
