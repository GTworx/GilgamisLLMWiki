## Executive Summary

Published by the United Nations Global Compact (UNGC) in association with the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA), this monumental 224-page global sustainability treatise evaluates the worldwide corporate alignment with the Ten Principles of the UN Global Compact and the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Drawing upon extensive survey data from thousands of corporate signatories across 160 nations, the report audits private sector leadership in human rights, labor standards, environmental stewardship, and anti-corruption.

The volume delivers a rigorous mid-point assessment of the UN 2030 Agenda, tracking progress across climate action (SDG 13), clean water and sanitation (SDG 6), affordable energy (SDG 7), and responsible consumption (SDG 12). It provides comparative regional benchmarks, detailing how European and multinational enterprises are adapting to stringent legal compliance mandates (EU CSRD, CSDDD), while offering guidance for small-and-medium enterprises (SMEs) entering sustainable global trade value chains.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Mid-Point 2030 Agenda Audit**: Concludes that only 15% of SDG targets are on track globally, calling for a massive acceleration in private sector capital deployment and operational restructuring.
- **The Ten Principles Operational Framework**: Analyzes how global corporations embed human rights due diligence, fair labor standards, environmental precaution, and anti-corruption compliance into core operations.
- **SME Supply Chain Inclusion**: Formulates actionable capacity-building frameworks to ensure small and medium enterprises in developing economies are not excluded from global trade due to ESG compliance barriers.
- **Accountability and Anti-Greenwashing**: Outlines enhanced Communication on Progress (CoP) digital reporting requirements, mandating audited, quantifiable metrics to combat greenwashing.
