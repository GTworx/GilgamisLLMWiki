## Summary
Monumental industry intelligence and macroeconomic compendium synthesizing over 100 benchmark global reports. Features Nathan Benaich's definitive State of AI Reports (2020–2024), Stanford HAI's AI Index Reports, Goldman Sachs' macroeconomic growth projections ($7 trillion global GDP expansion), McKinsey's 'Economic Potential of Generative AI' ($4.4 trillion annual value creation), and ARK Invest Big Ideas.

## Analytical Overview
This volume constitutes the definitive multi-year market, macroeconomic, and geopolitical record of the artificial intelligence revolution during its hyper-scaling era.

Key analytical components:
1. **The State of AI Reports (Air Street Capital)**: Features Nathan Benaich’s multi-year longitudinal assessments tracking breakthrough research, open-source model proliferation (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek), hardware chokepoints (Nvidia H100/B200 GPU clusters), and sovereign AI initiatives.
2. **Stanford HAI AI Index**: Compiles comprehensive quantitative data tracking global private AI investment, compute consumption trends, benchmarking saturation, patent filings, and the escalating cost of state-of-the-art training runs exceeding $100M+.
3. **Macroeconomic Value & Labor Impact**: Analyzes Goldman Sachs’ Briggs & Kodnani projections modeling a 7% (or nearly $7 trillion) increase in global GDP alongside McKinsey’s functional breakdown showing that 75% of generative AI value concentrates in Customer Operations, Marketing & Sales, Software Engineering, and R&D.
