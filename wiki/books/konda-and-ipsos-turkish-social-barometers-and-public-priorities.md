## Summary
Dual-volume quantitative sociological barometer synthesizing KONDA's March 2024 field report on urban life in Istanbul with IPSOS's 73-page priority report on Turkish economic anxieties and social cohesion.

## Analytical Overview
Presents empirical survey data on cost-of-living struggles, housing crisis pressures, earthquake preparedness fears in Istanbul, public trust deficits in judiciary and state institutions, and societal polarization in Turkey ahead of municipal and general electoral cycles.
