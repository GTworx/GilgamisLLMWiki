## Executive Summary

This two-part executive advisory dossier by Catalant Technologies establishes structured market-scoping frameworks for evaluating novel computing architectures and quantum technologies. Designed for corporate strategists, sovereign funds, and institutional private equity investors, the frameworks outline methodologies to define the 'Novel Computing (NQ) universe' across quantum computing, neuromorphic computing, optical/photonic processors, and reversible thermodynamic computing. The dossiers provide rigorous evaluation templates for mapping market dynamics, technology readiness levels (TRL), competitive barriers to entry, customer segmentation, regulatory export controls, and capital intensity requirements.

---

## Key Strategic, Scientific & Engineering Takeaways

- **Defining the Novel Computing Taxonomy**: Establishes a rigorous taxonomy categorizing post-Moore's law compute paradigms: Quantum Computing (annealing, analog simulation, universal gate-based), Neuromorphic Computing (spiking neural networks, memristors), Photonic Computing (optical tensor processing), and Thermodynamic Computing.
- **Technology Readiness Level (TRL) Assessment**: Provides stage-gate criteria to distinguish laboratory-scale physics demonstrations (TRL 3–4) from scalable enterprise-ready coprocessors (TRL 7–8).
- **Market Dynamics and Sizing Methodologies**: Outlines secondary and primary research methods to quantify total addressable market (TAM), serviceable addressable market (SAM), and serviceable obtainable market (SOM) across pharmaceutical drug discovery, cryptographic defense, and financial quantitative modeling.
- **Customer Segmentation and Buying Personas**: Details enterprise procurement friction points, identifying whether initial enterprise adoption is driven by corporate R&D innovation groups, chief technology officers (CTOs), or high-performance computing (HPC) datacenter leads.
- **Geopolitical and Export Control Vectors**: Examines how national security export controls (ITAR, EAR, Wassenaar Arrangement) on cryogenic dilution refrigerators, specialized microwave electronics, and high-purity silicon isotopes shape global market accessibility.
