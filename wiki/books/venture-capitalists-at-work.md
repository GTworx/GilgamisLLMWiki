## Executive Summary

Compiled by former SoftBank Capital venture professional **Tarang Shah** (creator of the Start-up Analysis Model) and **Sheetal Shah** (Apress, 2011, copyright p. 469; About the Authors p. 475), *Venture Capitalists at Work* presents 35 candid, long-form Q&A interviews with legendary investors and founder-investors responsible for over 70 transformative companies—including YouTube, Twitter, LinkedIn, PayPal, Dropbox, Salesforce, Skype, Baidu, Groupon, and Zappos (TOC pp. 3–4; Acknowledgments p. 476).

Featuring **Roelof Botha** & **Jim Goetz** (Sequoia Capital), **Mike Maples** (Floodgate), **Tim Draper** (DFJ), **David Cowan** (Bessemer), **Scott Sandell** & **Harry Weller** (NEA), **Ann Winblad** (Hummer Winblad), **Ken Howery** (Founders Fund), **Alfred Lin** (Sequoia/Zappos), **Alan Patricof** (Greycroft), **Randy Komisar** (Kleiner Perkins), and **Howard Morgan** (First Round Capital), the anthology captures the unvarnished decision heuristics behind billion-dollar pattern recognition.

## Cross-Interview Investment Heuristics

1. **Seizing Discontinuities (Foreword, pp. 471–472)**: Top VCs hunt for inflection points that initially look dubious or toy-like before rapidly becoming obvious—betting on authentic founders capable of creating customer delight and scaling past $100M+ in revenue.
2. **Jockey vs. Horse Calibration**: Reveals sharp stylistic diversity across stages—seed specialists like Mike Maples and Howard Morgan index heavily on founder grit, unique insight, and "thunder lizard" potential, whereas growth investors emphasize defensible unit economics and market size.
3. **Post-Investment Value Creation**: Documents how board partners actually help portfolio CEOs recruit executive teams, navigate near-death pivots (such as YouTube's shift from video dating to open video sharing, or PayPal's anti-fraud survival), and time strategic exits.
