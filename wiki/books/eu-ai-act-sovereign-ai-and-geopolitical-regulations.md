## Summary
Master legal, statutory, and geopolitical compendium tracking the global race to regulate artificial intelligence. Features section-by-section legal analyses of the European Union's landmark EU AI Act, President Biden's Executive Order 14110 on Safe and Trustworthy AI, Oliver Patel's Global AI Law Snapshot (EU, China, USA), and sovereign AI roadmaps across the UK, UAE, and Singapore.

## Analytical Overview
This volume gathers the legislative statutes, enforcement mechanisms, and sovereign strategies defining the emerging global regulatory order for artificial intelligence.

Key statutory frameworks:
1. **The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)**: Details the European Union's risk-tiered regulatory architecture: outright prohibitions (social scoring, cognitive manipulation, real-time biometric identification), stringent compliance mandates for High-Risk AI Systems (data governance, technical documentation, human oversight, cybersecurity), and specialized transparency obligations for General Purpose AI (GPAI) and systemic risk models ($>10^{25}$ FLOPs).
2. **US Executive Order 14110 & National Standards**: Dissects the White House Executive Order on Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, invoking the Defense Production Act to mandate red-team safety disclosures to NIST and the Department of Commerce for large frontier training runs.
3. **Sovereign AI & Global Divergence**: Incorporates comparative legal analyses by Oliver Patel alongside national dossiers from the UK, Singapore, and the UAE, contrasting the EU’s precautionary legislative codification with the US’s standards-driven executive guidance and China’s targeted Cyberspace Administration (CAC) algorithmic algorithms.
