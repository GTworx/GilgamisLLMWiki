## Summary
Four-dossier historical and legal compendium analyzing how real estate acquisitions have been deployed as tools of territorial sovereignty, comparing early Zionist land purchases in Ottoman Palestine and Mandatory Palestine with contemporary Israeli land acquisitions in Northern Cyprus (TRNC).

## Analytical Overview
### Real Estate as a Weapon of Territorial Sovereignty

*Land Purchases, Demographic Sovereignty, and Real Estate Statecraft: From Ottoman Palestine to Modern Northern Cyprus* investigates how private real estate transactions and capital accumulation have historically been weaponized to establish demographic and territorial control.

### Comparative Historical Trajectories

The compendium evaluates two interconnected historical arcs:
1. **The Ottoman Palestine Experience:** Documenting early Jewish Colonization Association (ICA) and Jewish National Fund (KKL) purchases in Late Ottoman Palestine; the failed Ottoman legislative decrees under Sultan Abdülhamid II restricting foreign land sales; and the subsequent British Mandatory land registry reforms that facilitated mass transfer of absentee Arab effendi estates.
2. **The Northern Cyprus (TRNC) Influx:** Investigative analysis into the surge of Israeli and foreign offshore entities acquiring massive coastal land parcels and luxury residential developments in the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC), analyzing the national security concerns raised by Turkish defense authorities regarding demographic sovereignty.
