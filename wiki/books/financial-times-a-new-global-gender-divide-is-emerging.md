## Summary
Landmark Financial Times statistical investigation documenting an unprecedented ideological polarization along gender lines among Gen Z adults across South Korea, the United States, Germany, the UK, and Poland.

## Analytical Overview
Reveals that while young women have moved sharply to the political left on cultural and economic issues, young men have simultaneously shifted rightward or remained conservative. Analyzes the social media algorithmic segregation, divergent economic pressures, dating market frictions, and anti-feminist backlash driving this unprecedented demographic split.
