## Executive Summary

This comprehensive 120-page two-year benchmarking report by venture studio High Alpha, in collaboration with SaaS Capital and industry partners, tracks the post-ZIRP (Zero Interest Rate Policy) operational realities and generative AI transitions across hundreds of private SaaS companies. The 2024–2025 editions analyze how software companies adapted to the end of growth-at-all-costs capital regimes by optimizing burn multiples, rightsizing go-to-market headcounts, and incorporating native generative AI features into subscription tiers. High Alpha details how top-quartile SaaS companies successfully monetize AI add-ons, maintain capital efficiency, and navigate shifting customer procurement cycles in an era of tightened enterprise software budgets.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **The Post-ZIRP Efficiency Shift**: Analyzes the dramatic operational pivot from hyper-growth to capital efficiency, tracking median Burn Multiples (Net Burn / Net New ARR) dropping from 2.0x+ down to disciplined 1.0x–1.2x ranges.
- **Generative AI Feature Monetization**: Investigates how SaaS vendors package AI: 65% of vendors launched AI features, but only 28% succeeded in monetizing them as distinct paid add-ons or usage tiers rather than base-tier table stakes.
- **Gross Margin Realities with LLM Inference**: Highlights that generative AI features introduce non-trivial compute costs (GPU/token inference overhead), compressing traditional 80%+ software gross margins down to 70%–75% for AI-heavy workflows.
- **Retention and Downgrade Pressures**: Documents enterprise software consolidation and vendor rationalization, which drove median gross retention rates down by 2%–3% across mid-market B2B software.
- **Founder-Led Sales to Scaled GTM**: Outlines benchmarks for scaling sales compensation, SDR-to-AE ratios, and outbound vs. inbound conversion rates as startups transition past the $10M ARR milestone.
