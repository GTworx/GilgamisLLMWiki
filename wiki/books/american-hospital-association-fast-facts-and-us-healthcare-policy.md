## Executive Summary
This institutional data dossier pairs the American Hospital Association (AHA) official 2023 *Fast Facts on US Hospitals* statistical reference with an analytical policy study comparing the Affordable Care Act (Obamacare) versus Trump Administration healthcare policy regarding immigrant health insurance coverage.

The AHA monograph provides census metrics on the 6,129 registered hospitals in the United States, auditing staffed bed capacity, admissions (exceeding 34 million annually), expenses (surpassing $1.3 trillion), uncompensated care burdens, and the financial stress confronting rural hospitals. The companion policy study dissects the statutory mechanisms of ACA exchange subsidies, Medicaid expansion, Section 1332 state innovation waivers, and the public charge rule affecting immigrant healthcare access.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **US Hospital Demographics and Bed Reductions**: Documents the ongoing contraction in staffed hospital beds (down to ~916,000 nationwide) despite an aging and clinically complex patient population.
- **Uncompensated Care and Operating Margin Pressures**: Analyzes how hundreds of rural and safety-net hospitals operate with negative operating margins due to uncompensated charity care and inadequate Medicaid reimbursement.
- **Affordable Care Act Coverage Expansions**: Details the impact of ACA health insurance marketplaces and federal premium tax credits in reducing the uninsured rate to historic lows.
- **Immigrant Health Coverage Policies**: Contrasts ACA non-discrimination provisions and marketplace access with federal restrictions (PRWORA 5-year Medicaid wait period) and changing administrative rules.
