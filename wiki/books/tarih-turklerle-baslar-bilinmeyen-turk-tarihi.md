## Executive Summary

Comprising an immense, 886-page compendium of lifelong published and unpublished research papers, epigraphic drawings, field notebooks, and dictionaries by **Kâzım Mirşan**, *Tarih Türk'lerle Başlar: Bilinmeyen Türk Tarihi* (*History Begins with the Turks: Unknown Turkish History*) brings together Mirşan's core decipherments of ancient rock inscriptions across Siberia, Central Asia, Anatolia, Etruria, and Western Europe. Explicitly dedicated to Mustafa Kemal Atatürk's historical vision, the volume asserts that human civilization, runic writing, and monotheistic philosophy originated among Proto-Turkic steppe confederations.

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **Inversion of the Mediterranean Paradigm**: Directly challenges Samuel Noah Kramer's classic dictum *'History Begins at Sumer'*, arguing instead that historical and written civilization originated in high Central Asia.
2. **Alphabet Evolution from Steppe Tamgas**: Claims that the Phoenician alphabet (and hence Greek and Latin) was derived from earlier Eurasian nomadic tamga systems rather than Egyptian hieroglyphs.
3. **Comprehensive Epigraphic Documentation**: Serves as a vital visual archive containing hundreds of hand-drawn facsimiles of remote rock petroglyphs across Inner Asia that are inaccessible to standard Western research.
