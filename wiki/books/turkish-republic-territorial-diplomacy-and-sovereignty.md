## Analytical Overview
### Peaceful Sovereign Diplomacy in the Post-Ottoman Era

*Turkish Republic Territorial Sovereignty: Borders, Accessions, and International Treaties Since 1923* examines how the Republic of Turkey successfully consolidated, defended, and peacefully expanded its sovereign borders following the Treaty of Lausanne (1923).

### Masterclasses in Interwar Statecraft

The treatise highlights two triumphs of Atatürkian diplomacy:
1. **The Montreux Straits Convention (1936):** Skillfully exploiting European power rivalries during the rise of Fascist Italy and Nazi Germany to peacefully remilitarize the Turkish Straits, ending the International Straits Commission and securing total Turkish sovereignty.
2. **The Hatay Accession (1939):** Orchestrating a masterclass in multi-stage diplomatic maneuvering, plebiscites, and League of Nations negotiations that peacefully incorporated the Sanjak of Alexandretta (Hatay) from French-mandated Syria into the Turkish Republic on the eve of World War II.

The text documents Turkey's strict adherence to "Peace at Home, Peace in the World" and international law.
