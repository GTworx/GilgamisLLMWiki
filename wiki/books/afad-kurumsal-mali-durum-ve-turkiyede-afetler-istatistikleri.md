## Executive Summary

This 150-page two-volume institutional data dossier integrates AFAD's official 2022 Financial Status and Expectations Report (*Kurumsal Mali Durum ve Beklentiler Raporu*, 82 pp.) with the historical statistical compendium *Türkiye'de Afetler: İstatistikler* (68 pp.). Together, these monographs provide an empirical foundation evaluating the budgetary allocations, expenditure execution, and historical incidence rates of catastrophic disasters in Türkiye over the last century.

The statistical monograph catalogs earthquakes, floods, landslides, rockfalls, and avalanches between 1900 and 2020, demonstrating that earthquakes account for over 70% of disaster-induced mortalities and direct economic damage, while landslides and floods constitute the highest operational frequency. The financial report details AFAD's institutional budget lines, emergency response reserve funds, procurement frameworks, and capital investments in early warning infrastructure.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Historical Disaster Incidence Profiling**: Quantifies disaster distribution across 81 provinces, highlighting the geological vulnerability of the North Anatolian, East Anatolian, and Western Anatolian graben systems.
- **Emergency Budget Mechanics**: Details the operational execution of disaster appropriations, emergency response contingency funds, and inter-budgetary transfers during acute crises.
- **Asset Investment and Technology Procurement**: Audits expenditures on advanced seismic monitoring stations, search-and-rescue equipment, mobile communications infrastructure, and emergency warehouse networks.
- **Multi-Hazard Risk Disaggregation**: Identifies regional threat multipliers, such as coastal floods and severe landslides in the Black Sea region versus massive seismic vulnerability in Marmara and Eastern Anatolia.
