## Summary
Expansive 22-issue archival compendium of The Economist from 2023 covering the collapse of Silicon Valley Bank and Credit Suisse, the explosive rise of generative AI, 'higher-for-longer' central bank policy rates, and the outbreak of the Gaza war.

## Analytical Overview
Examines the systemic banking turbulence of March 2023 (SVB, Signature Bank, emergency acquisition of Credit Suisse by UBS). Analyzes the economic potential and labor risks of LLMs (ChatGPT-4), resilient consumer spending amidst quantitative tightening, rising sovereign debt service costs, green industrial subsidies (IRA), and expanding trade conflicts in the Middle East and South China Sea.
