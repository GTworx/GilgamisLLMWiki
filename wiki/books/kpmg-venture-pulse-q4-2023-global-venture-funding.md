Monumental 96-page global market research report by KPMG analyzing venture capital investment, fundraising rounds, exit values, and sector breakdowns across the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific at the close of 2023.

## Analytical Overview
### Global Diagnostic of the Venture Capital Retrenchment

*Venture Pulse Q4 2023: Global Venture Funding Trends, Megarounds, and the Private Market Reset*, authored by KPMG Private Enterprise, delivers a comprehensive 96-page quantitative audit of global venture financing at the conclusion of 2023. Capturing the full magnitude of the post-2021 funding reset, the report tracks how venture capital investment contracted globally by over 30% from peak levels while artificial intelligence absorbed a disproportionate share of total capital.

### Granular Regional Analyses

The report provides detailed regional breakdowns across the globe:
- **Americas (US, Canada, Latin America):** Tracking the resilience of mega-rounds ($100M+) concentrated in foundation AI models (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI) against the severe drought in consumer internet and fintech rounds.
- **Europe:** Documenting the stabilization of European venture ecosystems, led by clean tech, battery gigafactories, and B2B SaaS hubs in the UK, France, and Germany.
- **Asia-Pacific:** Analyzing the profound slowdown in Chinese venture financing amid regulatory scrutiny and geopolitical decoupling, contrasted with surging venture activity in India and Southeast Asia.

### The Exit Conundrum

The report provides extensive data on the virtual freeze in venture-backed IPOs, analyzing the mounting backlog of late-stage unicorns requiring structural liquidity.
