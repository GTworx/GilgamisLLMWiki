## Summary
Monumental 155-page dual-volume sociological dissertation investigating why individuals voluntarily contribute knowledge to peer-production networks without monetary compensation, alongside empirical modeling of virtual community flame wars and moderation conflicts.

## Analytical Overview
Applies anthropological theories of gift economies, Marcel Mauss's reciprocal obligations, and Ostrom's common-pool resource governance to Wikipedia and open-source ecosystems. Models how status competition, cognitive dissonance, and territorial editing behaviors trigger intense flame wars, providing moderation protocols to preserve egalitarian collaboration.
