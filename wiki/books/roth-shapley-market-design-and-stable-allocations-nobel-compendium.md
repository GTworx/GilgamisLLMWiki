## Summary
Authoritative scientific compendium of the 2012 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences awarded to Alvin Roth and Lloyd Shapley for the theoretical foundation of cooperative game theory and the practical engineering of two-sided matching markets.

## Analytical Overview
Traces the evolution from David Gale and Lloyd Shapley's 1962 deferred acceptance algorithm (Gale-Shapley) to Alvin Roth's real-world institutional engineering of the National Resident Matching Program (NRMP), NYC high school placement algorithms, and national paired kidney exchange networks, establishing the discipline of market design without prices.
