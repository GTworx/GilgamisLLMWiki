## Executive Summary

Developed by the Center for Internet Security (CIS) in collaboration with the SANS Institute and international cyber defense agencies, this technical guide synthesizes the **CIS Critical Security Controls** into an actionable defensive blueprint against the top 20 real-world cyber attack techniques. Grounded in actual adversary tradecraft observed across global threat feeds (MITRE ATT&CK), the whitepaper proves that implementing the foundational baseline controls—such as comprehensive hardware/software inventorying, continuous vulnerability management, administrative privilege restriction, and centralized audit log monitoring—thwarts the vast majority of automated ransomware campaigns and opportunistic cyber intrusions.

---

## Key Strategic & Architectural Takeaways

- **Prioritized, Threat-Informed Defense**: Shifts organizational focus away from speculative compliance checkboxes toward prioritized technical controls directly mapped to adversary tactics (initial access, execution, persistence, privilege escalation, lateral movement).
- **The Supremacy of Basic Hygiene**: Over 85% of successful corporate breaches exploit well-known vulnerabilities for which patches have existed for months, unmanaged 'shadow IT' assets, or default administrative credentials.
- **Control 1 & 2: Complete Asset and Software Inventory**: An organization cannot defend what it does not know exists; active network discovery and automated software tracking represent the indispensable foundation of all security architecture.
- **Restricting Administrative Privileges (Control 5 & 6)**: Enforces the principle of least privilege, blocking non-admin accounts from executing arbitrary binaries, which decisively halts malware staging and lateral credential dumping (e.g., Mimikatz).
- **Continuous Log Collection and Endpoint Detection (Control 8 & 10)**: Highlights that median adversary dwell time before detection can exceed weeks without centralized SIEM/SOAR ingestion and automated endpoint detection and response (EDR) telemetry.
