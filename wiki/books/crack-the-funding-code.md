## Executive Summary

Written by strategic connector, angel investor, and former biotech CEO **Judy Robinett**—with a Foreword by original *Shark Tank* investor **Kevin Harrington** (pp. 13–17)—*Crack the Funding Code* (2019, copyright p. 7) demystifies early-stage fundraising from the investor's vantage point. Robinett argues that fundraising fails less often from weak ideas than from **misaligned communication and weak relationship networks**: founders pitch product features while investors evaluate risk-adjusted returns, leadership credibility, and warm trusted referrals (Part I–II, pp. 24–95).

Across four practical parts and extensive appendices (pp. 11–12, 190–218), Robinett pairs high-trust "Power Connecting" strategies with concrete financial preparation, risk-mitigation checklists, a full sample pitch deck, and due-diligence readiness guides.

---

## Key Fundraising Playbooks

1. **Investor Psychology & The 3 Cs (Part I, pp. 24–45)**: Details what angel groups, syndicates, family offices, and VCs filter for—and the instant dealbreakers (uncoachability, muddled unit economics, naive market sizing) that kill meetings within minutes.
2. **Mapping Hidden Capital & Power Connecting (Part II, pp. 46–95)**: Expands the founder's capital search beyond top-tier VCs (angel networks, grants, family offices, corporate VCs, crowdfunding) and teaches warm-introduction networking mechanics to reach decision-makers directly.
3. **Clear Financials, Pitch Craft & Risk Mitigation (Part III, pp. 96–162)**: Translates venture storytelling into investor-ready financials ("Nine Numbers", p. 2) and systematically retires execution, market, and team risks before asking for a check.
4. **Valuation, Deal Terms & Due Diligence (Part IV & Appendices, pp. 163–218)**: Covers term-sheet red flags, when to walk away from toxic capital, and exhaustive due-diligence question banks and checklists.
