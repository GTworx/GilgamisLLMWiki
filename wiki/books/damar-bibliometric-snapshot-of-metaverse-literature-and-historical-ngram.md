## Overview
Published in the inaugural issue of the Journal of Metaverse by Turkish informatics scholar Muhammet Damar, accompanied by longitudinal Google Ngram corpus data, this study conducts an empirical bibliometric evaluation of the emergence of the term 'Metaverse'. Tracing literature from Neal Stephenson's 1992 Snow Crash through the 2021 explosion, Damar maps authorship networks, institutional hubs, and dominant research clusters.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Documents the dramatic linguistic surge in 'Metaverse' occurrences in global literature following corporate rebrands in late 2021.
- **Key Finding**: Maps global research hubs, revealing South Korea, the United States, and China as early publishing leaders in virtual environments.
- **Key Finding**: Identifies dominant keyword clusters: virtual reality, blockchain, digital twins, and immersive education.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Bibliometric mapping reveals that what seemed like an overnight marketing explosion in 2021 was underpinned by three decades of steady, incremental engineering in distributed virtual environments."
