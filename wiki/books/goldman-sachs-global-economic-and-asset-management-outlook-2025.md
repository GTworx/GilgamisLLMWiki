## Summary
Comprehensive 69-page institutional macroeconomic compendium from Goldman Sachs evaluating the US economic trajectory under proposed tariff and tax policies alongside European growth stagnation and ECB rate cuts.

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes Jan Hatzius's US economic projections forecasting sustained above-trend GDP growth alongside sticky service inflation. Analyzes the Euro Area's industrial competitiveness headwinds and sluggish German export recovery, outlining tactical asset allocation strategies for public equities, private credit, and sovereign bond duration.
