## Executive Summary

This 39-page strategic technology treatise by Accenture Strategy & Technology Consulting outlines the imperative for Global 2000 enterprises to construct a modern 'Digital Core'. Moving beyond superficial cloud lift-and-shift migrations, Accenture formulates an integrated architectural framework across cloud platforms, modular data architectures, generative AI layers, and automated security fabrics. The report quantifies the financial outperformance of enterprises that achieve digital core maturity—demonstrating 2.6x higher revenue growth and 2.5x higher enterprise value multiples—while detailing transformation roadmaps across technical debt reduction, interoperability, and continuous cloud optimization.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **The Digital Core Architectural Framework**: Integrates cloud infrastructure, modern data platforms, generative AI execution layers, and automated DevSecOps into a unified operating engine.
- **Overcoming the Lift-and-Shift Trap**: Explains why legacy infrastructure migrations that fail to re-architect for cloud-native elasticity and microservices incur higher operating costs without delivering agility.
- **Data Architecture as AI Prerequisite**: Emphasizes that generative AI applications are constrained not by model architectures, but by the cleanliness, governance, and real-time accessibility of enterprise data lakes.
- **FinOps and Unit Economics Governance**: Details the institutional implementation of Cloud Financial Management (FinOps) to track compute spending per business transaction and eliminate idle infrastructure sprawl.
- **Quantified Business Outperformance**: Companies with mature digital core implementations achieve 60% higher total shareholder returns and dramatically compressed product release cycles compared to architectural laggards.
