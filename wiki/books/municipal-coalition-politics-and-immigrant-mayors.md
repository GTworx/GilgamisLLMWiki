Six-dossier urban political sociology compendium comparing the electoral strategies and coalition governance of immigrant-descendant leaders across London (Sadiq Khan) and New York City (Zohran Mamdani), analyzing demographic voting blocs and inter-ethnic tensions.

## Analytical Overview
### Ethnic Coalitions and Progressive Politics in Global Metropolises

*Municipal Coalition Politics and Immigrant Mayors* compiles six detailed political science and demographic dossiers examining how immigrant-descendant leaders build winning electoral coalitions in the world's most diverse global cities: London and New York.

### Comparing Sadiq Khan and Zohran Mamdani

The compendium contrasts two prominent political paths:
- **Sadiq Khan (Mayor of London):** A masterclass in center-left, Blairite managerial pragmatism; building broad coalitions across business elites, working-class Muslim minorities, and liberal middle-class professionals to win three consecutive historic mayoral terms.
- **Zohran Mamdani (New York State Assemblymember / NYC Mayoral Contender):** Representing democratic socialist (DSA) grassroots politics, organizing tenant unions, taxi workers, and progressive youth coalitions while challenging entrenched municipal party machines.
- **Navigating Ethnic and Religious Sensitivities:** Granular demographic analysis mapping Jewish, Muslim, South Asian, and Afro-Caribbean voting power in New York and London.
