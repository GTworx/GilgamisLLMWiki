## Executive Summary

Executive coach, attorney, and former senior corporate executive **Beverly E. Jones** (Career Press, 2016, copyright p. 5; Foreword by Kerry Hannon) translates entrepreneurial agency into a 50-tip career-resilience handbook for professionals operating inside or alongside modern organizations. Recognizing that lifetime single-employer security has vanished, Jones urges every knowledge worker—whether corporate manager, government lawyer, mid-career pivoter, or solopreneur—to manage their career as an independent enterprise of one.

Blending behavioral psychology, workplace pragmatism, and decades of executive coaching across industry and public service (pp. 2–4), *Think Like an Entrepreneur, Act Like a CEO* breaks career mastery into digestible, actionable micro-playbooks designed to help readers absorb organizational shocks, navigate office politics with integrity, reinvent skills across multiple career chapters, and sustain personal well-being alongside ambition.

---

## Core Career-Ownership Pillars

1. **Entrepreneurial Mindset Inside Organizations**: Treats every job as a portfolio of transferable capabilities, relationships, and measurable value creation rather than passive dependence on a single employer's org chart.
2. **Executive Presence & Strategic Self-Stewardship**: Applies CEO habits—deliberate communication, boundary-setting, reputational capital management, and calm crisis response—to day-to-day workplace friction and layoffs.
3. **Perpetual Reinvention Across Life Stages**: Champions lifelong adaptability (dedicated to Jones's 95-year-old artist mother, p. 6), equipping professionals to bounce back from setbacks and launch second or third careers intentionally.
