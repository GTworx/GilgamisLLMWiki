## Summary
Deep analytical white paper by Ray Dalio detailing the financial, monetary, and currency implications of universal tariffs, trade wars, retaliatory escalations, and global supply chain re-shoring.

## Analytical Overview
Explains how tariffs function as a domestic sales tax on consumers while shifting relative terms of trade. Examines the historical precedents of the 1930 Smoot-Hawley Tariff Act, currency depreciations as competitive counter-measures, the inflationary impact on supply chains, and how trade friction accelerates the geopolitical decoupling between the United States and China.
