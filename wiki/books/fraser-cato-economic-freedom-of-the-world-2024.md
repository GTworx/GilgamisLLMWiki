## Summary
Authoritative 110-page benchmark surveying economic freedom across 165 jurisdictions across five core pillars: size of government, legal system and property rights, sound money, freedom to trade internationally, and regulation.

## Analytical Overview
Ranks nations based on objective indicators measuring sound monetary policy, trade barriers, regulatory burdens, and rule of law. Documents the continued decline of economic freedom in Hong Kong under mainland integration, compares Singapore's top ranking with OECD economies, and demonstrates that nations in the top quartile of economic freedom exhibit far higher life expectancy, literacy, and lowest-decile income levels.
