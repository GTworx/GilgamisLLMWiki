## Executive Summary

Success coach and humorist **Jen Sincero** (Running Press, 2013, Copyright p. 7, ISBN 978-0-7624-4831-9; 689 mobile-reflowed PDF pages) delivers a blunt, irreverent, self-deprecating personal-transformation handbook designed for readers allergic to solemn New Age rhetoric. Organized into five punchy parts across twenty-seven short chapters—**How You Got This Way** (Ch. 1–5), **How to Embrace Your Inner Badass** (Ch. 6–9), **How to Tap into the Motherlode** (Ch. 10–16), **How to Get Over Your B.S. Already** (Ch. 17–22), and **How to Kick Some Ass** (Ch. 23–27, TOC pp. 10–15)—Sincero blends cognitive reframing, habit disruption, and unapologetic self-belief.

Her core psychological diagnosis centers on **"The Big Snooze"** (Ch. 4, p. 100)—the protective, comfort-seeking ego and inherited subconscious limiting beliefs (Ch. 1, p. 39) that disguise fear as "realism," procrastination, perfectionism, and financial guilt (Ch. 18–20, 24).

---

## Core Behavioral & Mindset Principles

1. **Interrupting Subconscious Scripts (Parts 1 & 4)**: Exposes how unexamined childhood beliefs about worthiness and money silently sabotage adult choices, prescribing daily gratitude (Ch. 14), forgiveness (Ch. 15), and deliberate reframing ("Fear Is for Suckers", Ch. 20).
2. **The Almighty Decision & Action Over Spewing (Part 5, Ch. 23 & 26)**: Insists that passive wishing changes nothing; transformation begins only with an irreversible commitment ("deciding" vs. "wanting") backed by messy, relentless real-world execution ("Doing vs. Spewing").
3. **Money Mindset & Environment Curation (Ch. 24)**: Challenges scarcity shame around earning and investing, urging readers to upgrade their peer group, invest in coaches/mentors, and act boldly before feeling ready.
