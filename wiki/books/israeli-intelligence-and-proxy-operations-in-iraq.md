## Analytical Overview
### Covert Statecraft and Intelligence Operations in Iraq

*Shadows over Mesopotamia: Israeli Intelligence, Covert Operations, and Kurdish Proxy Networks in Iraq* compiles two detailed intelligence histories tracking Israel's clandestine activities in Iraq over seven decades.

### Three Epochs of Clandestine Engagement

The dossier investigates:
- **The Early Operations (1950s):** Operation Ezra and Nehemiah, documenting the complex covert bombings in Baghdad that accelerated the emigration of Iraq's ancient Jewish community.
- **The Kurdish Alliance & Osirak (1960s–1980s):** Early Mossad military training and weapons supply to Mulla Mustafa Barzani's Peshmerga forces to tie down the Iraqi Army; and Operation Babylon (1981), the surgical airstrike that destroyed the Osirak nuclear reactor.
- **Post-2003 Surveillance Outposts:** Establishing intelligence listening posts and UAV launch bases in Iraqi Kurdistan to monitor Iranian IRGC cross-border movements and support covert assassinations of Iranian nuclear scientists.
