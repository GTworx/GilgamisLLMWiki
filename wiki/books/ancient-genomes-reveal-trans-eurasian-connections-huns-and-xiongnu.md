## Summary
Decisive archaeogenetic and philological breakthrough confirming direct trans-Eurasian biological, linguistic, and material connections between the East Asian Xiongnu Empire and the European Huns of the Migration Period.

## Analytical Overview
Presents high-resolution genomic data from 5th-century European Hunnic burials in the Carpathian basin and Scandinavia, cross-referenced with Moravcsik's Byzantinoturcica and Late Antique chronicles. Proves that Hunnic elite lineages carried predominant East Asian / Xiongnu paternal haplogroups and maternal ancestries, settling a centuries-old historical debate.
