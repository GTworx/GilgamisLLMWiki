## Executive Summary

This foundational executive guide by McKinsey & Company provides business leaders with an accessible yet rigorous breakdown of quantum computing principles, technological roadmaps, and economic value pools. Synthesizing quantum concepts (superposition, entanglement, interference) into strategic business implications, McKinsey estimates that quantum computing could unlock between $1.3 trillion and $2 trillion in global economic value across four core industries by 2035: chemicals and materials, automotive, financial services, and life sciences. The report outlines operational guidelines for developing enterprise quantum strategies and navigating talent shortages.

---

## Key Strategic, Scientific & Engineering Takeaways

- **Trillion-Dollar Economic Value Pools**: Projects that quantum computing will create up to $2 trillion in economic value by 2035, driven primarily by molecular simulation, complex system optimization, and machine learning enhancements.
- **The Four Lead Industries**: Details specific value creation mechanisms across chemicals (catalysis and battery design), automotive (battery optimization and lightweight materials), finance (portfolio optimization and risk modeling), and pharmaceuticals (drug candidate docking).
- **Hardware Modality Overview**: Translates complex quantum physics modalities (superconducting, trapped ion, neutral atom, photonic, silicon spin) into business metrics: gate speed, coherence time, physical footprint, and supply chain maturity.
- **The Fault-Tolerance Imperative**: Clarifies the distinction between current noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) systems and future fault-tolerant quantum computers (FTQC), highlighting why true enterprise value depends on logical qubits.
- **Executive Action Plan**: Outlines five immediate steps for corporate leaders: monitor technological milestones, evaluate sector-specific vulnerabilities, identify algorithmic use cases, partner with quantum cloud providers, and invest in talent development.
