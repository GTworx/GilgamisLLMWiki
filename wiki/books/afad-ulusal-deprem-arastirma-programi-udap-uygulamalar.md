## Executive Summary

This comprehensive 92-page compendium published by AFAD's Earthquake Department documents the competitive research achievements and applied breakthroughs supported under the National Earthquake Research Program (UDAP). UDAP serves as the primary national grant mechanism funding collaborative research between Turkish universities, research institutes (TÜBİTAK MAM, Kandilli), and public agencies in seismology, earthquake engineering, and disaster social sciences.

The monograph details multi-disciplinary projects, including real-time earthquake early warning algorithms for the Marmara region, empirical ground motion models (GMPEs) calibrated for Anatolian tectonic regimes, advanced soil-structure interaction simulations, non-destructive concrete strength assessment technologies, and geodetic strain-rate accumulation mapping using continuous GPS/GNSS networks along the North and East Anatolian faults.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Competitive Research Funding Architecture**: Analyzes the project selection, funding allocation, and industrial translation mechanisms that bridge academic earth science and municipal engineering practice.
- **Early Warning and Rapid ShakeMap Systems**: Highlights algorithms calculating automated shake maps and spectral intensity within seconds of P-wave arrival to trip automated shut-off valves in gas grids and industrial plants.
- **Geodetic Strain Accumulation Mapping**: Documents the integration of InSAR satellite interferometry and permanent GNSS stations to measure millimeter-scale crustal deformation along active faults.
- **International Scientific Case Studies**: Benchmarks UDAP funded initiatives against international programs in Japan (NIED), the United States (USGS/NEHRP), and Italy (INGV).
