Monumental 9-dossier research compendium tracking Israeli military operations since 1948, evaluating peace proposal rejections (Camp David, Taba, 2025 proposals), domestic Israeli protests against Netanyahu, Boston Consulting Group population transfer modeling, and the economic toll of the Gaza War.

## Analytical Overview
### Comprehensive Anatomy of the Israel-Palestine Conflict

*Cognitive Biases, Peace Rejections, and Military Operations in the Levant (1948–2025)* unites nine investigative and historical research dossiers providing an unvarnished, empirical examination of the century-long struggle for Palestine.

### Strategic and Historical Themes Covered

The compendium delivers rigorous historical documentation:
- **Deconstructing the 'No Partner for Peace' Myth:** Re-evaluating the historical peace negotiations (Oslo, 2000 Camp David, 2001 Taba, 2008 Olmert-Abbas talks), demonstrating that failures were driven by mutual domestic political constraints rather than a unilateral Palestinian rejection of peace.
- **Israeli Military Operations Ledger (1948–2025):** Chronological catalog of major campaigns (Suez 1956, Six-Day War 1967, Lebanon 1982/2006, Cast Lead 2008, Protective Edge 2014, Swords of Iron 2023–2025).
- **The Boston Consulting Group Gaza Transfer Controversy:** Investigative reporting on leaked strategic plans modeling the economic and demographic costs of mass Palestinian displacement into Sinai.
- **Economic Hemorrhage & Internal Division:** Quantifying the catastrophic fiscal impact of the multi-front war on Israel's high-tech economy alongside mass domestic protests against judicial overhauls.
