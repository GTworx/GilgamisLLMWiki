## Overview
Authored by prominent Turkish psychologist Nebi Sümer (Herkese Bilim Teknoloji) alongside comparative epistemic studies, this two-part treatise contrasts the cognitive architecture of political leaders versus scientific thinkers. The authors analyze confirmation bias, charismatic rhetoric, Machiavellian social intelligence, and intellectual humility in public governance.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Contrasts the scientist's truth-seeking, uncertainty-embracing cognitive style with the politician's tribal coalition-building logic.
- **Key Finding**: Explains why high raw intelligence frequently amplifies ideological rationalization rather than objective truth-seeking.
- **Key Finding**: Advocates for institutional mechanisms that embed scientific falsification principles into democratic policymaking.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Politikacının zekâsı kitleleri mobilize eden duygusal ikna üzerine kuruludur; bilim insanının zekâsı ise kendi hipotezini acımasızca çürütmeye hazır bir epistemik dürüstlük gerektirir."
