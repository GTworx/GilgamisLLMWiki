## Executive Summary

Published on September 20, 2024, by the Financial Times in an in-depth analytical interview and investigation featuring leading global energy economists, this 11-page geopolitical and industrial analysis dissects the unprecedented speed and sheer scale of China's clean energy transformation (*China's Accelerating Green Transition*).

The analysis documents how China has achieved overwhelming global manufacturing dominance across solar photovoltaic (PV) modules (>80% of world capacity), lithium-ion battery cells (>75%), and electric vehicles (EVs). It demonstrates that China is installing more solar and wind power capacity every single year than the rest of the world combined, putting the nation on track to peak its greenhouse gas emissions years ahead of its official 2030 target, while creating profound supply chain chokepoints and trade tensions with the EU and United States.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Unmatched Deployment Scale**: Details China's installation of over 200 gigawatts of solar and wind capacity annually, driven by colossal desert renewable energy megabases in Xinjiang and Inner Mongolia.
- **Clean Energy as Primary Economic Growth Engine**: Demonstrates that clean energy technologies (solar, batteries, EVs) became the single largest contributor to China's GDP growth in 2023, surpassing traditional real estate development.
- **Global Supply Chain Concentration**: Analyzes the intense western vulnerability to Chinese supply chain monopolies in polysilicon, solar wafers, battery anodes/cathodes, and rare-earth magnet refining.
- **Geopolitical Friction and Trade Barriers**: Explores Western protectionist countermeasures, including US Section 301 tariffs, EU anti-subsidy investigations into Chinese EVs, and cross-border carbon tariffs (CBAM).
