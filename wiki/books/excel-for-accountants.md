## Executive Summary

*Excel for Accountants* (CPA911 Publishing, Philadelphia, 2007, 289 pp.) by quantitative analyst and Excel author [[conrad-carlberg]] is a practitioner-focused manual tailored specifically to certified public accountants, financial controllers, and auditors who use Microsoft Excel alongside general-ledger platforms such as QuickBooks (source: BOOKS/Investment_/Excel for Accountant_.pdf, Title Page, Copyright & Table of Contents, pp. 2–4).

Moving far beyond basic spreadsheet entry, Carlberg teaches structured database hygiene, multi-key sorting, pivot-table financial reporting, dynamic range auditing, depreciation and amortization schedules, cash-flow forecasting, and statistical exception analysis—treating the spreadsheet as a disciplined accounting control environment rather than an ad hoc scratchpad (pp. 4–289).

## Key Arguments & Structural Breakdown

### Structured Data Hygiene & Ledger Integration
- **Lists as Relational Tables (Ch. 1)**: Establishes strict column-header, record-integrity, and multi-key sorting discipline so imported QuickBooks and ERP exports remain audit-ready for downstream pivot reporting (pp. 4–30).
- **Automated Reporting & Financial Controls**: Details PivotTables, lookup logic, variance tracking, and external database queries to eliminate error-prone manual copy-pasting across monthly close cycles.

### Quantitative Accounting & Decision Modeling
- **Forecasting, Depreciation & Working Capital**: Applies Excel's built-in accounting, time-value-of-money, and regression functions to fixed-asset schedules, budget-versus-actual diagnostics, and audit sampling.
