## Executive Summary

Delivered at Sainte-Anne Hospital in Paris during 1954–1955, edited by **Jacques-Alain Miller** (Seuil, 1978), and translated into Turkish by **Savaş Kılıç** (Metis Yayınları, *Ötekini Dinlemek* #25, Feb. 2022, 404 pp., pp. 2–4), **Jacques Lacan**'s *Seminar Book II* executes a radical "return to Freud" against postwar American ego psychology. Rather than viewing the **Ego (*Ben*)** as an autonomous, adaptive synthesizer of reality, Lacan demonstrates through Freud's *Beyond the Pleasure Principle*, *Project for a Scientific Psychology*, and *The Interpretation of Dreams* that the ego is an **imaginary object** formed through narcissistic misrecognition—decentered by the autonomous logic of the **Symbolic Order**.

Remarkably ahead of its time, Seminar II draws heavily on **cybernetics, information theory, and game theory** (Ch. VI–VII *"Freud, Hegel ve Makine"* and *"Çevrim"*, plus Lacan's famed reading of Edgar Allan Poe's *The Purloined Letter*, TOC p. 5) to show how repeating chains of signifiers determine human subjectivity independently of conscious intention.

---

## Core Lacanian Formulations in Seminar II

1. **Schema L & Imaginary vs. Symbolic**: Differentiates the dual, specular axis of ego-to-alter-ego rivalry ($a - a'$) from the unconscious Symbolic axis linking the subject ($S$) to the **Big Other ($A$)**—the treasury of language and law.
2. **Repetition Automaton & Cybernetic Circuits (Ch. III–VII)**: Interprets Freud's death drive and repetition compulsion not as biological self-destruction, but as the relentless insistence of the signifying chain—akin to closed cybernetic feedback loops and binary probability games.
3. **Reading Freud's *Irma's Injection* Dream & Poe's *Purloined Letter***: Shows how the circulating signifier positions every subject along a structural circuit regardless of individual psychology.
