## Analytical Overview
### The Definitive Longitudinal Census of Global Innovation Hubs

The *Startup Genome Global Startup Ecosystem Reports (2015–2023)* compendium unites five landmark research reports published by Startup Genome, the world's premier data and policy advisory organization for innovation hubs. Spanning nearly a decade of empirical research over millions of startups and thousands of venture transactions across 300+ metropolitan regions, this compendium provides the definitive longitudinal analysis of how startup ecosystems are born, mature, and achieve global scale.

### The Lifecycle Model of Ecosystem Evolution

Startup Genome formulates a four-stage developmental model:
1. **Activation:** Low startup density, limited local venture capital, and severe founder brain drain to Tier-1 hubs.
2. **Globalization:** Emergence of local series A/B funds, foreign VC syndication, and the first regional unicorn exits creating local angel capital.
3. **Expansion:** Dense talent clusters, multiple large exits ($1B+), and specialized sector leadership.
4. **Integration:** Fully self-sustaining global top-tier hubs (Silicon Valley, New York, London) maintaining multi-sector dominance.

### Specialized Sector Atlases

The compendium features deep vertical investigations into the **Blue Economy** (sustainable ocean logistics, offshore aquaculture) and **ClimateTech** (industrial decarbonization, grid storage).
