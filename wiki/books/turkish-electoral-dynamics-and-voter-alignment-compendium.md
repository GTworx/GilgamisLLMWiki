## Summary
Monumental 10-dossier empirical compendium analyzing Turkish voting behavior, party polarization, macroeconomic policy scenarios, and electoral outcomes across general and local elections from 2015 to 2024.

## Analytical Overview
### Decade-Long Empirical Anatomy of Turkish Elections

The *Turkish Electoral Dynamics and Voter Alignment Compendium (2015–2024)* brings together ten exhaustive field research studies, investment bank macro evaluations (Bank of America, Morgan Stanley), and polling datasets (Yöneylem, MetroPOLL, Ipsos) tracking Turkish political sociology.

### Key Electoral Watersheds Documented

The compendium chronicles the critical turning points of modern Turkish politics:
- **The 2023 Presidential & Parliamentary Watershed:** Comprehensive pre-election polling field surveys tracking voter shifts between the People's Alliance (Cumhur İttifakı) and Nation Alliance (Millet İttifakı), evaluating economic scenario projections under Orthodox vs. Heterodox fiscal tracks.
- **The March 2024 Local Elections Reset:** Post-election polling across major metropolitan centers (Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir) explaining the nationwide surge of the opposition (CHP) and the erosion of ruling party support under severe consumer inflation.
- **Sociodemographic Cleavages:** Granular cross-tabulations analyzing youth Gen-Z voting patterns, Kurdish voter realignments, and shifting economic grievances in Anatolian working-class districts.
