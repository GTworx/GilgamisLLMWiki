## Executive Summary
Comprising the official Turkish National Police (Emniyet Genel Müdürlüğü) TUBİM *Turkish Drug Report 2021* (140 pp.), Dr. Mahmut Cengiz's critical *Foreign Policy* investigation on narcotics enforcement, and regional Southeastern Anatolia Project (GAP) drug abuse surveys, this 210-page intelligence compendium audits Türkiye's position on the global drug trafficking transit routes. The dossier examines the historic Balkan Route (heroin from Afghanistan via Iran through Türkiye to Western Europe), the emerging maritime cocaine corridor from South America via West Africa into Turkish deep-water ports, and regional synthetic captagon flows across the Syrian border. The reports analyze interdiction seizures, domestic addiction demographics, and police intelligence strategies.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Strategic Crossroads of the Balkan Route**: Türkiye accounts for more heroin and opiate seizures annually than all 27 European Union member states combined, serving as the critical buffer intercepting Golden Crescent narcotics.
- **The Emergence of the Maritime Cocaine Hub**: Multi-ton maritime cocaine seizures in Mediterranean and Marmara ports reveal that South American cartels increasingly route cocaine through Türkiye toward European and Gulf markets.
- **Captagon and Syrian Border Dynamics**: Documents how a decade of Syrian civil war turned northern Syria into a narco-state producing billions of synthetic fenethylline (Captagon) pills transiting Turkish borders.
- **Interdiction Success vs. Domestic Demand**: While law enforcement achieves record seizure volumes, domestic addiction rates for methamphetamine and synthetics (Bonzai) continue to expand among youth in urban peripheries.
