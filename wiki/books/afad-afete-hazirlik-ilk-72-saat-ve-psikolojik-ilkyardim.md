## Executive Summary

This essential 156-page three-volume civil defense and clinical behavioral health manual synthesizes AFAD's definitive civilian survival guides: *İlk 72 Saat: Afetlere Hazırlık Kılavuzu* (68 pp.), *Olağandışı Durumlarda Yaşamın Sürdürülmesi* (52 pp.), and *Afet ve Acil Durumlarda Psikolojik İlk Yardım* (36 pp., co-developed with clinical psychiatric experts). Together, these manuals outline the essential protocols required for survival during the acute isolation phase of major catastrophes.

The first two manuals detail family disaster plans, emergency survival kit (afet çantası) assembly, structural non-structural hazard mitigation in residential apartments, water purification techniques, and field sanitation in collapsed urban environments. The third manual outlines evidence-based Psychological First Aid (PFA) protocols, detailing crisis de-escalation, active listening, acute stress reaction management, and secondary trauma mitigation for survivors and frontline rescue workers.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **The Critical First 72 Hours**: Establishes that citizens must achieve total self-sufficiency in potable water, non-perishable calories, medical supplies, and shelter for at least 72 hours following a major catastrophe.
- **Non-Structural Hazard Mitigation (YOTA)**: Details anchoring protocols for heavy furniture, gas heaters, water heaters, and overhead lighting fixtures to prevent severe blunt trauma injuries.
- **Field Water and Sanitation Hygiene (WASH)**: Details emergency water disinfection (boiling, chlorination, SODIS solar disinfection) and makeshift latrine construction to prevent lethal waterborne epidemic outbreaks.
- **Psychological First Aid (PFA) Principles**: Mandates the 'Look, Listen, Link' model, actively avoiding premature trauma debriefing while establishing immediate physical safety, comfort, and human connection.
