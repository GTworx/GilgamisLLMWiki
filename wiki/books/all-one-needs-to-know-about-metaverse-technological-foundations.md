## Overview
Authored by Lik-Hang Lee, Tristan Braud, Pan Hui, and a team of spatial computing experts, this massive survey (published in IEEE journals) provides the definitive foundational taxonomy of the metaverse. The authors establish an eight-pillar technological architecture—spanning hardware rendering, computer vision, networking/edge computing, artificial intelligence, blockchain, robotics, social interactions, and user-generated content.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Synthesizes the metaverse into eight fundamental pillars: networking, computing power, blockchain, spatial rendering, AI, UI, computer vision, and IoT.
- **Key Finding**: Quantifies network latency thresholds (<20ms motion-to-photon latency) required to prevent vestibular simulator sickness.
- **Key Finding**: Evaluates convergence trajectories between physical cyber-physical systems and persistent digital twin metaverses.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The metaverse is not merely Virtual Reality rebranded; it is the ultimate convergence of the physical and virtual realms enabled by edge intelligence, distributed ledger technologies, and ubiquitous spatial sensing."
