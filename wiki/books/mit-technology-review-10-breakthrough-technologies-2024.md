## Executive Summary
Comprising MIT Technology Review's authoritative 2024 *10 Breakthrough Technologies* annual report (108 pp.) and its September/October 2024 *Frontier Technology* monograph (92 pp.), this 200-page dossier chronicles the technological breakthroughs achieving commercial or societal inflection in 2024. The curated innovations span AI for everything (multimodal transformers, code synthesis), CRISPR treatments for sickle cell disease (Casgevy), super-efficient perovskite tandem solar cells, Apple Vision Pro and spatial micro-OLED interfaces, exascale supercomputers (Frontier, Aurora), enhanced geothermal energy, heat pumps, and personalized AI assistants. The collection evaluates the ethical, industrial, and social transformations wrought by these exponential breakthroughs.

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **10 Breakthrough Technologies (2024)**: Rigorously benchmarks AI code synthesis, CRISPR medicine approvals, perovskite tandem photovoltaics, Apple Vision Pro spatial computing, and exascale computing.
- **Perovskite Tandem Solar Cells**: Silicon-perovskite layered cells cross 33% laboratory efficiency, promising a step-function reduction in the levelized cost of solar electricity.
- **Exascale Scientific Discovery**: The arrival of exascale computing unlocks real-time molecular modeling, climate simulation, and materials genome discovery.
- **Spatial Computing User Paradigms**: The deployment of ultra-high-density micro-OLED displays and eye-and-gesture tracking redefines human-computer interaction beyond planar glass.
