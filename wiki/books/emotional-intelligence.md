## Executive Summary

Launching a global paradigm shift across psychology, education, and corporate leadership, science journalist and Harvard-trained psychologist **Daniel Goleman's** *Emotional Intelligence* (1995, pp. 5–8) synthesizes affective neuroscience (Joseph LeDoux's amygdala pathways) and developmental psychology (building on Peter Salovey and John Mayer) to answer **"Aristotle's Challenge"** (p. 7): how to harness emotion with intelligence—getting angry or passionate with the right person, to the right degree, at the right time, for the right purpose.

---

## Five-Domain EQ Architecture (Contents, p. 5)

1. **The Emotional Brain (Part One)**: Explains **amygdala hijacking**—why fast subcortical threat circuits can override the slower neocortex before conscious reason catches up.
2. **Five Pillars of Emotional Intelligence (Part Two)**:
   - *Self-Awareness* (recognizing feelings in real time)
   - *Self-Regulation* (managing destructive impulses and recovering from setbacks)
   - *Internal Motivation* (delaying gratification—citing Walter Mischel's Marshmallow Test—and entering Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi's **flow**)
   - *Empathy* (reading nonverbal emotional cues accurately)
   - *Social Skill* (attuning and leading relationships constructively)
3. **Applied EQ (Parts Three–Five)**: Demonstrates how emotional literacy improves marriage durability, clinical medicine, workplace leadership, and youth social-emotional schooling (*Self Science*).
