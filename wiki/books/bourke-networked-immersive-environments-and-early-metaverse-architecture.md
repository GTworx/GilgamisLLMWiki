## Overview
Authored by pioneering virtual reality researcher Paul Bourke in 2003, this foundational technical paper outlines an architectural blueprint for low-cost, self-configuring networked immersive environments. Anticipating modern distributed spatial computing, Bourke details display clustering, spherical projection mapping, and shared tele-immersive protocols.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Designs low-cost multi-projector spherical immersive displays replacing expensive CAVE systems.
- **Key Finding**: Formulates network synchronization protocols for real-time collaborative tele-immersion.
- **Key Finding**: Establishes foundational architectural criteria for open, scalable metaverse nodes.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The metaverse is conceived here not merely as virtual geometry, but as an interconnected network of physical and projected spaces synchronized across high-speed fabric."
