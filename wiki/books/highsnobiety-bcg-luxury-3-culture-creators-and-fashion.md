## Overview
Authored jointly by street-culture authority Highsnobiety and the Boston Consulting Group (BCG), this landmark 63-page fashion intelligence report defines 'Luxury 3.0'. Moving beyond traditional European heritage markers (Luxury 1.0) and streetwear hype-drops (Luxury 2.0), the authors demonstrate that the new luxury consumer values cultural credibility, creator communities, digital-physical fluidity, and authentic brand values above logo exclusivity.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Quantifies what matters most to next-generation luxury buyers: cultural credibility outranks legacy prestige by 3 to 1.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes how communities of creators, micro-subcultures, and niche aesthetics drive modern brand relevance.
- **Key Finding**: Outlines strategic imperatives for heritage fashion houses: mastering digital gaming integrations, circularity, and transparent sustainability.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Luxury is no longer defined by what a heritage brand tells consumers to desire; in Luxury 3.0, it is defined by cultural credibility earned within passionate creator communities."
