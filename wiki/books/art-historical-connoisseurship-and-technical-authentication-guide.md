## Overview
This concise practical connoisseurship and forensic guide outlines systematic protocols for identifying, dating, and authenticating fine art paintings. The guide details calligraphic signature verification, physical medium inspection (canvas weave, craquelure patterns, pigment aging), brushstroke stylometry, and cross-referencing auction catalog raisonnés to distinguish authentic originals from period copies and deliberate forgeries.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Establishes a step-by-step physical inspection protocol: analyzing signature placement, dating scripts, and verso gallery labels.
- **Key Finding**: Explains physical forensic indicators: natural pigment drying cracks (craquelure) versus artificial chemical distressing.
- **Key Finding**: Provides procedural guidance for contacting specialized international auction specialists and academic archives.
