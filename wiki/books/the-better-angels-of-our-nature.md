## Executive Summary

Harvard cognitive psychologist **Steven Pinker**'s monumental 1,061-page empirical study *The Better Angels of Our Nature* (Viking Penguin, 2011, copyright p. 8; TOC p. 6) advances one of modern social science's boldest counterintuitive theses: contrary to the availability bias of 24-hour news, **human violence has declined dramatically across nearly every scale and time horizon**—from prehistoric foraging societies and medieval Europe through modern interstate warfare, homicide, judicial torture, slavery, and domestic abuse.

Organizing massive historical, archaeological, and epidemiological datasets into **Six Major Declines of Violence** (Ch. 1–7), Pinker then turns to evolutionary psychology and cognitive neuroscience to map the **Five Inner Demons** that pull us toward aggression (Ch. 8) against the **Four Better Angels**—*Empathy, Self-Control, Moral Sense, and Reason*—that restrain it (Ch. 9), driven forward by **Five Historical Forces** (Ch. 10).

---

## The Six Declines & Five Historical Pacifying Forces

1. **Six Historical Transitions (Ch. 2–7)**: **The Pacification Process** (transition from hunter-gatherer anarchy to agricultural states), **The Civilizing Process** (Norbert Elias's European state consolidation and trade), **The Humanitarian Revolution** (Enlightenment abolition of torture, witch-hunts, and slavery), **The Long Peace** (post-1945 absence of great-power war), **The New Peace** (post-Cold War decline in civil wars), and **The Rights Revolutions** (civil, women's, children's, gay, and animal rights).
2. **Five Historical Forces (Ch. 10)**: The **Leviathan** (monopoly on legitimate force), **Gentle Commerce** (positive-sum trade making neighbors worth more alive than dead), **Feminization**, **Cosmopolitanism** (literacy, travel, and novels expanding the empathy circle), and **The Escalator of Reason** (applying objective logic to human affairs).
