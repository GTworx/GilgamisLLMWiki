## Overview
Authored by energy executive Georges Sassine, this definitive 47-page technical and economic monograph explores how distributed ledgers can revolutionize clean energy grids. Sassine analyzes peer-to-peer (P2P) transactive solar microgrids (e.g., the Brooklyn Microgrid), automated electric vehicle (EV) smart charging networks, and cryptographic verification of Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) to eliminate double-counting.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Demonstrates how distributed ledgers enable prosumers with rooftop solar to sell excess kilowatt-hours directly to neighbors.
- **Key Finding**: Automates renewable energy certificate issuance and retirement, ensuring verifiable carbon offset markets.
- **Key Finding**: Evaluates high-frequency smart meter telemetry integration with smart contracts for dynamic, localized grid load balancing.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The 21st-century grid is decentralizing from massive coal plants to millions of solar panels and batteries. Blockchain provides the transactive software backbone to coordinate this distributed energy democracy."
