## Overview
Published by Deloitte Insights, this monumental 52-page survey canvasses 1,386 C-suite executives and senior leaders across 11 nations, charting the critical transition of blockchain from experimental proof-of-concept to live production deployments. The report provides quantitative benchmarks on enterprise investment budgets, executive sentiment, and leading functional use cases across fintech, supply chain, and government.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Reveals that 53% of global executives view blockchain as a critical top-five strategic priority, moving past early cynicism.
- **Key Finding**: Identifies supply chain provenance, digital identity, and trade finance as the leading enterprise production applications.
- **Key Finding**: Documents key hurdles to mass adoption: legacy system integration, regulatory uncertainty, and consortium governance gridlock.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Executives no longer ask 'Will this technology work?'; they ask 'How do we organize a consortium of competitors to share a common cryptographic ledger?'"
