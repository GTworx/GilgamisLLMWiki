## Summary
Authoritative 57-page benchmark tracking $113 trillion in assets under management across the world's top 500 institutional asset managers, detailing market concentration among the top 20 titans (BlackRock, Vanguard, Fidelity).

## Analytical Overview
Evaluates passive indexing versus active management flows, the rapid integration of private market alternatives into traditional institutional portfolios, and the geographic concentration of asset stewardship across North America, Europe, and Asia. Examines how scale economies, technology infrastructure costs, and regulatory compliance drive mega-firm consolidation.
