## Summary
Landmark high-coverage ancient DNA time-course study sequencing human skeletal remains from eight distinct chronological periods over 4,000 years in the Near East, demonstrating long-term biological continuity alongside Bronze-to-Iron Age gene flows.

## Analytical Overview
Utilizes whole-genome ancient DNA sequencing from Levantine burial sites across Bronze Age, Iron Age, Hellenistic, Roman, and Crusader strata. Demonstrates substantial biological continuity among indigenous Levantine populations with episodic influxes of Steppe- and Zagros-related ancestry during the Middle to Late Bronze Age.
