## Summary
Foundational glossary and analytical framework from Data & Society establishing precise conceptual distinctions among disinformation, misinformation, malinformation, propaganda, gaslighting, and astroturfing.

## Analytical Overview
Traces the etymological, institutional, and political history of deceptive communication terminology from early Cold War Russian 'dezinformatsiya' to contemporary digital media manipulation. Cautions against collapsing distinct rhetorical practices into monolithic buzzwords like 'fake news', providing researchers with precise taxonomic tools to analyze informational harm.
