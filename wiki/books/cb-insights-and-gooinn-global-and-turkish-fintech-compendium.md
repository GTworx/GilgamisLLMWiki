## Summary
Comprehensive 278-page global and Turkish fintech benchmark examining post-2022 venture funding corrections, neobank unit economics, open banking API expansion, and digital payment regulation.

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes CB Insights' 197-page global market overview charting the fintech funding reset with GOOINN's and national fintech industry reports on Turkey. Documents the growth of digital payment platforms (FAST, QR code protocols), open banking integrations, electronic money institutions (TCMB licenses), and cross-border remittance innovations.
