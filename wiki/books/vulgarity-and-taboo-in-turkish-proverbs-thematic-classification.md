## Overview
Published in the Journal of Academic Language and Literature (2025), this courageous 35-page sociolinguistic monograph investigates the presence, function, and psychological catharsis of vulgar words, scatological idioms, and anatomical taboos within traditional Turkish proverbs. The author categorizes taboo expressions across historical anthologies, revealing how folk humor bypasses formal societal censorship.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Constructs a rigorous thematic taxonomy of vulgarity and anatomical taboos in centuries of documented Turkish proverbs.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes the social function of obscenity in folklore: coping with rural powerlessness, deflating hypocrisy, and comedic relief.
- **Key Finding**: Contrasts sanitized literary anthologies with unexpurgated oral folklore collections.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
