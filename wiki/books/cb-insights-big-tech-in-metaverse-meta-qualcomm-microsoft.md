## Overview
This 60-page intelligence report by CB Insights dissects the strategic roadmaps, patent acquisitions, venture investments, and hardware supply chains of Big Tech titans—specifically Meta Platforms, Qualcomm, and Microsoft—as they vie for supremacy in spatial computing. The analysts map the entire technology stack from Snapdragon XR silicon to enterprise Mesh collaboration platforms.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes Qualcomm's dominance in dedicated XR silicon (Snapdragon XR2) powering the vast majority of standalone headsets.
- **Key Finding**: Evaluates Microsoft's enterprise strategy pairing HoloLens, Azure spatial anchors, and Teams Mesh for industrial operations.
- **Key Finding**: Maps the competitive venture landscape: tracking Big Tech acquisitions of computer vision, microLED, and spatial audio startups.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Big Tech's battle for the metaverse is a multi-layered war fought across silicon fab capacity, optics supply chains, and enterprise cloud distribution channels."
