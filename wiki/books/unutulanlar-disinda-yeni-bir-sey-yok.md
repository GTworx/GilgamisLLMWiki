## Executive Summary

*Unutulanlar Dışında Yeni Bir Şey Yok* ("Nothing New Except the Forgotten: Soldiers in the Mountains of Hakkâri and Northern Iraq") is retired Turkish Major General **Osman Pamukoğlu's** bestselling firsthand military memoir—a book that spent sixteen consecutive weeks at #1 on Turkish bestseller lists (noted in *Cehennemdere Kanyonu* metadata). Drawing on his **778 days** in command of the Hakkâri Mountain and Commando Brigade and regional security forces between **1993 and 1995** (pp. 2, 9), Pamukoğlu chronicles high-altitude counterinsurgency operations across Hakkâri's 3,000–4,000 meter peaks and cross-border sweeps into Northern Iraq against the PKK.

Dedicated to the mothers of the **427 personnel killed** (365 military, 60 village guards, 2 police) and **853 wounded** under his command (pp. 5, 9), the memoir combines blunt operational diaries, tactical lessons in irregular mountain warfare, and blistering criticism of political complacency in Ankara.

## Five-Part Chronological Architecture (Contents, p. 6)

1. **Önsöz (Preface, pp. 7–9)**: Sets an unsentimental, anti-romantic tone on war—noting that wars are started by people who never stand under fire, and that in every war throughout 5,000 years of written history, "only mothers lose" (p. 8).
2. **Birinci Bölüm: Öncesi (Before, pp. 9–22)**: Recounts Pamukoğlu's early command experience as a First Lieutenant commanding the Taşburun Border Company along the Aras River facing the Soviet-Armenian frontier (1974–1975, p. 12), illustrating his formative lessons in austere border vigilance and human psychology.
3. **İkinci Bölüm: 1993 Dönemi (pp. 23–160)**: Details assuming command in Hakkâri at the height of the 1993 insurgency crisis, dismantling static defensive garrisons, shifting to mobile night-and-winter pursuit operations, and restoring troop morale.
4. **Üçüncü Bölüm: 1994 Dönemi (pp. 161–278)**: Documents major alpine engagements and cross-border operations across rugged southeastern valleys and border ridges (including Zeli/Hakurk/Cilo sectors), emphasizing terrain mastery and small-unit initiative.
5. **Dördüncü Bölüm: 1995 Dönemi & Beşinci Bölüm: Ekler (pp. 279–386)**: Covers the 1995 campaign (including large-scale cross-border operations) followed by three documentary appendices: a complete roll of honor listing the fallen (*İsimleriyle Güneşi Yükseltenlerin Kimlikleri*, pp. 346–372), regional operational maps (p. 373), and field photographs (pp. 374–386).

## Key Operational & Leadership Principles

1. **Command From the Front**: Pamukoğlu insists that in irregular mountain combat, officers cannot direct operations from sheltered headquarters; tactical credibility flows from sharing identical cold, hunger, and danger with conscripts.
2. **Offensive Mobility Over Static Bunkers**: Argues that passive base defense surrenders time, terrain, and psychological initiative to guerrillas; only continuous, unpredictable offensive patrolling across winter highlands breaks insurgent sanctuary.
3. **Civil-Military Tension**: Contrasts the stoic endurance of young conscripts against what he condemns as strategic myopia and indifference among urban political elites.
