## Executive Summary

Compiled and released explicitly as a free copyleft ("açık kaynak / miri malı") public resource encouraging readers to donate to the Darüşşafaka Society (p. 2), **Burak Dalgın**'s *Benim Pencerem* (December 2022; 213 pp.) brings together **162 essays** written between November 2019 and December 2022—comprising 149 concise ~400-word weekly columns from *Dünya Gazetesi* plus 13 long-form policy analyses from *T24*, *Politikyol*, *Euronews*, and *Karar*.

Drawing on the Persian etymology of *pencere* (*penç + reh*, "the fifth way" out of four confining walls, p. 2), Dalgın organizes his essays into **eight windows** (Contents, p. 3): *Profesyonelin Dünyası* (18), *Yönetim Dünyası* (22), *Finans/Yatırım Dünyası* (19), *Strateji Dünyası* (25), *Yarının Dünyası* (35), *İlham Dünyası* (19), *Türkiye Dünyası* (11), and *Başka Dünyalar* (13). Together, they synthesize private-equity investing, executive decision-making, AI/algocracy disruption, and data-driven Turkish economic reform (*Kalkınma Seferberliği*).

---

## Core Frameworks & Insights

1. **Executive Craft & Cognitive Hygiene (Windows 1–2, pp. 4–47)**: Distills practical frameworks on Eisenhower prioritization, overcoming procrastination, escaping FOMO vs. TINA traps, delivering five-step constructive criticism, and structured problem solving.
2. **Capital Allocation, Strategy & Future Disruption (Windows 3–5, pp. 48–133)**: Connects corporate finance, venture/PE valuation discipline, strategic moats, platform economics, and emerging debates over "algocracy," new labor, and digital public spheres.
3. **Data-Driven Turkish Development Agenda (Windows 7–8, pp. 155–211)**: Evaluates Turkey's standing in global competitiveness indices, ISO 500 industrial performance, and the 2023 macro targets—proposing institutional, educational, and productivity reforms.
