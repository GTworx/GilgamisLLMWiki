## Executive Summary
This 68-page engineering treatise provides a comprehensive mathematical and biomechanical formulation of snake robots—hyper-redundant robotic mechanisms inspired by biological serpents. The monograph establishes the forward and inverse kinematics of serially linked multi-joint actuators, modeling lateral undulation, sidewinding, concertina motion, and obstacle-aided locomotion. The authors demonstrate how snake robots traverse highly constrained, hazardous physical spaces (collapsed earthquake rubble, nuclear reactor cooling pipes, subsea oil manifolds, and minimally invasive surgical cavities) that defeat wheeled or legged robots.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Hyper-Redundant Kinematics**: Formulates gait patterns and continuous curve approximations that coordinate 10–30 articulated joints without singularity lockup.
- **Obstacle-Aided Locomotion**: Unlike traditional robots that avoid obstacles, snake robots intentionally leverage contact points in their environment to push forward, increasing energy efficiency.
- **Disaster and Nuclear Application**: Validates search-and-rescue reconnaissance in collapsed voids and pipe-crawling inspection in nuclear power plants.
- **Surgical Robotics Translation**: Shows how sub-centimeter articulated snake catheters enable minimally invasive cardiac and laparoscopic procedures.
