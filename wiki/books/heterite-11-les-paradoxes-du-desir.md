## Executive Summary

Published in French by the **Internationale des Forums – École de Psychanalyse des Forums du Champ Lacanien (IF-EPFCL)** under editorial commission CRIF 2014–2016 (general production Beatriz Elena Maya Restrepo; final revision Géraldine Philippe & **Patrick Barillot**, pp. 2–6), *Hétérité* Issue 11 (*Les paradoxes du désir*, 464 pp.) collects the international clinical and theoretical papers of the **2014 Paris Rendez-vous International**. Translated across four working languages of the Lacanian Field (French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian; Presentation p. 7), this substantial multilingual-rooted psychoanalytic review examines why human desire remains structurally indestructible, unarticulable, and paradoxical for the speaking being (*parlêtre*).

---

## Central Clinical & Theoretical Threads

1. **Desire Between Symptom and *Sinthome* (p. 7)**: Traces the evolution of Lacanian clinical practice from deciphering symbolic meaning in early symptom interpretation to working with Lacan's late **sinthome**—where irreducible bodily *jouissance* is knotted into a singular creative defense against raw *jouissance* itself.
2. **Interpretation Beyond Meaning**: Investigates how contemporary psychoanalytic interpretation targets the real *cause of desire* (*objet a*) rather than endlessly inflating imaginary narratives—rethinking the end of analysis and the analyst's desire in the twenty-first century.
3. **Desire and *Jouissance* in Contemporary Culture**: Brings together dozens of European and Latin American clinicians addressing how hyper-consumerist imperatives to "enjoy!" mutate hysteria, obsession, melancholy, and psychosis.
