## Summary
Three-volume digital currency investigation analyzing fiat-backed stablecoins as alternative global cross-border settlement rails (DBS Bank), crypto-asset risk indexes (Coincub), and digital asset adoption across Central Asia and the Caucasus.

## Analytical Overview
Explores how dollar-backed stablecoins (USDT, USDC) have emerged as primary store-of-value instruments and trade settlement mechanisms in emerging economies facing currency volatility. Details regulatory frameworks in Central Asia (Kazakhstan's Astana International Financial Centre crypto licenses), crypto tax frameworks, and consumer protection protocols.
