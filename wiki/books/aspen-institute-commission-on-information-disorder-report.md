## Overview
Published by the Aspen Institute, co-chaired by journalist Katie Couric, cybersecurity chief Chris Krebs, and Prince Harry, this landmark 80-page consensus report tackles the crisis of digital 'information disorder'. The Commission outlines actionable policy, technical, and regulatory interventions to restore public trust, combat algorithmic radicalization, and mandate algorithmic transparency for digital social monopolies.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Identifies information disorder as an existential threat accelerating public health crises and democratic decay.
- **Key Finding**: Demands legal mandates compelling social media platforms to share systemic algorithmic and advertising data with independent researchers.
- **Key Finding**: Proposes federal standards holding platforms accountable for amplification of coordinated inauthentic disinformation.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Information disorder is not a technology problem; it is a crisis of trust and accountability. When algorithms monetize outrage over truth, the shared epistemic reality required for self-governance collapses."
