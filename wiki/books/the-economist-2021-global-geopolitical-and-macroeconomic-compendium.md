## Summary
Four-volume archival compendium from The Economist documenting the volatile 2021 post-lockdown macroeconomic rebound, vaccine rollouts, global supply chain bottlenecks, and early inflation warning signs.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the uneven global economic recovery from COVID-19 across North America, Europe, and Asia. Analyzes semiconductor shortages, port congestion, surging shipping rates, the initial surge in consumer price inflation, the geopolitics of the US withdrawal from Afghanistan, and escalating Sino-American technological rivalry.
