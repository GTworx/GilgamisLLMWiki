## Summary
Comprehensive interdisciplinary compendium integrating hydrodynamic modeling of the Tigris-Euphrates alluvial fan with archaic diplomatic treaties, frontier demarcations, and early inter-city statecraft in Sumer and Akkad.

## Analytical Overview
Combines geomorphological analysis of avulsion channels and irrigation networks with diplomatic records of ancient Mesopotamian international relations. Explores how water management, temple redistributive logistics, and border agreements (such as the Lagash-Umma border conflict) established the earliest codified norms of international law and arbitration.
