## Overview
Published by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), this monumental 3-volume longitudinal statistical series (99 pages total) provides authoritative global benchmarking of the international book publishing industry through the pandemic and post-pandemic recovery (2020–2022). WIPO audits revenues, digital vs physical formats, copyright licensing, educational publishing, and trade market resilience across 35+ reporting nations.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Tracks the global publishing market navigating pandemic bookstore shutdowns through explosive growth in e-books and audiobooks.
- **Key Finding**: Audits national market sizes: US ($25B+), Germany, UK, Japan, and emerging book publishing powers.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes copyright registration data, cross-border translation rights, and public lending rights economics.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The global publishing industry proved remarkably resilient during the pandemic crisis, demonstrating that books remain an essential pillar of human knowledge, entertainment, and mental resilience."
