## Analytical Overview
### Conservative Scholarship and Grand Strategy at Stanford

The Winter 2024 edition of *Hoover Digest* (208 pages), published by the Hoover Institution at Stanford University, convenes leading American public intellectuals, economists, and historians—including Victor Davis Hanson, Niall Ferguson, John B. Taylor, and Michael McFaul—delivering analytical perspectives on domestic policy and global statecraft.

### Strategic Highlights of the Winter 2024 Edition

The issue spans critical geopolitical and economic debates:
- **Taiwan Deterrence and Western Pacific Defense:** Formulating combined arms deterrent strategies to prevent Chinese amphibious invasion across the Taiwan Strait.
- **Ukraine War Attrition Dynamics:** Evaluating military industrial capacity, drone warfare innovation, and European rearmament timelines.
- **Fiscal Trajectory and Federal Debt:** Re-evaluating the macroeconomic risks of sustained $2 trillion U.S. annual federal deficits in a high-interest-rate environment.
- **Higher Education and Institutional Neutrality:** Revitalizing meritocratic standards and intellectual pluralism within American research universities.

The volume serves as an authoritative window into conservative public philosophy and national security strategy.
