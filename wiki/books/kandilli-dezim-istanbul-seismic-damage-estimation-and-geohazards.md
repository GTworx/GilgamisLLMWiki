## Summary
Monumental seismological and structural loss estimation compilation for the Marmara region and Istanbul, featuring the 341-page Boğaziçi Kandilli DEZİM Earthquake Damage Estimation Report, geodetic fault locking models, and spatial integration of earth science into urban zoning.

## Analytical Overview
This volume is an indispensable scientific foundation for understanding seismic risk in the Istanbul metropolitan area and western Anatolia. The centerpiece of the collection is the landmark 341-page Boğaziçi University Kandilli Observatory Earthquake Engineering Department (DEZİM) report (*Deprem Hasar Tahmin Raporu*), authored by leading earthquake engineers Mustafa Erdik, Ufuk Hancılar, and Haluk Özener. Utilizing deterministic and probabilistic scenarios for a Mw 7.2–7.5 event along the locked Main Marmara Fault segment of the North Anatolian Fault Zone (NAFZ), the report provides building-by-building and neighborhood-level loss estimations for all 39 Istanbul districts.

The Kandilli model incorporates non-linear structural fragility functions calibrated against the 1999 Kocaeli and Düzce earthquakes, soil amplification microzonation, and liquefaction susceptibility. The calculations predict that upwards of 48,000 buildings would suffer very heavy or complete collapse, with an additional 194,000 experiencing moderate to severe structural damage, resulting in massive casualties and isolating key peninsula transport arteries.

These models are paired with Oktay Gökçe's foundational 167-page treatise on the *Integration of Earth Science Data into Spatial Planning*, detailing how geotechnical boring data, active fault setback buffers, landslide hazard maps, and liquefaction indices must be legally mandated in municipal master zoning plans. The volume also contains formal statements from the Turkish Earthquake Engineering Association submitted to the Turkish Grand National Assembly (TBMM) Earthquake Investigation Commission, demanding mandatory periodic structural health inspections and the criminalization of unauthorized modifications to load-bearing columns in ground-floor commercial premises.
