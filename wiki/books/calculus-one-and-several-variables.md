## Executive Summary

*Calculus: One and Several Variables (Tenth Edition)* (published by John Wiley & Sons, Inc., 2007, 1,171 pp., dedicated in fond remembrance of Einar Hille) by mathematicians [[saturnino-l-salas]], [[einar-hille]], and [[garret-j-etgen]] is a classic, mathematically rigorous university calculus treatise celebrated for its exceptional expositional precision, proof-backed theorems, and over **1,200 geometric illustrations** (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, pp. 7–11).

Anchoring the entire 1,171-page sequence firmly on the **three foundational pillars of calculus—limit, derivative, and integral**—Salas, Hille, and Etgen balance theoretical rigor (proving core theorems rather than merely asserting rules) with rich interdisciplinary projects across physics, engineering, biology, and economics (such as *Flight Paths of Birds*, *Energy of a Falling Body*, and *Marginal Cost, Marginal Revenue, Marginal Profit*) (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, Outline & Preface, pp. 9–12). Covering single-variable limits, differentiation, the Mean-Value Theorem, optimization, Newton–Raphson approximation, Riemann integration, transcendental functions, differential equations, **infinite sequences and series (Ch. 11–12)**, vectors, partial derivatives, multiple integrals, and vector calculus (Green's, Stokes', and Divergence theorems), it forms the mathematical backbone for physics, machine learning, and quantitative finance (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, Outline & Preface, pp. 9–12).

## Key Arguments / Chapter Breakdown

* **Single-Variable Calculus: Limits, Differentiation & Integration (Ch. 1–10)**:
  * *Exact Foundations & Applications*: Builds $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ limits and continuity; develops differentiation, the Mean-Value Theorem, curve sketching, related rates, differentials, and **Newton–Raphson** root finding (Ch. 4); constructs definite/indefinite integration (Ch. 5), transcendental/inverse functions, integration techniques, and first- and second-order linear differential equations (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, Outline & Preface, pp. 9–12).

* **Infinite Series, Vectors & Multivariable Vector Calculus (Ch. 11–18)**:
  * *Power Series & Higher Dimensions*: Rigorously treats convergence tests, Taylor/Maclaurin series (Ch. 11–12), parametric/polar geometry, 3D vector analysis, partial differentiation, gradients, multiple integrals, and the fundamental integral theorems of vector fields—reinforced by comprehensive 50+-problem chapter-end **Skill Mastery Review Exercise Sets** (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, Preface, pp. 11–12).
