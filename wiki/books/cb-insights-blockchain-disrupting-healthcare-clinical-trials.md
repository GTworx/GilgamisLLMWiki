## Overview
This 27-page strategic intelligence report by CB Insights maps how distributed ledger technologies can transform fragmented global healthcare systems. The report analyzes immutable electronic health record (EHR) interoperability, cryptographic patient consent management, anti-counterfeiting tracking across pharmaceutical supply chains (DSCSA compliance), and verifiable integrity in clinical trial data.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Solves EHR silo fragmentation by putting patient identity and permission hashes on-chain while keeping health data stored off-chain.
- **Key Finding**: Eliminates lethal counterfeit pharmaceuticals through end-to-end serialized barcode tracking on distributed ledgers.
- **Key Finding**: Prevents scientific fraud and p-hacking in clinical trials by immutably timestamping trial protocols and raw patient endpoints.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Healthcare is plagued by multi-billion-dollar data fragmentation and drug counterfeiting. Cryptographic ledgers enable trustless verification of medical provenance without violating patient privacy."
