## Executive Summary

In this concise philosophical treatise, *Türk Bilgi* (*Turkic Epistemology / Knowledge*), **Kâzım Mirşan** explores the epistemological foundations, ethical philosophy, and cosmological concepts encoded in Proto-Turkic runic inscriptions. Mirşan defines *Bilgi* (knowledge/wisdom) in archaic Turkic thought not merely as factual information, but as the cosmic synchronization of human consciousness with the divine order of the Universe (*Gök/Tengri*), analyzing key ethical terms such as *Kut*, *Töre*, *Er*, and *Ulus*.

| Section | Philosophical & Epigraphic Exposition |

| **I. Bilgi Kavramının Kökleri** | Etymology of *Bil-* (to know, discern); the difference between worldly cunning and cosmic wisdom in ancient inscriptions |

| **III. Töre ve Adalet Felsefesi** | *Töre* as natural cosmic law regulating human relations; why no ruler or kağan can stand above the law |
| **IV. Yazıtların Öğrettiği Ahlâk** | Analysis of moral admonitions on Yenisei and Orkhon stelae regarding truthfulness, loyalty, and protection of the weak |

---

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **Epistemology Grounded in Cosmology**: In archaic Turkic thought, true knowledge (*Bilgi*) is inherently ethical and connected to the harmonious maintenance of societal justice.
2. **Language as Philosophical Mirror**: Shows that the morphological structure of early Turkic roots inherently connects knowing, managing, and moral responsibility.
3. **The Sovereign Bound by Law**: Reaffirms that pre-Islamic Turkic political philosophy rejected absolute despotic tyranny in favor of a rule-of-law order governed by the unchangeable *Töre*.
