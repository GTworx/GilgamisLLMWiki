## Summary
Foundational monument of Turkic epigraphy and historical linguistics containing the definitive decipherment and grammatical analysis of the 8th-century Göktürk inscriptions of Bilge Khagan and Kül Tegin.

## Analytical Overview
Presents Thomsen's monumental decipherment of the Old Turkic runiform script, translated and annotated by Ross. Provides running transcriptions and English translations of the Kül Tegin and Bilge Khagan stelae on the Orkhon River in Mongolia, preserving the oldest known continuous Turkic literary monuments and political philosophy of the nomadic Khaganate.
