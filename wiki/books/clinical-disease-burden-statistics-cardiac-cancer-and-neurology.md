## Executive Summary
This clinical statistical reference dossier provides an annotated compilation of global morbidity, mortality, and disability-adjusted life year (DALY) statistics across the three dominant categories of non-communicable diseases: Cardiovascular Diseases (CVD), Cancers (Malignancies), and Neurological/Neurodegenerative Disorders (Alzheimer's, Stroke, Parkinson's).

The reference compiles authoritative data from the Global Burden of Disease (GBD) Study, the American Heart Association (AHA), the International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC), and the World Federation of Neurology. It details the physiological mechanisms, global prevalence (e.g., CVD causing over 19 million deaths annually), sex-specific disparities, risk-factor attributions (hypertension, smoking, air pollution, high LDL cholesterol), and projected shifts in disease burden over the next two decades.

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Cardiovascular Disease Primacy**: Confirms ischemic heart disease and stroke as the primary causes of death worldwide, responsible for over one-third of all global mortalities.
- **Cancer Mortality Transition**: Tracks the epidemiological transition in middle-income countries where cancer mortality is rapidly overtaking infectious disease deaths.
- **Neurological Disease Disability Surge**: Highlights Alzheimer's disease and other dementias as the fastest-growing contributors to global disability-adjusted life years in aging populations.
- **Modifiable Risk Factor Impact**: Demonstrates that over 70% of cardiovascular deaths and 40% of cancer deaths are preventable through the aggressive control of blood pressure, metabolic health, and tobacco cessation.
