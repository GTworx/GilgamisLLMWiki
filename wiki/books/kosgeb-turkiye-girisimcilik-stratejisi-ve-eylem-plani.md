## Analytical Overview
### The National Master Plan for Turkish Entrepreneurship

The *Türkiye Girişimcilik Stratejisi ve Eylem Planı (GİSEP 2015–2018)*, prepared by KOSGEB and approved by the High Planning Council (Yüksek Planlama Kurulu), constitutes the official strategic master plan governing state entrepreneurial policy in the Republic of Turkey.

### Six Strategic Targets

GİSEP coordinates inter-ministerial policies across six strategic pillars:
1. **Developing an Entrepreneur-Friendly Regulatory Environment:** Streamlining company formation, simplifying bankruptcy and debt-restructuring procedures, and reducing commercial bureaucracy.
2. **Expanding Entrepreneurship Education:** Integrating entrepreneurship and financial literacy into primary, secondary, and higher education curricula.
3. **Enhancing Access to Finance:** Expanding angel investor accreditation tax exemptions, credit guarantee funds (KGF), and venture capital matching grants.
4. **Fostering High-Growth & Innovative Startups:** Providing specialized incubation support for university deep-tech spinouts and women entrepreneurs.
5. **Promoting Sustainable Entrepreneurship:** Incentivizing energy efficiency and green technology adoption among industrial SMEs.
6. **Disseminating Entrepreneurial Culture:** Celebrating successful role models and shifting societal attitudes toward celebrating commercial risk-taking.

### Definitive Policy Blueprint

The document outlines 44 concrete, measurable policy actions assigned to specific state ministries, establishing a milestone in institutional public planning.
