## Executive Summary
This public finance and socio-economic policy compendium pairs the official 2022 Central Government Budget Law of the Republic of Türkiye (*Mali Yılı Bütçesi*, comprehensive fiscal line items, revenue appropriations, and debt service allocations) with the 2025 empirical monograph *What Happened to Equality of Opportunity*. The authors examine how fiscal taxation structures, public expenditures, and educational allocations either promote intergenerational social mobility or entrench socio-economic stratification. The analysis audits budget deficits, interest burden shares, social transfer efficacies, and structural policy barriers hindering meritocratic advancement in emerging economies.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Fiscal Resource Allocation in Emerging Markets**: Audits statutory allocations across education, national defense, healthcare, and infrastructure, evaluating the rising fiscal pressure of debt service amid inflation.
- **Intergenerational Mobility Decay**: Documents how rising educational inequality, spatial segregation, and non-meritocratic networks have eroded upward economic mobility for lower-income cohorts.
- **Tax Structure and Indirect Tax Burdens**: Highlights that heavy reliance on indirect consumption taxes (VAT, Special Consumption Tax) disproportionately burdens lower-income households, dampening egalitarian redistributive outcomes.
- **Policy Levers for Opportunity Renewal**: Proves that early childhood education investments and transparent merit-based public civil service examinations deliver the highest societal returns for long-term growth.
