## Analytical Overview
### The Dissolution of the Corporate Social Contract

In *Quitting Time*, renowned NYU Stern marketing professor and serial entrepreneur Scott Galloway analyzes the profound cultural and economic realignment governing modern white-collar careers. Examining demographic data, declining corporate loyalty, and the return-to-office (RTO) conflict, Galloway argues that the post-war corporate bargain—sacrificing personal autonomy in exchange for lifetime employment security and steady pension accumulation—is fundamentally broken.

### The Rise of the Solo Enterprise

Galloway details the structural forces driving knowledge workers away from corporate middle management into independent consulting, freelancing, and venture entrepreneurship:
- **The Leverage of Digital Tooling:** Generative AI, cloud infrastructure, and low-code platforms allow individual creators and single-operator businesses to achieve revenue scale that previously required dozens of corporate employees.
- **The Failure of RTO Mandates:** The executive push to force knowledge workers back into physical cubicles has triggered an exit of top performers who prioritize spatial autonomy over corporate titles.
- **The Economic Imperative of Equity:** Galloway demonstrates that wage labor cannot keep pace with asset inflation; building sustainable personal wealth requires equity ownership in an enterprise.

### Unsentimental Career Counsel

The text offers Galloway's trademark razor-sharp advice on building professional moats, diversifying income streams, and navigating high-stakes career transitions.
