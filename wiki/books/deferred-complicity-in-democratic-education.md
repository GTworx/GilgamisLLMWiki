## Summary
Scholarly essay in Democracy & Education articulating the concept of 'Deferred Complicity'—how liberal educational reforms perpetuate systemic inequality by deferring fundamental structural redistribution.

## Analytical Overview
### Critique of Performative Reform in Public Education

*Deferred Complicity: Institutional Racism, Social Justice Pedagogies, and Educational Equity*, published in *Democracy & Education*, delivers a trenchant theoretical critique of contemporary diversity and inclusion initiatives within public school systems.

### The Mechanism of Deferred Complicity

The authors formulate the concept of **Deferred Complicity**:
- Well-intentioned educational institutions adopt progressive rhetoric, multicultural curricula, and symbolic anti-racist statements.
- However, by refusing to challenge property-tax school funding disparities, residential redlining, and elite tracking programs, the institution *defers* its own complicity in perpetuating generational social stratification.
- The text argues that authentic democratic education requires moving beyond performative discourse toward tangible material equity.
