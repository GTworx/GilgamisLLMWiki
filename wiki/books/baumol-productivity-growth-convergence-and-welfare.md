## Summary
Seminal empirical macroeconomic treatise testing cross-country convergence across sixteen industrialized nations over a century (1870–1979), demonstrating distinct convergence clubs among market economies.

## Analytical Overview
Utilizes Angus Maddison's historical datasets to demonstrate that market-industrialized nations converged in labor productivity, capital intensity, and real GDP per capita, while centrally planned and underdeveloped economies formed separate, non-converging clubs. Analyzes the role of technology transfer, public education, and investment rates in driving long-run economic expansion.
