## Executive Summary

Curated from hundreds of live Q&A sessions hosted on **Product Hunt** (founded by [[ryan-hoover]]) and edited by **Melissa Joy Kong** and **Niv Dror** with community makers (Title Page p. 1, Acknowledgements p. 3; 2017; 105 pp.), *How to Build a Career in Tech* distills tactical career wisdom from leading Silicon Valley founders, investors, engineers, product managers, and creators—including Kevin Rose, Alexis Ohanian, Paul Graham, Marc Andreessen, Reid Hoffman, Ben Horowitz, and Tony Robbins.

Organized chronologically along the modern technology career arc (Table of Contents, p. 2), the guide moves from existential starting questions (*What Should You Do With Your Life?*, *Education or Lack Thereof*) through **Startups vs. Big Companies**, **The Art of Networking**, **Finding Great Mentors**, **Getting Hired & Hiring**, **Women and Inclusion**, **Role Deep-Dives** (Investing, Engineering, Product Management, Media/Art), and **Overcoming Career Challenges**.

---

## Core Frameworks & Insights

1. **Startups vs. Big Companies & Non-Linear Paths (pp. 25–37, 50–53)**: Weighs the broad ownership, speed, and steep learning curves of early-stage startups against the mentorship, brand signaling, and scaled systems of established tech firms.
2. **Authentic Networking, Mentorship & Hiring Signals (pp. 35–49)**: Emphasizes building public portfolios and shipping side projects over cold credentials, seeking mentors through demonstrated curiosity and value-add, and hiring for trajectory and mission alignment.
3. **Functional Craft Across Tech Disciplines (pp. 64–97)**: Contrasts the distinct skill loops of venture investing, software engineering, product management, and creative media, closing with retrospective advice from veteran operators to their younger selves.
