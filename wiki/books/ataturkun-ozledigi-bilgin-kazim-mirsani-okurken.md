## Executive Summary

In this monumental, 753-page intellectual biography and research compendium, researcher **Necdet Sümer** provides an exhaustive analytical survey of the life, linguistic methodology, epigraphic decipherments, and historical theories of controversial Crimean Tatar civil engineer and runologist **Kâzım Mirşan** (1919–2016). Sümer situates Mirşan as the living embodiment of the intellectual pioneer Mustafa Kemal Atatürk sought during the 1930s to realize the **Türk Tarih Tezi** (Turkish Historical Thesis), detailing Mirşan's lifelong decipherment of over 40 ancient runic scripts across Eurasia.

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **The Fulfillment of Atatürk's Dream**: Frames Mirşan's decodings as the technical realization of Atatürk's intuitive thesis that pre-Islamic Turkic civilizations played a foundational role in Old World civilizational origins.
2. **The Battle with Academic Inertia**: Criticizes conventional Turkish university faculties for neglecting Mirşan's immense multilingual archive out of subservience to Western academic orthodoxies.
3. **Epigraphic Independence**: Argues that regardless of controversial linguistic claims, Mirşan preserved and documented hundreds of endangered Siberian and Central Asian petroglyphs and inscriptions that would otherwise have been lost to modern history.
