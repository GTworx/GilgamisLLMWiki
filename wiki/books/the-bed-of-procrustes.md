## Executive Summary

Named after the Greek myth of Procrustes—who stretched short guests or amputated tall ones to force every traveler to fit his iron bed—**Nassim Nicholas Taleb's** *The Bed of Procrustes* (2010, Random House, p. 5) distills the entire *Incerto* philosophy into razor-sharp, standalone philosophical and practical aphorisms.

Modeling his concision on ancient Mediterranean wisdom literature and modern aphorists, Taleb targets modern "Procrustean beds": forcing messy human biology, complex economies, artistic creativity, and unpredictable reality into rigid bureaucratic metrics, IQ tests, corporate HR templates, and sterile academic models—while celebrating intellectual courage, erudition over credentials, and ancient Stoic dignity.

---

## Core Aphoristic Domains

- **Epistemology & Knowledge**: Exposes the illusion of certainty, the tyranny of averages, and the difference between lived empirical wisdom and manicured credentialism.
- **Ethics, Courage & Modernity**: Contrasts classical honor and direct accountability against bureaucratic cowardice, corporate euphemisms, and sterile "modern" convenience that strips life of robustness.
- **Stoicism & Aesthetics**: Urges cultivating calm detachment in prosperity and dignity in adversity, echoing [[letters-from-a-stoic]] and [[meditations-marcus-aurelius]].
