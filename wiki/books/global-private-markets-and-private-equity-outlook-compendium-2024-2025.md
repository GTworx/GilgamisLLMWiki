## Summary
Monumental 10-report private markets intelligence compendium tracking private capital fundraising, the exit liquidity freeze, DPI (Distributed to Paid-In Capital) pressures, and secondary market growth.

## Analytical Overview
Details how higher base interest rates ended the decade-long cheap-debt buyout boom. Explores how general partners (GPs) utilize continuation funds, dividend recapitalizations, and NAV loans to hold portfolio companies longer amidst depressed M&A and IPO valuations, projecting private market assets under management toward $18 trillion by 2029.
