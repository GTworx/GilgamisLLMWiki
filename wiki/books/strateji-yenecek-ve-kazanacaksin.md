## Executive Summary

Written at the encouragement of Turkish historian Sinan Meydan (p. 8) and published by İnkılap Kitabevi in 2016 (p. 2), *Strateji: Yenecek ve Kazanacaksın* ("Strategy: You Will Defeat and Win") is Osman Pamukoğlu's sweeping 450-page treatise on classical and modern strategic theory. Defining strategy (*strategos*) as "the masterful orchestration of time, force, and space" and "grand foresight across every arena of struggle" (p. 9), Pamukoğlu synthesizes two and a half millennia of military commanders, strategic philosophers, geopolitical theorists, total-war statesmen, and political economists into a single Turkish-language reference manual.

## Key Strategic Takeaways

1. **Canonical Four Pillars of Strategy**: Anchors modern strategic literacy in four foundational texts—Machiavelli's *Art of War*, Jomini's *Summary of the Art of War*, Clausewitz's *On War*, and B. H. Liddell Hart's *Strategy: The Indirect Approach* (p. 9)—before broadening the lens across maritime, aerial, psychological, and economic domains.
2. **Human Psychology as the Center of Gravity**: Devoting nearly seventy pages to *Savaşta İnsan ve Psikoloji* (pp. 213–280) plus *Sinir Savaşı* (p. 173), Pamukoğlu stresses that fear, morale, surprise, and willpower ultimately govern whether material superiority translates into victory.
3. **Industrial & Economic Sinews of Victory**: Concluding with Adam Smith, Alexander Hamilton, and Friedrich List (p. 417), Pamukoğlu emphasizes that sustainable strategic autonomy is inseparable from productive industrial capacity and national economic sovereignty.
4. **Cautionary Case Studies of Hubris**: Uses the Stalingrad disaster (p. 101) and Napoleonic overreach (p. 84) to show how strategic blindness destroys even formidable tactical armies.
