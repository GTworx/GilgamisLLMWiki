## Summary
Two-part historical study examining the operational origins and border disputes of the 1980–1988 Iran-Iraq War alongside ancient Persian cultural, philosophical, and architectural transmissions to Western civilization.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes the triggers of the 1980 Iraqi invasion of Iran, including the 1975 Algiers Agreement, Shatt al-Arab territorial sovereignty, and revolutionary export fears. Traces ancient Iranian civilizational contributions to the West, from Zoroastrian eschatological concepts (dualism, messianism, angels) to post-roads, paradisiacal garden design, and Achaemenid royal administrative tolerance.
