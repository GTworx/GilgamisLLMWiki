## Executive Summary

This 64-page multi-part dossier synthesizes foundational engineering guidelines and provincial action frameworks for mitigating structural seismic vulnerability across Türkiye's most exposed urban centers. It combines the Ministry's classic engineering manual *Türkiye'de Deprem Yapısal Risklerinin Azaltılması* (56 pp.), the specialized provincial action matrix for Hatay (*İl İl Deprem Kaynaklı Eylemler Tablosu - Hatay*, 7 pp.), and geological active fault trace mapping for the metropolitan area of İzmir (*İzmir Fay*, 1 p.).

The collection examines the mechanical mechanisms of structural vulnerability in Turkish reinforced concrete building typologies, including soft stories, captive columns, asymmetric floor plans, unconfined beam-column connections, and smooth reinforcing steel. It contrasts these structural deficiencies with active tectonic fault slip parameters in Hatay and İzmir, providing actionable retrofitting strategies and regulatory zoning restrictions adjacent to active fault traces.

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Structural Vulnerability Typologies**: Details the primary causes of pancake collapses during earthquakes, focusing on soft-story configurations (commercial ground floors) and inadequate transverse shear reinforcement.
- **Retrofitting Engineering Solutions**: Explains carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) wrapping, reinforced concrete shear wall additions, and steel cross-bracing as cost-effective retrofitting methods.
- **Hatay Provincial Action Inventory**: Formulates specific structural risk reduction projects across Antakya, Defne, İskenderun, and Kırıkhan, detailing hospital and school reinforcement targets.
- **İzmir Fault Mechanics and Zoning**: Maps active fault segments across the İzmir graben system (İzmir Fault, Seferihisar Fault, Gülbahçe Fault), demonstrating severe ground motion risks for coastal alluvial plains.
