## Summary
Methodological demographic guide defining Total Fertility Rate (TFR), calculating cohort and period measures, and modeling the fiscal and civilizational consequences of sub-replacement fertility (TFR < 2.1).

## Analytical Overview
Details calculation methodologies for Age-Specific Fertility Rates (ASFR) and synthetic cohort fertility. Evaluates why industrial and post-industrial nations across East Asia and Southern Europe have collapsed to ultra-low fertility levels (TFR < 1.0), analyzing fiscal pressures on pension solvency, inverted demographic pyramids, and economic stagnation.
