## Executive Summary

This 38-page comprehensive market analysis by CBRE Research surveys global data center real estate performance across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and Latin America. CBRE documents record-low vacancy rates (under 3% across major primary markets), historic absorption levels driven by generative AI pilots, and escalating construction delays caused by power utility grid constraints and electrical equipment lead times. The report details how power scarcity is driving up asking rental rates by 15%–30% year-over-year, forcing cloud tenants to execute pre-leases years ahead of delivery and catalyzing institutional capital deployment into secondary and tertiary regional hubs.

---

## Key Strategic, Scientific & Engineering Takeaways

- **Record-Low Global Vacancy**: Primary data center vacancy rates dropped to historic lows (Northern Virginia 1.8%, Silicon Valley 2.9%, Frankfurt 3.2%), creating an extreme landlord's market.
- **Pricing Power and Rental Re-acceleration**: Driven by intense supply-demand imbalances, wholesale colocation rental rates increased double-digits for the first time in a decade, reversing years of secular price erosion.
- **Construction Pipeline Constraints**: Despite record gigawatts under development, completion timelines have lengthened by 12–24 months due to power transformer delays, local utility moratoriums, and environmental permitting hurdles.
- **Secondary Market Proliferation**: Demand is actively spilling over into secondary hubs (Phoenix, Columbus, Austin, Salt Lake City, Milan, Warsaw, Osaka) where electrical capacity can still be allocated.
- **Hyperscale Pre-Leasing Dominance**: Hyperscale cloud operators are routinely pre-leasing 100% of planned facility capacity prior to groundbreaking, effectively shutting out mid-market enterprise tenants from tier-1 wholesale availability.
