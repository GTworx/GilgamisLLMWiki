## Overview
This multidisciplinary monograph explores the intersections between Buddhist mindfulness practices, non-dual contemplative states, and quantum mechanical theories of consciousness—specifically Roger Penrose and Stuart Hameroff's Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR) model of microtubule quantum coherence in brain neurons.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Explores quantum biological coherence in neuronal tubulin dimers as a candidate physical substrate for non-computational conscious moments.
- **Key Finding**: Connects meditative dissolution of the subject-object boundary with quantum superposition and state reduction.
- **Key Finding**: Critiques both reductive materialism and naive mystical interpretations, establishing rigorous interdisciplinary dialogue.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Mindfulness training systematically quietens the narrative self-referential default mode network, revealing an underlying unified awareness that contemplative traditions and quantum physics describe in strikingly convergent terms."
