## Executive Summary

This monumental 364-page two-volume compendium by Boğaziçi University Kandilli Observatory and Earthquake Research Institute (KOERI / DEZİM) represents the definitive scientific and engineering assessment of probabilistic seismic hazard and structural loss scenarios for the Istanbul Metropolitan Area. Spearheaded by Prof. Dr. Mustafa Erdik and leading earthquake engineers, the treatise integrates three-dimensional kinematic fault rupture simulations along the Main Marmara Fault (MMF) segment of the North Anatolian Fault Zone (NAFZ) with parcel-level building inventory databases and high-resolution geotechnical soil amplification profiles.

The compendium updates casualty, building collapse, and infrastructure disruption models for an Mw 7.5 earthquake scenario. It models ground motion attenuation across Istanbul's 39 districts, demonstrating severe spectral acceleration amplification across European-side Neogene sedimentary basins (Bakırköy, Avcılar, Küçükçekmece, Zeytinburnu) compared to bedrock formations on the Anatolian block. The analysis estimates that over 48,000 buildings would suffer very heavy or heavy damage, with an additional 194,000 sustaining moderate damage, accompanied by massive disruptions to drinking water conduits, natural gas pipelines, and maritime transportation links.

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Kinematic Fault Modeling of the Marmara Segment**: Evaluates an Mw 7.2 to 7.6 characteristic rupture along the locked Central and Northern Marmara Fault strands, computing synthetic seismograms and peak ground acceleration (PGA exceeding 0.45g in southern coastal districts).
- **District-Level Damage Disaggregation**: Identifies critical vulnerability clusters across Avcılar, Bakırköy, Bahçelievler, Fatih, and Zeytinburnu due to soft-story layouts, unreinforced masonry infill, and substandard concrete quality predating the 1999 Kocaeli earthquake.
- **Infrastructure Lifeline Interactions**: Models simultaneous seismic damage to natural gas distribution grids, electrical substations, and pressurized water mains, quantifying secondary hazards including urban conflagrations and water shortages.
- **Casualty and Hospital Care Demand**: Estimates peak emergency medical demand and immediate hospitalization requirements, establishing that local healthcare infrastructure will experience severe surge pressure requiring maritime casualty evacuation corridors.
