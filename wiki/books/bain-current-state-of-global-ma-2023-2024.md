## Analytical Overview
### Dealmaking in a Higher-for-Longer Interest Rate Regime

*The Current State of M&A*, authored by the global M&A practice of Bain & Company, evaluates global corporate transaction trends across 2023 and 2024. After the historic, cheap-money M&A peak of 2021, the market experienced a sharp contraction as rising debt costs and antitrust regulatory scrutiny slowed mega-cap deal activity.

### The Shift from Scale to Scope Deals

Bain's empirical data reveals critical strategic shifts:
- Rather than pursuing classical scale mergers (buying direct competitors to strip costs), acquirers pivoted toward **scope deals**: buying specialized technology, talent, or sustainability capabilities to accelerate business model reinvention.
- Antitrust authorities (US FTC, European Commission, UK CMA) escalated aggressive enforcement, scrutinizing platform ecosystem consolidation and vertical integration.
- Private equity sponsors faced a severe liquidity crunch, with exit volumes dropping to decade lows and driving the use of continuation funds.

### The Post-Merger Integration Imperative

The report proves that top quartile performers succeed through disciplined, programmatic M&A: executing small, continuous strategic bolt-ons paired with rigorous cultural and IT integration.
