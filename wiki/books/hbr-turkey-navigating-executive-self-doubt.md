## Analytical Overview
### The Psychology of Executive Insecurity and Upward Navigation

In *Kendi Liderliğinizden Şüphe Duyduğunuzda ve Umursamaz Yöneticileri Aşmak*, published in *Harvard Business Review Türkiye*, executive coaches Anne Sugar and Octavia Goredema address two deeply personal, pervasive career challenges: overcoming internal executive self-doubt (imposter syndrome) and protecting career momentum when managed by an indifferent or disengaged boss.

### Reframing Leadership Doubt as a Cognitive Asset

Sugar explains that experiencing self-doubt does not signify incompetence; rather, it is a natural symptom of operating at the frontiers of one's capability:
- Deconstructing unrealistic 'heroic leadership' illusions: leadership is an ongoing iterative practice, not innate perfection.
- Developing a trusted 'personal board of directors' outside formal reporting lines to provide objective reality checks and reality-based reassurance.

### Overcoming Disengaged and Passive Superiors

Goredema provides practical tactics for managing an indifferent boss who provides no mentorship, feedback, or sponsorship:
- Taking proactive ownership of one's visibility and accomplishments across peer departments.
- Seeking lateral alliances and project sponsorships with other senior executives to ensure professional growth is not blocked by a single indifferent gatekeeper.
