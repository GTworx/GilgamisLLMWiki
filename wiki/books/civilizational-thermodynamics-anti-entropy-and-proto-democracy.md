## Summary
Pioneering theoretical and quantitative treatise modeling human civilization as an anti-entropic dissipative structure, cross-referencing urban population thresholds with assemblies in Mesopotamia, Harappa, and pre-Athenian polities.

## Analytical Overview
Applies Prigogine's dissipative systems theory and energetic metabolism models to explain how human societies organize complex social structures to minimize local entropy. Re-examines political decision-making in ancient settlements (Tell Brak, Mohenjo-Daro, early Sumerian assemblies), demonstrating that participatory governance and communal councils preceded classical Athenian democracy by millennia.
