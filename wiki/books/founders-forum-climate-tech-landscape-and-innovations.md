## Executive Summary

Published by Founders Forum Group in partnership with leading European climate venture capital funds, this comprehensive 34-page strategic intelligence report audits the global Climate Tech investment ecosystem heading into 2023. The report maps capital flows, technological breakthrough horizons, and entrepreneurial scaling dynamics across five critical decarbonization verticals: Energy & Power, Built Environment, Mobility & Transport, Food & Agriculture, and Carbon Removal & Industrial Decarbonization.

The report tracks how climate tech venture investment displayed structural resilience against the broader 2022–2023 global tech downturn, attracting over $40 billion in venture and growth capital. It investigates catalytic policy drivers—including the US Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) and the EU Green Deal Industrial Plan—and profiles breakthrough startups pioneering direct air capture (DAC), green hydrogen electrolysis, advanced sodium-ion and solid-state batteries, and bio-engineered alternative proteins.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Macro Venture Capital Resilience**: Documents that climate tech captured over 25% of all venture dollars invested in 2022, demonstrating strong resilience against macroeconomic head-winds and interest rate hikes.
- **The 'First-of-a-Kind' (FOAK) Financing Gap**: Identifies the critical valley of death facing deep-tech climate startups transitioning from successful laboratory pilot to capital-intensive commercial-scale industrial plants.
- **Policy Catalysts (IRA and EU Green Deal)**: Details how massive production tax credits ($3/kg for green hydrogen, $180/ton for point-source carbon capture) radically alter unit economics and accelerate institutional adoption.
- **Hardware-Software Convergence**: Analyzes the synergy between physical decarbonization hardware and enterprise carbon accounting SaaS, grid-scale virtual power plant (VPP) software, and satellite methane monitoring.
