## Summary
Authoritative quantitative benchmark report from Carta analyzing actual fund return metrics—Internal Rate of Return (IRR), Total Value to Paid-In (TVPI), and DPI distributions—across venture capital vintages from 2017 to 2023.

## Analytical Overview
### Ground-Truth Return Analytics for Venture Capital

*Carta VC Fund Performance (Q1 2024)* provides an unvarnished, empirical examination of venture capital performance metrics derived directly from Carta's proprietary fund administration platform. In an asset class notorious for opaque reporting and self-reported valuation marks, Carta's dataset delivers ground-truth analytics on how venture capital funds are truly performing across historical vintages.

### The Great Valuation Hangover and the DPI Drought

The report exposes the severe liquidity challenges confronting limited partners (LPs) and general partners (GPs) in the post-ZIRP environment:
- **Divergence of TVPI and DPI:** While funds from the 2020–2021 bubble vintages maintain paper Total Value to Paid-In (TVPI) multiples of 1.5x to 2.0x, their Distributed to Paid-In (DPI) cash returns remain near zero due to an frozen IPO and M&A market.
- **Vintage Year Corrections:** Older vintages (2017–2019) continue to show healthy cash distributions, while 2021-vintage funds are suffering deep downward markdowns as portfolio startups execute flat or down-rounds.
- **Fund Dispersion:** Top-quartile venture funds continue to significantly outperform, but the median venture fund is struggling to match public market S&P 500 benchmarks.

### LP-GP Dynamics and Capital Calls

The report serves as an essential manual for institutional allocators evaluating manager re-ups and emerging fund managers modeling cash distribution timelines.
