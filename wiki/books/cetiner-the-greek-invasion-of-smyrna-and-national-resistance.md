## Summary
Centennial historical study documenting the May 15, 1919 Greek military landing in Izmir under Allied auspices, the civilian atrocities that followed, and the immediate catalyst it provided for Mustafa Kemal's national resistance.

## Analytical Overview
Examines the geopolitical maneuvers behind British Prime Minister Lloyd George's authorization of the Greek landing under Article 7 of the Mudros Armistice. Details the initial resistance of journalist Hasan Tahsin, the Inter-Allied Commission of Inquiry report (Bristol Commission) confirming Greek violations, and how the landing electrified Turkish national resistance culminating in the Amasya Circular and Sivas Congress.
