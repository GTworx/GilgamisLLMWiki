## Summary
Critical sociological study analyzing how caste stratification operates across non-Hindu religious communities in South Asia, with specific focus on Ashraf, Ajlaf, and Arzal social divisions among Muslims in India and Pakistan.

## Analytical Overview
Examines the persistent gap between Islamic theological egalitarianism and South Asian social reality. Documents the occupational stratification, caste-based occupational boundaries, endogamy, and political under-representation of Dalit Muslims (Pasmanda) across northern India and Pakistani rural society.
