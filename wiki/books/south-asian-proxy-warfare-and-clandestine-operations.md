## Analytical Overview
### Sub-Conventional Warfare in Nuclear-Armed South Asia

*Proxy Warfare in South Asia: State-Sponsored Paramilitaries, Covert Financing, and Sub-Conventional Conflict* provides a granular intelligence matrix detailing cross-border proxy engagements between South Asian nuclear rivals.

### Granular Asymmetric Matrix

The dataset cross-tabulates:
- Target regions, alleged intelligence agency involvement (e.g. India's RAW, Pakistan's ISI), operational purposes, and organizational affiliations across Balochistan, Kashmir, the Punjab, and Northeast India (ULFA, Naga insurgents).
- Examining how sub-conventional proxy conflict functions as an asymmetric substitute for conventional interstate war beneath the threshold of nuclear retaliation.
