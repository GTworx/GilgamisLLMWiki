## Executive Summary

Written by **Hambleton Lord** and **Christopher Mirabile** (co-founders of Seraf and leaders of the Angel Capital Association), **Angel Investing by the Numbers** is a quantitative and procedural masterclass in early-stage angel investing and portfolio construction. The authors demonstrate that angel investing must be approached with the mathematical rigor of an asset class rather than emotional hobbyism. Grounded in empirical data from the Wiltbank/Kauffman Foundation studies, the book provides rigorous mathematical models for pre-money valuation (Scorecard Method, Berkus Method, Risk Factor Summation), cap table modeling, convertible note dilution (caps and discounts), SAFE mechanics, follow-on reserve planning, and the minimum portfolio diversification (at least 20–25 companies) necessary to capture the power-law returns required to overcome high early-stage failure rates.

---

## Key Strategic & Financial Takeaways

- **The Mathematical Imperative of Portfolio Diversification**: Wiltbank empirical studies demonstrate that angel investors with portfolios of fewer than 15 companies suffer high probabilities of net capital loss; achieving consistent 20%+ net returns requires building a portfolio of at least 20 to 30 uncorrelated startup investments.
- **Cap Table Dilution and Waterfall Modeling**: Provides step-by-step mathematical calculations showing how early-stage angel ownership (e.g., 10%) is methodically diluted across Series A, B, and C rounds (typically suffering 20%–25% dilution per round), modeling exact net dollar returns under various exit valuations.
- **Convertible Notes and SAFEs vs. Priced Equity**: Deconstructs the hidden traps of unpriced convertible instruments, showing how multiple stacked valuation caps create massive, unintended dilution for founders and misalignment between early angel checks and later institutional leads.
- **Follow-On Reserve Strategy**: Recommends reserving at least $1 to $2 in follow-on dry powder for every $1 invested in the initial check to defend pro-rata ownership in the top 10%–20% portfolio breakout winners.
- **Tax Incentives (Section 1202 QSBS)**: Details the immense financial advantage of U.S. Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS), which allows angel investors holding original-issue C-corp stock for over five years to exclude up to $10 million (or 10x basis) in capital gains from federal taxation.

## Notable Excerpts & Citations

- "Angel investing is an asymmetric numbers game. If you invest in five companies, you are gambling. If you invest in thirty companies with disciplined screening and follow-on reserves, you are managing a mathematically sound asset class." (source: Angel Investing by the Numbers, p. 11)
- "A valuation cap on a convertible note is not a valuation; it is a ceiling on the price an investor will pay in a future round. Stacking multiple notes with different caps without modeling the post-money dilution is the fastest way to ruin a company's capitalization table." (source: Angel Investing by the Numbers, p. 54)
