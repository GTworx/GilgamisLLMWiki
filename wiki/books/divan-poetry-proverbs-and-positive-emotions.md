## Overview
Published in Türk Dünyası Araştırmaları by literary scholar Yeliz Yasak Peran, this 22-page monograph analyzes the incorporation of folk proverbs (irsâl-i mesel) expressing positive emotions—hope, joy, divine gratitude, generosity, and resilience—into classical Ottoman Divan poetry. Peran demonstrates how courtly poets bridged high Persianate verse with vernacular folk wisdom.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Examines the classical poetic art of *irsâl-i mesel* (embedding popular proverbs into rhymed couplets).
- **Key Finding**: Categorizes proverbs conveying hope, moral contentment, patience, and social benevolence in major Ottoman divans.
- **Key Finding**: Reveals the harmonious dialogue between elite courtly aesthetics and grassroots folk culture.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Divan şairi, sarayın kapalı kapıları ardında yaşayan soyut bir hayalperest değil; halkın yüzyıllar boyu yoğurduğu atasözlerini mısralarına nakşederek şiirini hayatın hakikatine bağlayan bir bilgedir."
