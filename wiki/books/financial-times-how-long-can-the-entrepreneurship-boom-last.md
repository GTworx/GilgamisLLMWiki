## Summary
Financial Times macroeconomic investigation evaluating whether the historic surge in post-pandemic new business creation can withstand rising interest rates, tightening venture credit, and economic slowdowns.

## Analytical Overview
### Macro Stress-Testing the Post-Pandemic Startup Wave

In *How Long Can the Entrepreneurship Boom Last?*, the *Financial Times* investigates the sustainability of the historic global wave of new business formation in an era marked by aggressive central bank interest rate hikes and the end of zero-interest-rate policy (ZIRP).

### Headwinds vs. Structural Drivers

The investigation analyzes the competing economic forces:
- **Severe Macro Headwinds:** Soaring cost of capital, tightening bank credit following the regional banking crisis, declining consumer real wages, and a drastic contraction in early-stage venture funding.
- **Durable Structural Drivers:** The permanent normalization of distributed remote work, the collapse in software development costs driven by generative AI co-pilots, and cultural shifts toward self-sovereignty and autonomous careers.

### The Survival Playbook

The report chronicles how founders are adapting from growth-at-all-costs blitzscaling toward default-alive cash-flow discipline, proving that companies founded in high-interest-rate environments build more durable, disciplined economic foundations.
