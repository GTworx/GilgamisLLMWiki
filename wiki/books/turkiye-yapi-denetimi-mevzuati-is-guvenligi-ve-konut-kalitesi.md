## Executive Summary

This monumental 167-page four-study academic and professional compendium provides the definitive critical analysis of Türkiye's building inspection system established under Law No. 4708 (*Yapı Denetimi Hakkında Kanun*). Combining the legal-engineering master's thesis of Barış Duman (26 pp.), the extensive empirical field research in Bursa by Seda Yağız (107 pp.), the foundational critique by former TMMOB Chairman Mehmet Soğancı at TESKON (24 pp.), and the economic study by Atalay and Engin (10 pp.), the volume uncovers the systemic structural and ethical flaws of private inspection in construction.

The compendium documents the historical genesis of Law No. 4708 following the 1999 Marmara catastrophe, transitioning from municipal inspection to private building inspection companies (*Yapı Denetim Kuruluşları*). It details the deep conflict of interest that plagued the system for nearly two decades—wherein contractors hired and directly paid their own private inspectors—leading to superficial oversight, falsified concrete cube test reports, neglected occupational health and safety (OHS) duties, and compromised structural integrity.

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **The Commercialized Inspection Paradox**: Exposes the fatal design flaw of private inspection where inspectors depended commercially on the builders they were legally mandated to police.
- **Bursa Fieldwork Findings**: Analyzes comprehensive survey data from contractors, supervising engineers, and residents in Bursa, revealing widespread discrepancies between architectural designs and built reality.
- **Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Liabilities**: Clarifies the complex civil and criminal responsibilities of building inspectors under Law No. 6331 and Law No. 4708 for fatal construction site accidents.
- **Transition to the National Electronic Assignment System**: Evaluates the pivotal 2019 reform (e-Dağıtım) that ended contractor selection by assigning building inspection firms through an automated governmental lottery.
