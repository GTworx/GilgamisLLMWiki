## Overview
This comprehensive technical treatise analyzes emerging cybersecurity and identity threat models unique to metaverse ecosystems. The authors evaluate vulnerabilities across user avatars (avatar cloning, man-in-the-room attacks, identity theft), sensor hardware telemetry interception, smart contract exploits in virtual asset marketplaces, and propose zero-trust spatial security architectures.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Identifies the 'Man-in-the-Room' (MitR) attack: invisible, eavesdropping phantom avatars operating within encrypted virtual spaces.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes kinetic signature spoofing where malicious actors clone unique human movement profiles.
- **Key Finding**: Proposes decentralized zero-trust identity proofs (DIDs) and zero-knowledge spatial authentication.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "In immersive spatial networks, an adversary does not need to compromise your password; by cloning your unique biomechanical kinematic signature, they can impersonate your embodied digital presence."
