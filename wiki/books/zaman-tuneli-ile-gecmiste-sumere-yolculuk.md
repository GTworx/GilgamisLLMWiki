## Executive Summary

Originally commissioned and published by the Turkish Ministry of Culture, *Zaman Tüneli ile Geçmişte Sümer'e Yolculuk* (*Journey to Sumer through a Time Tunnel*) is an educational and cultural journey designed by **Muazzez İlmiye Çığ** to introduce students, educators, and general readers to the daily life, school system, sciences, and inventions of third-millennium BCE Sumer. Using the device of two modern children traveling back in time to the ancient city of Nippur, Çığ illustrates how the Sumerians invented writing, legal codes, schools (*Edubba*), irrigation canals, geometry, arithmetic, and astronomy.

## Structural & Chapter Breakdown

| Chapter | Educational Theme |
|---|---|
| **I. Zaman Tüneline Giriş** | Modern children transported to Nippur; encountering mudbrick ziggurats, bustling markets, and reeds along the Euphrates |
| **II. Sümer Tablet Okulu (*Edubba*)** | The tablet house; schoolmasters, monitor assistants, clay styluses, writing exercises, discipline, and daily homework |
| **III. Sümerlerin İcatları ve Bilim** | Invention of the wheel, plow, sailboat, base-60 sexagesimal mathematics (60 minutes, 360-degree circle), and lunar calendar |
| **IV. Kanunlar ve Adalet Sarayı** | King Ur-Nammu's law codes; contracts for trade, marriage, and property; court trials inscribed on clay envelopes |
| **V. Eve Dönüş ve Miras** | Realizing that modern civilization walks on foundations laid five thousand years ago in Mesopotamia |

---

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **Pedagogical Democratization of Archaeology**: Proves that complex ancient Near Eastern philology can be translated into engaging, scientifically accurate literature for younger generations.
2. **The Sexagesimal System in Modern Life**: Teaches readers that our 60-second minutes, 60-minute hours, 24-hour days, and 360-degree geometric circles are direct Sumerian mathematical inventions.
3. **The Edubba as the First University**: Illustrates that systematic curricula, textbooks, academic grading, and homework originated in Sumerian tablet houses over 4,500 years ago.
