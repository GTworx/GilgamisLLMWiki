## Executive Summary

In *Avrupa ve Biz* (*Europe and Us*), **İlber Ortaylı** deconstructs Turkey's centuries-long diplomatic, cultural, and intellectual entanglement with Europe. Rejecting both superficial Eurocentric chauvinism and reactive isolationist xenophobia, Ortaylı provides a deep historical reconstruction of mutual interaction: from Byzantine-Ottoman diplomatic continuities and Renaissance diplomatic embassies to the Enlightenment, the Tanzimat modernization reforms, and modern European integration debates.

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **The Shared Mediterranean Continuum**: Europe and the Ottoman world were never isolated, hermetic civilizations; they shared a common Mediterranean maritime and diplomatic ecosystem for centuries.
2. **Modernization as Sovereign Survival**: Turkish Westernization (*Batılılaşma*) was not cultural surrender, but a conscious, deliberate strategy by Ottoman-Turkish elites to preserve sovereign independence against European imperial aggression.
3. **The Intellectual Depth of the Tanzimat**: Vindicates the intellectual caliber of 19th-century Ottoman reformers (Mustafa Reşid Pasha, Âli Pasha, Fuad Pasha), who mastered European languages and statecraft without losing their imperial identity.
