## Summary
Comprehensive 3-volume, 275-page institutional intelligence compendium from the EIU assembling 17 cross-sector industry briefs, the Country Risk Service methodology handbook, and longitudinal global political risk rankings.

## Analytical Overview
Details the quantitative scoring methodologies utilized by the EIU to measure sovereign creditworthiness, currency risk, banking sector stability, and political risk across 140+ countries. Formulates cross-industry outlooks across energy, automotive, financial services, healthcare, and telecom, tracking post-pandemic supply chain restructuring and geopolitical friction.
