## Executive Summary

*Korkunun Ötesi* (*Beyond Fear: A Toltec Guide to Freedom and Joy*, compiled by Mary Carroll Nelson from the oral teachings of Mexican surgeon-turned-Toltec *nagual* **Don Miguel Ruiz**, author of *The Four Agreements*; Turkish edition 2008, pp. 2–3) is an experiential spiritual-psychology guide rooted in modern Toltec wisdom centered on the ancient Mesoamerican pyramid city of **Teotihuacan**—"the place where humans become gods" (p. 4).

Combining autobiographical storytelling (Ruiz's near-fatal car accident awakening and lineage initiation), Mesoamerican symbolic cosmology, and practical cognitive-emotional exercises, the book argues that most human suffering originates in an unconscious collective conditioning Ruiz calls **"The Dream of Hell"** (*Cehennem Rüyası*)—a fear-driven mental parasite maintained by internalized judgment and social domestication that can be dismantled through awareness, recapitulation, and unconditional love (pp. 4–5).

---

## Thirteen-Chapter Journey (Contents, pp. 4–5)

- **Foundations & Teotihuacan Symbolism (Chapters 1–3, pp. 1–78)**: Introduces Teotihuacan as an architectural allegory for spiritual awakening, outlining core Toltec concepts: the *Tonal* (ordered perceptual world), *Nagual* (unseen creative awareness), *Mitote* (noisy mental chatter), and Silent Knowledge (*Sessiz Bilgi*).
- **Diagnosis of Conditioning (Chapters 4–5, pp. 79–102)**: Analyzes how childhood "domestication" installs an internal Judge and Victim running the *Dream of Hell* (fear, guilt, shame) and contrasts evolutionary habit with conscious dreaming.
- **Practical Tools of Transformation (Chapters 6–8, pp. 103–174)**: Presents concrete inner practices—recapitulation, stalking one's own beliefs, breaking fear-based agreements, forgiveness, and cultivating an inner "Just World" (*Adil Dünya*).
- **Mortality, Lineage & Feminine Wisdom (Chapters 9–13, pp. 175–308)**: Meditates on death as a liberating teacher, traces global *nagual* traditions, honors feminine spiritual wisdom (*Gaya'nın Hikayesi*), and closes with Toltec prophecies of a shifting collective consciousness (*Sixth Sun*) plus a structured Reader's Guide (p. 309).
