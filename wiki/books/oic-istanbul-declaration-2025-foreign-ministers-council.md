## Summary
Official multilateral declaration adopted by the 51st Session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) in Istanbul (2025), establishing unanimous diplomatic positions on Palestinian statehood, combating Islamophobia, and multilateral institutional reform.

## Analytical Overview
### Multilateral Consensus of the Islamic World

The *OIC Istanbul Declaration 2025*, adopted at the 51st Session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC/CFM-51/2025/DEC/FINAL), stands as an authoritative diplomatic statement representing 57 member states.

### Core Resolutions Adopted

The Istanbul Declaration codifies unanimous diplomatic mandates:
- **The Palestine Question:** Reaffirming the pre-1967 borders with East Jerusalem as the capital of an independent sovereign Palestinian state; demanding an immediate, permanent ceasefire in Gaza; and calling for full UN membership for Palestine.
- **International Legal Accountability:** Supporting South Africa's genocide proceedings at the International Court of Justice (ICJ) and calling for the enforcement of ICC arrest warrants against Israeli leadership.
- **Combating Islamophobia:** Establishing monitoring observatories to counter anti-Muslim discrimination and religious desecration in Western nations.
- **UN Security Council Reform:** Calling for permanent representation of the Islamic world on a restructured UN Security Council.
