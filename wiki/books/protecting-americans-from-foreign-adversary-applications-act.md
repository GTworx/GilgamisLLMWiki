## Overview
This 12-page official legislative document comprises H.R. 7521 as introduced in the 118th US Congress. The bipartisan legislation mandates the forced divestiture or nationwide ban of software applications controlled by designated foreign adversary entities—specifically targeting ByteDance and TikTok on national security, data exfiltration, and algorithmic manipulation grounds.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Codifies legal statutory mechanisms for prohibiting mobile app hosting and CDN distribution of adversary-controlled software.
- **Key Finding**: Establishes a 180-day divestiture clock requiring complete severance from foreign parent companies.
- **Key Finding**: Balances national security counter-intelligence concerns with constitutional First Amendment speech protections.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "An application controlled by a foreign adversary poses an unacceptable risk to United States national security by providing foreign adversaries with access to sensitive personal data and algorithmic influence over millions of Americans."
