## Executive Summary
This 48-page executive study by KPMG International surveys 2,100 C-suite executives and technology leaders worldwide to benchmark enterprise digital transformation roadmaps. The report evaluates how organizations balance aggressive generative AI adoption, hybrid cloud migrations, and modern data architecture with zero-trust cybersecurity posture and stringent data privacy compliance. The findings highlight the widening gap between 'digital leaders' who achieve measurable ROI from digital investments and laggards struggling with legacy technical debt.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Digital Leadership Divide**: Top-quartile digital leaders achieve 2.5x higher revenue growth and 3x faster time-to-market compared to technological laggards.
- **Zero-Trust Security Imperative**: Cybersecurity is no longer an afterthought but a prerequisite for deploying public cloud and generative AI workflows.
- **Cloud ROI Realization**: Enterprises are shifting from unconstrained cloud migration toward FinOps financial governance, optimizing workloads across hybrid architectures.
