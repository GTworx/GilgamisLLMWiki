## Summary
Three-part comparative treatise benchmarking Abrahamic and Dharmic faiths in terms of spiritual equality and social justice, combined with Turkish Ministry of Justice translations of European jurisprudence on religious symbols.

## Analytical Overview
Compares theological positions on human dignity, racial parity, and gender roles across Judaism, Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, and Buddhism. Analyzes landmark European Court of Human Rights rulings (such as Leyla Şahin v. Turkey and SAS v. France) concerning headscarf bans, religious neutrality in civil service, and freedom of conscience.
