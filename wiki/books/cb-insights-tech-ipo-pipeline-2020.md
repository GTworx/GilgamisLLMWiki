## Analytical Overview
### Pre-IPO Anatomy of the 2020 Unicorn Cohort

*The 2020 Tech IPO Pipeline*, authored by CB Insights, delivers an exhaustive quantitative examination of 281 highly valued, late-stage venture-backed private companies in the United States approaching public market liquidity events (via traditional IPO, direct listing, or SPAC).

### Key Metrics and Cohort Diagnostics

The report examines critical financial and operational benchmarks:
- **Unit Economics and Path to Profitability:** Evaluating the divergence between high-margin cloud software companies and low-margin, capital-intensive on-demand gig economy platforms.
- **Valuation Step-Ups vs. Public Multiples:** Analyzing whether late-stage private mega-rounds from crossover investors (such as SoftBank and Tiger Global) created unrealistic valuation expectations that face severe corrections in public trading.
- **Sector Concentration:** Deep dives into fintech infrastructure, cloud cybersecurity, digital health telemetry, and collaborative enterprise productivity tools.

### Investor Implications

The dossier serves as an indispensable reference for institutional public equity asset managers and investment banking underwriters preparing for the monumental 2020–2021 tech IPO liquidity wave.
