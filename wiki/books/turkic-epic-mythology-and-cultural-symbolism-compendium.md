## Summary
Expansive 7-source compendium surveying Turkic epic cycles (Oguz Kağan, Dede Korkut oral formulas), comparative Tree of Life symbolism across Turkic and Norse pantheons, Tahtacı Turkmen rites, and folkloric critical investigations.

## Analytical Overview
Assembles rigorous philological translations of the Oguz Kağan Epic with deep semantic analysis of the formulaic phrase 'Boy boyladı, soy soyladı' in Dede Korkut manuscripts. Examines shared cosmological archetypes between Eurasian steppe World Tree myths and Scandinavian Yggdrasil, while critically debunking recent pseudo-historical inventions regarding 'Nardugan' winter solstice celebrations.
