## Summary
Comprehensive 45-page policy manual outlining structural reforms for the multilateral trading system under weaponized interdependence, paired with comparative scenarios of a multipolar post-dollar reserve order.

## Analytical Overview
Diagnoses the structural paralysis of the World Trade Organization's Appellate Body, the proliferation of national security tariff exemptions, and unilateral carbon border adjustments (CBAM). Evaluates how a fragmented reserve currency architecture (BRICS clearing mechanisms, bilateral currency swaps, digital gold rails) affects sovereign bond yields and trade financing.
