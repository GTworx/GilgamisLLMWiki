## Summary
Three-part treatise applying physics-based self-organized criticality (SOC) to financial market crashes, cross-referencing high-frequency trading desk notes from Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan on instantaneous market eruptions.

## Analytical Overview
Demonstrates that financial markets behave like non-equilibrium complex systems where tiny shocks can trigger catastrophic cascading liquidations. Examines how automated delta-hedging by options dealers, systematic trend-following CTAs, and flash liquidity evaporation create non-linear market avalanches independently of external macro catalysts.
