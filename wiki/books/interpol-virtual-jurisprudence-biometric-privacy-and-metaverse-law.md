## Summary
Master legal, criminological, and regulatory treatise addressing the jurisdictional, criminal, and human rights frontiers of immersive virtual environments. Features INTERPOL's official 2024 Law Enforcement Perspective on policing crime in the metaverse, Brittan Heller's landmark Vanderbilt Law analysis 'Watching Androids Dream of Electric Sheep: Immersive Technologies, Biometric Psychography, and Privacy', Ronald Leenes' pioneering privacy regulation scholarship, and Reed Smith's intellectual property frameworks.

## Analytical Overview
This comprehensive legal and criminological compendium establishes the foundational doctrine of **Virtual Jurisprudence** and digital sovereignty in synthetic spaces. The volume is anchored by two landmark institutional publications:
1. **INTERPOL’s 2024 White Paper on Metaverse Crime**: Details law enforcement capabilities in investigating physical threats mediated through VR, child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in virtual environments, non-consensual avatar assault, financial terrorism via in-world digital tokens, and cross-border jurisdictional voids where crimes are committed across decentralized server nodes.
2. **Brittan Heller’s Biometric Psychography Doctrine**: Published in the *Vanderbilt Journal of Entertainment and Technology Law*, Heller's *Watching Androids Dream of Electric Sheep* introduces the legal concept of **biometric psychography**—using eye-tracking pupillometry, galvanic skin response, micro-expressions, and spatial motion telemetry captured by VR headsets to infer an individual's subconscious thoughts, sexual desires, political leanings, and psychological vulnerabilities without explicit consent.

The volume integrates Ronald Leenes' foundational 2008–2009 analyses of privacy regulation in Second Life, European Union white papers on responsible immersive technologies (*Livre Blanc Technologies Immersives*), Reed Smith's entertainment trademark protection analyses, and NYU Center for Business and Human Rights guidelines on algorithmic moderation and physical harassment in social virtual worlds.
