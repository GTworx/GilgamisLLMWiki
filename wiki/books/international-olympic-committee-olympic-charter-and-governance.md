## Overview
This authoritative 113-page constitutional codification of the International Olympic Committee (IOC), in force as of October 2023, establishes the fundamental principles of Olympism, administrative statutes, and binding international sports law. The Charter governs the organization of the Olympic Games, national Olympic committees, international federations, and the jurisdictional authority of the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS).

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Codifies the Fundamental Principles of Olympism: sports as a human right, political neutrality, and social solidarity.
- **Key Finding**: Establishes strict anti-discrimination, athlete welfare, and gender equality mandates across international sports federations.
- **Key Finding**: Outlines the legal arbitration protocols and anti-doping mechanisms (WADA code integration) resolving international athletic disputes.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The practice of sport is a human right. Every individual must have the possibility of practising sport, without discrimination of any kind and in the Olympic spirit, which requires mutual understanding with a spirit of friendship, solidarity and fair play."
