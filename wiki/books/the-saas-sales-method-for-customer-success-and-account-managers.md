## Executive Summary

*The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success & Account Managers: How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)* (published by Winning by Design LLC, San Francisco, 2018, Revision 5.0, 83 pp., edited by [[fernando-pizarro]] and Dan Smith) by [[dominique-levin]] and [[jacco-van-der-kooij]] codifies the post-sale recurring-revenue growth engine that separates top-quartile SaaS leaders from stagnating subscription vendors (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, pp. 3–8). Challenging the passive view of customer success as mere churn defense, the authors establish that **static customers are no longer enough**: elite SaaS organizations keep annual logo churn below 5% and achieve **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) exceeding 130%** by orchestrating continuous, measurable **Business Impact** across the entire customer lifecycle (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, p. 8).

Built around Winning by Design's signature **SPICED Diagnostic Framework** (*Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical Event, Decision Criteria*), the manual walks practitioners step-by-step through three lifecycle phases: **1. Onboarding** (Preboarding during the sales cycle, Internal Handoff, Customer Handoff, and Business Orchestration—never forcing the customer to repeat themselves); **2. Use** (Achieving First Value, Recurring Value, Customer Contact Cadence, Customer Health Scores, and **The Golden Sequence** for de-escalating customer frustration); and **3. Expansion** (Executive Impact Reviews, Account Management, and Organizational Multi-Threading) (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, Contents & Overview, pp. 7–16, 48–76).

## Key Arguments / Chapter Breakdown

* **Foundations: NRR > 130%, SPICED & Impact vs. Needs (pp. 8–14)**:
  * *Peeling the Onion to Impact*: Differentiates **Needs** (inputs/features) from **Impact** (business outputs), showing that discovering true impact requires peeling 6–7 layers of diagnostic questions; defines a **Critical Event** as a deadline carrying *natural consequences* if missed, allowing teams to reverse-engineer an immutable onboarding timeline (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, pp. 8–14).

* **Section 1: Onboarding — Preboarding, Handoff & Business Orchestration (pp. 15–47)**:
  * *Eliminating Handoff Drop-Off*: Details the three-step sales-to-CS handoff (Preboarding, Internal Handoff, Customer Handoff) using the ACE opening and shared SPICED notes; emphasizes pairing every technical kickoff with a **business orchestration meeting** affirming one-year client outcomes, noting that *"CSMs can ask questions that AEs cannot"* because clients perceive aligned non-quota incentives (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, pp. 10, 15–19).

* **Section 2: Use — First Value, Health Scores & The Golden Sequence (pp. 48–61)**:
  * *Sustaining Recurring Impact*: Establishes playbooks for compressing Time-to-First-Value, designing proactive customer contact cadences, constructing leading-indicator **Customer Health Scores** (monitoring usage signals rather than waiting for renewal shocks), and resolving escalations constructively via **The Golden Sequence** (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, pp. 7, 48–61).

* **Section 3: Expansion — Impact Reviews, Account Management & Organizational Selling (pp. 62–77)**:
  * *Turning Results into Compounding Growth*: Replaces backward-looking QBR slide dumps with **Impact Reviews** anchored to original orchestration goals, turning documented ROI into natural renewal, seat expansion, and cross-departmental organizational selling (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, pp. 7, 10, 62–77).

## Key Quotes & Citations

* "Static customers are no longer good enough. The top companies grow customers along a logically orchestrated customer journey, leading to positive Net Revenue Retention (NRR). Companies with a customer success operating model often have Net Revenue Retention (NRR)... that is greater than 130 percent." (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, Introduction, PDF p. 8)
* "Definition of Need: Something that is required. Needs are about inputs. Definition of Impact: Something that has a strong effect. Impact is about output... Ultimately, you are trying to achieve outcomes for your customer's business, not line up inputs with product features!" (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, Introduction, PDF pp. 11–12)
* "CSMs can ask questions that AEs cannot. Customers know that an AE has a goal of hitting quota, but that it is a CSM's job to make sure that the customer is successful. The customer may feel more comfortable sharing information with customer success because they share the same goal." (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, ch. 1.1, PDF p. 16)
