## Summary
Monumental 432-page historical and epigraphic study examining the fierce resistance of the Homonades in the rugged Taurus mountains against Augustus's legions, alongside Late Antique Armenian border statecraft between Rome and Ctesiphon.

## Analytical Overview
Combines geographical field surveys of Isauria and Pisidia with Strabo, Tacitus, and Ammianus Marcellinus to locate Homonad fortified settlements and trace the Bellum Homonadense led by Quirinius. Examines how the buffer kingdom of Greater Armenia negotiated political autonomy, religious conversion, and military obligations between the Roman and Sasanian Empires.
