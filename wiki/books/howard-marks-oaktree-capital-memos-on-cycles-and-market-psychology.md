## Summary
Three seminal investment memos by Howard Marks examining the limits of macroeconomic forecasting, the mechanics of Fed-induced engineered economic slowdowns, and the psychological discipline required for contrarian investing.

## Analytical Overview
Marks dissects the epistemological folly of basing portfolio decisions on macro forecasts ('Nobody Knows II'), demonstrating that the future is fundamentally uncertain. Explores how central banks orchestrate controlled recessions to extinguish inflation without triggering debt depressions, and illustrates the second-level thinking necessary to exploit manic-depressive market cycle extremes.
