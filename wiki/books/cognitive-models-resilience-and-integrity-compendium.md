## Analytical Overview
### Cognitive Heuristics for High-Pressure Leadership

*Mental Models for Executive Resilience and Ethical Decision-Making* synthesizes practical philosophy, cognitive behavioral psychology, and decision theory into a tactical toolkit for enterprise leaders operating under extreme cognitive load.

### Core Cognitive Heuristics and Razors

The compendium explores:
- **Powerful Life Razors:** Hanlon's Razor (never attribute to malice that which is adequately explained by stupidity), the Feynman Razor (if someone cannot explain an idea in simple language, they do not understand it), and the Regret Minimization Framework.
- **Resilience Models:** Neutralizing the **Spotlight Effect** (realizing people pay far less attention to your errors than you assume), the Stoic **Dichotomy of Control** (radically separating what is up to us from what is not), and Cognitive Reframing.
- **Responding with Integrity:** Seven specific communicative scripts for establishing boundaries, resisting unethical organizational pressure, and de-escalating interpersonal hostility without compromising moral values.

### Actionable Value for Leaders

The collection serves as a practical, pocket mental operating system for maintaining emotional composure and ethical clarity during organizational crises.
