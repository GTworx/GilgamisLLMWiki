## Overview
This entertaining and chemically precise practical mixology manual outlines foundational recipes, ingredient balancing heuristics, and spirits taxonomy for crafting classic cocktails. The guide deconstructs the balancing triangle between base spirit (whiskey, tequila, rum, gin), acid (citrus), and sweet/aromatic components (simple syrups, bitters, liqueurs), celebrating the artisanal culture of social drinking.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Establishes a starter shopping list and baseline spirits taxonomy for high-craft home bartending.
- **Key Finding**: Explains the chemistry of balancing sweet, sour, bitter, and boozy taste profiles.
