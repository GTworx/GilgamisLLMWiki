## Executive Summary

Compiled and published by the Union of Turkish Bar Associations (Türkiye Barolar Birliği - TBB) in February 2023 following the catastrophic Kahramanmaraş earthquakes, this 48-page practical legal handbook (ISBN: 978-605-7848-75-8) serves as the primary legal guide for disaster survivors, volunteer attorneys, and judicial actors. The manual systematically demystifies Turkish civil, criminal, administrative, and insurance law in the context of mass building collapses.

The guide outlines rigorous procedures for preserving physical evidence from collapsed building rubble (core sampling, reinforcement inspection, architectural discrepancy documentation) before emergency debris removal. It details legal rights regarding official damage assessments (hasar tespiti objections), rights-holder status (hak sahipliği), DASK insurance claims, tortious liability of building contractors and municipal inspectors, employment rights under emergency decree, and criminal liability for negligent homicide.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Criminal Evidence Preservation in Rubble**: Details urgent court applications for evidence preservation (*delil tespiti*) under the Code of Civil and Criminal Procedure, mandating forensic concrete core testing and structural steel tensile assays.
- **Contractor and Municipal Tort Liability**: Analyzes strict liability, gross negligence, and breach of building code duties for structural engineers, building inspection firms (yapı denetim firmaları), and municipal licensing authorities.
- **Rights-Holder Status and Public Housing**: Explains the legal mechanics of Law No. 7269 (Disasters Law), detailing qualifications, subsidized loan repayments, and property restitution frameworks.
- **Insurance and DASK Claims Processing**: Formulates procedural timelines for filing compulsory earthquake insurance claims, appealing property devaluation assessments, and recovering private life and property policies.
