## Analytical Overview
### The 2022 Geoeconomic Shockwave

The June 11-12, 2022 weekend edition of *Financial Times Europe* captures the intensifying polycrisis that defined the global economy in mid-2022. Reporting across Frankfurt, London, Washington, and Kyiv, the paper chronicles the simultaneous convergence of war, energy crisis, and monetary policy tightening.

### Core Economic Developments

The issue details:
- Wall Street's sharp selloff as US headline CPI hit a 40-year high of 8.6%, cementing expectations for aggressive 75-basis-point interest rate hikes by the Federal Reserve.
- The European Central Bank's historic pivot under Christine Lagarde toward ending eight years of negative interest rates.
- The deepening European energy crisis as Gazprom slashed Nord Stream 1 flows, triggering industrial rationing plans across German manufacturing sectors.
- Geopolitical reporting on the Battle of Severodonetsk in eastern Ukraine and Western allied sanctions enforcement.

### Historical Value as Economic Benchmark

The compendium serves as an essential primary document tracking the end of the post-2008 era of cheap money, zero interest rates, and secure global supply chains.
