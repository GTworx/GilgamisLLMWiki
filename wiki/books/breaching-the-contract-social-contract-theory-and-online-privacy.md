## Summary
Rigorous empirical study utilizing Integrative Social Contracts Theory (ISCT) to explain how perceived psychological contract breaches by social platforms trigger privacy-protective countermeasures by users.

## Analytical Overview
Applies macrosocial and microsocial contract principles to evaluate how individuals navigate opaque digital terms of service. Demonstrates that when platforms covertly commercialize user biometric or behavioral telemetry, users experience a moral contract breach, leading to deliberate obfuscation, ad-blocking, multi-accounting, and network withdrawal.
