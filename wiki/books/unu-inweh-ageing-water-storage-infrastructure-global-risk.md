## Executive Summary

Published by the United Nations University Institute for Water, Environment and Health (UNU-INWEH), this 36-page landmark global risk report identifies and quantifies the escalating planetary hazard of aging large water storage dams. Authored by Duminda Perera, Vladimir Smakhtin, and international dam safety experts, the report reveals that the vast majority of the world's 58,700 large dams were constructed between 1930 and 1970 with a design life of 50 to 100 years, meaning tens of thousands are now operating well beyond their planned structural lifespans.

The report examines the structural degradation mechanisms of aging concrete and embankment dams—including alkali-aggregate reactions, internal core erosion, spillway cavitation, foundation leaching, and severe reservoir sedimentation that drastically reduces storage capacity. It highlights how climate change accelerates rainfall extremes and peak flood inflows beyond historical design baselines, heightening the risk of catastrophic dam failure for hundreds of millions of people living downstream in the US, China, India, Japan, and Mediterranean nations.

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Global Dam Ageing Demographics**: Documents that by 2050, most humans will live downstream of thousands of large dams constructed in the 20th century operating at or beyond their design limits.
- **Structural Degradation Mechanisms**: Details alkali-silica reactivity (ASR), concrete micro-cracking, loss of foundation shear strength, and the silting up of reservoirs which compromises flood control buffer capacity.
- **Climate Baseline Drift**: Demonstrates that historical Probable Maximum Precipitation (PMP) baselines used in 20th-century dam engineering are invalidated by 21st-century climate change-induced atmospheric river storms.
- **Decommissioning Economics**: Formulates international policy frameworks for the controversial, costly, and technically complex process of dam decommissioning, sediment release management, and riverine ecological restoration.
