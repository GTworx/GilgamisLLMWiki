## Executive Summary

This consolidated compendium brings together **PitchBook's annual U.S. and European Private Equity Outlooks**, Middle-Market transaction surveys, and **Affinity's 2025 Private Equity Benchmark Report** to provide an operational and transactional blueprint of buyouts across the transatlantic corridor. Analyzing deal pacing, EV/EBITDA acquisition multiples, debt-to-equity ratios, and add-on acquisition strategies, the reports reveal how mid-market sponsors utilized 'buy-and-build' rollups to blend down elevated entry multiples. Concurrently, Affinity's relationship intelligence data tracks how top-performing deal teams shifted from cold outbound sourcing to relationship-driven proprietary deal pipelines to source resilient industrial, software, and healthcare platforms.

---

## Key Strategic & Financial Takeaways

- **Transatlantic Buyout Multiple Compression**: Median EV/EBITDA purchase multiples contracted from peaks of 13.5x–14.5x down to 11.0x–12.0x in the middle market, driven by higher base rates and more conservative leverage packages (debt contributions shrinking from 60% of enterprise value to 45%–50%).
- **The Buy-and-Build Add-On Engine**: Add-on acquisitions represented over 72% of all U.S. private equity buyout activity—a record high. Sponsors acquired smaller bolt-ons at 6x–8x EBITDA to integrate into a larger platform company valued at 12x+, manufacturing multiple arbitrage without relying on market-wide re-rating.
- **European Private Capital Dynamics**: European PE faced unique headwinds from higher energy costs, German industrial stagnation, and fragmented regulatory environments (CSRD reporting), but demonstrated surprising resilience in UK and Nordic technology and B2B services buyouts.
- **Middle-Market Resilience**: The core middle market ($100M–$500M enterprise value) outperformed mega-cap buyouts in deal volume, supported by regional private debt funds and lower reliance on syndicated broadly syndicated loan (BSL) markets.
- **Dealmaker Relationship Intelligence (Affinity Benchmark)**: Data from 200+ PE firms proves that proprietary deal flow generated via warm executive and advisory networks closed at 3x the rate of competitive bank-run auctions, leading firms to adopt relationship CRM intelligence to systematically map target founders.
