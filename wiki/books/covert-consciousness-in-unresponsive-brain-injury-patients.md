## Overview
Published in Nature Medicine (2025), this breakthrough clinical paper demonstrates how high-speed computer vision and deep learning facial landmark tracking can detect imperceptible, sub-millimeter voluntary micro-movements in patients diagnosed as comatose or vegetative. The technique uncovers 'covert consciousness' (cognitive motor dissociation) at the bedside without expensive fMRI scanners.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Deploys micro-expression computer vision algorithms detecting intentional motor commands invisible to the human eye.
- **Key Finding**: Identifies covert awareness in ~15-20% of seemingly vegetative patients, opening channels for two-way communication.
- **Key Finding**: Provides a portable, inexpensive bedside diagnostic system transforming neurocritical care and end-of-life bioethics.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Sub-millimeter facial micro-movements, imperceptible to expert bedside clinicians but captured by computer vision, reveal that patients locked inside unresponsive bodies are consciously listening and attempting to answer."
