## Analytical Overview
### Algorithmic Prediction of Venture Capital Outcomes

*Understanding Tech Startups' Health: The Mosaic Score Algorithm for Identifying Future Unicorns*, published by CB Insights, documents the computational and predictive architecture behind the **Mosaic Score**—an algorithmic framework funded in part by the National Science Foundation (NSF) to quantitatively evaluate the health and future growth probability of private, venture-backed companies.

### The Four Pillars of the Mosaic Engine

Unlike traditional credit scoring models that rely purely on historical accounting data, Mosaic evaluates four dynamic data pillars:
1. **Momentum (M):** Digital footprint expansion, customer sentiment signals, web traffic growth velocity, and media mindshare.
2. **Market (MK):** The overall health, funding velocity, and TAM expansion of the target industry sector.
3. **Money (MN):** Financial runway, burn rate sustainability, round frequency, and the historical prestige and track record of participating venture investors.
4. **Management (MG):** Founder pedigree, previous successful exits, network centrality, and executive hiring/turnover velocity.
