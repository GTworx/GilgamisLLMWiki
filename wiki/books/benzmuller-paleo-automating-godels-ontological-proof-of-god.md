## Summary
Breakthrough computational metaphysics paper successfully formalizing and verifying Kurt Gödel's modal ontological argument for the existence of God using automated theorem provers (LEO-II, Satallax, Isabelle/HOL).

## Analytical Overview
Translates Gödel's higher-order modal logic axioms (Dana Scott's version) into machine-checkable formal logic. Demonstrates that Gödel's axioms are logically consistent, confirming that the existence of a God-like being follows necessarily from the premises in modal logic S5, while discovering an unexpected modal collapse theorem that eliminates contingency.
