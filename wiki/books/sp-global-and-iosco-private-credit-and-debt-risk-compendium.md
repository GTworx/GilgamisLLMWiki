## Summary
Critical 110-page regulatory and credit analysis from S&P Global and IOSCO investigating the explosive expansion of direct lending ($1.7T private debt), opaque valuations, leverage layering, and corporate default risks.

## Analytical Overview
Details the structural migration of corporate leverage from public bond markets and syndicated leveraged loans into lightly regulated private credit funds. Examines synthetic leverage, payment-in-kind (PIK) interest toggles, lack of secondary market price discovery, and interconnectivity between alternative asset managers (such as Blackstone) and retail insurance annuity balance sheets.
