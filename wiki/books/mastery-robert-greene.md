## Executive Summary

Moving from external power dynamics to internal creative excellence, **Robert Greene's** *Mastery* (2012, Viking, pp. 3–7) examines the biographies of iconic historical masters (Leonardo da Vinci, Charles Darwin, Michael Faraday, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Benjamin Franklin, Marcel Proust) alongside interviews with nine contemporary leaders (including Temple Grandin, Yoky Matsuoka, Santiago Calatrava, and Paul Graham) to argue that supreme genius is not a magical genetic accident—it is the predictable harvest of a disciplined, multi-stage **apprenticeship** accessible to anyone willing to align deep curiosity with years of focused practice.

---

## Six-Stage Path to Mastery

1. **Discover Your Calling (Life's Task)**: Reconnect with childhood primal inclinations rather than conforming to status-chasing parental or peer scripts.
2. **Submit to Reality (The Ideal Apprenticeship)**: Prioritize skill acquisition over early salary across three phases—deep observation, skill acquisition, and active experimentation.
3. **Absorb the Master's Power**: Seek demanding mentors who accelerate feedback loops, then boldly outgrow them.
4. **Social Intelligence**: Replace naive idealism with realistic reading of organizational politics and human motives.
5. **Awaken the Dimensional Mind & Fuse Intuition with Rationality**: After internalizing foundational rules, break rigid convention through creative synthesis until mastery becomes effortless intuition.
