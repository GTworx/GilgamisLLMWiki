## Summary
Historical and legal dossier tracing the political downfall, international criminal trials at the Hague (ICTY), and strategic failure of Slobodan Milošević's ultra-nationalist regime following the 1999 Kosovo War.

## Analytical Overview
### The Autopsy of Greater Serbian Ultra-Nationalism

*Balkan Nationalist Collapse: Slobodan Milošević, Serbian Leadership, and the Legacy of the Kosovo War* delivers a comprehensive historical and legal autopsy of the ultra-nationalist political project that precipitated the bloody disintegration of Yugoslavia in the 1990s.

### The Political Fate of the Belgrade Leadership

The dossier tracks the accountability of Serbian political and military elites:
- **The Fall of Slobodan Milošević:** How fraudulent elections and the popular Otpor revolution of October 2000 toppled Milošević, culminating in his dramatic extradition to the International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia (ICTY) in The Hague, where he died in 2006 during his genocide trial.
- **Convictions of Political & Military Brass:** The trial verdicts of Radovan Karadžić, Ratko Mladić, Nikola Šainović, and General Nebojša Pavković for crimes against humanity and ethnic cleansing in Bosnia and Kosovo.
- **The Catastrophic Cost of Ethnic Chauvinism:** Demonstrating how aggressive ethno-nationalism isolated Serbia internationally, destroyed its economy through hyperinflation, and permanently severed Kosovo from Belgrade.

### Lessons in International Accountability

The text serves as a definitive case study in transitional justice and the catastrophic failure of revanchist nationalism.
