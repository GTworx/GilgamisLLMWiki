Investigative counter-intelligence dossier analyzing declassified FBI Section 265 investigative files, East Rutherford police reports, and detainee depositions regarding the five Israeli nationals arrested in New Jersey on September 11, 2001.

## Analytical Overview
### Declassified FBI Records and Counter-Intelligence Telemetry

*Declassified 9/11 Counter-Intelligence: The Urban Moving Systems Detentions and Foreign Intelligence Operations* compiles exhaustive primary documentation—including declassified FBI Foreign Counterintelligence (FCI) case files, witness statements, and municipal police arrest reports—concerning one of the most controversial incidents of September 11, 2001.

### The Verified Documentary Record

The dossier synthesizes verified facts established by declassified federal records:
- On the morning of September 11, 2001, five Israeli nationals were observed on the roof of a commercial van in Liberty State Park and arrested by police in East Rutherford, New Jersey.
- **The FBI Investigation:** FBI counter-intelligence conducted extensive polygraphs and forensic examinations of their employer, Urban Moving Systems (a commercial moving company whose owner abruptly fled the U.S. for Israel).
- **The Intelligence Assessment:** While the FBI concluded that the individuals had no foreknowledge of or involvement in the al-Qaeda terrorist attacks, subsequent counter-intelligence disclosures indicated that the moving company functioned as a surveillance front tracking Middle Eastern financial and charitable networks in Northern New Jersey.

### Historiographical Clarity

The dossier cuts through conspiratorial internet myths by providing the exact declassified documentary transcripts and diplomatic deportation negotiations.
