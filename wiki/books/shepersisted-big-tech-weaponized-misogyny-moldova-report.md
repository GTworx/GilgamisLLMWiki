## Analytical Overview
### Digital Information Warfare on the Eastern European Frontier

*Big Tech and the Weaponization of Misogyny in Moldova's Online Ecosystem*, published by the She Persisted Global Initiative (2024, 29 pp.), provides an investigative case study into how state-sponsored computational propaganda weaponizes misogyny to undermine democratic institutions in frontline European states.

### Anatomy of Gendered Disinformation

The report documents the tactics deployed in Moldova:
- **Targeting Female Leadership:** Russian-aligned troll farms, Telegram channels, and automated bot networks systematically deploy deepfakes, sexualized smears, and fabricated corruption claims against Moldovan President Maia Sandu and female pro-European reform leaders.
- **Algorithmic Amplification by Big Tech:** Exposing how Meta (Facebook) and TikTok's recommendation algorithms actively prioritized and monetized inflammatory, fraudulent attack advertisements because outrage drives user engagement and ad impressions.
- **Democratic Subversion:** Analyzing how covert foreign financing exploits social media platform neglect in non-English-speaking frontline states to swing referendums and destabilize democratic sovereignty.
