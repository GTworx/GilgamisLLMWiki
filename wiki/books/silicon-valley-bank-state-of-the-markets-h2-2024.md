## Analytical Overview
### The Realities of Venture Capital in a Normalized Interest Rate Regime

*SVB State of the Markets H2 2024*, authored by the research group at Silicon Valley Bank, provides a comprehensive macroeconomic and financial diagnostic of the global innovation economy following two years of painful valuation recalibration. Published in the wake of the 2023 regional banking crisis, the report highlights the remarkable systemic resilience of the venture ecosystem as it establishes a new equilibrium.

### Definitive Private Market Data

The report delivers granular benchmarks across critical financing metrics:
- **The Divergence in Deal Flow:** Late-stage growth financing remains highly selective and concentrated in AI and defense tech, while early-stage seed and Series A rounds have stabilized at rational, pre-2020 valuation multiples.
- **The Ascendance of Venture Debt:** As equity dilution becomes costly, founders and boards increasingly utilize structured venture debt, recurring-revenue lines of credit, and mezzanine facilities to extend runway.
- **Secondary Market Liquidity:** The rapid expansion of structured tender offers, GP-led continuation funds, and LP secondary transactions providing liquidity in the absence of a public IPO window.

### Guidance for Founders and Institutional Allocators

The report outlines strategic recommendations for capital preservation, customer unit economics, and governance alignment.
