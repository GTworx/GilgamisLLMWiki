## Executive Summary

*How Crypto & NFT Philanthropy Helped the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention Diversify Its Revenue Streams* (published by [[the-giving-block]], October 2022, 1 p.) is a concise nonprofit-innovation case study documenting how the **American Foundation for Suicide Prevention (AFSP)**—founded in 1987 with 74 local chapters across all 50 U.S. states—adopted cryptocurrency and NFT donation rails in 2021 to tap new Web3 communities for mental-health research and suicide prevention (source: BOOKS/Anthropology_/CASE STUDY-TGB-AFSP.pdf, p. 1).

Prompted by an inbound major crypto donor whom the foundation initially lacked technical infrastructure to serve, AFSP onboarded with The Giving Block and raised **$400,000 in cryptocurrency during 2021**—anchored by a nearly **$221,000 gift from Trippy Bunny's NFT mint proceeds** announced on Suicide Prevention Day—illustrating how digital subcultures and blockchain payment rails expand institutional philanthropy beyond traditional donor demographics (source: BOOKS/Anthropology_/CASE STUDY-TGB-AFSP.pdf, p. 1).
