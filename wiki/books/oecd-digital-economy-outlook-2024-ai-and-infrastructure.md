## Summary
Authoritative OECD flagship report surveying broadband connectivity, high-performance computing, generative AI infrastructure investments, and international digital governance principles.

## Analytical Overview
Details the exponential surge in datacenter power consumption, subsea cable fiber investments, and the global concentration of foundational AI compute among hyperscalers. Evaluates digital divide metrics across OECD member states, establishing international policy benchmarks for algorithmic transparency, consumer data portability, and ethical AI deployment.
