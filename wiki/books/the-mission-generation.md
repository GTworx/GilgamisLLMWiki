## Executive Summary

Co-authored by venture investor/educator **Arun Gupta** and researcher **Thomas J. Fewer** (Wiley, 2026, Title Page p. 7; 273 pp.), *The Mission Generation* maps how professionals and emerging leaders can navigate "perpetual disruption"—defined by five converging shocks: Pandemic Shock, Artificial Intelligence, Geopolitical Instability, Environmental Breakdown, and the Collapse of Trust (Ch. 2, pp. 34–37). When traditional corporate ladders and "the old bargain" dissolve, Gupta and Fewer argue that **mission replaces stability as the primary career compass** (Ch. 1–2, pp. 19–49).

Rejecting the false dichotomy between purpose and financial independence ("The Myth of the Either/Or Career", Ch. 4, p. 75), the book introduces a holistic portfolio model—the **Six Compass Capitals**—showing how professionals convert resistance into conviction and compound long-term impact through a repeatable **Mission Flywheel** (Ch. 7, p. 127).

---

## Core Frameworks & Insights

1. **Four Modes of Resistance to Purpose (Ch. 3, pp. 53–71)**: Diagnoses *Psychological* (inner saboteur), *Relational* (well-meaning loved ones), *Cultural* (unquestioned prestige tracks), and *Systemic* resistance that keep talent trapped in misaligned careers.
2. **The Six Compass Capitals (Ch. 4–6, pp. 73–125)**: Balances *Financial Capital* (engine of autonomy) and *Mission Capital* with four hidden compounding assets: *Trust Capital*, *Health Capital*, *Learning Capital*, and *Experience Capital*.
3. **The Mission Flywheel (Ch. 7, pp. 127–135)**: Reframing past adversity, running small mission experiments, and integrating all six capitals so breakthroughs emerge from "a thousand small ones."
