## Executive Summary

Prepared by retired military historian and Ankara University lecturer **Col. Ali Mithat İnan** (b. 1940; originally his 1987 Ankara University Turkish Revolution History Institute master's thesis, first published 1996 by Gündoğan Yayınları, pp. 5–8; 309 pp.), *Atatürk'ün Not Defterleri* transcribes, catalogs, and analyzes **Mustafa Kemal Atatürk's personal pocket notebooks** spanning his Ottoman military campaigns, Karlsbad diaries, War of Independence field inspections (e.g., March 1922 Akşehir front notes, p. 155), and early Republican planning.

Structured into two major parts plus documentary appendices (**Part I**: Source evaluation and notebook-by-notebook presentation, pp. 34–174; **Part II**: Thematic content analysis, personality traits revealed in private entries, and historical significance, pp. 175–218), the volume gives historians unfiltered access to Atatürk's private working mind—far removed from ceremonial rhetoric.

---

## Core Archival Insights

1. **Tactical Precision & Daily Field Discipline (Part I, pp. 43–174)**: Reveals terse, methodical entries recording troop inspections, armistice diplomacy, artillery logistics, and reading notes on philosophy, French literature, and European politics.
2. **Private Psychology & Self-Interrogation (Part II, pp. 186–209)**: Shows how Atatürk used personal notebooks as an instrument of self-discipline—rehearsing bold reforms (including women's rights and social modernization) in private years before enacting them publicly.
3. **Methodological Historiography**: Models rigorous source criticism by distinguishing diary jottings, draft speeches, and operational memoranda within the Genelkurmay ATASE and Anıtkabir archives.
