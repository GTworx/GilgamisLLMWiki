## Summary
Historic 969-page master dataset of World Values Survey Wave 7 (2017–2022) covering 80+ countries, documenting longitudinal shifts between Traditional vs. Secular-Rational values and Survival vs. Self-Expression values.

## Analytical Overview
Presents exhaustive country-by-country percentage cross-tabulations on democratic legitimacy, gender equality, trust in institutions, environmental attitudes, subjective well-being, and social tolerance. Serves as the global gold standard for mapping Inglehart-Welzel cultural coordinates across human civilizations.
