## Summary
Quantitative demographic and retail benchmark charting Generation Z's $12 trillion lifetime purchasing power, budget prioritization, brand loyalty erosion, and financial habits.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes how economic headwinds, student debt, and soaring living costs have created a hyper-pragmatic Gen Z consumer. Documents their preference for peer recommendations over celebrity endorsements, the rapid adoption of alternative payment rails (BNPL), and the blending of physical and digital retail channels.
