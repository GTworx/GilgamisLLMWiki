## Executive Summary

Promulgated in the Official Gazette No. 30364 and enacted on January 1, 2019, this 416-page legal and engineering code represents the state-of-the-art building seismic standard of the Republic of Türkiye (TBDY 2018). Developed through a multi-year national consensus committee comprising leading structural engineering scholars and AFAD experts, TBDY 2018 replaced the 2007 earthquake code, introducing revolutionary paradigm shifts in seismic hazard definition and structural design methodology.

The code abandoned traditional monolithic earthquake zones (Zones 1 through 4) in favor of location-specific, coordinate-based seismic hazard maps based on 3D probabilistic ground motion calculations. It introduced rigorous performance-based design criteria across four distinct earthquake hazard levels (DD-1 to DD-4), comprehensive non-linear time-history analysis requirements, detailed provisions for seismic base isolation, tall buildings, cold-formed steel structures, and non-structural architectural/mechanical components.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Transition to Site-Specific Seismic Hazard**: Adopts coordinate-based spectral response accelerations ($S_S$, $S_1$) computed from the interactive Turkish Earthquake Hazard Map, accounting for local soil classes (ZA to ZF) and non-linear site coefficients.
- **Performance-Based Design Philosophy**: Formulates distinct performance targets—Immediate Occupancy (KK), Life Safety (CG), and Collapse Prevention (GC)—tailored to structural importance classes and hazard exceedance probabilities (43-year to 2475-year return periods).
- **Non-Linear Analysis and Seismic Isolation**: Establishes mandatory dynamic non-linear response-history analysis procedures for tall buildings ($H_N > 70m$) and formalizes regulatory design requirements for elastomeric and friction-pendulum seismic isolation systems.
- **Ductility and Detailing Rigor**: Codifies stringent confinement reinforcement, joint core shear verifications, capacity design principles, and strong-column/weak-beam checks to prevent brittle shear failures.

## Notable Excerpts & Citations

- "TBDY 2018 completely replaces obsolete zonal approximations with precise, geographical-coordinate spectral acceleration parameters reflecting the true probabilistic ground motion hazard of the Anatolian fault networks."
