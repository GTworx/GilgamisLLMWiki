## Summary
Monumental 419-page empirical monograph establishing a comprehensive global index across 174 countries, proving a decisive causal correlation between economic, political, and legal freedom and broad-based societal prosperity.

## Analytical Overview
Constructs two robust multidimensional indexes: the Freedom Index (measuring legal security, property rights, political elections, and civil liberties) and the Prosperity Index (measuring income, health, education, environment, and minority rights). Demonstrates through cross-country regression models that institutional freedom is the primary long-term determinant of durable wealth generation.
