## Summary
Master empirical dossier on the geographical distribution and localized mechanics of US venture capital. Features PitchBook's Venture Ecosystem FactBooks across Austin, Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, New York, and Seattle, Jeffrey Bussgang's Boston Startup Scene analysis, MIT's 'Innovation Orchards' concept, the Texas Rising Tide report, 1776's Innovation That Matters, and Carta's State of Startups 2023.

## Analytical Overview
This volume provides a granular metropolitan and regional breakdown of venture capital allocation, founder networks, and regional specialization across the United States.

Key analytical components:
1. **PitchBook Metropolitan FactBooks**: Compiles data-driven city profiles across Boston (biotech/deep tech), Austin (software migration/lifestyle hub), Chicago (fintech/logistics), Los Angeles (media/aerospace), New York (SaaS/fintech), and Seattle (cloud/enterprise AI), analyzing round sizes, valuation medians, angel participation, and corporate venture activity.
2. **Boston & the 'Innovation Orchard' Paradigm**: Features Jeffrey Bussgang’s Boston ecosystem diagnostics and MIT’s framework of *Innovation Orchards*—places where deep-tech startups scaling capital-intensive physical technologies (fusion, robotics, biomanufacturing) access shared wet labs, specialized machine tooling, and non-dilutive grant funding.
3. **Sunbelt Realignment & Carta Benchmarks**: Incorporates *The Texas Rising Tide* documenting the migration of tech talent and headquarters to Austin and Dallas, contrasted with California policy defenses, alongside Carta's 2023 *State of Startups* tracking employee option exercise behavior, secondary liquidity discounts, and founder equity dilution.
