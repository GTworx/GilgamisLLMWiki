## Summary
Six-part financial and macroeconomic compendium analyzing Turkey's post-May 2023 transition to monetary orthodoxy, aggressive policy rate hikes by the TCMB (to 50%), local currency bond market foreign inflows, and current account adjustment.

## Analytical Overview
Chronicles the dramatic pivot in Turkish macroeconomic policy under Mehmet Şimşek and the new TCMB leadership. Synthesizes JPMorgan and Ashmore analyses detailing the dismantling of foreign exchange-protected deposit schemes (KKM), the rebuilding of central bank net foreign exchange reserves, sovereign credit rating upgrades by major agencies, and structural methodologies for tracking Turkish balance-of-payments data.
