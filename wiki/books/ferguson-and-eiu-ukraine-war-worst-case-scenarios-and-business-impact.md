## Executive Summary
This high-impact geopolitical and economic dossier combines historian Niall Ferguson's provocative *7 Worst-Case Scenarios From Putin's Ukraine War* (Bloomberg) with the Economist Intelligence Unit's (EIU) strategic report on how the conflict fundamentally alters global business. Ferguson models dangerous escalation pathways—including tactical nuclear use, protracted war of attrition, NATO direct intervention, and Chinese economic leverage. The EIU analyzes structural shifts in global trade: supply chain fragmentation, permanent European energy decoupling from Russian hydrocarbons, food insecurity across the Global South, and the weaponization of financial clearing systems (SWIFT).

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Ferguson's Seven Escalation Trajectories**: Details systemic risks ranging from protracted multi-year trench attrition and cyber infrastructure decapitation to direct Russia-NATO skirmishes in the Baltic and Black Seas.
- **The End of Peacetime Globalization**: The conflict finalized the demise of post-1989 'Chimerica' globalization, accelerating friend-shoring and sovereign supply chain self-sufficiency.
- **Weaponized Interdependence**: Western asset freezes and financial sanctions demonstrated that global dollar reserves and SWIFT messaging networks are geopolitical weapons, accelerating non-Western interest in de-dollarization.
- **Commodity and Fertilizer Shocks**: Examines how disruptions in Black Sea grain corridors and potash exports triggered cascading food inflation and political instability across North Africa and the Levant.
