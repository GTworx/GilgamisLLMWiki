## Analytical Overview
### The Structural Trajectory of the Pax Americana

*Comparative Analysis: U.S. Global Business Decline, Hegemonic Overstretch, and Future Multipolar Scenarios* synthesizes historical hegemonic transition theories (Paul Kennedy, Robert Gilpin, Giovanni Arrighi) to evaluate the contemporary trajectory of the United States.

### Symptoms of Hegemonic Maturation

The treatise analyzes core structural vulnerabilities:
- **Imperial Overstretch:** Operating over 750 foreign military bases while domestic physical infrastructure and educational foundations suffer acute deficits.
- **De-Industrialization & Financialization:** The migration of national capital from physical manufacturing to speculative financial engineering, leaving the defense industrial base reliant on foreign supply chains.
- **De-Dollarization & Weaponized Interdependence:** How aggressive unilateral sanctions have accelerated alternative international payment rails (BRICS cross-border currency mechanisms, digital yuan).

### Future Multipolar Scenarios

The study models three alternative world orders: chaotic multipolarity, regional spheres of influence, or catastrophic hegemonic war.
