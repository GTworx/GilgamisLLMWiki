## Summary
Three-part quantitative wealth preservation study modeling decile-by-decile real portfolio returns under 60%+ inflation across cash, gold, FX, equities, and housing, combined with Thomson Reuters private equity legal guides and national construction sector analytics.

## Analytical Overview
Simulates 1-year real purchasing power outcomes across Turkish wealth deciles, proving that cash and unhedged deposits suffer catastrophic real capital destruction, while diversified holdings in physical gold, foreign currency, BIST equities, and residential real estate achieve positive real net returns. Features Thomson Reuters' comprehensive regulatory guide on Turkish private equity funds (GSYO/GSYF) and Turkish Contractors Association construction indices.
