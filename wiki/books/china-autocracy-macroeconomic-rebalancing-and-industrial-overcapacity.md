## Summary
Comprehensive 7-source compendium investigating China's property deflation, local government debt, domestic consumption deficits, industrial overcapacity dumping (EVs, batteries, solar), and macroeconomic stimulus maneuvers.

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes NBER working papers and World Bank updates to examine China's economic crossroads. Details how an over-reliance on supply-side industrial subsidies generates massive global export frictions and deflationary price wars, analyzes the Nomura 'bazooka stimulus' market reactions, and contrasts China's centralized manufacturing scale with India's persistent infrastructure constraints.
