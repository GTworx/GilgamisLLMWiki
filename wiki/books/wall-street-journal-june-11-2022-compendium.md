## Summary
Weekend edition of The Wall Street Journal documenting the historic June 2022 US market selloff, tech sector workforce contractions, and the unraveling of algorithmic stablecoins.

## Analytical Overview
### Chronicle of the 2022 Financial Reversal

The June 11-12, 2022 weekend edition of *The Wall Street Journal* (Vol. CCLXXIX, No. 135) stands as an essential primary document of the rapid unwinding of pandemic-era financial excesses.

### Front-Page Investigative Inquiries

The edition covers:
- The fallout from the blistering 8.6% consumer price inflation print, sending the S&P 500 into bear market territory and triggering sharp rate-hike repricing.
- The sudden end of the Silicon Valley hiring boom, detailing the first major waves of corporate layoffs across Meta, Amazon, and venture-backed tech startups.
- The implosion of the Terra-Luna algorithmic stablecoin ecosystem and the cascading margin calls across crypto hedge funds (Three Arrows Capital, Celsius).
- Corporate supply-chain bottlenecks and diesel fuel price spikes threatening US logistics networks.

### Economic Historical Archive

The issue provides an unvarnished snapshot of the psychological inflection point when corporate America transitioned from growth-at-all-costs to margin defense and cash preservation.
