## Summary
Annual regional impact report detailing the incubation, capital access, mentorship, and economic growth delivered by the Dallas Entrepreneur Center across the North Texas startup ecosystem.

## Analytical Overview
### Regional Ecosystem Acceleration in North Texas

*The DEC Network Impact Report*, published by the Dallas Entrepreneur Center (The DEC Network), provides a detailed examination of regional entrepreneurial ecosystem building in North Texas. As metropolitan areas outside Silicon Valley and New York aggressively compete to foster high-growth technology clusters, regional non-profit accelerators and community hubs play an indispensable role in bridging resource gaps.

### Key Operational Benchmarks

The report details the tangible metrics of regional acceleration:
- Providing physical co-working hubs, structured incubator programs, and mentor networks for thousands of local entrepreneurs.
- Facilitating direct capital connections, pitch competitions, and angel investor showcases for historically underserved minority and female founders.
- Tracking cumulative regional job creation, follow-on venture capital funding, and commercial lease expansions across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

### Lessons for Urban Innovation Policy

The document highlights effective public-private partnership models that link corporate sponsors, municipal governments, and grassroots founders into a thriving regional network.
