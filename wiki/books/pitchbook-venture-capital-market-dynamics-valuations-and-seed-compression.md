## Summary
Master empirical and quantitative compendium on venture capital market corrections, valuation reset dynamics, and fund management math. Features PitchBook's US Venture Capital Outlooks (2024–2025), US VC Valuations and Returns Reports, 'Seed Under Pressure' analyst notes, Quantitative Perspectives ('Turning the Tide' and 'Balancing Act'), 'The Big Book of Venture Capital 2023', and modern VC portfolio construction theory.

## Analytical Overview
This volume documents the great venture capital valuation recalibration following the peak bubble year of 2021, providing quantitative depth on modern tech financing.

Key analytical components:
1. **Valuation Resets & Down-Round Dynamics**: Features PitchBook’s 2024–2025 *US VC Valuations and Returns Reports*, charting the collapse of revenue multiples from 40x+ to historical means (8–12x) and the surge in structured deal terms (senior liquidation preferences, pay-to-play clauses, warrant coverage) utilized to preserve optical valuations.
2. **Seed Under Pressure & Early-Stage Realignment**: Analyzes PitchBook’s *Seed Under Pressure* note, detailing how late-stage caution migrated upstream, causing seed round sizes to plateau, diligence timelines to extend from days to months, and graduation rates from Seed to Series A to drop to historical lows.
3. **VC Portfolio Construction Mathematics**: Synthesizes *The Big Book of Venture Capital 2023* and quantitative portfolio management treatises, demonstrating power-law return distributions, fund reserve allocation strategies, follow-on check sizing, and capital pacing across multiple market vintages.
