## Summary
Philological analysis of 11th-century Karakhanid legal contracts, land grants, and multilingual administrative documents from Yarkand, alongside Silk Road Buddhist text transitions and the status of Semu-ren in the Yuan Empire.

## Analytical Overview
Erdal provides line-by-line transcription, translation, and grammatical analysis of the Yarkand Arabic-script Old Turkic legal deeds. Shows how early Islamic Karakhanid legal institutions incorporated pre-Islamic nomadic administrative terminology, while companion papers analyze Bactrian/Serindian linguistic transfer and Central Asian bureaucratic status under Mongol rule.
