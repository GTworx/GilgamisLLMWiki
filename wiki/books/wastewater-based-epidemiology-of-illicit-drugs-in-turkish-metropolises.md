## Executive Summary
Published between 2018 and 2021 across *Water Research*, *Science of the Total Environment*, and *Environmental Science and Pollution Research* by pioneer forensic toxicologists (Prof. Dr. Faruk Aşıcıoğlu, Prof. Dr. Nebile Dağlıoğlu, Dr. Selda Mercan), this monumental four-study compendium establishes Wastewater-Based Epidemiology (WBE) across Türkiye. Analyzing composite wastewater samples from 14 municipal treatment plants in Istanbul and across 11 major Turkish cities (representing over 25 million people), the authors quantify real-time community consumption rates of illicit drugs (cannabis, cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, MDMA/ecstasy), alcohol, and tobacco. The studies expose alarming surges in crystalline methamphetamine consumption across western Anatolia, providing objective biomarker data independent of police seizure biases.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Wastewater Biomarker Methodology**: Liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) measures excreted parent drug molecules and human urinary metabolites (e.g., benzoylecgonine for cocaine, morphine for heroin), normalizing by daily wastewater flow and population size.
- **The Rise of Methamphetamine in Türkiye**: Documents a dramatic epidemiological shift from traditional opiates toward cheap, potent synthetic methamphetamine, with consumption rates in Istanbul and Adana climbing to European top-tier levels.
- **Cannabis and Alcohol Dominance**: Cannabis (THC-COOH) and alcohol represent the highest mass consumption loads, with alcohol and MDMA showing statistically significant spikes during weekend nights.
- **Real-Time Public Policy Telemetry**: Proves that wastewater monitoring provides municipal health and narcotics enforcement authorities with objective, real-time consumption trends months before survey data or hospital admissions.
