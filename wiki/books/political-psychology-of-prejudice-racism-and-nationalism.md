## Summary
Cognitive science and political theory compendium analyzing the cognitive biases underlying racism (availability heuristic, outgroup homogeneity), while demarcating the structural and philosophical boundary between civic nationalism and ethnic racism.

## Analytical Overview
### The Cognitive Architecture of Prejudice and National Identity

*Prejudice, Nationalism, and Systemic Racism: Cognitive Biases, Tribal Heuristics, and Civic Identity* unites two analytical treatises exploring the psychological roots and political philosophy of identity-based exclusion.

### Cognitive Foundations of Bigotry

The compendium examines:
- **Evolutionary Tribal Heuristics:** How human brains evolved under ancestral hunter-gatherer pressures to utilize quick, stereotypic heuristics (In-Group Favoritism, Out-Group Homogeneity Effect, Availability Cascade) that misfire in modern multi-ethnic societies.
- **Racism vs. Nationalism:** Constructing a rigorous philosophical demarcation between **Ethno-Racial Supremacism** (based on immutable biological determinism and inherent hierarchy) and **Civic Nationalism** (based on shared adherence to constitutional laws, public language, and democratic institutions, open to all citizens regardless of ethnic ancestry).

### Depolarization Protocols

The text provides educational and sociological interventions to dismantle cognitive prejudice.
