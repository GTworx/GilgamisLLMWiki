## Summary
Cutting-edge morphological and chronometric study analyzing ~300,000-year-old hominin cranial and dental specimens from Morocco, demonstrating pan-African polycentric origins for early Homo sapiens.

## Analytical Overview
Examines anatomical facial architecture, endocranial morphology, and Middle Stone Age lithic technology associated with early human fossils at Jebel Irhoud. Refutes strict single-region East African speciation models in favor of an interconnected, pan-African metapopulation framework characterized by episodic physical and cultural gene flow across Pleistocene green Sahara corridors.
