## Summary
Seven-part specialized sectoral compendium evaluating Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 sports entertainment investments (EY), Indian agricultural procurement dynamics, the European Central Bank's assessment of China's growth slowdown, and the gap between US economic statistics and voter perceptions.

## Analytical Overview
Details how Saudi Arabia deploys sovereign capital into sports megaprojects to diversify away from hydrocarbons and project soft power. Analyzes Indian agrarian supply chains and minimum support prices, the structural bottlenecks in China's consumption-to-investment ratio documented by the ECB, and the psychological mechanisms explaining why strong US macroeconomic data often fails to resonate with household consumer sentiment.
