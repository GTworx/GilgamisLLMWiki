## Summary
Bridgewater's proprietary quantitative index evaluating the structural health, competitive power, and long-range trajectories of the world's 24 leading economies across eight core drivers of national rise and decline.

## Analytical Overview
Applies Dalio's Big Cycle framework measuring education, innovation, economic output, share of world trade, military strength, financial center dominance, reserve currency status, and cost-competitiveness. Concludes that the United States remains the preeminent superpower but faces acute internal debt and political conflict risks, while China exhibits surging technological capacity alongside debt and demographic headwinds.
