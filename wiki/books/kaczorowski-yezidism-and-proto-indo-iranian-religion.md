## Overview
Published in Fritillaria Kurdica (Bulletin of Kurdish Studies) by Karol Kaczorowski, this 16-page religious history treatise investigates the archaic theological stratigraphy of Yezidism. Kaczorowski demonstrates that beneath medieval Sufi overlays (Sheikh Adi ibn Musafir), Yezidi cosmological doctrines—specifically the veneration of Melek Taus (the Peacock Angel), holy fire, and sacred oaths—preserve living direct survivals of Proto-Indo-Iranian religion and pre-Zoroastrian Mazdaism.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Traces Yezidi ritual practices directly to Bronze Age Proto-Indo-Iranian solar cosmologies.
- **Key Finding**: Deconstructs the demonic mischaracterization of Melek Taus by hostile neighboring Abrahamic orthodoxies.
- **Key Finding**: Documents the sacred geography of Lalish and oral hymnology (Qawls) as an irreplaceable ancient living archive.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Yezidism is not a late Islamic heresy; it is an extraordinary living fossil of ancient Indo-Iranian spirituality that survived centuries of violent persecution by retreating into sacred oral tradition."
