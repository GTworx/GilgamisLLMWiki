## Executive Summary

*Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization: How to Design, Build and Scale a Customer-Centric Sales Organization* (published by Winning by Design, San Francisco, 2nd/3rd Edition 2017 / © 2018, 196 pp., ISBN `978-1-387-02732-3`) by Winning by Design founder [[jacco-van-der-kooij]] and co-author [[fernando-pizarro]] is an engineering-grade operational blueprint for scaling recurring-revenue B2B sales organizations beyond Series A product-market fit (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, pp. 4–7). Diagnosing the **"Innovation Gap"**—the chasm between frictionless modern B2C buying experiences (instant pricing, peer reviews, online self-service) and century-old push-based B2B sales processes—the authors invert traditional sales management with a foundational axiom: *"The question to ask is not 'how are we going to sell it?' but rather 'how is the customer going to buy it?'"* (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, pp. 9, 32).

Across fourteen structured blueprint chapters, van der Kooij and Pizarro treat recurring-revenue growth as a **repeatable scientific system** built on unit economics (`CAC`, `LTV`, payback periods) and specialized pods: segmenting the business by ACV tiers (Ch. 1), mapping the buyer's end-to-end journey (Ch. 2), scaling SaaS sales costs sustainably (Ch. 3), aligning online inbound marketing, content fuel, and sales-enablement tooling as force multipliers (Ch. 4, 8–9), instituting data-driven funnel diagnostics and specialized team pods—SDRs, Account Executives, Customer Success Managers, and Account Managers—governed by a central **Sales Operations Center** and structured career ladders (Ch. 5–7, 10–14) (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, Contents, pp. 5, 19–186).

## Key Arguments / Chapter Breakdown

* **Chapters 1–3: Business Tiering, The Customer Journey & Scaling SaaS Economics (pp. 19–60)**:
  * *Aligning Go-to-Market Motion to ACV*: Matches sales complexity (self-service, transactional inside sales, enterprise field sales) to Annual Contract Value (ACV) tiers so Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) scales profitably relative to recurring Lifetime Value (LTV); maps buyer-initiated triggers across content marketing, referrals, outbound prospecting, SEO, and community touchpoints (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, Ch. 1–3, pp. 19–60).

* **Chapters 4–7: Online Buying, Evolution of Sales & Data-Driven SaaS Processes (pp. 61–104)**:
  * *Closing the Innovation Gap*: Traces the shift from legacy field-rep relationship selling and Solution Selling to transparent, buyer-empowered SaaS journeys; establishes quantitative conversion metrics across every funnel stage (awareness, education, selection, onboarding, expansion) to replace intuition with data-driven coaching (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, Ch. 4–7, pp. 61–104).

* **Chapters 8–10: Tools as Force Multipliers, Content as Fuel & The SaaS Sales Model (pp. 105–146)**:
  * *Revenue Factory Architecture*: Integrates CRM, sales-engagement automation, analytics, and contextual micro-content into a unified **SaaS Sales Model** where marketing, sales, and customer success share a single customer-impact vocabulary (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, Ch. 8–10, pp. 105–146).

* **Chapters 11–14: Structuring Pods, Career Development & Sales Operations Center (pp. 147–196)**:
  * *Specialized Pods & RevOps*: Replaces monolithic "full-cycle" reps with agile cross-functional pods (SDR + AE + CSM), constructs transparent skill-based career progressions to combat SaaS rep attrition, and institutes a **Sales Operations Center** as the analytical nerve center of the revenue engine (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, Ch. 11–14, pp. 147–186).
