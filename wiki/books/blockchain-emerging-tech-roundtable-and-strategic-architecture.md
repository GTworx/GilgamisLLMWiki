## Overview
Compiled from the Executive Emerging Tech Roundtable in Fairfield, CT, accompanied by rigorous architectural decision flowcharts ('Do You Need a Blockchain?'), this strategic dossier establishes enterprise criteria for adopting distributed ledgers. The authors strip away crypto-hype to evaluate genuine multi-party coordination problems where relational databases fall short.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Establishes a rigorous decision heuristic: identifying when multiple untrusted writers require consensus without a central intermediary.
- **Key Finding**: Compares permissionless public blockchains against permissioned enterprise consortium ledgers.
- **Key Finding**: Outlines governance and regulatory compliance frameworks for Fortune 500 corporate deployment.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "If you have a trusted third party, you do not need a blockchain; if all parties trust each other, use a database. Distributed ledgers are for adversarial coordination across institutional boundaries."
