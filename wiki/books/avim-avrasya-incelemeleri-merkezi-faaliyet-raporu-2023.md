## Analytical Overview
### Eurasian Strategic Intelligence from Ankara

The *AVİM Yıllık Raporu 2023* (Center for Eurasian Studies, 108 pp.) provides an authoritative strategic review of Turkish foreign policy and geopolitical developments across Eurasia in 2023, a year marked by historic shifts in regional alignments.

### Core Regional Focus Areas

The report provides granular analytical coverage across critical theatres:
- **The South Caucasus and Karabakh:** Analyzing the full restoration of Azerbaijani territorial sovereignty over Karabakh in September 2023, the dissolution of unrecognized separatist structures, and the diplomatic mechanics of opening the Zangezur transport corridor.
- **The Middle Corridor (TITR):** The expansion of the Trans-Caspian International Transport Route linking China to Europe via Central Asia and Turkey, bypassing Russian northern railways.
- **Balkan Fragility and NATO Expansion:** Monitoring inter-ethnic tensions in Northern Kosovo and institutional stalemates in Bosnia and Herzegovina.
- **Historical Controversies & International Law:** Defending Turkish diplomatic archives against politicized historical allegations in Western legislatures.

### Strategic Resource for Diplomatic Historians

The volume preserves the analytical output of Turkey's foremost Eurasian strategic research scholars.
