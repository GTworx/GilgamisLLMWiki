## Overview
Authored by Grammy-nominated composer, musicologist, and multi-instrumentalist Mehmet Ali Sanlıkol (New England Conservatory), this scholarly ethnomusicological essay explores Mantle Hood's concept of 'Bi-Musicality' in the 21st century. Sanlıkol dissects the complex microtonal, melodic, and philosophical synthesis between classical Ottoman Turkish Makam traditions and African-American modern jazz.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Articulates a rigorous theoretical model for reconciling non-tempered microtonal Makam scales with Western harmonic jazz progressions.
- **Key Finding**: Critiques superficial 'world music' fusion, arguing for deep structural bilingualism in both compositional systems.
- **Key Finding**: Demonstrates the intercultural historical lineage connecting Mediterranean modal improvisations with American modal jazz (Miles Davis, Coltrane).

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Genuine bi-musicality is not pasting an exotic ornament over a Western rhythm; it is internalizing two distinct sonic cosmologies so deeply that a new, authentic musical grammar is born."
