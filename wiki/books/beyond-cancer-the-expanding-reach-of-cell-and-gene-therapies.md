## Executive Summary

*Beyond Cancer: The Expanding Reach of Cell and Gene Therapies* (published by STAT News, September 2026, 73 pp.) by [[stat-news-editorial]]—featuring investigative biomedical journalism by Isabella Cueto, Megan Molteni, Paul Knoepfler, Jason Mast, and Allison DeAngelis—chronicles the pivotal **2025–2026 inflection point** in which **CAR-T cell therapy** and **CRISPR gene editing** broke out of oncology to reshape autoimmune medicine, prenatal/in-utero genetic intervention, bespoke N-of-1 rare-disease cures, and FDA regulatory doctrine (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2026 STAT_Ebook_BeyondCancer_September2026.pdf, pp. 2–5, 7, 13, 29, 69).

Across nine deeply reported dispatches, STAT documents how German physician-scientist **Georg Schett**'s lupus breakthrough sparked a biotech gold rush applying CAR-T cells to reset severe autoimmune diseases (pp. 7, 35); how UCSF surgeon-scientist **Tippi MacKenzie** is advancing pioneering **in-utero gene therapy trials** to treat inherited disorders *before birth* (p. 13); how bespoke CRISPR editing (the landmark **Baby KJ** and **Grace** cases) is forcing clinicians and the **FDA** to adopt flexible, platform-based cell and gene therapy oversight amid both excitement and safety wariness (pp. 18, 26, 29, 44); and how CRISPR Nobel laureate **Jennifer Doudna**'s spinout (**Azalea**, backed by $82M) aims to engineer **in-vivo single-shot CAR-T fighters** directly inside patients' bodies, bypassing costly ex-vivo cell manufacturing while confronting unresolved challenges of reimbursement, long-term safety follow-up, and equitable global access (pp. 5, 69) (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2026 STAT_Ebook_BeyondCancer_September2026.pdf, pp. 2, 4–5, 7–69).

## Key Arguments / Chapter Breakdown

* **Autoimmune CAR-T Revolution (Articles 1 & 5, pp. 7–12, 35–43)**:
  * *From Oncology to Immune Reset*: Chronicles five years of astonishing clinical durability following Georg Schett's first compassionate-use CAR-T treatment for severe lupus, triggering a wave of biotech investment targeting myositis, systemic sclerosis, and refractory autoimmunity (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2026 STAT_Ebook_BeyondCancer_September2026.pdf, pp. 2, 7, 35).

* **In-Utero & Bespoke N-of-1 CRISPR Gene Editing (Articles 2, 3 & 7, pp. 13–21, 44–68)**:
  * *Intervening Before Birth & Rare-Disease Equity*: Profiles Tippi MacKenzie's two-decade march from curing fetal mice with hemophilia toward human prenatal genetic trials, alongside the **Baby KJ** and **Grace** bespoke gene-editing journeys charting new regulatory pathways for ultra-rare fatal genetic diseases (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2026 STAT_Ebook_BeyondCancer_September2026.pdf, pp. 2, 13, 18, 44).

* **FDA Oversight Shifts & Single-Shot In-Vivo Engineering (Articles 4, 6 & 8, pp. 22–34, 69–73)**:
  * *Regulatory Flexibility & Manufacturing Breakthroughs*: Examines leadership transitions and the FDA's controversial pivot toward more flexible CGT regulatory frameworks (Jan 2026), culminating in **Jennifer Doudna's spinout Azalea ($82M)** working to turn patients' own immune cells into cancer- and autoimmunity-fighting CAR-T cells via a single in-vivo infusion (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2026 STAT_Ebook_BeyondCancer_September2026.pdf, pp. 2, 26, 29, 69).
