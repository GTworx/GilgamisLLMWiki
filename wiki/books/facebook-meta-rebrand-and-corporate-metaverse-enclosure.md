## Overview
This critical business and political economy analysis investigates Facebook's October 2021 corporate rebrand to Meta Platforms Inc. The authors dissect Mark Zuckerberg's multi-billion dollar pivot toward Quest hardware and Horizon Worlds, analyzing it as a strategic gambit to bypass Apple's mobile app store duopoly and enclose the emerging spatial internet under proprietary surveillance terms.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Deconstructs Meta's hardware subsidies as an aggressive strategy to achieve vertical monopoly over spatial operating systems.
- **Key Finding**: Examines the defensive motivation: escaping Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) privacy controls on iOS.
- **Key Finding**: Assesses the antitrust risks of a single corporate entity controlling the avatars, identity layer, and economic payment rails of the metaverse.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Meta's metaverse pivot was not a romantic embrace of science fiction; it was an urgent corporate attempt to escape mobile platform taxation and build the ultimate closed operating system."
