## Analytical Overview
### Cognitive Profiling of Four Dominant Geopolitical Strongmen

*Trump, Putin, Netanyahu, Erdoğan: Comparative Leadership Psychometrics, Negotiation Doctrines, and Crisis Decision-Making* delivers a comprehensive behavioral intelligence dossier comparing the psychological archetypes, rhetorical strategies, and high-stakes negotiation behaviors of four defining leaders of contemporary international statecraft.

### The Four Distinct Negotiation Doctrines

The compendium contrasts their operational methodologies:
1. **Donald Trump (The Transactional Dominance Archetype):** Anchored in real estate dealmaking, anchoring extremes, personal flattery paired with sudden tariff brinkmanship, and viewing international relations strictly through bilateral zero-sum balance-of-trade lenses.
2. **Vladimir Putin (The Chekist Operational Planner):** Formed by KGB operational psychology; masters asymmetric leverage, emotional detachment, long-range attrition, and calculating Western risk-aversion.
3. **Benjamin Netanyahu (The Existential Security Strategist):** Shaped by Revisionist Zionism and elite Sayeret Matkal military background; masters Washington congressional lobbying, manipulating coalition survival, and framing all tactical disputes as existential civilizational threats.
4. **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan (The Pragmatic Charismatic Street-Fighter):** Rooted in Kasımpaşa grassroots politics and National Outlook movement; masters domestic populist mobilization, strategic pivoting between East and West, and leveraging Turkey's geographic transit choke points.

### Strategic Utility

The compendium establishes predictive frameworks for diplomatic negotiators modeling multi-party crisis diplomacy.
