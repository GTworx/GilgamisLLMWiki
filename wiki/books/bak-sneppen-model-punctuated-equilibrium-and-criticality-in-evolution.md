## Executive Summary
Comprising Per Bak and Kim Sneppen's seminal 1993/1994 *Physical Review Letters* paper and a modern theoretical overview, this foundational physics and evolutionary biology dossier establishes the Bak-Sneppen Model of self-organized criticality (SOC) in biological macro-evolution. Moving beyond Darwinian gradualism, Bak and Sneppen model an ecosystem of interacting species arranged on a lattice or fitness landscape, where the least-fit species is mutated along with its immediate ecological neighbors. The model demonstrates that biological ecosystems naturally evolve toward a critical state characterized by power-law distributed extinction avalanches, explaining Stephen Jay Gould's paleontological punctuated equilibrium without requiring external asteroid impacts.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Self-Organized Criticality in Biology**: Complex ecosystems spontaneously tune themselves to a critical state without any external parameter tuning, operating at the boundary between rigid stasis and chaotic collapse.
- **Power-Law Extinction Cascades**: Mass extinction events naturally arise from internal co-evolutionary chain reactions rather than solely external extraterrestrial cataclysms; extinction avalanche sizes follow a 1/f power-law distribution.
- **Mathematical Basis for Punctuated Equilibrium**: Formulates a rigorous physical mechanism for Gould and Eldredge's punctuated equilibrium: long periods of apparent morphological stasis punctuated by sudden, explosive co-evolutionary reorganization.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Translation**: The Bak-Sneppen mathematical framework has been successfully adapted to model financial market crashes, traffic jams, power grid cascading failures, and neuronal firing avalanches.
