## Executive Summary

Authored by **Professor Stefano Caselli** (Dean of SDA Bocconi and leading European private equity scholar at Università Bocconi, Milan), this complete Master's-level curriculum provides the definitive academic and professional foundation for **Private Equity and Venture Capital Deal Structuring, Valuation, and Fund Lifecycle Management**. Spanning four comprehensive lecture modules and an integrated dynamic financial model, the course covers: (1) The Private Equity Ecosystem and Fund Lifecycle (fundraising, GP/LP fund formation, closed-end vehicles, management fees, hurdle rates, and carried interest waterfalls); (2) Early-Stage Venture Capital and Growth Financing (business plan screening, venture capital valuation methods, DCF vs. multiple approaches, and deal pricing); (3) Leveraged Buyout (LBO) Engineering (senior debt, subordinated mezzanine debt, debt-service coverage, and exit waterfalls); and (4) Valuation, Governance, and Exit Strategies (M&A trade sales, secondary buyouts, and IPO listings).

## Key Strategic & Financial Takeaways

- **Fund Economics and Waterfalls**: Mathematical modeling of the European vs. American carried interest waterfall: explaining the critical distinction between deal-by-deal carry (American) and whole-fund return-of-capital carry (European), alongside 80/20 carried interest splits and 8% preferred return hurdle rates.
- **LBO Value Drivers (The Three Engines of LBO Returns)**: Deconstructs how private equity buyouts generate equity returns through: (1) **Deleveraging** (using target company free cash flow to pay down principal debt over the holding period), (2) **Operational Improvement** (EBITDA margin expansion through revenue growth and cost rationalization), and (3) **Multiple Expansion** (selling the asset at a higher EV/EBITDA exit multiple than entry).
- **Debt Sizing and Coverage Ratios in LBOs**: Modeling senior debt (revolving credit, Term Loan A/B), mezzanine financing, and equity cushions, establishing strict constraints on Debt Service Coverage Ratios (DSCR $\ge 1.3	ext{x}$) and Leverage Ratios ($	ext{Debt}/	ext{EBITDA} \le 4	ext{x}$–$6	ext{x}$).
- **Venture Capital Valuation Method**: Demonstrates the classical VC method: working backward from estimated terminal exit value at Year 5, applying target venture discount rates (35%–50% IRR to account for early-stage failure risk), and calculating required post-money and pre-money ownership stakes adjusted for anticipated future dilution.
- **Legal Structures across Anglo-Saxon and Continental Europe**: Compares UK/Delaware Limited Partnerships (LP/GP) with European vehicles (SICAR, FCPR, SIF), contrasting tax transparency and regulatory capital requirements.

## Notable Excerpts & Citations

- "Private equity is not simply investment management; it is active corporate governance. A private equity investor does not bet on stocks; they buy entire companies, sit on the board, install new executive leadership, restructure balance sheets, and exit with a transformed enterprise." (source: Bocconi Module 1, p. 4)
- "An LBO is a financial machine where debt is both the fuel and the risk. If operational cash flow falters, the leverage that amplified equity returns in a bull market will accelerate insolvency in a downturn." (source: Bocconi Module 3, p. 12)
