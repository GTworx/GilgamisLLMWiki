## Executive Summary

*100 Math Brainteasers: Arithmetic, Algebra, and Geometry Brain Teasers, Puzzles, Games, and Problems* (131 pp.) by Polish mathematician and olympiad problem-composer [[zbigniew-romanowicz]] presents one hundred graduated mathematical challenges spanning natural numbers, parity, divisibility and prime factorization, Diophantine equations, combinatorics, and elementary geometry (source: BOOKS/Fun & Puzzles_/100 Math Brainteasers...pdf, Outline, pp. 4–26).

Rooted in the celebrated Central European mathematical-circle tradition, Romanowicz designs narrative problems (*Wax Clocks*, *A One Hundred-Headed Dragon*, *Zero-Sum Game*, *Digits 'Reshuffle'*, *Nostradamus and His Prophecy*) that require zero advanced calculus—only clear logical deduction, invariant discovery, modular arithmetic, and creative reframing (pp. 4–131).

## Key Arguments & Structural Breakdown

### Chapter 1: Natural Numbers, Parity & Sequences (Problems 1–25, pp. 4–15)
- **Pattern & Invariant Recognition**: Uses everyday and whimsical setups (bookstores, aquariums, chocolate bars, wax clocks, PIN digits) to cultivate parity checks, pigeonhole reasoning, and telescoping sequences.

### Chapter 2: Divisibility & Prime Numbers (Problems 26–40, pp. 16–23)
- **Number-Theoretic Deduction**: Trains solvers to exploit prime factorization, remainders, and divisibility constraints to narrow infinite search spaces to unique integer solutions.

### Chapters 3+: Equations, Algebra & Geometry (Problems 41–100, pp. 24–131)
- **Creative Modeling**: Advances into systems of equations, zero-sum strategic games, geometric dissections, and full step-by-step proofs designed for middle/high-school math circles and adult puzzle enthusiasts alike.
