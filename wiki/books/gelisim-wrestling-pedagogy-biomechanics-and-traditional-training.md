## Overview
Authored by the Physical Education and Sports Faculty at Istanbul Gelişim University, this comprehensive 104-page academic textbook covers the pedagogy, biomechanics, physiological conditioning, and historical heritage of wrestling (Güreş). The text bridges traditional Turkish oil wrestling (Yağlı Güreş) with modern Olympic Freestyle and Greco-Roman technical regimens.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Examines the biomechanical leverage, center of gravity manipulation, and neuromuscular force generation in classical wrestling holds.
- **Key Finding**: Details periodized athletic conditioning: anaerobic endurance, isometric neck strength, and injury prevention protocols.
- **Key Finding**: Preserves the historical, philosophical, and ritualistic culture of traditional wrestling as an intangible cultural heritage.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Güreş is both ancient martial science and modern athletic geometry; victory depends on mastering the physical laws of kinetic leverage and anaerobic endurance under extreme physiological duress."
