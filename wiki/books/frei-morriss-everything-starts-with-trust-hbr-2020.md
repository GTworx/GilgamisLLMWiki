## Analytical Overview
### Deconstructing the Universal Currency of Leadership

In *Begin with Trust*, published in *Harvard Business Review* (May–June 2020), Harvard Business School professor Frances X. Frei and executive coach Anne Morriss provide a brilliant, actionable deconstruction of leadership trust. Trust is the foundation of all human cooperation; without it, friction paralyzes organizations, employees withhold effort, and change initiatives fail. Frei and Morriss prove that trust is not a mysterious emotional gift, but an engineered capability.

### The Trust Triangle: Authenticity, Logic, and Empathy

The authors illustrate that trust breaks down when any one of three distinct pillars ('wobbles') fails:
1. **The Authenticity Wobble:** People doubt your motives if they sense you are performing a corporate mask or concealing the truth. *Remedy: Radically increase transparency and embrace individuality.*
2. **The Logic Wobble:** People lose faith if they perceive your reasoning is flawed or that you lack domain competence. *Remedy: Structure arguments cleanly, lead with your conclusion (Minto Pyramid Principle), and substantiate claims with evidence.*
3. **The Empathy Wobble:** The most common failure mode—people believe you care only about your personal career or agenda rather than their welfare. *Remedy: Put down your phone, listen deeply, and visibly demonstrate commitment to their development.*

### Rebuilding Broken Organizational Trust

Drawing on Frei's high-stakes experience turning around Uber's toxic workplace culture in 2017, the authors provide a practical diagnostic roadmap for leaders seeking to restore credibility following institutional scandals.
