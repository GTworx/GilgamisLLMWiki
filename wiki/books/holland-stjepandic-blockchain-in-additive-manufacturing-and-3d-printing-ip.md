## Overview
This comprehensive 4-study dossier—led by Martin Holland, Josip Stjepandic, and the 3D-Token industrial collaborative—investigates how distributed ledgers solve the existential copyright and security crises in additive manufacturing and 3D printing. The authors develop cryptographic authentication platforms that track digital CAD design files, enforce print-count licensing via smart contracts, and prevent counterfeit spare parts in aerospace and defense supply chains.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Designs smart contract DRM frameworks enforcing single-print execution of proprietary CAD engineering files.
- **Key Finding**: Prevents counterfeit parts in critical safety industries by anchoring physical part provenance to immutable on-chain hashes.
- **Key Finding**: Evaluates tokenomic incentive networks (3D-Token) for decentralized distributed manufacturing networks.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "In distributed additive manufacturing, the product is no longer a physical object shipped across oceans, but an encrypted digital design file. Blockchain provides the missing cryptographic trust layer preventing unauthorized duplication."
