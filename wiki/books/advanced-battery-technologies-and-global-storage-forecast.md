## Summary
Comprehensive technological roadmap and global market forecast tracking post-lithium-ion battery innovations, compiling BCC Research's 183-page study on next-generation advanced batteries (FCB062A), Rethink Research's global battery forecast to 2040, and Gartner's tech innovator benchmarks in grid-scale energy storage.

## Analytical Overview
As existing liquid-electrolyte lithium-ion batteries approach their theoretical volumetric and gravimetric energy density ceilings (~300 Wh/kg at cell level), massive research capital is flooding into next-generation electrochemical architectures. This canonical volume compiles the definitive market forecasts and technological assessments mapping this next-generation battery frontier. The centerpiece is BCC Research's 183-page study *Next-Generation Advanced Batteries: Global Markets* (FCB062A), which details the development pipelines of solid-state batteries (utilizing oxide, sulfide, or polymer solid electrolytes paired with lithium-metal anodes), sodium-ion (Na-ion) batteries, lithium-sulfur (Li-S), and vanadium redox flow batteries (VRFB) for stationary grid storage.

The technical roadmap is accompanied by Rethink Technology Research’s *Global Battery Forecast 2023–2040* by Joss Escott. Escott models the explosive scale-up of global battery cell manufacturing capacity from under 1 TWh in 2022 to over 8 TWh by 2040. The forecast demonstrates how sodium-ion batteries—relying on abundantly available, low-cost sodium carbonate instead of volatile lithium salts and eliminating cobalt and nickel entirely—are poised to capture over 25% of the stationary storage and entry-level urban micro-mobility markets by the 2030s.

The volume is rounded out by Gartner’s *Emerging Tech: Tech Innovators in Energy Storage*, providing enterprise evaluations of proprietary battery management systems (BMS), cloud-based battery health analytics utilizing AI impedance spectroscopy, and Honeywell Sensing and Productivity Solutions (SPS) industrial co-innovation presentations detailing real-time sensor integration within advanced battery packs.
