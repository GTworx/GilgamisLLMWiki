## Summary
Master legal compendium synthesizing international human rights resolutions, economic sanctions regimes, corporate compliance forecasting to 2030, Supreme Court ethics, and legal tech industry metrics. Features the 2025 UN Resolution Against Torture, international law governing secondary economic sanctions, Clio's Legal Trends Report on legal practice automation, and the US Supreme Court's historic 2023 Code of Conduct.

## Analytical Overview
This volume documents the structural transformation of public international law, corporate compliance, and judicial governance in the 2020s.

Key legal pillars:
1. **Economic Sanctions and Global Financial Chokepoints**: Formulates an exhaustive doctrine of *The Law of Economic Sanctions*, detailing OFAC enforcement, secondary sanctions extraterritoriality, blocking statutes, asset freezes, and the weaponization of SWIFT clearing channels in great power competition.
2. **Human Rights Jus Cogens & Anti-Torture Resolutions**: Compiles the 2025 United Nations General Assembly Resolution Against Torture, analyzing the non-derogable *jus cogens* status of torture prohibitions, universal jurisdiction mandates, and state responsibility for extraordinary rendition and proxy detention.
3. **Corporate Compliance 2030 & Judicial Ethics**: Integrates predictive legal industry roadmaps (*Future of Compliance 2030*), Clio's empirical *Legal Trends Report* tracking AI automation in law firms, and the official 2023 Code of Conduct adopted by the Supreme Court of the United States.
