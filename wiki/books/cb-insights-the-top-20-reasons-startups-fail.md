## Analytical Overview
### The Definitive Autopsy of Entrepreneurial Mortality

*The Top 20 Reasons Startups Fail: An Autopsy of 101 Venture Post-Mortems*, authored by the quantitative research group at CB Insights, is universally acknowledged as one of the most widely cited and influential empirical research papers in modern entrepreneurship. Analyzing the candid, unvarnished post-mortem essays of 101 startup founders and investors, the report constructs a definitive taxonomy of venture failure.

### The Hierarchy of Startup Failure

The report decisively demonstrates that the greatest threat to a startup is not technological difficulty or competition, but building something nobody wants:
1. **Tackling Problems That Lack Market Need (42%):** Founders building elegant technical solutions for non-existent consumer or enterprise problems.
2. **Running Out of Cash (29%):** Misjudging burn rate velocity and failing to reach fundable milestones before runway exhaustion.
3. **The Wrong Team (23%):** Lacking complementary skill sets, technical execution capability, or co-founder interpersonal alignment.
4. **Getting Outcompeted (19%):** Ignoring incumbent responses or aggressive blitzscaling competitors.
5. **Pricing/Cost Issues (18%):** Flawed unit economics where Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) permanently exceeds Lifetime Value (LTV).

### Essential Lessons for Builders

The study serves as the foundational text reinforcing Eric Ries' Lean Startup and Steve Blank's Customer Development doctrines.
