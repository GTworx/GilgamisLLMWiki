# The Genetic History of Admixture Across Inner Eurasia and the Steppe Corridor

## Summary
Comprehensive genomic transect analyzing 763 individuals across Inner Eurasia, tracking clinal gene flows from the Caucasus to Siberia, Eneolithic migrations from Nalchik, and Neolithic Elamite-Dravidian Y-chromosome phylogeography.

## Analytical Overview
Integrates genome-wide data from across northern, central, and eastern Eurasia. Documents three distinct temporal waves of massive population replacement and admixture: the early steppe pastoralist expansion (Yamnaya/Afanasievo), the Bronze Age Sintashta-Andronovo chariot horizon, and the medieval Turkic-Mongol dispersals.
