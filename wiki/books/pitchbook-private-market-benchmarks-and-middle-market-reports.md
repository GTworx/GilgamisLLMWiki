## Summary
Authoritative 7-volume PitchBook benchmarking compendium providing empirical horizon IRRs, PME (Public Market Equivalent) metrics, middle-market buyout leverage multiples, and medtech investment trends.

## Analytical Overview
Establishes rigorous statistical standards for evaluating private market returns: why internal rate of return (IRR) is easily distorted by subscription credit lines, why pooled TVPI and KS-PME benchmarks are essential, and how US middle-market sponsor-backed companies navigated elevated debt-service burdens and supply chain inflation.
