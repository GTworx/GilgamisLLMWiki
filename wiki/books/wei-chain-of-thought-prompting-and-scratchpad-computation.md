## Summary
Landmark cognitive architecture and prompt engineering compendium documenting the discovery of emergent reasoning in large language models. Centers around Jason Wei et al.'s NeurIPS 2022 breakthrough 'Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models', Maxwell Nye's scratchpad computational mechanisms, self-consistency sampling, and few-shot numerical reasoning.

## Analytical Overview
This volume compiles the foundational papers that unlocked algorithmic and mathematical problem-solving in transformer language models.

Key discoveries:
1. **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting**: Features Jason Wei and Denny Zhou’s historic NeurIPS 2022 paper, demonstrating that encouraging models to generate a step-by-step intermediate reasoning chain prior to outputting a final answer transforms performance on multi-step arithmetic (GSM8K), symbolic manipulation, and commonsense reasoning from near-zero to state-of-the-art.
2. **Scratchpad Computation for Intermediate States**: Integrates Nye et al.’s foundational *Show Your Work* architecture, proving that allowing neural networks to write intermediate computational tape steps circumvents fixed-depth transformer computational limits, enabling arbitrary-precision polynomial addition and Python execution.
3. **Self-Consistency & Latent Verification**: Analyzes Xuezhi Wang’s self-consistency decoding (sampling diverse reasoning paths and taking the marginal majority vote), deconstructing the underlying mechanics of pre-training term frequency and prompt phrasing in numerical inference.
