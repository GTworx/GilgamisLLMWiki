## Executive Summary

This strategic executive eBook by PwC Advisory provides an enterprise procurement and architectural framework for selecting, evaluating, and deploying Internet of Things solutions. Designed for corporate CIOs, Chief Operations Officers, and facility managers, the guide deconstructs the essential building blocks of successful enterprise IoT deployments: scalable sensor hardware integration, carrier-agnostic connectivity, secure cloud data orchestration, enterprise software interoperability (ERP, CRM, WMS), and demonstrable return on investment (ROI). PwC outlines procurement scorecards to help organizations avoid common pitfalls and scale beyond fragmented proof-of-concept experiments.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Overcoming the IoT Proof-of-Concept Trap**: Explains why over 70% of enterprise IoT pilots stall before scaled deployment—citing lack of clear business cases, complex systems integration, and cybersecurity ambiguity.
- **The Five-Layer Enterprise IoT Stack**: Formulates a clear reference architecture: Physical Sensors/Actuators, Secure Connectivity, Cloud Ingestion Platform, Advanced Analytics Engine, and Business Application Integration.
- **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Evaluation**: Details hidden operational costs in IoT lifecycles: field maintenance, battery replacement logistics, cellular connectivity data plans, and regulatory certification compliance.
- **Pre-Configured Industry Solutions vs. Custom Builds**: Recommends adopting modular, pre-configured commercial solutions for common use cases (asset tracking, cold chain monitoring, worker safety) rather than attempting bespoke internal software development.
- **Measuring Value and ROI Milestones**: Identifies immediate quantifiable value pools: asset utilization increases, loss prevention, energy reduction, and automated compliance reporting.
