## Summary
Pioneering archaeogenetic and military-historical study reconstructing the demographic origins, nomadic pastoral adaptations, military technology, and genetic legacy of the Scythians across the Eurasian steppe corridor.

## Analytical Overview
Combines genomic analysis of ancient Scythian, Sarmatian, and Pazyryk individuals with rigorous historiographical analysis of Scythian composite reflex bows, cavalry tactics, and iron metallurgy. Disproves single-origin models by revealing a multi-regional synthesis of Western Yamnaya/Timber-grave and Eastern Siberian/Altaic ancestral components.
