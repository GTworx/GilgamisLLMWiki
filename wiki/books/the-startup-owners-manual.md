## Executive Summary

Authored by Silicon Valley serial entrepreneur and Stanford/Berkeley educator **Steve Blank** and veteran founder/investor **Bob Dorf** (K&S Ranch Press, 2012, p. 4), *The Startup Owner's Manual* is the exhaustive 987-page reference encyclopedia of the **Customer Development** movement. Opening with Blank's famous dictum—*"A startup is not a smaller version of a large company"*—the manual defines a startup as a **temporary organization searching for a repeatable and scalable business model** (Part I, Ch. 1–2).

Unlike traditional MBA execution plans built on untested assumptions ("no business plan survives first contact with customers"), Blank and Dorf pair **Alexander Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas** with Agile Engineering and a relentless injunction to **"Get Out of the Building!"** (p. 2). The 987-page PDF includes all three complete volumes: the **Strategy Guide** (pp. 11–156), the **Web/Mobile Channel Startups Manual** (pp. 157–590), and the **Physical Channel Startups Manual** (pp. 591–987).

## The Four-Step Customer Development Architecture

1. **Step 1 — Customer Discovery (Phases 1–4)**: Translates founder vision into explicit Business Model Canvas hypotheses, leaves the building to test whether real customers suffer the problem ("Do People Care?"), tests MVP solutions, and verifies whether to **Pivot or Proceed** (pp. 81–98, 188–397).
2. **Step 2 — Customer Validation (Phases 1–4)**: Builds a repeatable, scalable sales roadmap ("Get Ready to Sell" → "Sell to Earlyvangelists" → develop positioning → rigorous financial/metric audit to verify repeatability before scaling spend, pp. 99–156, 398–590).
3. **Step 3 & Step 4 — Customer Creation & Company Building**: Transitions from *Search* into *Execution* only after validated repeatability—aligning demand generation with market type (Existing, Resegmented, New, or Clone markets) and scaling functional departments.
4. **Dual-Channel Precision**: Explicitly separates acquisition, activation, viral loops, and unit economics for **Web/Mobile** startups from distribution-channel margins, slotting fees, and physical logistics for **Physical** startups.
