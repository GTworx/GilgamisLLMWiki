## Summary
Monumental 326-page dual flagship surveillance publication by the IMF analyzing the 'last mile' of global disinflation, commercial real estate vulnerabilities, non-bank financial intermediation (shadow banking), and medium-term growth prospects.

## Analytical Overview
Evaluates systemic stability risks in a high-rate macroeconomic environment. Explores the rapid expansion of private credit markets ($1.7 trillion), liquidity mismatches in open-ended investment funds, sovereign debt distress in frontier markets, and the medium-term slowdown in global total factor productivity amidst geoeconomic fragmentation.
