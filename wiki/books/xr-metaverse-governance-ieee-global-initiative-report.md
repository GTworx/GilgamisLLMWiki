## Overview
Published by the IEEE Standards Association's Global Initiative on Ethics of Extended Reality, this landmark 31-page consensus report establishes comprehensive governance standards for immersive environments. Addressing identity, content moderation, data sovereignty, mental wellbeing, and technical interoperability, the report outlines how open engineering standards can safeguard human autonomy in spatial computing.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Articulates core governance pillars: agency, safety, inclusivity, data protection, and open interoperability.
- **Key Finding**: Defines standards for real-time spatial moderation that protect users from virtual assault without imposing totalitarian surveillance.
- **Key Finding**: Mandates universal accessibility and non-discriminatory spatial interface standards across all IEEE member organizations.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Governance in the spatial web cannot be retrofitted through terms of service; it must be baked into the foundational network protocols, physics engines, and sensor hardware drivers."
