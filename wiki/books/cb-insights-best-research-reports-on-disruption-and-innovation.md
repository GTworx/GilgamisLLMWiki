## Analytical Overview
### Anthology of Foundational Technology Intelligence

*The Year's Best Research on Disruption, Startups, and Corporate Innovation*, curated by CB Insights, brings together the firm's most seminal empirical studies analyzing the economics of innovation, venture financing mechanics, and incumbent disruption.

### Core Research Papers Anthologized

The volume contains four flagship empirical investigations:
- **The Top 20 Reasons Startups Fail:** The landmark autopsy of 101 startup failures identifying no market need (42%) and running out of cash (29%) as the overwhelming drivers of entrepreneurial mortality.
- **Unbundling the Bank:** The legendary visual teardown illustrating how venture-backed fintech startups unbundled Wells Fargo and Citi into dozens of specialized digital lending, wealth management, and payment services.
- **The Demise of Corporate R&D Laboratories:** Tracing the decline of centralized corporate labs (like Bell Labs and Xerox PARC) and the rise of Corporate Venture Capital (CVC) and external venture client acquisition models.
- **Patent Intelligence and Big Tech Moats:** Analyzing patent filing velocities across Amazon, Google, Apple, and Microsoft to expose secret hardware projects.

### Indispensable Compendium

The volume provides technology practitioners with a dense, visually rich handbook on market mechanics and corporate survival.
