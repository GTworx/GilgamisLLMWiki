## Summary
Monumental 13-source scholarly compendium examining late Ottoman Armenian civil servants, Operation Nemesis and ASALA terrorism, missionary school infiltration in Bitlis, Zangezur territorial transfers, and the Pontus question.

## Analytical Overview
Assembles rigorous peer-reviewed studies and archival dissertations documenting: Yücel Güçlü's methodological dismantling of Taner Akçam's citations; Rabia Biçici's 108-page doctoral thesis comparing reciprocal wartime relocations in Eastern Anatolia; Yusuf Halaçoğlu's forensic Alaca excavation report; the historical presence of Armenian state officials in Van under the Ottoman bureaucracy; the role of foreign missionary schools in Bitlis; and the territorial transfer of Zangezur between 1918 and 1921.
