## Executive Summary

Published in March 2023 by the Republic of Türkiye Presidency Strategy and Budget Directorate (Strateji ve Bütçe Başkanlığı - SBB), this 145-page macroeconomic, structural, and social assessment represents the official state documentation of the catastrophic double earthquakes of February 6, 2023 (Mw 7.7 Pazarcık and Mw 7.6 Elbistan) and February 20, 2023 (Mw 6.4 Defne). Spanning 11 heavily impacted provinces across southeastern Türkiye, the report provides an exhaustive, data-driven synthesis of physical damage, casualty statistics, economic losses, and reconstruction expenditure requirements.

The SBB assessment calculates the total economic burden of the disaster at approximately $103.6 billion (equivalent to ~9% of Türkiye's 2023 projected GDP). It meticulously audits sectoral damage across residential housing (comprising 54.9% of total damage), public infrastructure, energy distribution networks, industrial manufacturing facilities, agriculture, and cultural heritage assets. Furthermore, it outlines short-, medium-, and long-term fiscal frameworks for resilient urban reconstruction and industrial revitalization.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Total Economic Impact Quantification**: Documents an aggregate financial loss of 2 trillion TL ($103.6 billion), with private housing destruction constituting $56.9 billion and public physical infrastructure losses reaching $12.9 billion.
- **Scale of Structural Destruction**: Evaluates damage across 1.9 million residential buildings, recording over 518,000 housing units categorized as collapsed, heavily damaged, or requiring emergency demolition.
- **Supply Chain and Agricultural Impairment**: Details disruptions across the regional agricultural heartland (accounting for 14.5% of Türkiye's agricultural output) and industrial export corridors (textiles, steel, and food processing in Gaziantep and Hatay).
- **Reconstruction and Financial Architecture**: Establishes principles for seismic microzonation, relocation away from active fault lines and liquefiable alluvial plains, and multi-year public budget reallocation.

## Notable Excerpts & Citations

- "The February 6, 2023 Kahramanmaraş and Hatay earthquakes constitute the most devastating seismic disaster in the modern history of the Republic of Türkiye, inflicting losses exceeding 9% of our gross domestic product."
- "Reconstruction must transcend mere replacement; it requires systematic integration of seismic isolation, geological microzonation, and climate-resilient civil engineering."
