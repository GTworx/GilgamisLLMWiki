## Executive Summary
Authored by economic theorist Philip Trammell and frontier macro-forecaster Leopold Aschenbrenner, this rigorous 42-page mathematical treatise establishes a macroeconomic growth model that endogenizes existential risk from transformative artificial intelligence. Building upon endogenous growth theory (Romer, Jones) and decision-theoretic risk frameworks, the paper formalizes the tension between explosive economic output generated by automated cognitive labor and the escalating cumulative probability of planetary catastrophe or loss of human control. The authors analyze optimal growth paths, demonstrating that under standard social welfare functions, society should sacrifice substantial short-term growth in favor of safety, alignment verification, and defense-dominant institutional hardening.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Endogenous Catastrophe Modeling**: Formalizes existential risk as an endogenous function of the pace of technological frontier expansion rather than an exogenous background lottery.
- **Explosive Growth Horizon**: If AI fully automates cognitive R&D, economic growth rates could accelerate from historical 2% annual rates to hyper-exponential regimes, radically compressing civilizational decision horizons.
- **The Optimal Alignment Discount**: Proves that even modest existential risks justify massive macroeconomic expenditures on alignment research and institutional deceleration of risky compute milestones.
- **Coordination Failure**: Highlights game-theoretic international races where strategic competition induces national actors to run near-catastrophic risk tolerances unless cooperative verification mechanisms exist.
