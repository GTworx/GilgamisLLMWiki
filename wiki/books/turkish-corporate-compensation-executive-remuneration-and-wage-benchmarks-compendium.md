## Summary
Monumental 5-source corporate human capital benchmark compiling proprietary compensation data across hundreds of multinational and domestic firms in Turkey, evaluating mid-year salary adjustments, FX-indexed perks, and wage inflation.

## Analytical Overview
Synthesizes empirical survey data from Mercer, Korn Ferry, Willis Towers Watson, and Reed. Details corporate compensation strategies implemented to retain talent amidst high domestic inflation: shifting from annual to quarterly wage reviews, implementing dollar/euro pegged retention bonuses for software and engineering personnel, and restructuring executive equity schemes.
