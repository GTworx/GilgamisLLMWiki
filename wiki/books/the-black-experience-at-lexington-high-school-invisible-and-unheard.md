## Summary
Qualitative sociological investigation into the educational, psychological, and social experiences of Black students in an elite, high-performing Massachusetts suburban public high school.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes student narratives, academic level tracking, disciplinary disparities, and social microaggressions within a nominally progressive suburban educational environment. Exposes the psychological toll of hyper-visibility and racial alienation, proposing concrete school committee policy interventions for equitable honors placement, mental health support, and faculty diversification.
