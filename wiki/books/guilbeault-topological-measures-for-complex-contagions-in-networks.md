## Summary
Breakthrough Nature Communications paper establishing mathematically rigorous topological metrics to predict the diffusion of complex contagions (behaviors requiring multiple social reinforcements) across networks.

## Analytical Overview
Demonstrates that classical centrality measures (degree, betweenness, PageRank) optimized for simple viral contagions (diseases, simple rumors) fail completely to predict complex contagions like social movements, product adoption, or behavioral shifts. Introduces 'complex centrality' based on overlapping wide neighborhoods, showing that decentralized network perimeters spread complex behaviors far more effectively than central hubs.
