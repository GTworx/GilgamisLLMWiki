## Executive Summary

The Turkish Catastrophe Insurance Pool (Doğal Afet Sigortaları Kurumu - DASK) published this 60-page annual activity report documenting the financial architecture, reinsurance treaties, claims processing capabilities, and national penetration of compulsory earthquake insurance (Zorunlu Deprem Sigortası - ZDS) heading into 2022. Established following the devastating 1999 Marmara earthquake under Decree Law No. 587 (later solidified under Law No. 6305), DASK is one of the world's premier public-private catastrophe risk pools.

The report audits DASK's balance sheet, total underwriting capacity (exceeding 25 billion TL through international catastrophe excess-of-loss reinsurance programs and capital reserves), and nationwide insurance penetration rate (reaching approximately 59% across 10.6 million active policies). It examines the digital transformation of damage assessment claims, claims handling during the 2020 Elazığ and İzmir earthquakes, and public awareness campaigns promoting insurance literacy.

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Public-Private Risk Transfer Model**: Details the mathematical and actuarial structuring of Türkiye's national risk pool, transferring domestic seismic exposure to international retrocession markets.
- **Underwriting and Claim-Paying Capacity**: Documents DASK's layered capital structure combining retained reserves, international reinsurance treaties, and catastrophe bond instruments to withstand an Istanbul-scale earthquake shock.
- **Regional Insurance Penetration Disparities**: Analyzes policy distribution rates, demonstrating high compliance in Marmara (~65%) contrasted with lower rural uptake in Eastern and Southeastern Anatolia (~50%).
- **Automated and Expedited Claims Adjustment**: Outlines the operational deployment of mobile assessment teams, algorithmic damage triage, and fast-track banking payouts directly to affected citizens.
