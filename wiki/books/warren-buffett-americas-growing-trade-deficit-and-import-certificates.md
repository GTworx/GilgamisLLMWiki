## Summary
Warren Buffett's famous 2003 Fortune thesis warning that persistent U.S. current account deficits act as a voluntary transfer of national wealth and equity to foreign creditors, proposing a market-based 'Import Certificates' system.

## Analytical Overview
Buffett uses the metaphor of 'Squanderville' versus 'Thriftville' to demonstrate how running continuous 4–5% trade deficits forces the United States to net sell productive capital assets and debt to foreign entities. Formulates the Import Certificates (IC) mechanism where exporters earn tradable credits required by importers, naturally balancing international merchandise trade without government tariffs.
