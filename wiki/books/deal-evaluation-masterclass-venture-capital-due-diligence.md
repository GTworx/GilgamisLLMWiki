## Analytical Overview
### The Rigorous Science of Venture Underwriting

*The Art of Evaluating a Deal: Due Diligence Frameworks, Term Sheet Architecture, and Investment Memos* establishes a structured, institutional curriculum for venture capital due diligence. In an asset class where early-stage startups often possess minimal financial history, rigorous evaluation requires an interdisciplinary toolkit combining forensic accounting, technical code audits, competitive moats analysis, and founder psychological assessment.

### Core Due Diligence Pillars

The masterclass guide structures investment evaluation across four rigorous stages:
1. **Commercial & Market Due Diligence:** Sizing bottom-up Total Addressable Market (TAM), verifying customer retention cohorts, calculating Net Revenue Retention (NRR), and conducting un-blinded customer discovery calls.
2. **Technical & Architectural Audit:** Reviewing codebase modularity, technical debt, proprietary AI model weights, IP ownership, and infrastructure unit economics.
3. **Financial & Cap Table Hygiene:** Auditing historical Burn Multiple, CAC Payback periods, convertible note dilution, and option pool overhang.
4. **Legal & Governance Review:** Scrutinizing liquidation preferences, protective provisions, drag-along rights, and founder vesting schedules.

### The Investment Committee Memo

The guide provides a standardized, battle-tested template for writing persuasive, objective investment memos for partner review.
