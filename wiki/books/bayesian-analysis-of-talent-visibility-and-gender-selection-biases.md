## Summary
Four-part mathematical modeling suite applying Bayesian probability and population sorting equations to reveal how small upstream perceptual biases create massive downstream visibility skews across comedy, cuisine, art, and leadership.

## Analytical Overview
Formulates competing hypotheses (process bias vs latent talent distribution) to test how gatekeeper priors skew probability updates. Draws structural parallels between gender bias, height bias in corporate leadership, and educational sorting, demonstrating that 'early, late, and laggard' adoption curves reflect underlying network threshold dynamics rather than intrinsic competence.
