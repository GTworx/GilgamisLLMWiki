## Executive Summary

Compiled by **Buğra Atasayar** and edited by **Burcu Alver** (p. 60; 2022 commemorative edition marking the 107th anniversary of 18 March 1915; 60 pp.), *Zafer Yolunda: 107. Yılında, 107 Fotoğrafla Çanakkale Destanı* presents a curated visual chronicle of the **1915–1916 Gallipoli (Çanakkale) Campaign** through 107 archival photographs with concise historical captions.

Progressing chronologically from the November 1914 mobilization at Fatih Mosque, Mevlevi volunteer units, high-school student volunteers from Çanakkale Lisesi, and Thracian peasant supply lines (pp. 3–7) to Allied naval bombardments (HMS *Majestic*, Seddülbahir, Dardanos, and Anadolu Mecidiye batteries during the decisive **18 March 1915 Naval Victory**, pp. 8–12), ANZAC and multi-ethnic Entente landings, trench warfare, field hospitals, and prisoners of war on both sides (pp. 58–59), the volume captures the human cost and national-foundational memory of *"Çanakkale Geçilmez"* ("Gallipoli is Impassable").

---

## Core Historical & Memorial Themes

1. **Total Societal Mobilization**: Visually documents how teenagers, students, Anatolian peasants, and multi-regional Ottoman troops stood shoulder to shoulder against the world's premier naval empires.
2. **Global Coalition Dimensions**: Highlights the international character of the campaign—British, French, Australian, New Zealand (ANZAC), Indian, and Senegalese troops facing Ottoman coastal artillery and infantry defenses.
3. **Crucible of Republican Leadership**: Illuminates the battlefield theater where Lt.-Col. Mustafa Kemal's decisive leadership at Conkbayırı and Anafartalar forged the moral capital that later sustained the Turkish War of Independence.
