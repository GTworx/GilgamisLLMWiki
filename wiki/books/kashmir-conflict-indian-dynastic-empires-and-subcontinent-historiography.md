## Summary
Comprehensive historical analysis of the Kashmir dispute from its mythological and Dogra princely origins through the 1947 partition, paired with a chronological taxonomy of indigenous vs. foreign-founded empires across Indian history.

## Analytical Overview
Investigates the Instrument of Accession signed by Maharaja Hari Singh, subsequent Indo-Pakistani wars, UN Security Council resolutions, and the revocation of Article 370 in 2019. Categorizes two millennia of subcontinental empires (Maurya, Satavahana, Gupta, Chola, Delhi Sultanate, Mughal, Maratha, British Raj) by indigenous vs foreign dynastic origin.
