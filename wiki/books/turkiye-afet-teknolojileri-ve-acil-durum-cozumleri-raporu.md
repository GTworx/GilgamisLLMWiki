## Executive Summary

This landmark 152-page two-volume engineering and digital policy study, spearheaded by the Turkish Informatics Foundation (Türkiye Bilişim Vakfı - TBV), civil society technologists, and leading university computer science faculties, represents the definitive post-2023 audit of disaster technologies (*Afet Teknolojileri*). It analyzes the extraordinary explosion of civic software, digital volunteers (Afet Haritası, Deprem İmece, Açık Yazılım Ağı), open-source algorithms, and hardware tools deployed during the Kahramanmaraş disaster.

The compendium systematically audits technologies across four operational disaster stages: pre-disaster modeling (AI-driven seismic hazard mapping, satellite remote sensing, structural health IoT sensors), emergency response (crowdsourced rescue mapping, NLP parsing of social media distress calls, thermal drone imaging, mesh networks), post-disaster logistics (autonomous warehouse matching, digital aid vouchers), and long-term recovery (digital twin city reconstructions).

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Open-Source Civic Tech Explosion**: Details how thousands of software engineers built and deployed open-source distress heatmaps (e.g., AfetBilgi, DepremYardim) within hours, processing millions of tweets into geolocated rescue coordinates.
- **Natural Language Processing for Distress Filtering**: Examines the architecture of transformer models fine-tuned to parse unstructured Turkish social media feeds, extracting addresses, blood needs, and building collapse reports while filtering spam.
- **Autonomous Drones and Satellite Damage Mapping**: Evaluates the use of synthetic aperture radar (SAR), high-resolution optical satellite imagery, and thermal drones to rapidly classify building damage levels without human ground reconnaissance.
- **Decentralized Communication and Mesh Networks**: Highlights critical communication failures caused by base station power loss, proposing portable LoRaWAN mesh communication networks and satellite backhauls.
