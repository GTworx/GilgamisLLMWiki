## Executive Summary

Published on March 3, 2023, by the Turkish Medical Association (Türk Tabipleri Birliği - TTB) Central Council and Public Health Branch, this 52-page independent clinical and epidemiological report provides an unvarnished audit of the public health landscape across the 11 earthquake-devastated provinces during the first month following the February 6 disasters. Prepared by physician volunteer teams operating across Hatay, Kahramanmaraş, Adıyaman, Malatya, and Gaziantep, the report evaluates healthcare infrastructure destruction, disease outbreaks, and primary care delivery.

The report documents the near-total collapse of regional hospital facilities in Antakya and İskenderun, the disruption of cold-chain routine childhood vaccinations, the acute shortage of potable drinking water, and severe hygiene deficiencies in temporary tent encampments. It provides clinical guidelines for managing crush syndrome, hypothermia, limb amputations, tetanus prevention, scabies epidemics, and the management of chronic conditions (dialysis, diabetes, hypertension).

---

## Key Strategic, Operational & Technical Takeaways

- **Crush Syndrome and Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)**: Documents clinical protocols for rapid intravenous fluid resuscitation to prevent lethal myoglobinuric acute renal failure in extricated entrapment victims.
- **Epidemiological Surveillance and Waterborne Risks**: Warns of explosive gastroenteritis, cholera, and hepatitis A outbreaks due to broken sewage lines and contaminated groundwater, demanding immediate municipal chlorination.
- **Primary Care and Chronic Disease Disruption**: Highlights the catastrophic interruption of cancer oncology treatments, kidney dialysis, and pediatric immunization schedules across temporary displacement settlements.
- **Health Worker Burnout and Workplace Trauma**: Details the profound psychological trauma and physical exhaustion experienced by local surviving medical personnel who lost families while staffing makeshift clinics.

## Notable Excerpts & Citations

- "Disasters do not end when search and rescue operations terminate; the public health crisis that follows is an ongoing emergency of equal lethality if clean water, sanitation, and primary care are not restored immediately."
- "Healthcare facilities must be constructed with seismic base isolation; a society whose hospitals collapse during an earthquake loses both its healing capacity and its moral anchor."
