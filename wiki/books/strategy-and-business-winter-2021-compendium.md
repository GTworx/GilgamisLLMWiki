## Analytical Overview
### Strategic Execution in an Era of Reinvention

The Winter 2021 issue (Issue 105) of *strategy+business*, published by Strategy& (PwC's strategy consulting business), addresses the fundamental reshaping of corporate operating models in the wake of the global pandemic. Bringing together insights from strategy consultants, corporate chairpersons, and business academics, the issue provides an executive guide to sustainable transformation.

### Core Strategic Dimensions

The volume explores:
- **Trust as a Core Competency:** How organizations operationalize trust across consumer privacy, employee engagement, and transparent ESG reporting.
- **The Decoupling of Office and Work:** Structural models for managing high-trust hybrid workplaces without falling into surveillance traps.
- **Ecosystem Orchestration:** Why traditional linear supply chains are being replaced by collaborative business ecosystems.

### Practical Frameworks for Executive Leadership

The issue delivers concrete diagnostic tools for C-suite leaders evaluating whether their corporate capabilities are aligned with market opportunities.
