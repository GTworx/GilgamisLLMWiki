## Overview
This 52-page official United States Patent Application specification (US 2018/0096175 A1) details an advanced technical architecture for decentralized identity verification and cryptographically protected token transactions. The patent claims detail zero-knowledge authentication flows, distributed key management, and cryptographic attestation mechanisms that verify identity attributes without revealing underlying personally identifiable information.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Claims novel methods for decentralized cryptographic identity proofs decoupled from central certificate authorities.
- **Key Finding**: Specifies secure key-rotation protocols preventing compromise across distributed consumer hardware nodes.
- **Key Finding**: Provides granular flow diagrams and algorithmic specifications for verifiable zero-knowledge credential exchange.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Disclosed is a system and method for sovereign identity verification wherein a client device cryptographically asserts claims regarding attributes without exposing raw data payloads."
