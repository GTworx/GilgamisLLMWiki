## Overview
Published in the Financial Times by investigative financial reporters Anna Nicolaou and Eric Platt, this incisive report reveals the mounting financial crisis threatening multi-billion-dollar private equity investments in legacy music publishing catalogs (KKR, Blackstone, Hipgnosis Songs Fund). As global interest rates surged, the cheap debt that fueled astronomical catalog valuations dried up, exposing debt-service strains and royalty securitization vulnerabilities.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Explains the financial engineering behind catalog buyouts: treating classic songs as bond-like predictable annuity streams.
- **Key Finding**: Details how rising central bank interest rates crushed net-present-value multiples on historic song rights.
- **Key Finding**: Audits the corporate governance disputes, independent asset revaluations, and debt restructuring among major catalog funds.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Private equity treated rock 'n' roll royalties like municipal bonds; when interest rates soared, the financial alchemy behind billion-dollar song catalogs began to unravel."
