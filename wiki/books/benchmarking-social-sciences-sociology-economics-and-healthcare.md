## Summary
Three-part quantitative monograph detailing how to construct rigorous benchmarking frameworks in sociology, development economics, and healthcare systems, using post-2024 India as a central case study.

## Analytical Overview
Deconstructs vanity GDP metrics in favor of multi-dimensional poverty indexes, dependency ratios, total factor productivity, and out-of-pocket health expenditure ratios. Provides a concrete operational roadmap for evaluating state capacity, infrastructure efficiency, and maternal-infant health outcomes in emerging economies.
