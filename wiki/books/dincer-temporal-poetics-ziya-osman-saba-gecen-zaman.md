## Overview
Published in Edebî Eleştiri Dergisi (Journal of Literary Criticism) by literary scholar Özlem Dinçer, this 14-page poetic monograph investigates the temporal architecture of time, hours of the day, and seasonal metaphors in Ziya Osman Saba's celebrated poetry collection Geçen Zaman. Dinçer analyzes Saba's melancholy, spiritual surrender, and domestic intimacy against the rapid, disorienting modernization of mid-20th-century Istanbul.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes the symbolic function of evening (akşam) and twilight as thresholds between domestic peace and existential longing.
- **Key Finding**: Interprets seasonal cycles in Saba's verse as meditations on transience, mortality, and religious metaphysical contentment.
- **Key Finding**: Situates Saba within the 'Yedi Meşaleciler' literary movement, highlighting his unique lyrical purity and quietude.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Ziya Osman Saba's poetry does not fight the passage of time; it wraps itself in the passing hours, transforming melancholy into an intimate sanctuary of gratitude and spiritual stillness."
