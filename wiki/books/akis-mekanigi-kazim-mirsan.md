## Executive Summary

Reflecting his professional academic training as a German-educated civil and hydraulic engineer (Dipl.-Ing. from the Technische Universität Berlin), *Akış Mekaniği* (*Fluid Mechanics*) by **Kâzım Mirşan** is a rigorous mathematical and physical engineering textbook. The treatise investigates the fundamental laws of hydrodynamics, fluid kinetics, Navier-Stokes equations, laminar and turbulent flows in open channels and closed pipes, and boundary layer theory—illustrating the exact mathematical and physical scientific background that shaped Mirşan's intensely systematic, structural approach to linguistic decipherment.

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **The Engineer as Epigrapher**: Demonstrates that Mirşan's controversial philological methods were rooted not in literary speculation, but in an engineer's insistence on geometric symmetry, structural systems, and law-governed flow mechanics.
2. **Applied Hydraulic Science**: Represents a high-caliber Turkish-language engineering text designed for municipal water infrastructure, dam construction, and canal hydraulics.
3. **Intellectual Duality**: Highlights the fascinating biographical duality of Mirşan as both a practicing civil engineer designing infrastructure and a passionate decoder of prehistoric inscriptions.
