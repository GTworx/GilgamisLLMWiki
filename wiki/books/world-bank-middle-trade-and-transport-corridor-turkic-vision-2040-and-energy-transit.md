## Summary
Comprehensive 4-part geoeconomic compendium detailing the World Bank's 70-page Middle Trade and Transport Corridor study across the Caspian Sea, the Organization of Turkic States' 2040 strategic vision, and investigative reporting on maritime oil transshipment.

## Analytical Overview
Models how the Trans-Caspian International Transport Route (TITR) connecting China, Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Georgia, and Turkey can triple trade volumes and halve transit times by 2030 through digital customs, fleet expansion, and bottleneck removal. Synthesizes OTS regional energy integration treaties alongside forensic investigations of Mediterranean oil terminal transshipments.
