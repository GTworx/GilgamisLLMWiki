## Analytical Overview
### The Collapse of the Behavioral Economics House of Cards

In *The Fraudulent Science of Success*, investigative journalist Gideon Lewis-Kraus reports on the devastating data-fabrication and fraud scandals that tore through Harvard Business School and Duke University in the mid-2020s. Centered on the downfall of superstar behavioral scientists Francesca Gino and Dan Ariely—celebrated globally for books and TED talks on honesty, nudges, and decision-making—the expose documents how the Open Science movement and Data Colada auditors exposed fabricated datasets.

### Data Colada and the Forensic Unmasking

The article details the meticulous forensic methods deployed by Uri Simonsohn, Leif Nelson, and Joe Simmons of **Data Colada**:
- Detecting anomalies in Excel spreadsheets where data rows were copy-pasted, distributions were artificially manipulated, and responses were reversed to manufacture statistically significant findings on 'moral reminders' and 'signing at the top'.
- Documenting how university administrations, academic journals, and corporate consulting syndicates suppressed whistleblowers for years to protect lucrative brand reputations.

### Systemic Diagnosis of the Replication Crisis

Lewis-Kraus connects individual misconduct to systemic institutional rot: the commodification of academic science by publishers, business school consulting incentives, and a sensationalist media ecosystem that prioritized viral, counter-intuitive 'life-hacks' over rigorous, reproducible empirical reality.
