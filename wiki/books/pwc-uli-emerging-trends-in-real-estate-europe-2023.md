## Summary
Landmark 86-page annual benchmark from PwC and ULI surveying European real estate markets, detailing property asset repricing amidst higher borrowing costs, ESG retrofit mandates, and city investment rankings (London, Paris, Berlin, Madrid).

## Analytical Overview
Examines the structural challenges confronting European commercial and residential property: rising debt service costs, construction inflation, tenant affordability pressures, and strict carbon performance mandates. Ranks top European metropolitan investment destinations, tracking institutional capital rotation toward digital infrastructure (datacenters), logistics, student housing, and renewable energy assets.
