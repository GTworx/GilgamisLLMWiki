## Executive Summary

This 77-page flagship institutional research report by Morgan Stanley Research delivers a comprehensive cross-industry assessment of the long-term impact of quantum computing on artificial intelligence and global computing markets. Morgan Stanley evaluates the convergence of quantum processing units (QPUs) with classical GPU/TPU accelerator clusters, analyzing where quantum algorithms offer true structural speedups versus where classical Moore's law scaling and transformer optimizations will prevail. The report assesses quantum commercialization timelines, post-quantum cybersecurity liabilities, national security investments, and enterprise adoption curves across finance, pharmaceuticals, energy, and materials design.

---

## Key Strategic, Scientific & Engineering Takeaways

- **Quantum Computing and AI Symbiosis**: Analyzes quantum machine learning (QML) algorithms, concluding that while general-purpose LLM training will remain dominated by classical GPU clusters, quantum coprocessors will revolutionize specific sub-problems: molecular energy landscapes, combinatorial optimization, and synthetic data generation.
- **Commercialization Timeline & Milestones**: Constructs a phased timeline projecting NISQ error-mitigated utility (2024–2027), early logical fault-tolerant clusters (2028–2032), and scaled universal quantum advantage (post-2035).
- **Post-Quantum Cybersecurity Liability**: Explores the existential imperative for enterprise IT to migrate to NIST PQC standards, highlighting the 'Harvest Now, Decrypt Later' threat where adversarial nation-states intercept encrypted data for future quantum decryption.
- **Sovereign Quantum Geopolitics**: Evaluates national quantum initiatives across the U.S., China, the EU, and the UK, noting that quantum technology is treated alongside advanced semiconductors as a vital national security asset subject to strict export controls.
- **Ecosystem Value Capture**: Maps where financial value will accrue—from hardware component suppliers (dilution refrigerators, laser control systems, microwave transceivers) to cloud quantum providers (AWS Braket, Azure Quantum, IBM Quantum) and vertical application developers.
