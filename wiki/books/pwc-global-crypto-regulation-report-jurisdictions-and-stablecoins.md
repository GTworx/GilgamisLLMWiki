## Overview
Published by PwC's global regulatory network in late 2022/2023, this exhaustive 69-page global comparative report audits cryptocurrency, stablecoin, and DeFi regulatory frameworks across 25+ major jurisdictions. The report traces the post-FTX regulatory crackdown, analyzing the EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation, US SEC/CFTC enforcement actions, FATF Travel Rule implementations, and Basel Committee bank exposure rules.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Audits the European Union's MiCA regulation as the world's first comprehensive harmonized crypto market regime.
- **Key Finding**: Details stringent stablecoin reserve backing requirements, liquidity mandates, and redemption rights post-Terra/Luna collapse.
- **Key Finding**: Provides a country-by-country comparative matrix spanning licensing regimes, AML/KYC obligations, and taxation treatment.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "The era of regulatory arbitrage in crypto has ended. Jurisdictions are implementing rigorous supervisory frameworks prioritizing consumer protection, reserve audits, and systemic financial stability."
