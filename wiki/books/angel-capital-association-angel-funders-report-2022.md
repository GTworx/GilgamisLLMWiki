## Analytical Overview
### Definitive Benchmark Data on Angel Capital

*The Angel Funders Report 2022*, published by the Angel Capital Association (ACA), provides the most comprehensive data-driven snapshot of early-stage accredited angel investing across the United States and Canada. Derived from direct transaction-level data submitted by member angel groups, the report cuts through anecdotal hype to establish empirical truths about seed-stage startup financing.

### Core Quantitative Benchmarks

The report delivers vital statistics on early-stage transactions:
- **Pre-Money Valuations:** Tracking the sharp run-up and subsequent stabilization of seed and pre-seed pre-money valuations ($6M to $10M median).
- **Syndication and Check Size:** The expanding role of multi-group angel syndication, allowing individual angel investors to lead institutional-sized $1M to $2M seed rounds.
- **Sector Allocations:** Software and digital health continuing to capture the largest share of angel dollars, with climate tech and B2B SaaS accelerating.
- **Female and Minority Founders:** Benchmarking the measurable expansion of funding allocated to diverse founding teams.

### Practical Value for Founders and Angels

The report serves as an essential compass for entrepreneurs negotiating their first institutional seed term sheets and angels constructing diversified portfolios.
