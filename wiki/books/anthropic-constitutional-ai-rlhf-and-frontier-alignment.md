## Summary
Master AI safety, alignment engineering, and mechanistic interpretability compendium spanning 62 specialized papers. Compiles Anthropic's landmark 'Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback' (RLAIF), mathematical formulations of Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) vs Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), Chris Olah's transformer circuit interpretability, and representation engineering.

## Analytical Overview
This volume gathers the technical foundations of modern frontier AI alignment and mechanistic model transparency.

Key alignment methodologies:
1. **Constitutional AI & RLAIF**: Synthesizes Bai and Amodei’s landmark Anthropic architecture, which replaces massive, subjective human feedback loops with Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback (RLAIF) guided by a written 'Constitution' (derived from the UN Declaration of Human Rights and commercial safety principles), training models that are simultaneously helpful, honest, and harmless without human red-teaming trauma.
2. **Mechanistic Interpretability & Monosemanticity**: Details Chris Olah and Anthropic’s interpretability breakthroughs, decomposing transformer layers into monosemantic features using sparse autoencoders, identifying the exact neural circuits responsible for sycophancy, deception, and bias.
3. **Representation Engineering & Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)**: Analyzes mathematical alternatives to PPO (such as Rafailov et al.’s DPO) that directly optimize implicit reward functions in closed form, alongside representation engineering techniques that monitor and steer latent activation vectors during inference.
