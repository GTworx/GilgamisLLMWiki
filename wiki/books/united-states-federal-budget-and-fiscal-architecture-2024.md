## Summary
Two-volume executive fiscal blueprint outlining the $7.3 trillion United States federal budget for FY2025, detailing mandatory entitlement outlays (Social Security, Medicare), discretionary defense appropriations, and net interest costs.

## Analytical Overview
Decomposes the structural dynamics of US sovereign fiscal policy. Analyzes proposed tax reforms (25% billionaire minimum tax, 28% corporate rate), investments in domestic industrial capacity (CHIPS Act, IRA), and the rapid expansion of net federal interest expenses exceeding $1 trillion annually as public debt passes 120% of GDP.
