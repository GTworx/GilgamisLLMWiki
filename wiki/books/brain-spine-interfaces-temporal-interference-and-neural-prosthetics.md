## Summary
Breakthrough neuroengineering, surgical, and neurotechnology compendium. Highlights Courtine and Bloch's historic 2023 Nature study restoring natural walking in a paralyzed human via an implantable Brain-Spine Interface (BSI) digital bridge, Grossman and Boyden's Cell breakthrough on non-invasive deep brain stimulation via temporal interference electric fields, high-density neural probe architectures in Science, and Lieber Institute neurodevelopmental genomics.

## Analytical Overview
This volume documents the therapeutic frontier where electronic neuroprosthetics directly bypass biological neurological lesions.

Key neuroengineering breakthroughs:
1. **The Brain-Spine Interface (BSI) Digital Bridge**: Features Courtine and Bloch’s landmark 2023 *Nature* clinical triumph, detailing the implantation of a 64-channel WIMAGINE electrocorticographic (ECoG) grid over the sensorimotor cortex paired with a targeted dorsal-root spinal cord neurostimulator. Driven by real-time adaptive decoding algorithms, the system translates cortical motor intentions into biomimetic spinal stimulation within milliseconds, allowing a chronic tetraplegic patient to walk naturally over stairs, steep terrain, and ramps.
2. **Temporal Interference (TI) Non-Invasive Deep Brain Stimulation**: Compiles Grossman and Boyden’s seminal *Cell* treatise demonstrating that two high-frequency electrical fields (>1 kHz) applied externally across the skull with a slight frequency offset (e.g., 2 kHz vs. 2.01 kHz) cancel out in the cortex but generate a low-frequency envelope (10 Hz) in deep structures (hippocampus, subthalamic nucleus), selectively driving deep neural firing without activating overlying cortical tissue.
3. **Microelectrode Silicon Probes & Neuro-Epigenomics**: Details high-density silicon neural probe architectures in *Science*, evaluating long-term biocompatibility and neural recording bandwidth alongside the Lieber Institute for Brain Development's large-scale genomic mapping of psychiatric disorders.
