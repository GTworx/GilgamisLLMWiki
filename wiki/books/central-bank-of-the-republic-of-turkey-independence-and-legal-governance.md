## Summary
Two-volume legal and economic study examining the statutory and de facto independence of the Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey from the 2001 banking crisis reforms to the 2018 Presidential government system.

## Analytical Overview
Analyzes the 2001 amendments to Law No. 1211 that granted instrument independence to the TCMB to pursue price stability. Compares legal safeguards across the European System of Central Banks (ESCB) with the operational realities of presidential decree powers under the executive presidency, cross-referencing official 2024 Presidential Directorate of Legal Affairs presentations on legislative coordination.
