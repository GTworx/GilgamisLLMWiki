## Executive Summary

Subtitled *'Geçmişe Dönük Bilim Kurgu'* (*Retrospective Science Fiction*), *Sümerli Ludingirra* is one of **Muazzez İlmiye Çığ's** most celebrated literary and educational masterworks. Written in the intimate first-person autobiographical voice of **Ludingirra**, an elderly Sumerian poet, scribe, and teacher living in the ancient city of Nippur four thousand years ago, the novel synthesizes authentic cuneiform inscriptions, school tablets, and archaeological findings into a rich, living chronicle of Sumerian urban civilization, domestic family customs, schooling, medical treatments, poetry, and philosophy.

## Structural & Chapter Breakdown

| Chapter | Ludingirra's Life Stage & Theme |
|---|---|
| **I. Ben Ludingirra** | Ludingirra introduces himself as a retired scribe; his desire to write down the story of his people before Sumerian culture is swallowed by Amorites and Babylonians |
| **II. Çocukluğum ve Evimiz** | Domestic family life in Nippur; courtyard houses, food, beer brewing, bread making, clothing, and childhood games |
| **III. Tablet Okulundaki Yıllarım** | Entering the *Edubba*; learning cuneiform signs on clay; strict discipline, tests, and graduating as a royal scribe (*Dubsar*) |
| **IV. Şairlik ve Aşk** | Court poetry, love lyrics, singing to the gods in the temple; falling in love and marriage rituals |
| **V. Hekimlik ve Bilim** | Medical treatments: herbs, poultices, diagnostic surgical techniques, and exorcisms; mathematical calculations for canal construction |
| **VI. Şehrimiz Nippur ve Tapınaklar** | Topography of Nippur; the great temple of Enlil (Ekur); ziggurat rituals, musicians, and religious festivals |
| **VII. Geleceğe Mektup** | Ludingirra buries his clay tablets, trusting that thousands of years later, curious archaeologists will unearth his voice and remember Sumer |

## Key Theses & Analytical Frameworks

1. **Experiential Reconstruction of Antiquity**: By personifying the archaeological record through a relatable narrator, Çığ transforms dry museum artifacts and broken clay tablets into vibrant, breathing human reality.
2. **The Defense of Cultural Memory**: Ludingirra's underlying motivation—documenting his dying culture before it is submerged by foreign conquest—mirrors modern anxieties regarding cultural preservation and historical continuity.
3. **Rigorous Authenticity**: Every personal detail, school punishment, medical recipe, and hymn in the novel is directly derived from excavated cuneiform texts translated by Çığ and Kramer.
