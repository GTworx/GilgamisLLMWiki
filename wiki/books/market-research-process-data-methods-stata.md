## Executive Summary

Written by University of Melbourne and Otto-von-Guericke University scholars **Erik Mooi**, **Marko Sarstedt**, and **Irma Mooi-Reci** (2018, Springer, pp. 1, 5), *Market Research: The Process, Data, and Methods Using Stata* is an authoritative graduate and practitioner textbook bridging business problem formulation, empirical data collection, ethical research governance, and rigorous econometric/multivariate estimation executed directly in **Stata** (pp. 7–8).

Framing data as a foundational commodity of the digital economy—spanning web traffic, social networks, online surveys, and sensor telemetry (p. 7)—the authors pair accessible conceptual explanations with hands-on Stata workflows covering sampling, questionnaire design, hypothesis testing, ANOVA, linear/logistic regression, factor analysis, and cluster segmentation.

---

## Core Methodological Architecture

1. **Research Design & Data Integrity (Chapters 1–4)**: Clarifies when market research should (and should *not*) be conducted (p. 23), distinguishes exploratory/descriptive/causal designs, and addresses measurement validity, sampling bias, and survey ethics.
2. **Quantitative Modeling in Stata (Chapters 5–10)**: Walks readers step by step through descriptive diagnostics, hypothesis testing, OLS regression assumptions and diagnostics, principal-component/factor reduction, and market segmentation via hierarchical and k-means clustering.
3. **Decision Communication**: Emphasizes translating statistical outputs into transparent managerial recommendations without p-hacking or overstating causal certainty.
