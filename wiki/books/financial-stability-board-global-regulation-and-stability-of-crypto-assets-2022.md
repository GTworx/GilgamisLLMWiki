## Summary
Authoritative international policy assessment by the FSB evaluating the structural vulnerabilities, run risks, leverage, and cross-border regulatory gaps of crypto-assets and unbacked stablecoins.

## Analytical Overview
Published ahead of the 2022 algorithmic stablecoin collapses (Terra-Luna, FTX), this report warns of the growing transmission channels between decentralized digital assets and the traditional banking system. Outlines international standards for stablecoin reserve transparency, capital adequacy, and cross-border supervisory cooperation under the 'same activity, same risk, same regulation' principle.
