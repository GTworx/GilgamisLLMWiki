## Executive Summary

Edited with lucid introductions by **Lee Nichol** and prefaced by a personal reminiscence from **H.H. The Dalai Lama** (Routledge, 2003, ISBN 0-415-26174-0, pp. 4–5; 360 pp.), *The Essential David Bohm* is the definitive single-volume reader of maverick theoretical physicist and philosopher **David Bohm** (1917–1992). Bringing together twelve landmark texts spanning 1957 to 1989—including three previously unpublished chapters (p. 2)—the anthology traces how Bohm's dissent from orthodox Copenhagen quantum mechanics blossomed into an integrated cosmology of matter, consciousness, meaning, and collective social dialogue.

At the center of Bohm's lifework stands the **Implicate Order** (Ch. 3–4, 6, pp. 89–168, 194–209)—a holographic, "enfolded" deeper reality from which our familiar space-time **Explicate Order** continuously unfolds—paired in his later years with **Soma-Significance** (mind and matter as two sides of one activity of meaning, Ch. 5) and **Bohmian Dialogue** (Ch. 11–12, pp. 300–351), a transformative group practice for suspending rigid assumptions and healing human fragmentation.

## Core Philosophical & Scientific Breakthroughs

1. **Causal-Ontological Quantum Theory & Implicate Order (Ch. 1, 3–4, 6)**: Challenges mechanistic atomism with *undivided wholeness in flowing movement* (*holomovement*), proposing the quantum potential and active information as bridges between physics and meaning.
2. **Fragmentation, Ego & "Knowledge as Endarkenment" (Ch. 2, 7–10, pp. 50–88, 210–299)**: Diagnoses how rigid tacit thought-structures mistake mental maps for reality, generating ecological, political, and personal crises when unexamined assumptions operate automatically ("proprioception of thought").
3. **Dialogue as a New Creative Order (Ch. 11–12, pp. 300–351)**: Contrasts *discussion* (breaking ideas apart to win) with true *dialogue* (*dia-logos*, meaning flowing through a group), creating a shared participatory container for collective intelligence and leadership.
