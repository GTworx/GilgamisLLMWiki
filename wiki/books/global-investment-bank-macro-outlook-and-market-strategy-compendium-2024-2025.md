## Summary
Expansive 9-report institutional strategy compendium synthesizing cross-asset global market forecasts from HSBC, KKR, Bank of America, and Morgan Stanley spanning 2024–2025 and projecting macro themes toward 2030.

## Analytical Overview
Provides comprehensive institutional roadmaps for: equity quality factors; private equity exit bottlenecks and sponsor liquidity; global M&A resurgence; Bank of America's 'World in 2030' megatrends (demographic peaks, sovereign re-militarization, power infrastructure bottlenecks); and cross-asset allocations balancing corporate credit spreads against volatile rates.
