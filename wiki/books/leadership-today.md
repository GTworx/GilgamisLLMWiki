## Executive Summary

Edited by Woodbury University scholars **Joan Marques** and **Satinder Dhiman** (Springer, 2017, DOI `10.1007/978-3-319-31036-7`, Contents p. 6; 412 pp.), *Leadership Today* is a comprehensive scholarly anthology bridging soft-skill leadership competencies with ethical, contemplative, and systems-level organizational practice. Rather than treating leadership purely as command-and-control execution, the volume organizes 20+ peer-reviewed chapters across **Purpose, Trust, Moral Behavior, Values, Vision, Authenticity, Emotional Intelligence, Spirituality, Sustainability, Mindfulness, Empathy, Motivation, Communication, Creativity, Resilience, and Perseverance** (Part I overview, pp. 8–13).

Each chapter combines empirical literature reviews with concrete organizational case studies—such as **Buurtzorg Nederland**'s self-managing nursing teams analyzed through authentic and yogic leadership lenses (Ch. 6, pp. 93–105) and Richard Barrett's **Seven Levels of Leadership Consciousness** and cultural entropy framework (Ch. 4, pp. 64–78).

## Core Frameworks & Insights

1. **Trust, Morality & Cultural Entropy (Ch. 2–4, pp. 27–78)**: Maps the antecedents of leader-follower trust, applies classical moral theories to executive decision-making via a "Simple Moral Test" (p. 59), and quantifies organizational dysfunction as *Cultural Entropy* across seven developmental levels from Crisis Manager (Level 1) to Wisdom/Visionary (Level 7).
2. **Complexity Vision & Self-Managing Authenticity (Ch. 5–6, pp. 79–105)**: Reconceptualizes vision through Complexity Leadership Theory and systems inquiry ("vision as focus of inquiry") alongside Buurtzorg's decentralized autonomy model.
3. **Mindful, Sustainable & Compassionate Leadership (Ch. 7–19)**: Integrates emotional intelligence, workplace spirituality, ecological sustainability, and resilient communication into a unified twenty-first-century competency architecture.
