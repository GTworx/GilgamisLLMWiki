## Executive Summary

Authored by the engineering and product team at Kraków-based venture studio **Pragmatic Coders** with a Foreword by co-founder **Wiktor Żołnowski** (p. 3), this practitioner manual distills lessons from building over 80 early-stage digital products. Addressing the single biggest cost trap facing non-technical and first-time software founders—burning scarce seed capital coding unvalidated software—the guidebook translates Product Discovery theory into a visual, step-by-step pre-development workflow (pp. 2–5).

The manual moves cleanly through three sequential stages—**Problem Discovery & Definition** (pp. 8–57), **Solution Discovery & Definition** (pp. 58–87), and **Commercial Outcomes** (pp. 88–141)—before closing with a curated founder tool stack and practical glossary of product/VC terminology (TAM/SAM/SOM, POC, user story mapping, wireframes, pp. 147–159).

---

## Practical Pre-Build Playbook

1. **Problem Discovery & UX Research (pp. 8–57)**: Combines structured competitor benchmarking, market sizing, qualitative user interviews, and persona/pain-point synthesis to verify genuine market need before writing code.
2. **Solution Validation & Prototyping (pp. 58–87)**: Uses ideation workshops, low-fidelity wireframes, clickable prototypes, usability testing, and tree testing to de-risk UX and technical feasibility early.
3. **Founder Commercial Artifacts (pp. 88–141)**: Translates validated discovery into four concrete deliverables: a **Lean Canvas**, a monetizable **Business Model**, an investor-ready **Pitch Deck**, and a prioritized **MVP Roadmap** designed to ship minimum-scope value quickly.
