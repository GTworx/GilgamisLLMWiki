## Executive Summary
This 12-page clinical innovation report by the Tolan Center at University of Chicago Medicine outlines advanced patient safety protocols, multidisciplinary clinical pathways, and technology-enabled outpatient care delivery.

The report details clinical quality improvements across ambulatory surgical procedures, chronic care disease management for underserved South Side Chicago communities, rapid transition protocols from inpatient hospitalization to home health care, and the integration of electronic clinical decision-support algorithms to prevent adverse medication interactions and diagnostic delays.

---

## Core Thematic Insights & Structural Framework

- **Urban Health Equity and Underserved Care**: Formulates specialized community outreach and outpatient care delivery models addressing high rates of diabetes, hypertension, and asthma in urban populations.
- **Multidisciplinary Triage Pathways**: Analyzes collaborative care models integrating primary care physicians, clinical pharmacists, social workers, and nurse navigators into unified patient care teams.
- **Clinical Decision Support in Medication Safety**: Documents reductions in adverse drug events through automated EHR prescription screening and computerized physician order entry (CPOE).
- **Outpatient Surgical Quality Assurance**: Benchmarks post-operative infection rates and readmission statistics, demonstrating superior outcomes achieved through standardized pre-operative patient optimization.
