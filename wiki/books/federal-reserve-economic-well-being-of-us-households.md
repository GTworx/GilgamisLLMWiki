## Summary
Four-volume empirical sociology and household finance compendium analyzing the Federal Reserve Board's SHED report, detailing how inflation eroded household savings, the record surge in young adults living with parents, and the pandemic 'Great Reshuffle' residential sorting.

## Analytical Overview
Examines household financial vulnerability in the wake of 2021–2023 inflation shocks, tracking the percentage of American adults unable to cover a $400 unexpected expense with cash. Evaluates the demographic drivers behind young adults remaining in the parental home, spatial equilibrium shifts away from high-tax dense metros, and the nationwide state push for mandatory high school financial literacy curriculum.
