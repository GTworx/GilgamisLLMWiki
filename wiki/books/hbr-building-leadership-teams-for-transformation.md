## Analytical Overview
### Assembling the High-Stakes Transformation Coalition

The January–February 2022 edition of *Harvard Business Review*, focused on *Building a Leadership Team for Transformation*, provides an exhaustive, multi-author guide for boards and CEOs confronting radical market disruption. When an enterprise must pivot its core business model (e.g., from physical retail to digital, or fossil energy to renewables), traditional executive management structures inevitably fail.

### Core Strategic Focus Areas

The volume details:
- **Diagnosing Executive Team Dysfunction:** Identifying passive-aggressive alignment, departmental fiefdoms, and defensive inertia in legacy C-suites.
- **The Architecture of the Transformation Office:** Establishing a dedicated transformation governance board with direct CEO reporting lines to bypass operating friction.
- **Balancing Continuity and Disruption:** Managing the delicate tension between executives running the legacy cash-cow business and those building the high-growth digital future.
- **Executive Compensation Realignment:** Structuring equity and milestone incentives that tie C-suite payouts directly to multi-year transformation objectives.

### An Indispensable Field Guide

The edition serves as an essential manual for corporate directors and executives tasked with steering large-scale organizational renewal.
