## Summary
Interdisciplinary educational technology, pedagogical theory, and developmental psychology compendium spanning 16 papers. Analyzes the impact of generative AI on first-year university writing programs, cognitive scaffolding vs. cognitive offloading, cross-disciplinary child development perspectives between AI and developmental experts, and automated personal tutoring.

## Analytical Overview
This volume examines the profound pedagogical transformation occurring across primary, secondary, and higher education in the wake of conversational AI.

Key educational dimensions:
1. **The Reconceptualization of Writing Pedagogy**: Features empirical analyses of university first-year composition courses, examining how generative AI shifts writing instruction away from mechanical prose generation toward critical framing, dialectical evaluation, rhetorical positioning, and iterative synthesis.
2. **Child Cognitive Development & AI Companions**: Compiles cross-disciplinary studies between AI engineers and child psychologists, interrogating the cognitive impacts of young children forming emotional attachments to synthetic anthropomorphic AI agents, evaluating language acquisition, emotional regulation, and attention span.
3. **Socratic AI Tutoring**: Evaluates Khan Academy’s deployment of Socratic pedagogical guardrails—preventing AI tutors from supplying direct answers and instead scaffolding student inquiry through guided probing questions.
