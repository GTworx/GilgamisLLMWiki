## Overview
Authored by Ben Beckmann and researchers at General Electric's Global Research Center (GRC), this high-level industrial white paper explores the integration of distributed ledgers into industrial IoT (IIoT) ecosystems. GE examines autonomous machine-to-machine micropayments, aircraft engine turbine lifecycle tracking, and smart grid coordination without centralized single-points-of-failure.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Applies cryptographic consensus to heavy industrial equipment: tracking maintenance and flight hours for jet engines.
- **Key Finding**: Explores machine-to-machine (M2M) economic transactions where autonomous devices negotiate energy purchases.
- **Key Finding**: Evaluates edge-computing cryptographic constraints: running light-node consensus on resource-constrained industrial controllers.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Industrial blockchain transforms machines into autonomous economic actors capable of verifying their own maintenance history and transacting value securely."
