## Summary
Pioneering macro-epidemiological, clinical cardiology, and biodemographic compendium. Compiles The Lancet's landmark Global Burden of Disease Study, 2024 clinical breakthroughs in cardiovascular CT imaging perivascular coronary inflammation to predict heart attack risk, Bryan Johnson's extreme Blueprint longevity manifesto 'Don't Die', Saul Justin Newman's rigorous biodemographic demolition of supercentenarian 'Blue Zone' records (identifying widespread clerical errors and pension fraud), and mRNA vaccine advances.

## Analytical Overview
This volume unites macro-level global population health statistics with revolutionary cardiovascular clinical imaging and critical biodemographic inquiries into human lifespan.

Key analytical pillars:
1. **The Lancet Global Burden of Disease (GBD)**: Synthesizes comprehensive global epidemiological tracking of 333 diseases and injuries across 195 countries, charting the epidemiological transition from infectious maternal/neonatal diseases to non-communicable ischemic heart disease, stroke, diabetes, and cancer.
2. **Coronary Perivascular Inflammation & AI Tomography**: Features 2024 breakthroughs from the *Journal of Cardiovascular Computed Tomography* and Oxford research, evaluating how the Fat Attenuation Index (FAI) quantifies perivascular adipose tissue radiodensity to detect inflamed coronary plaques years before arterial luminal stenosis or rupture occurs.
3. **Longevity Demography & the 'Blue Zone' Debunking**: Contrasts Bryan Johnson’s radical, hyper-quantified biological age reversal protocol (*Don’t Die*) with Dr. Saul Justin Newman’s devastating Oxford biodemographic investigation into supercentenarians. Newman demonstrates that purported regional longevity hotspots (Okinawa, Sardinia, Ikaria) correlate almost perfectly with high poverty, low literacy, missing birth certificates, and systemic pension fraud where deceased relatives' deaths go unreported by surviving kin to cash pension checks.
