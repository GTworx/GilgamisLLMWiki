## Executive Summary

*Parkinson Hastalığı: Hasta ve Yakınları için El Kitabı (Gözden Geçirilmiş 5. Baskı)* (*Parkinson's Disease: Handbook for Patients and Caregivers*, Revised 5th Edition, published by Bayçınar Tıbbi Yayıncılık, İstanbul, August 2013, 98 pp., ISBN `978-605-88789-3-8`) authored by Istanbul University Cerrahpaşa Faculty of Medicine movement-disorders neurologists Prof. Dr. [[hulya-apaydin]] and Prof. Dr. [[sibel-ozekmekci]] alongside specialist physiotherapists [[semra-oguz]] and [[ismail-zileli]] is Türkiye's premier patient- and family-centered clinical handbook on living with Parkinson's disease (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, pp. 1–4, 10).

Bridging clinical neurology with practical daily rehabilitation, the handbook translates complex dopaminergic pathophysiology into compassionate, actionable guidance: distinguishing idiopathic Parkinson's disease from broader parkinsonism syndromes (pp. 1–6); explaining core motor symptoms (**bradykinesia** / *hareketlerde yavaşlama*, rigidity, resting tremor, postural instability) alongside non-motor symptoms; systematically detailing **pharmacotherapy**—including Levodopa optimization, **COMT inhibitors** (entacapone, tolcapone), **MAO-B inhibitors** (rasagiline, selegiline), and **dopamine agonists** (piribedil, pramipexole, ropinirole) (pp. 25–28); and providing an illustrated, step-by-step **physiotherapy and daily-movement exercise regimen** (*beden eğitimi hareketleri*, supine/seated/standing routines, gait and balance training) designed to preserve patient autonomy, dignity, and caregiver resilience (pp. 48–90) (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, İçindekiler, pp. 4–6).

## Key Arguments / Chapter Breakdown

* **Clinical Foundations: Parkinsonism, Etiology & Diagnosis (pp. 1–20 / PDF pp. 4–24)**:
  * *Demystifying Diagnosis*: Clarifies the distinction between secondary parkinsonism and idiopathic Parkinson's disease, explains substantia nigra dopaminergic neuron loss, and catalogs early warning signs and cardinal motor diagnostic criteria starting with **bradykinesia** (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, İçindekiler, p. 4).

* **Pharmacological Management & Symptom Control (pp. 21–47)**:
  * *Precision Dopaminergic Therapy*: Guides patients and families through Levodopa therapy, timing around meals, enzymatic enhancers (**COMT inhibitors** like entacapone/Stalevo and **MAO-B inhibitors** like rasagiline/selegiline), dopamine agonists, and managing motor fluctuations and side effects (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, İçindekiler, p. 5).

* **Physiotherapy, Daily Living & Caregiver Empowerment (pp. 48–90)**:
  * *Movement as Medicine*: Demonstrates when and how often to perform structured physical-therapy exercises (supine, seated, gait, posture, facial/speech mobility) and equips caregivers with daily ergonomic adaptations to safeguard quality of life (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, İçindekiler, p. 6).
