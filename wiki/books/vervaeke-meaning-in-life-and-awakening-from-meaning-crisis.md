## Overview
Authored by cognitive scientist John Vervaeke (University of Toronto), this comprehensive monograph captures the core insights of his celebrated series 'Awakening from the Meaning Crisis'. Vervaeke integrates 4E cognitive science (embodied, embedded, enactive, extended), Neoplatonism, and Buddhist mindfulness to define wisdom as optimized 'Relevance Realization'—overcoming self-deception and cultivating deep existential connectedness.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Defines the '4 Ways of Knowing': propositional, procedural, perspectival, and participatory knowing.
- **Key Finding**: Identifies the root of the modern Meaning Crisis: the reduction of knowing to mere propositional belief, severing existential connectedness.
- **Key Finding**: Solves the frame problem via 'Relevance Realization'—the subconscious cognitive ability to zero in on what matters in complex environments.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Meaning in life is not a theory you believe; it is a relational ecology of connectedness you inhabit through participatory and perspectival knowing."
