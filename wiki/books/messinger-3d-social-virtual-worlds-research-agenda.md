## Overview
Published in Decision Support Systems by Paul R. Messinger, Eleni Stroulia, and Kelly Lyons, this seminal 2008/2009 survey establishes a comprehensive research agenda for 3D social virtual worlds. The authors evaluate virtual commerce, behavioral avatar economics, social presence, and the technological barriers inhibiting mass adoption beyond early platforms like Second Life.

## Key Takeaways
- **Key Finding**: Categorizes virtual worlds into social spaces, goal-directed games, and specialized training simulators.
- **Key Finding**: Analyzes consumer behavior, virtual goods monetization, and micro-transaction mechanics.
- **Key Finding**: Identifies early technological bottlenecks: bandwidth latency, avatar interoperability, and cross-platform identity.

## Critical Excerpts & Analysis
> "Virtual worlds represent a paradigm shift from 2D web browsing to persistent, embodied 3D social interaction where economic exchange and identity play coalesce."
