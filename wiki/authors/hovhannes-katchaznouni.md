# Hovhannes Katchaznouni

## Biography
Hovhannes Katchaznouni (1868–1938) was an Armenian politician, founding leader of the Armenian Revolutionary Federation (Dashnaktsutyun), and the first Prime Minister of the First Republic of Armenia (1918–1919).
