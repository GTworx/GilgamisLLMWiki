## Biography & Context
Brian Solis is a world-renowned digital anthropologist, futurist, nine-time bestselling author, and Head of Global Innovation at ServiceNow (formerly Principal Analyst at Altimeter Group and Global Innovation Evangelist at Salesforce).

In [[infinite-how-visionary-leaders-transform-todays-businesses-into-ai-forward-companies]] (2026, co-authored with [[dave-wright]]), Solis diagnoses the 'Iteration Trap' of bolting generative AI onto legacy processes and formulates the **Infinite Company** operating model—orchestrating human–agent collaboration, cultural re-coding, and the Chief Workflow Officer role across enterprise platforms (source: BOOKS/AI/2026 Infinite- Leadership in AI Forward Companies.pdf, pp. 7–21, 116–204).
