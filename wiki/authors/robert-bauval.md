## Biography & Context

Robert Bauval (born 5 March 1948 in Alexandria, Egypt) is an author, lecturer, and former construction engineer of Belgian and British heritage. Living and working across the Middle East and Africa, Bauval developed an intimate understanding of architectural surveying and celestial alignments.

In 1989, Bauval formulated the **Orion Correlation Theory (OCT)**, which established that the spatial layout and relative magnitudes of the three Giza pyramids (Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure) mirror the three stars of Orion’s Belt (Alnitak, Alnilam, and Mintaka). In collaboration with Adrian Gilbert in *The Orion Mystery* (1994) and Graham Hancock in *Keeper of Genesis* (1996) and *The Master Game* (2011), Bauval argued that the Giza plateau constitutes an astronomical clock locking into the sky of 10,500 BC—the epoch of *Zep Tepi* ("The First Time") when Orion reached its lowest point on the meridian during the 25,920-year precessional cycle.
