## Biography & Institutional Context
Frank G. Holz is Professor and Chairman of the Department of Ophthalmology at the University of Bonn, Germany. A world authority on age-related macular degeneration and retinal imaging, he served as co-principal investigator of the PRIMA subretinal bionic implant trial.
