## Biography & Context
The Earthquake Department of AFAD (Deprem Dairesi Başkanlığı) oversees Türkiye's National Strong Motion and Weak Motion Seismic Observation Networks, operating over 1,100 stations. It coordinates national seismic hazard mapping, co-authors building seismic design codes (TBDY 2018), and administers the National Earthquake Research Program (UDAP).
