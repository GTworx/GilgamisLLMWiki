## Biography & Contributions

British Indian Civil Service officer and Persian scholar (1836–1922) who translated Rumi's *Masnavi*, Jami, and a landmark 500-quatrain scholarly English edition of Omar Khayyam featured in *The Sufistic Quatrains of Omar Khayyam* (1903).
