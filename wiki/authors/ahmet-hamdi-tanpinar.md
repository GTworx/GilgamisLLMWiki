# Author: Ahmet Hamdi Tanpınar

**Bio / Role**: Canonical Turkish modernist novelist, poet, essayist, and professor of 19th-century Turkish literature at Istanbul University (1901–1962). Author of *Huzur*, *Beş Şehir*, and the satirical masterpiece *Saatleri Ayarlama Enstitüsü* (1961), exploring cultural memory, Bergsonian temporality, and Turkey's East–West synthesis.
