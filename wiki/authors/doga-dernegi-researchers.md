## Biography & Context
Doğa Derneği (BirdLife Turkey) is Türkiye's premier nature-conservation organization partnering with ecologists, marine researchers (SAD-AFAG), and regional birding societies to inventory and protect Anatolia's Key Biodiversity Areas (*Önemli Doğa Alanları*).

In [[turkiyenin-onemli-doga-alanlari-ege-bolgesi]] (2007, featuring Eray Çağlayan, Cem O. Kıraç, Bahtiyar Kurt, Yalçın Savaş, Ferdi Akarsu, and Rukiye Tanyeri), the team catalogs the Aegean Region's wetlands, coastal habitats, endemic taxa, and conservation pressures (source: BOOKS/Math/03_EGE.pdf, pp. 1–7).
