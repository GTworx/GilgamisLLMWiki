## Biography & Context
**Kim Richard Nossal** is Professor Emeritus of Political Studies and former Director of the Centre for International and Defence Policy at **Queen's University** (and former Head of Political Science at McMaster University), widely recognized as a dean of Canadian foreign and defense policy studies. As co-editor of [[developing-the-super-soldier-enhancing-military-performance]] (*Developing the Super Soldier: Enhancing Military Performance*, 2017, with [[william-g-braun-iii]] and [[stefanie-von-hlatky]]), Nossal examines the institutional, political, and ethical governance of military performance enhancement across Western allied armed forces (source: [[developing-the-super-soldier-enhancing-military-performance]], pp. 1–6, viii).
