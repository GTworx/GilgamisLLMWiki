## Biography & Context
**Amy C. Edmondson, PhD**, is the Novartis Professor of Leadership and Management at **Harvard Business School** (source: [[leading-for-tomorrow]], Chapter 20 & Contributors, pp. 247–256, 329). Ranked **#1 on the Thinkers50** global list of management thinkers in both 2021 and 2023, Edmondson earned her PhD in organizational behavior, AM in psychology, and AB in engineering and design from Harvard University, having begun her early career working as Chief Engineer for architect and futurist Buckminster Fuller.

Edmondson pioneered the empirical construct of **team psychological safety**—defined as a shared belief that a team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking, speaking up with questions, and admitting errors without fear of humiliation or retaliation. Author of more than eighty scholarly papers and several foundational books, including *Teaming* (2012), *The Fearless Organization* (2018), and *Right Kind of Wrong: The Science of Failing Well* (2023, Financial Times and Schroders Business Book of the Year), she contributes Chapter 20 (*"Failing Well to Succeed Tomorrow"*) to [[leading-for-tomorrow]] (2026), distinguishing between preventable basic errors, complex systems breakdowns, and **intelligent failures** in novel territory.
