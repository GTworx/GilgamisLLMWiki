## Biography & Context
**Christopher Miller** is Professor of International History at the **Fletcher School of Law and Diplomacy at Tufts University** and Nonresident Senior Fellow at the American Enterprise Institute (AEI), specializing in the history of technology, US–China–Russia geopolitics, and economic statecraft. His international bestseller [[chip-war-the-fight-for-the-worlds-most-critical-technology]] (*Chip War: The Fight for the World's Most Critical Technology*, 2022) won the *Financial Times* Business Book of the Year Award and the Arthur Ross Book Award from the Council on Foreign Relations (source: [[chip-war-the-fight-for-the-worlds-most-critical-technology]], pp. 1–22, 473).

In [[chip-war-the-fight-for-the-worlds-most-critical-technology]], Miller traces the seventy-five-year co-evolution of Silicon Valley, the Pentagon's **Offset Strategies**, East Asian manufacturing ecosystems, **ASML**'s extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography monopoly, and [[morris-chang]]'s **TSMC** pure-play foundry in Taiwan, demonstrating how ultra-concentrated semiconductor chokepoints have become the decisive arena of 21st-century military deterrence and artificial intelligence supremacy.
