## Biography & Context
**Siddharth Pritam** is a mathematician and faculty member in the Department of Computer Science at the **Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI)**, India (source: [[the-non-orientable-topology-of-condorcets-paradox]], Title Page, p. 1). Formerly a postdoctoral researcher at INRIA (DataShape) in France and the Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA), Pritam specializes in computational topology, topological data analysis (TDA), persistent homology, simplicial collapse, and nerve complexes of posets.

In [[the-non-orientable-topology-of-condorcets-paradox]] (2026), co-authored with [[ori-livson]] and [[mikhail-prokopenko]], Pritam brings combinatorial and algebraic topology to social choice theory—constructing explicit triangulations of preference nerves $\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{U})$ and $\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{V})$ and proving via 2-sheeted covering maps and Euler characteristic calculations ($\chi(\mathbb{RP}^2) = \frac{1}{2}\chi(S^2) = 1$) that realising a contradictory Condorcet cycle transforms the preference sphere into the non-orientable real projective plane $\mathbb{RP}^2$ (Theorem 5 & Appendix A.1, Theorem A.2, pp. 11–13, 21–23).
