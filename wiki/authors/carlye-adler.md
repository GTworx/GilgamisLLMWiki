## Biography & Context
Carlye Adler is an award-winning New York–based business journalist and bestselling co-author who was twice named one of the most influential business journalists under 30 by TJFR and selected for *The Best Business Stories of the Year*.

Collaborating with [[marc-benioff]] on [[behind-the-cloud]] (2009) as well as *The Business of Changing the World*, Adler translated Salesforce's first decade of cloud innovation into 111 crisp operational plays (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Behind the Cloud_ The Untold Story of How Salesforce.com Went from Idea to Billion-Dollar Company-and Revolutionized an Industry   ( PDFDrive ).pdf, pp. 2–6, 291).
