## Biography & Context
Samer Salam is a Principal Engineer at Cisco Systems specializing in system and software architecture for networking products, semantic technologies, machine reasoning, IoT data management, M2M communications, and carrier-grade Layer 2 protocols.

In [[internet-of-things-from-hype-to-reality]] (2019, co-authored with [[ammar-rayes]]), Salam articulates how IoT bends the classic IP protocol hourglass—requiring a 'second thin waist' for application-service normalization and semantic interoperability (source: BOOKS/IoT/2019_Book_InternetOfThingsFromHypeToReal.pdf, pp. 5, 11, 26).
