## Biography & Context
The Dyadic Behavioral Science Consortium brings together clinical psychologists, relationship scientists, and behavioral econometricians analyzing micro-interactions, emotional contagion, mutual escalation, and non-linear affective feedback loops in interpersonal and romantic dyads.
