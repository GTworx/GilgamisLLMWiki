## Biography & Institutional Context
Ibn al-Nafis was an Arab polymath and physician renowned for being the first to describe the pulmonary circulation of blood (preceding William Harvey by 300 years). His philosophical novel 'Theologus Autodidactus' combines medical science, spontaneous generation, and rationalist cosmology, standing as one of the earliest Arabic science fiction works.
