## Biography & Context
Curtis W. Johnson (1938–2023) was a senior policy advisor, former Chief of Staff to Minnesota Governor Arne Carlson, Chair of the Twin Cities Metropolitan Council, and co-founder of Education Evolving.

In [[disrupting-class-how-disruptive-innovation-will-change-the-way-the-world-learns]] (2008), co-authored with [[clayton-m-christensen]] and [[michael-b-horn]], Johnson analyzed the political consensus and autonomous charter/team governance structures needed to enable school innovation.
