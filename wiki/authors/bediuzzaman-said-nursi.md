# Bediüzzaman Said Nursi

## Biography
Bediüzzaman Said Nursi (1877–1960) was a prominent Islamic scholar and thinker from Turkey, authoring the *Risale-i Nur* collection which harmonized Islamic theology with modern science and spiritual psychology.
