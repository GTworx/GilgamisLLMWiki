## Biography & Context
STAT News (founded in Boston in 2015) is a premier health, medicine, and life-sciences journalism organization covering biomedical breakthroughs, biotech boardrooms, clinical trials, and FDA policy.

In [[beyond-cancer-the-expanding-reach-of-cell-and-gene-therapies]] (September 2026), STAT journalists (including Isabella Cueto, Megan Molteni, Paul Knoepfler, Jason Mast, and Allison DeAngelis) chronicle CAR-T's leap into autoimmunity, in-utero and bespoke CRISPR gene editing, FDA oversight shifts, and single-shot in-vivo CAR-T engineering (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2026 STAT_Ebook_BeyondCancer_September2026.pdf, pp. 2–5, 7–69).
