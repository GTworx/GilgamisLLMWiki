## Biography & Context
Tom Millar is a senior cybersecurity leader at the United States Computer Emergency Readiness Team (US-CERT) / Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) within the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, specializing in interagency cyber incident coordination and automated indicator sharing.

In [[computer-security-incident-handling-guide-nist-sp-800-61r2]], Millar co-authored the federal reporting, cross-agency coordination, and threat-sharing frameworks.
