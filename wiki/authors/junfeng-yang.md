## Biography & Context
Junfeng Yang is Professor of Educational Technology at the School of Education, Hangzhou Normal University (China), specializing in smart learning environments and learner experience evaluation.

In [[educational-technology-a-primer-for-the-21st-century]] (2019), co-authored with [[ronghuai-huang]] and [[j-michael-spector]], Yang develops the Learner Experience (LX) indicators and technology integration frameworks.
