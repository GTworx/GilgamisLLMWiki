## Biography & Context
Roy F. Baumeister is one of the world's most cited social psychologists, Professor Emeritus at Florida State University, and author of over 700 publications and 40 books including *Willpower* and *The Cultural Animal*. His foundational research established ego depletion, the fundamental human need to belong, and the universal evolutionary asymmetry codified in his landmark 2001 treatise 'Bad is Stronger Than Good'.
