## Biography & Context

John Grigsby is a British historian, researcher, and author specializing in comparative mythology, ancient religions, and archaeoastronomy. Holding a degree in European History, Grigsby collaborated with Graham Hancock and Robert Bauval on the landmark investigative work *The Mars Mystery* (1998), focusing on cross-cultural mythological records of celestial dragon-combats and apocalyptic impacts. Grigsby subsequently authored works on Arthurian mythology and the Celtic Grail mysteries (*Warriors of the Wasteland*).
