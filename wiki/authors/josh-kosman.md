## Biography & Context
Josh Kosman is an investigative business journalist who covered leveraged buyouts and M&A for *Buyouts Newsletter*, *The Deal*, *Mergermarket* (where he headed North American operations), and the *New York Post* after beginning his career at the Warburg Pincus–acquired *Middletown Press*.

In [[the-buyout-of-america]] (2009), Kosman exposes the debt-loading mechanics, dividend recapitalizations, service cuts, and systemic credit-crisis hazards of the 2001–2007 private equity buyout boom (source: BOOKS/Private Equity/2009 The_Buyout_of_America_-_Josh_Kosman.pdf, pp. 9–15).
