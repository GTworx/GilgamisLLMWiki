## Biography & Context

Dean Cheng is a leading American defense analyst and former Senior Research Fellow for Chinese Political and Military Affairs at the Asian Studies Center of The Heritage Foundation, currently Senior Advisor to the China Program at the U.S. Institute of Peace. Fluent in Mandarin, Cheng has written extensively on Chinese military modernization, aerospace capabilities, information warfare, and Chinese strategic doctrine.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Cheng authored Chapter 10 ("An Overview of Chinese Thinking About Deterrence"), providing a comprehensive deconstruction of the Chinese concept of *weishe* (coercion/deterrence), explaining how Beijing views deterrence not merely as passive war prevention, but as an active, offensive-defensive instrument to shape adversary behavior and secure strategic objectives without fighting.
