## Biography & Context
Lane DeNicola is an anthropologist and Science and Technology Studies (STS) scholar researching geospatial information systems, satellite imaging, ubiquitous computing, and the intersection of ethnography and industrial interaction design.

In [[the-digital-as-para-world]] (2011) and his *Geomedia* chapter in [[digital-anthropology]] (2012), DeNicola frames digital technologies as a 'para-world' intersecting pre-existing physical spaces and social orders (source: BOOKS/Anthropology_/denicola-in-clarke The Digital as Para-world.pdf, pp. 1–2).
