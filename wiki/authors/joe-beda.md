## Biography & Context
Joe Beda is a cofounder of Kubernetes, cofounder of Heptio (acquired by VMware), and former Principal Engineer at VMware and Staff Software Engineer at Google, where he pioneered Google Compute Engine and cloud-native container orchestration.

As co-author of [[kubernetes-up-and-running]] (3rd Ed., 2022), Beda illuminates how Kubernetes decouples application teams from underlying datacenter hardware through standardized APIs and Pods (source: BOOKS/Cloud/kubernetesup&running.pdf, pp. 2, 23–35).
