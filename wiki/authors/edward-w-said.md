# Author: Edward W. Said

**Bio / Role**: Palestinian-American literary theorist, cultural critic, and University Professor of English and Comparative Literature at Columbia University (1935–2003). Widely recognized as a founder of postcolonial studies, Said examined how Western scholarship, literature, and imperial power mutually constructed representations of the Middle East and Asia.
