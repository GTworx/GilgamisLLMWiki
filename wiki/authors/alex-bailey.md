## Biography & Context
**Alex Bailey** is a British organizational psychologist, researcher, and the CEO and co-founder of **Bailey & French**, a global leadership and cultural consultancy headquartered in Brighton, UK (source: [[rapid-reculturing]], About the Authors, p. 295). Over the past two decades, Bailey has led large-scale behavioral and cultural transformation programs across multinational corporations, public-sector ministries, healthcare trusts, and non-governmental organizations in more than forty countries.

Anchored in empirical positive psychology, social neuroscience, and human-centered systems design, Bailey's work challenges top-down corporate change programs that treat employees as mechanical cogs. In [[rapid-reculturing]] (2026), co-authored with [[kerri-oneill]], he introduces the paradigm of **Organizational Rewilding**—demonstrating how four foundational pillars (**Human**, **Connect**, **Simplicity**, and **Empowerment**) and the five-step **HUMAN Model** rapidly restore psychological energy, cross-silo collaboration, and self-sustaining performance across complex enterprises.
