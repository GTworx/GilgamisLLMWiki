## Biography & Context

Paul van Hooft is a Senior Strategic Analyst at The Hague Centre for Strategic Studies (HCSS) and former post-doctoral fellow at the Security Studies Program (SSP) at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). He specializes in grand strategy, extended nuclear deterrence, alliance commitments, and maritime security in East Asia and Europe.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, van Hooft authored Chapter 6 ("The US and Extended Deterrence"), analyzing the structural credibility problems inherent in extended nuclear guarantees.
