## Biography & Context
Dr. John Hunt is a technology trainer, software architect, and Managing Director of Midmarsh Technology Ltd (Chippenham, Wiltshire, UK), having authored over a dozen Springer textbooks on object-oriented and functional programming.

In the two-volume 2019 Springer *Undergraduate Topics in Computer Science* treatise—*A Beginners Guide to Python 3 Programming* (441 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-030-20290-3`) and *Advanced Guide to Python 3 Programming* (494 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-030-25943-3`), synthesized in [[python-and-r-scientific-computing-foundations-for-ai-and-data-science]]—Hunt covers the complete Python 3 ecosystem from object-oriented design, closures, currying, and metaclasses to Matplotlib/Seaborn visualization, SQL/NoSQL databases, asyncio/multiprocessing concurrency, and reactive programming (source: BOOKS/AI/2019_Book_ABeginnersGuideToPython3Progra.pdf, pp. 1–9; BOOKS/AI/2019_Book_AdvancedGuideToPython3Programm.pdf, pp. 1–9).
