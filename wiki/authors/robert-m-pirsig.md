## Biography & Context
Robert M. Pirsig (1928–2017) was an American philosopher and writer educated in biochemistry, philosophy (including studies at Banaras Hindu University in India), and journalism at the University of Minnesota and the University of Chicago.

In his 1974 philosophical masterpiece [[zen-and-the-art-of-motorcycle-maintenance]] (and its 1991 sequel *Lila*), Pirsig formulated the **Metaphysics of Quality**, healing the Western schism between **Romantic** (aesthetic/intuitive) and **Classical** (rational/technological) understanding by showing that pre-intellectual **Quality** (Greek *Arete* / Sanskrit *Dharma*) is the primordial source of both subject and object.
