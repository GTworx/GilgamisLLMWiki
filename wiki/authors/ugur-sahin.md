## Biography & Institutional Context
Prof. Dr. Uğur Şahin is a Turkish-German physician, immunologist, CEO of BioNTech, and Professor of Translational Oncology at the University of Mainz. A pioneer in cancer immunotherapy and mRNA engineering, he co-developed the BNT162b2 COVID-19 vaccine.
