## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Hülya Apaydın is a senior neurologist in the Parkinson's Disease and Movement Disorders Unit, Department of Neurology, Istanbul University Cerrahpaşa Faculty of Medicine.

Co-authoring [[parkinson-hastaligi-hasta-ve-yakinlari-icin-el-kitabi]] (5th Ed., 2013), she provides accessible clinical guidance on Parkinson's diagnosis, Levodopa/COMT/MAO-B pharmacotherapy, and holistic care for Turkish patients and families (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, pp. 1–5, 10).
