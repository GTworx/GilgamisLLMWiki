## Biography & Context

Brigadier General Prof. Dr. Paul Ducheine is Professor of Cyber Operations and Cyber Security at the Faculty of Military Sciences, Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA), and Professor of Law of Military Cyber Operations at the Amsterdam Center for International Law, University of Amsterdam. A former combat engineer officer and military legal advisor, Ducheine is a leading European authority on the international legal frameworks governing cyber operations and targeted military coercion.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Ducheine (with Peter Pijpers) authored Chapter 25 ("The Missing Component in Deterrence Theory: The Legal Framework"), constructing a comprehensive matrix linking deterrence instruments to international legal bases (UN Charter Article 51 self-defence, countermeasures, retorsion, and necessity).
