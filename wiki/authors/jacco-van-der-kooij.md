## Biography & Context
Jacco van der Kooij is the founder of Winning by Design (San Francisco), a global revenue-architecture advisory firm that pioneered scientific, bowtie-based recurring-revenue operating models and the SPICED diagnostic framework for B2B SaaS companies.

Across [[blueprints-for-a-saas-sales-organization]] (2018, with [[fernando-pizarro]]) and [[the-saas-sales-method-for-customer-success-and-account-managers]] (2018, with [[dominique-levin]]), van der Kooij codifies buyer-centric pods, Impact-over-Needs diagnosis, and Net Revenue Retention above 130% (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, pp. 6–9, 32; BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, pp. 8–16).
