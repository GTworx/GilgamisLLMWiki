## Biography & Context
Nicholas Humphrey is a British theoretical psychologist and Emeritus Professor at the London School of Economics. Known for his work on blindsight, the social function of intellect, and the evolution of phenomenal consciousness (*A History of the Mind*, *Soul Dust*, *Sentience*), Humphrey analyzes consciousness as an evolved self-enchanting illusion that bestows immense adaptive value upon human existence.
