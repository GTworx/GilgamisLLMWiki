# Author: Francis Fukuyama

**Bio / Role**: American political scientist, political economist, and senior fellow at Stanford University (formerly Deputy Director of the U.S. State Department Policy Planning Staff and RAND consultant). Author of *The End of History and the Last Man* (1992), *The Origins of Political Order*, and *Identity*, analyzing Hegelian recognition (*thymos*), liberal democracy, and state capacity.
