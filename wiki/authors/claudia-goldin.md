# Claudia Goldin

## Biography
Claudia Goldin (b. 1946) is the Henry Lee Professor of Economics at Harvard University and 2023 Nobel Laureate in Economic Sciences, acclaimed for over four decades of historical research on women in the labor market and the gender wage gap.
