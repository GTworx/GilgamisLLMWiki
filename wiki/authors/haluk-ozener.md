# Haluk Özener

## Biography
Haluk Özener is a Turkish geodesist, seismologist, and Director of Boğaziçi University Kandilli Observatory and Earthquake Research Institute, directing national geodetic GPS slip monitoring along the North and East Anatolian Faults.
