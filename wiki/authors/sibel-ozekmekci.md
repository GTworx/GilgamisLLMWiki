## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Sibel Özekmekçi is an emeritus professor of neurology from Istanbul University Cerrahpaşa Faculty of Medicine's Parkinson's Disease and Movement Disorders Unit and a pioneer of Parkinson's patient advocacy and education in Türkiye.

In [[parkinson-hastaligi-hasta-ve-yakinlari-icin-el-kitabi]] (5th Ed., 2013, with [[hulya-apaydin]], [[semra-oguz]], and [[ismail-zileli]]), she demystifies dopaminergic therapy and multidisciplinary care (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, pp. 1–5, 10).
