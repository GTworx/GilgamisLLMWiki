# Author: Robert Arnot

**Bio / Role**: Scottish-American literary editor (M.A.) and author of *The Vine in Symbolism* who edited the 1903 Universal Classics Library compendium *The Sufistic Quatrains of Omar Khayyam*, uniting FitzGerald, Whinfield, Nicolas, and Heron-Allen under a comparative Sufi-philosophical introduction.
