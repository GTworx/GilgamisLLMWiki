## Biography & Context

Sir Lawrence Freedman (born 1948) is Professor Emeritus of War Studies at King's College London, widely regarded as the dean of modern British strategic studies. Knighted in 2003 and appointed to the UK Privy Council in 2009 (where he served on the Chilcot Inquiry into the Iraq War), Freedman is the author of classic works including *The Evolution of Nuclear Strategy* (1981), *Strategy: A History* (2013), and *The Future of War: A History* (2017).

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Freedman contributed the introductory chapter tracing the conceptual history of deterrence research through its major historical waves—from the early nuclear revolution to contemporary multi-domain deterrence.
