## Biography & Context
Brent Adamson is a Distinguished Vice President at Gartner (formerly CEB) and co-author of *The Challenger Sale* and *The Challenger Customer*, advising global B2B organizations on commercial insight and stakeholder sense-making.

In [[the-challenger-sale-challenge-yourself-action-book]] (2021), Adamson and [[matthew-dixon]] codify the 4-step **Commercial Teaching Reframe** (*Share Reframe*, *Introduce Impact*, *New Way*, *Reveal Value*).
