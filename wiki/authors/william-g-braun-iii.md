## Biography & Context
**William G. Braun III** is Professor of Practice at the **Strategic Studies Institute (SSI) of the U.S. Army War College** in Carlisle, Pennsylvania, and a retired U.S. Army Aviation Colonel specializing in defense policy, landpower strategy, and military organizational transformation. As co-editor (with [[stefanie-von-hlatky]] and [[kim-richard-nossal]]) of [[developing-the-super-soldier-enhancing-military-performance]] (*Developing the Super Soldier: Enhancing Military Performance*, 2017), Braun helped lead the Kingston Conference on International Security examining how modern armed forces integrate neuro-cognitive, physical, psychological, and ethical soldier enhancements (source: [[developing-the-super-soldier-enhancing-military-performance]], pp. 1–6, vii).
