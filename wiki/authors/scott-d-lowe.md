## Biography & Context
Scott D. Lowe is a veteran enterprise IT architect, former CIO, prolific technical author, and cofounder of ActualTech Media specializing in virtualization, flash storage, software-defined datacenters, and hyperconverged infrastructure.

In [[enterprise-cloud-for-dummies-nutanix-special-edition]] (2016), Lowe explains how collapsing compute and storage into hyperconverged Enterprise Clouds unites public-cloud agility with private-cloud control (source: BOOKS/Cloud/Book Enterprise Cloud for Dummies_.pdf, pp. 3–11, 27–40).
