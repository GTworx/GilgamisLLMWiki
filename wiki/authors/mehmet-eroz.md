## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Mehmet Eröz (1930–1986, born in Söke, Aydın) was a pioneering Turkish sociologist and ethnographer at Istanbul University's Faculty of Economics who earned his PhD (1965) on Yörük socio-economic organization, his docentship (1971) on Marxist critique, and his professorship (1977) on Alevism and Bektashism.

In his classic posthumous monograph [[yorukler]] (1991), Eröz preserves primary highland (*yayla*) fieldwork documenting the social, religious, linguistic, and pastoral-economic institutions of Anatolia's nomadic Turkish tribes (source: BOOKS/Anthropology_/Yorukler_Mehmet_Eroz.pdf, pp. 2, 7–10).
