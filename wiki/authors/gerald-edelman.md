## Biography & Context
Gerald M. Edelman (1929–2014) was an American biologist who shared the 1972 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering the structure of antibody molecules. He later founded The Neurosciences Institute and developed 'Neural Darwinism' (the Theory of Neuronal Group Selection), explaining brain development and primary/higher-order consciousness through somatic selection, reentry, and dynamic core thalamocortical loops.
