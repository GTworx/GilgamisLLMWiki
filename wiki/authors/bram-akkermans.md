## Biography & Context
Bram Akkermans is Professor of European Private Law at Maastricht University and Co-Director of the Maastricht European Private Law Institute (M-EPLI), specializing in comparative property law (*numerus clausus* across Civil Law and Common Law Equity).

In [[introduction-to-law]] (2017), Akkermans co-edits the volume and authors the comparative analysis of Property Law across Civil and Common Law systems.
