## Biography & Context

Prof. Dr. Isabelle Duyvesteyn is Professor of International Studies and Global History at the Institute of History, Leiden University. An elected member of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences (KNAW), Duyvesteyn is an internationally prominent scholar of the history of war, strategic culture, emotions in international conflict, and non-state violence.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Duyvesteyn (with Samuel Zilincik) authored Chapter 24 ("Deterrence: A Continuation of Emotional Life with the Admixture of Violent Means"), examining how fear, anger, pride, and humiliation shape state behavior in military crises.
