## Biography & Context

İbrahim Gösterir (born 1965 in Çorum) is a Turkish educator, folklorist, writer, and poet (writing under the pen name *Ozanî*). He graduated from Ankara University's Faculty of Language, History, and Geography (DTCF) in 1986 and has served as a history teacher and cultural researcher across Turkey (source: BOOKS/Art/73010,cilt1pdf.pdf, p. 33).

Gösterir has dedicated his scholarly career to documenting, archiving, and analyzing the oral traditions, dialects, and folk literature of the Çorum and Central Anatolian regions. His published works include the comprehensive *Çorum Yöresi Ağızları Sözlüğü* (Dictionary of Çorum Regional Dialects, 500 pp., 4th ed. 2020), *Ölüm Var Ayrılık Var - Çorum Manileri* (360 pp., 2011), *Bahar Türküleri* (2014), *Çorum Halk Kültüründe Gülmece* (Humor in Çorum Folk Culture, 320 pp., 2016), as well as monographic studies on regional minstrels *Âşık Haydar Kılıç (Ozan Garip)* (2017) and *Alıcıklı Âşık Hicabî* (2017).

As editor and compiler of *Âşık Borani: Söz İnsanın Aynasıdır* (2020), Gösterir performed a comprehensive decade-long critical compilation of Borani's printed books, archival manuscripts, newspaper columns, and oral performances into a definitive two-volume academic edition (source: BOOKS/Art/73010,cilt1pdf.pdf, pp. 31–36).
