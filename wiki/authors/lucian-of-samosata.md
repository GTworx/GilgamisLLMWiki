## Biography & Institutional Context
Lucian of Samosata was a Hellenized Syrian rhetorician and satirist. His masterpiece, 'A True Story' (Verae Historiae), is universally recognized as the earliest surviving work of science fiction, anticipating space travel, extraterrestrial warfare, artificial life, and optical surveillance mirrors.
