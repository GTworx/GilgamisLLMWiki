## Biography & Context
Daniel Kirsch is Managing Director at Hurwitz & Associates and an enterprise technology analyst specializing in machine learning governance, hybrid cloud security, and DevOps.

Co-authoring [[machine-learning-for-dummies-ibm-limited-edition]] (2018) with [[judith-hurwitz]], Kirsch maps supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement, and instance-based learning algorithms onto real-world fraud, healthcare, and supply-chain deployments (source: BOOKS/AI/2017 Book ML for Dummies by IBM_.pdf, pp. 2–16, 28–61).
