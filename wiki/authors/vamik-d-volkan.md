## Biography & Context
Vamık D. Volkan is Emeritus Professor of Psychiatry at the University of Virginia, a Training and Supervising Psychoanalyst, and Nobel Peace Prize nominee. A pioneer in international political psychology, Volkan coined the concepts of 'chosen trauma' and 'chosen glory', explaining how collective historical humiliations are transgenerationally mythologized to fuel ethnic conflict, political paranoia, and mass violence.
