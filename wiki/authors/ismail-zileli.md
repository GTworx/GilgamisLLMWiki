## Biography & Context
Specialist Physiotherapist İsmail Zileli specializes in movement rehabilitation and caregiver-assisted exercise design for chronic neurological conditions.

Co-authoring [[parkinson-hastaligi-hasta-ve-yakinlari-icin-el-kitabi]] (2013), he contributed practical home-exercise routines supporting mobility and quality of life in Parkinson's care (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, pp. 1, 6).
