## Biography & Context
Dr. Ammar Rayes is a Distinguished Engineer at Cisco's Customer Experience Technology Office, former Director of Traffic Engineering at Bellcore (formerly Bell Labs), and founding President of the International Society of Service Innovation Professionals (ISSIP). He holds over 30 patents and has authored more than 100 refereed publications on IoT, machine learning, network analytics, and data security.

In [[internet-of-things-from-hype-to-reality]] (2019, 2nd Ed., co-authored with [[samer-salam]]), Rayes synthesizes the full protocol stack, Fog Computing architecture, IoT security, and blockchain integration underpinning the Fourth Industrial Revolution (source: BOOKS/IoT/2019_Book_InternetOfThingsFromHypeToReal.pdf, pp. 7, 25).
