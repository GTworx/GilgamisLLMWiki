# Lloyd S. Shapley

## Biography
Lloyd S. Shapley (1923–2016) was an American mathematician and economist at UCLA and RAND Corporation, 2012 Nobel Laureate, renowned for the Gale-Shapley deferred acceptance algorithm and the Shapley value in cooperative game theory.
