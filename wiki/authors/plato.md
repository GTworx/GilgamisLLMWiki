# Author: Plato

**Bio / Role**: Classical Athenian philosopher (c. 428–348 BCE), student of Socrates, teacher of Aristotle, and founder of the Academy, whose surviving dialogues (*Republic*, *Symposium*, *Phaedo*, *Apology*, *Theaetetus*, *Timaeus*, *Laws*) established the core architecture of Western metaphysics, epistemology, ethics, and political theory.
