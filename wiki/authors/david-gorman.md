## Biography & Context
David Gorman is a Senior Technical Staff Member and Enterprise Blockchain Architect at IBM specializing in Hyperledger Fabric network topology, distributed systems security, and enterprise integration.

In [[getting-started-with-enterprise-blockchain]], Gorman co-authors the architectural blueprints for consortium governance, endorsement policies, and private data collections.
