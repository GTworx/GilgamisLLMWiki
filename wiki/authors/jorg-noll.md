## Biography & Context

Dr. Jörg Noll is Assistant Professor of Military Operational Art and Science at the Faculty of Military Sciences, Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA). He specializes in contemporary deterrence operations, NATO forward deployments, and conflict stability in Eastern Europe.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Noll (with Osman Bojang and Sebastiaan Rietjens) authored Chapter 7 ("Deterrence by Punishment or Denial? The eFP Case"), evaluating NATO's enhanced Forward Presence battlegroups in the Baltic states and Poland.
