## Biography & Contributions

Retired Turkish Major General (b. 1947, Gerze, Sinop), former Commander of the Hakkâri Mountain and Commando Brigade (1993–1995), founder of the Rights and Equality Party (HEPAR), and prolific author of military memoirs (*Unutulanlar Dışında Yeni Bir Şey Yok*), war fiction (*Cehennemdere Kanyonu*), political essays (*Ey Vatan*, *İnsan ve Devlet*, *Korkunun Çocukları*), and grand-strategy treatises (*Strateji*).
