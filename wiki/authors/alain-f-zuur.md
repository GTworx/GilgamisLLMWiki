## Biography & Context
Dr. Alain F. Zuur is senior statistician and Director of Highland Statistics Ltd. (Newburgh, UK), having taught R and advanced biostatistics to more than 5,000 life scientists across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East, and New Zealand.

In *A Beginner's Guide to R* (Springer *Use R!* series, 2009, 228 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-0-387-93837-0`, co-authored with [[elena-n-ieno]] and [[erik-h-w-g-meesters]] and synthesized in [[python-and-r-scientific-computing-foundations-for-ai-and-data-science]]), Zuur provides a zero-prerequisite foundation in R vectorization, data wrangling, lattice graphics, and reproducible epidemiological/ecological data analysis (source: BOOKS/AI/2009_Book_ABeginnerSGuideToR.pdf, pp. 1–8).
