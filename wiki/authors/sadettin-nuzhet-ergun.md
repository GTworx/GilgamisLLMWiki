## Biography & Contributions

Pioneering Turkish literary historian, bibliographer, and musicologist (1901–1946) whose multi-volume biographical encyclopedia *Türk Şairleri* rescued centuries of Divan, Sufi *tekke*, and folk *sazşairi* poetry from Ottoman manuscripts into modern Turkish scholarship.
