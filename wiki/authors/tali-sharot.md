## Biography & Context
Tali Sharot is Professor of Cognitive Neuroscience at University College London and MIT, and Director of the Affective Brain Lab. Author of *The Optimism Bias* and *The Influential Mind*, her neuroimaging and psychophysical research isolates the rostral anterior cingulate and ventral striatal circuits driving asymmetric belief updating and optimistic episodic future simulation in humans.
