# Author: Adam Smith

**Bio / Role**: Scottish Enlightenment moral philosopher and founder of modern classical political economy (1723–1790). Author of *The Theory of Moral Sentiments* (1759) and *An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations* (1776), establishing division of labor, market coordination, and anti-mercantilist critique.
