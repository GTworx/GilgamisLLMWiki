## Biography & Context
Li-Huei Tsai is Picower Professor of Neuroscience at MIT and Director of The Picower Institute for Learning and Memory. Her laboratory made the landmark discovery that 40Hz non-invasive sensory stimulation (GENUS: Gamma ENtrainment Using Sensory stimuli) recruits microglia, promotes glymphatic clearance of amyloid-beta and tau proteins, and preserves synaptic integrity in Alzheimer's models, published across *Nature* and *Cell*.
