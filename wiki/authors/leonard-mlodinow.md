## Biography & Context
Leonard Mlodinow is a Caltech-affiliated theoretical physicist and bestselling science author (*The Drunkard's Walk*, *Euclid's Window*, *Feynman's Rainbow*) who collaborated closely with [[stephen-hawking]] on *A Briefer History of Time* and *The Grand Design*.

In [[the-grand-design]] (2010, co-authored with [[stephen-hawking]]), Mlodinow articulates Model-Dependent Realism, Feynman sum-over-histories cosmology, and the M-theory multiverse (source: BOOKS/Physics/The Grand Design ( PDFDrive ).pdf, pp. 2, 6, 10, 37).
