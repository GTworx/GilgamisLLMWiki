## Biography & Context
John Sweller is Emeritus Professor of Educational Psychology at the University of New South Wales. He is the originator of Cognitive Load Theory (CLT), which articulates the severe working memory limitations governing human learning and problem-solving, formulating the distinctions between intrinsic, extraneous, and germane load, and discovering key instructional effects (split-attention, worked-example, redundancy).
