## Biography & Context
Daniel C.W. Ho is a professor of building surveying and real estate at the University of Hong Kong. He is the co-developer of the Building Safety and Conditions Index (BSCI), benchmarking structural safety, maintenance quality, and fire protection across dense urban residential typologies.
