## Biography & Context
Octave Plancherel is a cybersecurity researcher at the Cyber-Defence (CYD) Campus of armasuisse Science and Technology in Thun, Switzerland.

Co-editing [[large-language-models-in-cybersecurity-threats-exposure-and-mitigation]] (2024), Plancherel contributes to Switzerland's National Cyberstrategy (NCS) technology monitoring of generative AI threat vectors and mitigation architectures (source: BOOKS/AI/AI LLM Cybesecurity.pdf, pp. 1–6).
