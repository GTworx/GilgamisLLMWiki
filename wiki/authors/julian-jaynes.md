## Biography & Context
Julian Jaynes (1920–1997) was an American psychologist and lecturer at Princeton University. His provocative 1976 work, *The Origin of Consciousness in the Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind*, argued that archaic humans until ~1000 BCE lacked subjective introspective consciousness, instead experiencing auditory hallucinations generated by the right hemisphere interpreted as commands from the gods.
