## Biography & Context
Kevin McGinley is a veteran data-analytics architect and Snowflake technical leader specializing in modern cloud data warehousing, embedded analytics, and Customer 360 platforms.

As co-author of [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]] (2021), McGinley details how SaaS product teams offload infrastructure management onto multi-cloud data platforms to accelerate customer-facing analytics (source: BOOKS/SaaS/OReilly-Report-Architecting-Data-Intensive-SaaS-Applications.pdf, pp. 3–4, 7–30).
