## Biography & Context
Susan McClary (born 1946) is a MacArthur Fellow and Professor of Musicology (affiliated with UCLA, McGill, and Case Western Reserve University) who co-founded the 'New Musicology' (*Feminine Endings*, 1991), dismantling formalist claims that Western classical and popular music are autonomous, purely mathematical structures detached from gender, power, and ideology.

In her Afterword (*'The Politics of Silence and Sound'*) to [[jacques-attali]]'s [[noise-the-political-economy-of-music]], McClary connects Attali's fourth network (*Composing*) to avant-garde classical music, punk, New Wave, and vernacular musical resistance.
