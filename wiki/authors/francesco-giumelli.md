## Biography & Context

Dr. Francesco Giumelli is Associate Professor of International Relations at the University of Groningen. He is an internationally recognized scholar on economic sanctions, illicit trade, and EU foreign policy, having authored books such as *Coercing, Constraining and Signalling: Explaining UN and EU Sanctions After the Cold War*.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Giumelli authored Chapter 18 ("Targeted Sanctions and Deterrence in the Twenty-first Century"), analyzing the mechanics of smart sanctions within cross-domain deterrence frameworks.
