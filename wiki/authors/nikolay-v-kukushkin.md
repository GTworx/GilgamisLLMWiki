## Biography & Context
Nikolay V. Kukushkin is a neurobiologist, author of *This Thing Called Life*, and Clinical Associate Professor of Life Science at NYU Liberal Studies. His research uncovers universal biochemical memory mechanisms (such as ERK/MAPK cascades) demonstrating that spaced learning operates identically in non-neural somatic cells, revealing learning as an ancient biological property predating the nervous system.
