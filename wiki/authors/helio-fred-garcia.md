## Biography & Context
**Helio Fred Garcia** is President of the Logos Consulting Group and Executive Director of the Logos Institute for Crisis Management and Executive Leadership (source: [[influencing-leaders]], About the Authors, pp. 230–231). For more than four decades, Garcia has advised CEOs, heads of state, and senior military commanders on high-stakes crisis response, investor relations, and strategic persuasion.

Academically trained in philosophy and classical rhetoric at New York University and Columbia University, Garcia serves as an adjunct professor of management at **NYU Stern School of Business** and an adjunct professor of strategic communication at **Columbia University**. He is also a longtime guest lecturer and leadership instructor for the **United States Marine Corps Command and Staff College** and the **U.S. Army War College**. Author of *The Power of Communication*, *The Agony of Decision*, and *Words on Fire*, Garcia brings a rigorous philosophical and behavioral framework to executive counsel. In [[influencing-leaders]] (2026), co-authored with [[james-e-lukaszewski]], he translates the art of executive influence into teachable mental models—demonstrating how trusted advisors diagnose unstated leadership anxieties, distill complex options onto a single page, and protect institutional integrity under pressure.
