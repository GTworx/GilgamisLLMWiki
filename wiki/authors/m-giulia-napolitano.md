## Biography & Context
M. Giulia Napolitano is an Assistant Professor of Philosophy at Utrecht University specializing in social and formal epistemology, philosophy of mind, and the epistemic pathologies of conspiratorial belief. Her scholarship analyzes conspiracy theories as epistemically self-insulating networks that systematically reinterpret counter-evidence as confirmation of conspiracy scope.
