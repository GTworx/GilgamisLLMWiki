## Biography & Context
Derric Gilling is cofounder and CEO of Moesif, an API analytics and monetization platform helping API-first engineering and product teams track developer adoption, Time-to-First-Hello, and usage-based billing.

Co-authoring [[api-strategy-for-decision-makers]] (2022) with [[mike-amundsen]], Gilling shows how observability turns invisible API calls into measurable developer-funnel growth and retention metrics (source: BOOKS/Cloud/OReilly-API-Strategy-for-Decision-Makers.pdf, pp. 5–6, 19–43).
