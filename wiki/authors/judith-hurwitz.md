## Biography & Context
Judith Hurwitz is a technology strategist, corporate advisor, and President of Hurwitz & Associates, having co-authored over ten Wiley *For Dummies* enterprise computing guides including *Cognitive Computing and Big Data Analytics*.

In [[machine-learning-for-dummies-ibm-limited-edition]] (John Wiley & Sons, 2018, 75 pp., co-authored with [[daniel-kirsch]]), Hurwitz provides an executive and practitioner guide to transitioning from static rule-based software to dynamic, data-driven machine learning lifecycles (source: BOOKS/AI/2017 Book ML for Dummies by IBM_.pdf, pp. 1–16, 27–36).
