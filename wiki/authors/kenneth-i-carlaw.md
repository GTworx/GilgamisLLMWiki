## Biography & Context
Kenneth I. Carlaw is Professor of Economics at the University of British Columbia (Okanagan), specializing in formal simulation models of endogenous technological change, Total Factor Productivity (TFP) critique, and General Purpose Technologies.

In [[economic-transformations-general-purpose-technologies-and-long-term-economic-growth]] (2005), co-authored with [[richard-g-lipsey]] and [[clifford-t-bekar]], Carlaw develops the non-stationary three-sector GPT growth models and proves why TFP fails to measure technological change.
