## Biography & Context
Gary E. Marchant is Regents' Professor of Law and Director of the Center for Law, Science and Innovation at Arizona State University, and Chair of the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (NASEM) Committee on Assessment of Technologies for Improving Fuel Economy of Light-Duty Vehicles (Phase 3).

In [[electric-vehicles-light-duty-fuel-economy-and-shared-micromobility]], Marchant chairs the 457-page NASEM consensus study mapping the 2025–2035 transition from advanced internal combustion engines and hybrids to zero-emission battery electric and fuel-cell vehicles.
