## Biography & Context
Peter Pirolli is a Senior Research Scientist at the Florida Institute for Human & Machine Cognition (IHMC) and former Fellow at Xerox PARC. Together with Stuart Card, Pirolli created Information Foraging Theory, which adapts optimal foraging models from behavioral ecology (patch models, marginal value theorem) to explain how humans navigate web architectures and consume digital information.
