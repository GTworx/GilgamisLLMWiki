## Biography & Context
Michael Bhaskar is a British writer, researcher, and publishing entrepreneur (co-founder of Canelo and former Writer-in-Residence at DeepMind), known for *The Content Machine* (2013), *Curation* (2016), and *Human Frontiers* (2021).

Co-authoring [[the-coming-wave-ai-power-and-our-future]] (2023) with [[mustafa-suleyman]], Bhaskar situates the AI and synthetic biology supercluster within the macro-history of general-purpose technological waves and the fragility of the modern nation-state (source: BOOKS/AI/The Coming Wave_ AI, Power, and Our Future - Notebook.html, Ch. 2–12, pp. 37–276).
