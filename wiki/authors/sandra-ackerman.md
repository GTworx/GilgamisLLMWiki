## Biography & Context
Sandra J. Ackerman is an American science writer and editor (affiliated with the National Academy of Sciences, the Institute of Medicine, and *American Scientist*) renowned for translating complex neurobiological research into authoritative public and policy syntheses.

In [[discovering-the-brain]] (1992), written for the **Institute of Medicine / National Academy of Sciences** to inaugurate the 1990s *Decade of the Brain*, Ackerman synthesizes neuroanatomy, PET/MRI functional neuroimaging, the neurogenetics of mental illness, synaptic receptor chemistry, and Long-Term Potentiation (LTP).
