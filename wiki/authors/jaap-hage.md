## Biography & Context
Jaap Hage is Professor of Foundations and Methods of Law at Maastricht University Faculty of Law (The Netherlands), internationally recognized for his work on legal logic (*Reasoning with Rules*), defeasible legal reasoning, deontic ontology, and comparative jurisprudence.

As lead editor and author of Chapters 1, 2, 3, and 15 of [[introduction-to-law]] (2017) alongside [[antonia-waltermann]] and [[bram-akkermans]], Hage formulates the universal building blocks of positive law, legal certainty, duties/competences, and the Hart–Dworkin debate.
