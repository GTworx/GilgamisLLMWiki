## Biography & Context
Saturnino L. Salas is an American mathematician renowned for his clear, proof-oriented exposition of single- and multivariable calculus, scrutinizing every definition and theorem for conceptual precision.

In [[calculus-one-and-several-variables]] (10th Ed., 2007, with [[einar-hille]] and [[garret-j-etgen]]), Salas anchors calculus on limits, derivatives, integrals, infinite series, and vector calculus (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, pp. 7–11).
