## Biography & Context
The LearningExpress Skill Builder in Focus Writing Team comprises practicing middle-school, high-school, and college mathematics and literacy educators dedicated to structured self-study problem mastery.

Their workbook *501 Math Word Problems* (2003, paired with [[alex-oconnor]] in [[501-math-word-problems-and-middle-school-math-challenges]]) trains systematic real-world translation across fractions, percents, algebra, and geometry (source: BOOKS/Math/MathWorldProblems.pdf, pp. 5–8).
