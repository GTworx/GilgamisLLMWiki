## Biography & Context
AFAD is the central statutory institution responsible for disaster and emergency management in the Republic of Türkiye, established in 2009 under Law No. 5902. Operating under the Ministry of Interior, AFAD coordinates national disaster prevention, risk reduction (TARAP), emergency response (TAMP), recovery planning, international humanitarian missions, and seismic observation networks.
