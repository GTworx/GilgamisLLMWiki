## Biography & Context
Advocate Prof. Dr. Divya Singh is a South African legal scholar, Chief Academic Officer at STADIO Higher Education, former Vice Principal of Advisory and Assurance Services at the University of South Africa (UNISA), and Director of Globethics.net Southern Africa.

As lead co-editor of [[ethics-in-higher-education-values-driven-leaders-for-the-future]] (2017) with [[christoph-stuckelberger]], Singh examines university ethical governance, academic integrity in distance e-learning, and preparing graduates for global citizenship.
