## Biography & Context
Eli M. Noam is the Paul Garrett Professor Emeritus of Public Policy and Business Responsibility and Director of the Columbia Institute for Tele-Information (CITI) at Columbia Business School, having also served as a New York State Public Service Commissioner.

In [[media-and-digital-management]] (2019), Noam provides a comprehensive industrial-organization and management framework for content production, near-zero-marginal-cost digital economics, IP law, portfolio risk hedging, and streaming distribution.
