## Biography & Context
Neil Rackham (born 1936) is a British behavioral psychologist, founder of Huthwaite Inc., and visiting professor at the Universities of Sheffield, Portsmouth, and Cranfield. In the 1970s and 1980s, Rackham led a 30-researcher team that quantitatively coded **35,000 live sales calls** in 23 countries for major global corporations.

In his landmark book [[spin-selling]] (1988), Rackham overturned 60 years of transactional closing dogma, proving that major enterprise sales succeed by converting *Implied Needs* into *Explicit Needs* via **Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-Payoff (SPIN)** questions.
