## Biography & Context
Ronghuai Huang is Professor and Co-Dean of the Smart Learning Institute at Beijing Normal University, UNESCO Chair on Artificial Intelligence in Education, and Editor-in-Chief of the *Journal of Computers in Education* and Springer's *Lecture Notes in Educational Technology* series.

In [[educational-technology-a-primer-for-the-21st-century]] (2019), co-authored with [[j-michael-spector]] and [[junfeng-yang]], Huang formulates the **SMART** classroom model (*Showing, Manageable, Accessible, Real-time interactive, Testing*) and 5E smart learning ecology.
