## Biography & Context
Tim Grance served as Program Manager in the Computer Security Division at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), co-authoring foundational NIST Special Publications on cloud computing (SP 800-145) and incident handling.

In [[computer-security-incident-handling-guide-nist-sp-800-61r2]], Grance helped establish the organizational models and prioritization matrices for CSIRTs.
