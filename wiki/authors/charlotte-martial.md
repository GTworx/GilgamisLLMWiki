## Biography & Context
Charlotte Martial is a cognitive neuroscientist, neuropsychologist, and F.R.S.-FNRS Research Associate at the Coma Science Group (GIGA-Consciousness, University of Liège). Her clinical investigations systematically map near-death experiences (NDEs), cardiac arrest neurophysiology, and disconnected consciousness using high-density EEG, fMRI, and standardized psychometric scales.
