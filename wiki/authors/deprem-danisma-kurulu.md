## Biography & Context
The Earthquake Advisory Board of AFAD (Deprem Danışma Kurulu) is the statutory scientific council comprising Türkiye's leading seismologists, geological engineers, and structural professors that advises the state on national earthquake strategies, code revisions, and research allocations.
