## Biography & Context
Francesc Miralles (born 1968 in Barcelona) is a Spanish journalist, novelist, and bestselling author specializing in psychology, spirituality, and purposeful living.

In [[ikigai-the-japanese-secret-to-a-long-and-happy-life]] (2016/2017, with [[hector-garcia]]), Miralles weaves Viktor Frankl's Logotherapy, Csikszentmihalyi's Flow, Stoicism, and Japanese *wabi-sabi* into a holistic longevity framework (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Ikigai _ the Japanese secret to a long and happy life ( PDFDrive ).pdf, pp. 3, 8–108).
