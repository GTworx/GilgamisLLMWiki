## Biography & Context
Ben Mackenzie is Principal Data Science Engineer at Teradata Think Big Analytics specializing in deep learning, computer vision, and natural language processing across Europe and Asia.

In [[achieving-real-business-outcomes-from-artificial-intelligence-and-the-future-of-machine-intelligence]] (2019), Mackenzie details production AWS/TensorFlow/Keras GPU architectures for document optical recognition, fraud detection, and predictive maintenance (source: BOOKS/AI/2019 Achieving-Real-Business-Outcomes-from-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf, pp. 1–6, 51).
