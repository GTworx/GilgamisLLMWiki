## Biography & Context
Bridge to Türkiye Fund (BTF) is a US-based non-profit philanthropic organization founded in 2003 by Turkish-American professionals. BTF mobilizes global diaspora contributions to support grassroots education, public health, and post-catastrophe relief initiatives across Türkiye through transparent, community-verified partner organizations.
