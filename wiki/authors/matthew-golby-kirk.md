## Biography & Context
Matthew Golby-Kirk is a software architect and blockchain educator at IBM specializing in smart contract (chaincode) lifecycle management, DevOps, and cloud deployment.

In [[getting-started-with-enterprise-blockchain]], Golby-Kirk co-authors the practical design and development guides for enterprise distributed ledgers.
