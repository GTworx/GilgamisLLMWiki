## Biography & Context
Alex O'Connor (*Middle School Math Man*) is a middle-school mathematics educator and curriculum designer creating weekly enrichment challenges for 8th-grade learners.

His 27-problem collection (2019, paired with [[learningexpress-editors]] in [[501-math-word-problems-and-middle-school-math-challenges]]) develops proportional, algebraic, and functional reasoning prior to high-school STEM tracks (source: BOOKS/Math/8th Grade Math/8thGradeMathChallengeoftheWeekProblems.pdf, p. 1).
