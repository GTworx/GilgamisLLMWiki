## Biography & Context
César A. Hidalgo is a Chilean-Spanish-American physicist and complexity scientist, former Director of the Collective Learning group at MIT Media Lab, and Chair at the University of Toulouse and Corvinus University. He is the author of *Why Information Grows* and *How Humans Judge Machines*, pioneering quantitative models of economic complexity, cultural memory decay, and societal knowledge accumulation.
