## Biography & Context
Dr. Erik H.W.G. Meesters is a senior researcher in coral reef ecology and marine biometrics at Wageningen Marine Research / IMARES (Texel, The Netherlands).

In *A Beginner's Guide to R* (Springer *Use R!*, 2009, 228 pp., synthesized in [[python-and-r-scientific-computing-foundations-for-ai-and-data-science]]), Meesters co-develops the R programming, plotting, and statistical scripting curriculum for environmental and biological researchers (source: BOOKS/AI/2009_Book_ABeginnerSGuideToR.pdf, pp. 4–8).
