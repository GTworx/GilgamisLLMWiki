## Biography & Institutional Context
Philip Trammell is an economist at the Global Priorities Institute at the University of Oxford. His work applies mathematical economic theory, endogenous growth dynamics, and intertemporal social choice to high-stakes civilizational dilemmas, focusing on the optimal allocation of resources between technological acceleration and catastrophic risk mitigation.
