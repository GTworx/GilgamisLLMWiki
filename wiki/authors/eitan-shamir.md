## Biography & Context

Dr. Eitan Shamir is Director of the Begin-Sadat Center for Strategic Studies (BESA Center) and Associate Professor in the Political Science Department at Bar-Ilan University. A former Senior Officer in the Israeli Ministry of Strategic Affairs, Shamir is the author of *Transforming Command: The Pursuit of Mission Command in the U.S., British, and Israeli Armies*.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Shamir authored Chapter 14 ("Deterring Violent Non-state Actors"), analyzing cumulative deterrence strategies and Israel's operational doctrine against non-state groups like Hamas and Hezbollah.
