## Biography & Context
Valentin Mulder is a researcher in the Technology Monitoring team at the Cyber-Defence (CYD) Campus of armasuisse Science and Technology (Thun, Switzerland).

In [[large-language-models-in-cybersecurity-threats-exposure-and-mitigation]] (2024), Mulder co-edits the 25-chapter evaluation of LLM vulnerabilities, automated vulnerability detection, and federated privacy safeguards (source: BOOKS/AI/AI LLM Cybesecurity.pdf, pp. 1–10).
