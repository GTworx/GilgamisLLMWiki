# Author: Jacques Lacan

**Bio / Role**: French psychiatrist and towering twentieth-century psychoanalyst (1901–1981) whose Paris Seminars (*Book II: The Ego in Freud's Theory*, *Book VIII: Transference*) fused Freud with structural linguistics, cybernetics, and Plato to map the Imaginary, Symbolic, and Real orders and *objet petit a*.

## Biography & Contributions
