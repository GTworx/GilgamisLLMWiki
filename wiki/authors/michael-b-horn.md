## Biography & Context
Michael B. Horn is the co-founder of and Distinguished Fellow at the Clayton Christensen Institute for Disruptive Innovation, Lecturer at the Harvard Graduate School of Education, and a leading architect of competency-based and blended K–12 and higher education reform.

As co-author of [[disrupting-class-how-disruptive-innovation-will-change-the-way-the-world-learns]] (2008) with [[clayton-m-christensen]] and [[curtis-w-johnson]] and lead co-author of [[blended-using-disruptive-innovation-to-improve-schools]] (2015) with [[heather-staker]], Horn established the global taxonomy of blended learning (**Rotation, Flex, A La Carte, Enriched Virtual**) and applied **Jobs-to-Be-Done** to student motivation.
