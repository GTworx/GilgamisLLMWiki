# Guido Alberto Gnecchi-Ruscone

## Biography
Population geneticist at the Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology, pioneering ancient genomic analysis of Eurasian steppe migrations, Avars, Huns, and pastoralist ethnogenesis.
