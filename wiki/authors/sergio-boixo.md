## Biography & Context
Sergio Boixo is Chief Scientist of Quantum Computer Theory at Google Quantum AI. Boixo formulated the mathematical foundations of cross-entropy benchmarking and led the theoretical architecture behind Google's historic 2019 quantum supremacy milestone.
