## Biography & Context
Jonas Hübotter is a machine learning researcher at the Institute for Machine Learning, Department of Computer Science, ETH Zürich, specializing in information-directed active learning, test-time adaptation, and sequential decision-making under uncertainty.

Co-authoring [[probabilistic-artificial-intelligence]] (2025) with [[andreas-krause]], Hübotter develops the pedagogical synthesis bridging variational/MCMC posterior approximation with active data acquisition and constrained exploration in reinforcement learning (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 Probabilistic AI.pdf, pp. 1–10, 157–298).
