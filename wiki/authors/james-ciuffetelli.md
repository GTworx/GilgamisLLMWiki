## Biography & Context
James Ciuffetelli is Co-Founder and Director of Whiteark (Melbourne, Australia), specializing in buy-and-build M&A execution, strategic pricing, commercial due diligence, and digital retooling for private-equity-backed businesses.

Co-authoring [[the-ultimate-private-equity-playbook]] (2021) with [[jo-hands]], Ciuffetelli outlines how operational discipline and digital transformation drive superior buy-and-build IRRs and strategic exits (source: BOOKS/Private Equity/Private+Equity+Playbook+-+by+Whiteark.pdf, pp. 2–16, 42–44).
