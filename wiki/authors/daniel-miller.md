## Biography & Context
Daniel Miller is Professor of Anthropology at University College London (UCL) and a founder of modern material-culture studies and digital anthropology, directing landmark global comparative ethnographies of social networking, smartphones, and everyday digital life.

In [[digital-anthropology]] (2012, co-edited with [[heather-a-horst]]), Miller formulates the six principles of digital anthropology—comparing binary code to money as both universal abstraction and deeply localized cultural particularity (source: BOOKS/Anthropology_/Digital-Anthropology-edited-by-Heather-A.-Horst-and-Daniel-Miller.pdf, pp. 4–17).
