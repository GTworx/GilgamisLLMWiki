## Biography & Context
Fredric Jameson (1934–2024) was an American literary critic, philosopher, and Knut Schmidt-Nielsen Professor of Comparative Literature at Duke University, universally recognized as the foremost anglophone Marxist cultural theorist of the late 20th and early 21st centuries (*The Political Unconscious*, *Postmodernism, or, The Cultural Logic of Late Capitalism*).

In his substantive Foreword to [[jacques-attali]]'s [[noise-the-political-economy-of-music]], Jameson situates Attali's political economy of sound beyond Althusserian structuralism and Max Weber's sociology of musical rationalization, highlighting music's anticipatory capacity to register emergent social realities.
