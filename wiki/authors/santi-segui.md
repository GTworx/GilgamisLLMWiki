## Biography & Context
Dr. Santi Seguí is Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at the Universitat de Barcelona (Spain), specializing in machine learning, recommender systems, and medical image analytics.

Co-authoring [[introduction-to-data-science-a-python-approach-to-concepts-techniques-and-applications]] (2017) with [[laura-igual]] (with contributions from Jordi Vitrià, Eloi Puertas, Petia Radeva, Oriol Pujol, Sergio Escalera, Francesc Dantí, and Lluís Garrido), Seguí develops reproducible Jupyter Notebook case studies across supervised/unsupervised learning, collaborative filtering, and graph analytics (source: BOOKS/AI/2017_Book_IntroductionToDataScience.pdf, pp. 4–11, 169–181).
