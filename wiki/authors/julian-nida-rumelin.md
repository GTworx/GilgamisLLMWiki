## Biography & Institutional Context
Julian Nida-Rümelin is one of Germany's most distinguished philosophers, a former Minister of State for Culture, and professor at LMU Munich. He champions 'Digital Humanism', analyzing the ethical limits of algorithmic delegation and the preservation of human responsibility in autonomous systems.
