## Biography & Context

Stefan Soesanto is Senior Cyber Defense Researcher in the Risk and Resilience Team at the Center for Security Studies (CSS) at ETH Zurich. He specializes in state-sponsored cyber operations, cyber defense posture, and intelligence warfare.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Soesanto (with Max Smeets) authored Chapter 20 ("Cyber Deterrence: The Past, Present, and Future"), deconstructing the structural obstacles to effective cyber deterrence.
