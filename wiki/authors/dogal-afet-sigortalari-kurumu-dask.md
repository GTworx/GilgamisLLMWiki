## Biography & Context
The Turkish Catastrophe Insurance Pool (DASK) was established in 2000 under Decree Law No. 587 as an autonomous public institution managing compulsory earthquake insurance. Operating with international reinsurance treaties and capital reserves, DASK underwrites seismic property risk for millions of Turkish homeowners.
