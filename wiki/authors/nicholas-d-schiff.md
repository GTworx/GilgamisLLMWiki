## Biography & Context
Nicholas D. Schiff is the Jerold B. Katz Professor of Neurology and Neuroscience at Weill Cornell Medicine. A world leader in severe brain injury research, Schiff pioneered deep brain stimulation of the central thalamus to recover behavioral responsiveness in minimally conscious states, and spearheaded computer-vision and fMRI paradigms to detect covert consciousness in seemingly unresponsive patients.
