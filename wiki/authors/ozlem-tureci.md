## Biography & Institutional Context
Dr. Özlem Türeci is a German physician, cancer researcher, and Chief Medical Officer of BioNTech. Together with Uğur Şahin, she pioneered individual cancer vaccines and co-developed BNT162b2, the world's first approved mRNA COVID-19 vaccine.
