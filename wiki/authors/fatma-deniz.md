## Biography & Context
Fatma Deniz is a computational neuroscientist and data scientist at UC Berkeley (Gallant Lab) and Technical University of Berlin. Her research utilizes voxel-wise fMRI encoding models and natural language processing to map semantic representation across the human cerebral cortex, establishing that narrative comprehension engages identical modality-invariant semantic networks whether listening to spoken words or reading text.
