## Biography & Context
Igor Yevgenyevich Irodov (1923–2002) was a Soviet physicist, World War II veteran, and longtime professor of general physics at the Moscow Engineering Physics Institute (MEPhI), globally renowned for authoring benchmark university physics problem collections.

His classic [[problems-in-general-physics]] (Mir Publishers, 1981) remains the gold standard for Physics Olympiad and honors university training across mechanics, thermodynamics, electrodynamics, optics, and atomic/nuclear physics (source: BOOKS/Physics/1981 Irodov-Problems_in_General_Physics.pdf, pp. 10–11, 51).
