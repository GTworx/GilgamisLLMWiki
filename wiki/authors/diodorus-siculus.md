# Author: Diodorus Siculus

**Bio / Role**: Ancient Greek historian of Agyrium, Sicily (1st century BC), who spent over thirty years compiling the monumental 40-volume universal history *Bibliotheca historica* (*Library of History*), synthesizing archaic mythology, Near Eastern annals, and Mediterranean chronicle.
