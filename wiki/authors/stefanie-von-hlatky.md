## Biography & Context
**Stéfanie von Hlatky** is Professor of Political Studies, Canada Research Chair in Gender, Security, and the Armed Forces, and former Director of the **Centre for International and Defence Policy (CIDP)** at **Queen's University** in Kingston, Ontario. An authority on alliance deterrence, NATO burden-sharing, military interoperability, and the human dimension of warfare, she co-edited [[developing-the-super-soldier-enhancing-military-performance]] (*Developing the Super Soldier: Enhancing Military Performance*, 2017, with [[william-g-braun-iii]] and [[kim-richard-nossal]]) (source: [[developing-the-super-soldier-enhancing-military-performance]], pp. 1–6, viii).

Her scholarship bridges transatlantic alliance politics (aligning with [[sten-rynning]] and [[paul-van-hooft]] in [[deterrence-in-the-21st-century]]) with the operational, ethical, and unit-cohesion implications of emerging military technologies and human performance enhancement.
