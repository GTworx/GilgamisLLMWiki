## Biography & Context
Dr. Sandro Skansi is Associate Professor of Logic and Data Science at the University of Zagreb (Croatia), specializing in the intersection of formal logic, cognitive science, and deep neural networks.

In [[introduction-to-deep-learning-from-logical-calculus-to-artificial-intelligence]] (Springer *Undergraduate Topics in Computer Science*, 2018, 196 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-73004-2`), Skansi defines artificial intelligence as **'philosophical engineering'**—tracing a continuous conceptual arc from McCulloch and Pitts's 1943 logical calculus of neurons and Minsky's XOR critique to modern CNNs, LSTMs, autoencoders, and generative deep learning (source: BOOKS/AI/2018_Book_IntroductionToDeepLearning.pdf, pp. 1–15, 90–101, 142–151).
