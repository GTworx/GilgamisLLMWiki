## Biography & Context
1QBit was the world's first dedicated quantum computing software company, founded in Vancouver, Canada. The firm developed hardware-agnostic SDKs, quantum compilers, and mathematical optimization pipelines for enterprise applications across finance, chemistry, and materials.
