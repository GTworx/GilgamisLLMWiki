## Biography & Context
Founded in 1868 as the Imperial Meteorological Observatory and integrated into Boğaziçi University in 1982, Kandilli Observatory is Türkiye's premier scientific institution for geophysics, seismology, tsunami warning, and earthquake engineering research.
