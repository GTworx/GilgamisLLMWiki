## Biography & Context
The Disaster Technologies Consortium brings together open-source software engineers, civil society developers (Açık Yazılım Ağı, AfetBilgi, DepremYardım), and academic researchers who pioneer civic tech platforms, crowdsourced emergency mapping, and NLP triage during major humanitarian crises.
