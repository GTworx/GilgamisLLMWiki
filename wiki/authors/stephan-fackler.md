## Biography & Context
Stephan Fackler is a German mathematician at Ulm University specializing in functional analysis, operator semigroups, spectral theory, and harmonic analysis.

In [[mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-ulm-lecture-notes]] (2015), Fackler constructs measure theory, unbounded self-adjoint operators, the Spectral Theorem, Stone's Theorem, Kato–Rellich perturbation theory, and tempered distributions for graduate physicists and mathematicians (source: BOOKS/Physics/Ulm notes_mathematical_found_Quantum Mechanics.pdf, pp. 1–7).
