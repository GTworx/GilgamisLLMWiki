## Biography & Context

Dmitry (Dima) Adamsky is Professor of International Relations and Strategy at the Lauder School of Government, Diplomacy and Strategy at Reichman University (IDC Herzliya), Israel. He has held research appointments at Harvard University's Belfer Center, Columbia University, and the Norwegian Institute for Defence Studies. He is the author of celebrated books such as *The Culture of Military Innovation* (2010) and *Russian Nuclear Orthodoxy* (2019).

Adamsky is one of the world's preeminent experts on Russian strategic thought, military doctrine, and deterrence culture. In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Adamsky authored Chapter 9 ("Deterrence à la Ruse: Its Uniqueness, Sources and Implications"), detailing how the Russian concept of *sderzhivanie* blends non-military coercion, information confrontation, strategic deception (*maskirovka*), and holistic nuclear signaling across peacetime and wartime.
