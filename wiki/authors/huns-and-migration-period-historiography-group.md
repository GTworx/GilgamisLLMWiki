# Huns and Migration Period Historiography Group

## Biography
Scholarly group bridging Byzantine literary sources (Moravcsik's Byzantinoturcica, Priscus, Ammianus Marcellinus) and Scandinavian archaeological evidence to trace nomadic steppe incursions into Late Antique Europe.
