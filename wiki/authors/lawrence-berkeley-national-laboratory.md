## Biography & Context
Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) is a U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science national laboratory managed by the University of California. Its Energy Analysis and Environmental Impacts Division conducts foundational nationwide research into data center energy efficiency, industrial decarbonization, and grid integration under lead author Arman Shehabi.
