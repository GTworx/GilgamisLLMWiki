## Biography & Context
David Beyer is a Partner at Amplify Partners, focusing on early-stage venture investments in machine intelligence, data infrastructure, and developer tools.

In *The Future of Machine Intelligence: Perspectives from Leading Practitioners* (O'Reilly Media, 2016, 78 pp., synthesized in [[achieving-real-business-outcomes-from-artificial-intelligence-and-the-future-of-machine-intelligence]]), Beyer curates foundational dialogues with Turing Award laureate Yoshua Bengio, Anima Anandkumar, Brendan Frey, Risto Miikkulainen, Benjamin Recht, Daniela Rus, and Zoubin Ghahramani (source: BOOKS/AI/2016 Book OReilly future-of-machine-intelligence.pdf, pp. 1–11, 31, 61).
