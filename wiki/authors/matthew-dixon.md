## Biography & Context
Matthew Dixon is a business researcher, founding partner of DCM Insights, and former Executive Director at the Corporate Executive Board (CEB, now Gartner), where he co-authored *The Challenger Sale* (2011), *The Effortless Experience* (2013), *The Challenger Customer* (2015), and *The JOLT Effect* (2022).

In [[the-challenger-sale-challenge-yourself-action-book]] (2021), Dixon and [[brent-adamson]]'s empirical research demonstrates how top B2B advisors win by **Teaching for Differentiation**, **Tailoring for Resonance**, **Taking Control**, and calibrating **Constructive Tension**.
