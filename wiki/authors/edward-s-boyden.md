# Edward S. Boyden

## Biography
Edward S. Boyden is the Y. Eva Tan Professor in Neurotechnology at MIT, investigator at the Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI), and co-inventor of Optogenetics, Expansion Microscopy, and Temporal Interference stimulation.
