## Biography & Institutional Context
Pål Liljebäck is a Norwegian roboticist and research scientist at SINTEF and NTNU. He is a world-renowned pioneer in snake robotics, having authored foundational textbooks and papers on the kinematics, control, and obstacle-aided propulsion of hyper-redundant snake robots.
