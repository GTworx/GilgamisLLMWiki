## Biography & Context
Karen Scarfone is the Principal Cybersecurityister and Founder of Scarfone Cybersecurity and a former Senior Computer Scientist at NIST, where she authored or co-authored more than fifty NIST Special Publications and Interagency Reports on intrusion detection, firewalls, vulnerability management, and incident response.

In [[computer-security-incident-handling-guide-nist-sp-800-61r2]], Scarfone co-authored the technical detection, precursor/indicator analysis, and recovery guidelines.
