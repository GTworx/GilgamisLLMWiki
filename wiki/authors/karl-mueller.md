## Biography & Context

Karl P. Mueller is a Senior Political Scientist at the RAND Corporation and former Professor of Comparative Military Studies at the US Air Force School of Advanced Air and Space Studies (SAASS). Mueller holds a Ph.D. in Politics from Princeton University and is recognized internationally for his foundational scholarship on conventional deterrence, coercive air power, and strategic escalation.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Mueller authored Chapter 4 ("The Continuing Relevance of Conventional Deterrence"), categorizing conventional deterrence into four distinct operational and geographic modes and analyzing why conventional deterrence poses different structural and cognitive challenges than nuclear deterrence.
