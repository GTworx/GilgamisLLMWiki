## Biography & Context
Gjorgji Georgievski is a cloud data systems architect specializing in high-volume ELT pipelines, schema evolution, and streaming-vs.-batch ingestion architectures on Snowflake.

Co-authoring [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]] (2021), Georgievski outlines best practices for processing raw and conformed datasets at scale inside customer-facing applications (source: BOOKS/SaaS/OReilly-Report-Architecting-Data-Intensive-SaaS-Applications.pdf, pp. 3–4, 43–53).
