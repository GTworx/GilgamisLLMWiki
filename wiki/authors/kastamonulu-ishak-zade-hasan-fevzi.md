# Author: Kastamonulu İshak-zâde Hasan Fevzî

**Bio / Role**: Nineteenth-century Ottoman poet, minstrel (*âşık*), Sufi literary chronicler, and anthology compiler from Kastamonu (1857–1917). Compiled the monumental *Fusûl-ı Âşıkân* (1877–1906) preserving minstrel gatherings, classical verse, and musical *makam* notations.

## Biography & Contributions

Nineteenth-century Ottoman poet, minstrel (*âşık*), Sufi literary chronicler, and anthology compiler from Kastamonu (1857–1917). Compiled the monumental *Fusûl-ı Âşıkân* (1877–1906) preserving minstrel gatherings, classical verse, and musical *makam* notations.
