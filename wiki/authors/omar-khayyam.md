# Author: Omar Khayyam

**Bio / Role**: Seljuk-era Persian polymath, mathematician, astronomer, philosopher, and poet of Nishapur (1048–1131). Renowned in the Islamic world for his treatise on cubic equations and the Jalali solar calendar, and globally celebrated for the philosophical quatrains (*Rubaiyat*) meditating on mortality, skepticism, and spiritual longing.

## Biography & Contributions

Seljuk-era Persian polymath, mathematician, astronomer, philosopher, and poet of Nishapur (1048–1131). Renowned in the Islamic world for his treatise on cubic equations and the Jalali solar calendar, and globally celebrated for the philosophical quatrains (*Rubaiyat*) meditating on mortality, skepticism, and spiritual longing.
