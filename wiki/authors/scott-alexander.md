## Biography & Context
Scott Alexander Siskind is a psychiatrist and author of the influential rationalist publications *Slate Star Codex* and *Astral Codex Ten*.

As co-author of [[ai-2027]] (2025), Alexander synthesizes technical scaling laws, wargaming exercises, and alignment failure modes into a concrete chronological narrative of the 2025–2028 intelligence explosion (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 Book_ai-2027.pdf, pp. 1–36).
