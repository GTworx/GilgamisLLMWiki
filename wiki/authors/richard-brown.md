## Biography & Context
Richard Brown is Professor of Philosophy at the City University of New York (LaGuardia / CUNY Graduate Center). Alongside Joseph LeDoux and Hakwan Lau, Brown is a leading advocate of Higher-Order Theories (HOT) of consciousness, arguing that sensory representations become conscious only when targeted by higher-order meta-representations situated primarily in prefrontal cortex networks.
