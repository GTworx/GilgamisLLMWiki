## Biography & Context
David Cancel is a five-time serial SaaS entrepreneur—founder of Compete (acquired by WPP), Ghostery, Performable (acquired by HubSpot), and cofounder/CEO of Drift—and former Chief Product Officer at HubSpot, where his product restructuring halved churn and doubled LTV:CAC ahead of HubSpot's IPO.

In [[hypergrowth-how-the-customer-driven-model-is-revolutionizing-business]] (2016), Cancel declares the end of inward-facing Waterfall/Agile cycles in favor of 1:1 customer messaging, Responsible Development, and autonomous three-person engineering teams (source: BOOKS/SaaS/HYPERGROWTH-by-David-Cancel.pdf, pp. 2, 5–16, 22–28).
