## Biography & Context
Thomas Sowell (born 1930) is the Rose and Milton Friedman Senior Fellow on Public Policy at the Hoover Institution, Stanford University. Educated at Harvard, Columbia, and the University of Chicago (under George Stigler and Milton Friedman), Sowell is the author of over forty books on economics, intellectual history, knowledge and decisions, and ethnic studies.

In his personal favorite among his own works, [[a-conflict-of-visions-ideological-origins-of-political-struggles]] (1987/2007), Sowell traces two centuries of political, legal, and economic battles to the foundational clash between the **Constrained (Tragic) Vision** (Smith, Burke, Hamilton, Hayek—relying on systemic incentives and trade-offs) and the **Unconstrained (Utopian) Vision** (Godwin, Condorcet, Rousseau, Dworkin—relying on articulated elite reason and solutions).
