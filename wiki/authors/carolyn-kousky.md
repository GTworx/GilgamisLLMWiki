## Biography & Context
Carolyn Kousky is Associate Vice President for Economics and Policy at Environmental Defense Fund (EDF) and former Executive Director of the Wharton Risk Management and Decision Processes Center at the University of Pennsylvania. Her scholarship focuses on flood insurance, disaster risk financing, climate adaptation policy, and the economic impacts of disasters on children and low-income households.
