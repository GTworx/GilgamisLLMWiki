## Biography & Context
Mustafa Suleyman (CBE) is CEO of Microsoft AI, co-founder and former CEO of Inflection AI, and co-founder and former Head of Applied AI at DeepMind (acquired by Google in 2014).

In [[the-coming-wave-ai-power-and-our-future]] (Crown, 2023, 302 pp., co-authored with [[michael-bhaskar]]; ingested from the 673-entry full-book annotated study notebook `The Coming Wave_ AI, Power, and Our Future - Notebook.html`), Suleyman diagnoses **The Containment Problem** of artificial intelligence and synthetic biology, introduces **Artificial Capable Intelligence (ACI)** and the **Modern Turing Test**, and outlines ten concentric steps to navigate between existential catastrophe and surveillance 'AI-tocracy' (source: BOOKS/AI/The Coming Wave_ AI, Power, and Our Future - Notebook.html, Ch. 1–14, pp. 19–302).
