# Author: Shams-i Tabrizi

**Bio / Role**: Enigmatic thirteenth-century wandering Persian Sufi master (c. 1185–1248) whose 1244 encounter with Jalal al-Din Rumi in Konya ignited Rumi's mystical awakening. His recorded oral discourses (*Maqalat*) survive in English as *Me & Rumi* (tr. William C. Chittick, 2004).
