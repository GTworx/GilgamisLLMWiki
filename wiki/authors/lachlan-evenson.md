## Biography & Context
Lachlan Evenson is a Principal Product Manager on Microsoft Azure's container compute team and a Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) ambassador who has guided thousands of practitioners into Kubernetes adoption through hands-on engineering education.

Joining the Third Edition of [[kubernetes-up-and-running]] (2022), Evenson helped expand the manual to cover modern Container Runtime Interfaces, Ingress controllers, extensibility, and multicluster deployments (source: BOOKS/Cloud/kubernetesup&running.pdf, pp. 2, 6, 23–123).
