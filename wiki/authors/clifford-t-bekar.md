## Biography & Context
Clifford T. Bekar is Professor of Economics at Lewis & Clark College, specializing in long-run European and comparative economic history, institutional change, and the historical diffusion of General Purpose Technologies.

In [[economic-transformations-general-purpose-technologies-and-long-term-economic-growth]] (2005), co-authored with [[richard-g-lipsey]] and [[kenneth-i-carlaw]], Bekar reconstructs 10,000 years of GPT revolutions and the institutional conditions explaining why sustained intensive growth emerged in the West.
