## Biography & Institutional Context
Michael Snyder is the Stanford W. Ascherman Professor and Chair of the Department of Genetics at Stanford University School of Medicine. A pioneer in personalized medicine and multi-omics profiling, his laboratory discovered the non-linear aging surges occurring at ages 44 and 60.
