## Biography & Context
Lucia Melloni is a neuroscientist and Group Leader at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics (Frankfurt) and Research Professor at NYU Grossman School of Medicine. She serves as the Lead Principal Investigator of the COGITATE Consortium, an international multi-lab adversarial collaboration pitting Integrated Information Theory against Global Neuronal Workspace Theory through preregistered, multimodal neuroimaging (fMRI, MEG, intracranial EEG).
