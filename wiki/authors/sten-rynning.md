## Biography & Context

Sten Rynning is Professor of Political Science and Vice-Dean for Research at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU), where he founded the Center for War Studies. He is a leading European scholar on NATO strategy, modern warfare, and European defense cooperation.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Rynning authored Chapter 3 ("Deterrence Rediscovered: NATO and Russia"), examining the alliance's pivot back to collective defense and the complex relationship between deterrence and political dialogue.
