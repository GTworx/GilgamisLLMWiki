## Biography & Context
Adam B. Smith is an applied climatologist at the NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI) and the lead researcher behind the US Billion-Dollar Weather and Climate Disasters research program. His work combines spatial economics, actuarial loss modeling, and meteorological hazard footprinting to analyze systemic catastrophe risk and socioeconomic vulnerability.
