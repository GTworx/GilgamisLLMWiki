## Biography & Institutional Context
Wolf Reik is Director of the Altos Labs Cambridge Institute of Science, former Director of the Babraham Institute, and Honorary Professor of Epigenetics at the University of Cambridge. A world leader in epigenetic reprogramming, his laboratory pioneered partial reprogramming for human cellular rejuvenation.
