## Biography & Context

John Anthony West (1932–2018) was an American author, lecturer, and independent Egyptologist. West was the chief proponent of the "Symbolist" school of Egyptology pioneered by French philosopher and mathematician R. A. Schwaller de Lubicz, which views Egyptian hieroglyphic culture as the inheritor of a grand sacred science rather than a primitive bronze-age animism.

In *Serpent in the Sky* (1979), West picked up Schwaller de Lubicz's observation that the Sphinx exhibits water weathering. In the early 1990s, he recruited Boston University geologist Dr. Robert Schoch to scientifically survey the monument, triggering the worldwide controversy that overturned orthodox assumptions regarding the dating of early civilization. West received an Emmy Award for the 1993 NBC documentary *The Mystery of the Sphinx*.
