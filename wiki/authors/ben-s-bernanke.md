# Ben S. Bernanke

## Biography
Dr. Ben S. Bernanke (b. 1953) is an American economist, former Chair of the Federal Reserve (2006–2014), senior fellow at Brookings, and 2022 Nobel Laureate in Economic Sciences for research on banks and financial crises.
