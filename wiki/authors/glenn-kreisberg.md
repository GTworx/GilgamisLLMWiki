## Biography & Context

Glenn Kreisberg is a radio frequency engineer, researcher, and author based in Woodstock, New York. He is the former vice president of the New England Antiquities Research Association (NEARA) and the founder of the Empire State Antiquities Research Association. Kreisberg specializes in the archaeoastronomy, electromagnetic properties, and spatial orientations of prehistoric stone structures and petroforms across the Northeastern United States.

As editor of *Lost Knowledge of the Ancients* (2010) and *Mysteries of the Ancient Past* (Bear & Company), Kreisberg assembled leading alternative historians, geologists, and archaeoastronomers to challenge conventional dogmas regarding ancient technologies, acoustics, and celestial tracking systems.
