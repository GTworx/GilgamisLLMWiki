## Biography & Context
Thomas C. Veatch is a linguist and cognitive scientist who formulated the seminal Benign Violation Theory of humor (1998), postulating that laughter and humor arise precisely when a situation is perceived simultaneously as a violation of a subjective norm and as normal/benign. His theoretical model underpins contemporary evolutionary and neurocognitive frameworks of comedy, play, and social bonding.
