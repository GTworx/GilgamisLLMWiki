## Biography & Context
Nairán Ramírez-Esparza is an Associate Professor of Psychological Sciences at the University of Connecticut. Her cross-cultural research examines cultural frame switching among bilingual bicultural individuals, demonstrating how language acts as an active cultural prime that shifts personality profiles (extraversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness) across linguistic contexts.
