# Author: Jalil Mammadguluzadeh

**Bio / Role**: Azerbaijani satirist, playwright, journalist, and Jadid reformer (1866–1932) who founded and edited the pioneering illustrated satirical weekly *Molla Nasreddin* (1906–1930) across Tbilisi, Tabriz, and Baku, championing vernacular Azeri literacy, women's emancipation, secular education, and anti-colonial critique.

## Biography & Contributions

Azerbaijani satirist, playwright, journalist, and Jadid reformer (1866–1932) who founded and edited the pioneering illustrated satirical weekly *Molla Nasreddin* (1906–1930) across Tbilisi, Tabriz, and Baku, championing vernacular Azeri literacy, women's emancipation, secular education, and anti-colonial critique.
