## Biography & Context
Joyce Kay Avila is a Snowflake Data Superhero, Snowflake consulting practice manager, certified public accountant (CPA), MBA, and computer scientist who completed PhD coursework in accounting information systems.

In [[snowflake-the-definitive-guide]] (2022), Avila delivers an authoritative end-to-end engineering guide to Snowflake's three-layer decoupled architecture, virtual warehouses, Streams/Tasks, RBAC governance, Zero-Copy Cloning, Time Travel, and Secure Data Sharing (source: BOOKS/Cloud/Snowflake-The-Definitive-Guide-1.pdf, pp. 2, 16, 48–255).
