## Biography & Context
Hugh Everett III (1930–1982) was an American physicist and mathematician who completed his PhD at Princeton University's Palmer Physical Laboratory under John A. Wheeler before pioneering operations research, game-theoretic defense analysis, and generalized Lagrange multipliers at the Pentagon's Weapons Systems Evaluation Group.

In his legendary 1957 Princeton thesis [[on-the-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-relative-state-formulation]], Everett eliminated external wavefunction collapse in favor of pure universal unitary evolution and 'relative states'—birthing the Many-Worlds Interpretation of quantum mechanics (source: BOOKS/Physics/Everett short thesis as defended.pdf, pp. 1–2).
