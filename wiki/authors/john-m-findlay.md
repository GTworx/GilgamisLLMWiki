## Biography & Context
John M. Findlay is Emeritus Professor of Psychology at Durham University and co-author (with Iain D. Gilchrist) of *Active Vision: The Psychology of Looking and Seeing*. His empirical research pioneered the study of saccadic eye movements, visual search mechanisms, global visual processing ('center-of-gravity' effect), and the tight spatial coupling between oculomotor programming and covert attentional selection.
