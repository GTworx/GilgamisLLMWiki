## Biography & Context
Ashley Dawson is Professor of English at the Graduate Center and College of Staten Island, City University of New York (CUNY), specializing in postcolonial studies, diasporic culture, and environmental justice.

In [[mongrel-nation-colonization-in-reverse]] (2007), Dawson chronicles how post-1945 migration from the Caribbean, South Asia, and Africa—beginning with the 1948 *Empire Windrush* and Jamaican poet [[louise-bennett]]'s patois verse—challenged British imperial racism and forged a composite postcolonial 'contact zone.'
