## Biography & Context
Kelsey Hightower is a renowned distributed-systems engineer, open-source leader, and longtime Principal Engineer and Developer Advocate in Google's cloud computing division celebrated for minimalist, hands-on operational leadership and teaching cloud-native infrastructure.

In [[kubernetes-up-and-running]] (3rd Ed., 2022), Hightower brings production-hardened pragmatism to cluster lifecycle management, Pods, Services, RBAC security, and real-world deployments (source: BOOKS/Cloud/kubernetesup&running.pdf, pp. 2, 69–124).
