## Biography & Contributions

Persian Sunni theologian, jurist, philosopher, and Sufi (*Hujjat al-Islam*, 1058–1111 CE), author of *Ihya Ulum al-Din* (*Revival of the Religious Sciences*) and *The Incoherence of the Philosophers*, whose Book 40 meditation on mortality circulates in Turkish as *Ölüm ve Kıyamet*.
