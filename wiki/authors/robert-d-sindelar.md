## Biography & Context
Robert D. Sindelar, PhD, FCAHS, is Professor of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Medicine at the University of British Columbia and former Dean of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Research Leader at Providence Health Care.

As co-editor of [[pharmaceutical-biotechnology-fundamentals-and-applications]] (4th Ed., 2013), Sindelar bridges molecular cloning, monoclonal antibodies, and translational clinical therapeutics (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2013_Book_PharmaceuticalBiotechnology.pdf, pp. 5–9).
