## Biography & Context
The former Ministry of Public Works and Settlement (Bayındırlık ve İskân Bakanlığı, succeeded by the Ministry of Environment, Urbanisation and Climate Change) historically served as Türkiye's primary central authority for civil infrastructure construction, spatial planning, and seismic building codes.
