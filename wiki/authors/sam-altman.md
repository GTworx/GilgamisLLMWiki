## Biography & Institutional Context
Sam Altman is an American entrepreneur, investor, and CEO of OpenAI. Formerly the president of Y Combinator, Altman has spearheaded the commercialization and scaling of generative artificial intelligence while advocating for massive public-private investments in nuclear fission/fusion, domestic semiconductor manufacturing, and multi-gigawatt sovereign computing clusters.
