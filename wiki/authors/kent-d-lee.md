## Biography & Context
Prof. Kent D. Lee is Professor of Computer Science at Luther College (Decorah, Iowa), author of Springer textbooks on Python, data structures, and programming languages.

In *Python Programming Fundamentals (Second Edition)* (Springer *Undergraduate Topics in Computer Science*, 2014, 241 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-1-4471-6642-9`, synthesized in [[python-and-r-scientific-computing-foundations-for-ai-and-data-science]]), Lee pioneers debugger-driven computer science pedagogy, teaching students to inspect execution stack frames, recursion, object-oriented Tkinter GUIs, and algorithmic complexity from day one (source: BOOKS/AI/2014_Book_PythonProgrammingFundamentals.pdf, pp. 1–8).
