## Biography & Context
Dave Wright is Chief Innovation Officer at ServiceNow, where he advises global Fortune 500 executives on enterprise workflow architecture, autonomous AI agents, and organizational transformation.

Co-authoring [[infinite-how-visionary-leaders-transform-todays-businesses-into-ai-forward-companies]] (2026) with [[brian-solis]], Wright maps the five-stage evolutionary maturity curve toward the **Infinite Enterprise**, the ROI Paradox of AI valuation, and the emergence of the **Chief Workflow Officer (CWO)** (source: BOOKS/AI/2026 Infinite- Leadership in AI Forward Companies.pdf, pp. 7, 48–75, 148–203).
