## Biography & Context
Flexera Research is the empirical benchmarking arm of Flexera, publishing annual global surveys on enterprise cloud adoption, IT Asset Management (ITAM), Software Asset Management (SAM), and FinOps.

In [[state-of-it-asset-management-2022]] (2022, surveying 465 enterprise IT leaders), Flexera documents the acute governance gap between legacy vendor-audit compliance (consuming 83% of SAM time) and the rapidly expanding SaaS, public-cloud, and container estate (source: BOOKS/Cloud/itam-report-state-of-it-asset-management-2022.pdf, pp. 6–9, 33–42).
