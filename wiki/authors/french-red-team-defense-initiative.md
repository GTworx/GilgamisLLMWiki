## Biography & Institutional Context
The French Red Team (Red Team Défense) is an institutional foresight team established by the French Defense Innovation Agency (AID) and the French Armed Forces Ministry. Bringing together renowned science fiction writers, futurists, and military scientists, the Red Team designs disruptive future war scenarios (2030–2060) to stress-test national defense doctrines.
