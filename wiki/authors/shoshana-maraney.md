## Biography & Context
Shoshana Maraney is a Cambridge University–educated fraud-prevention specialist and industry analyst with extensive experience in ecommerce and fintech identity verification at Forter and Identiq.

Co-authoring [[practical-fraud-prevention]] (2022) with [[gilit-saporta]], Maraney bridges fraud research, customer-experience preservation, and cross-departmental defense against stolen cards, mules, friendly fraud, and money laundering (source: BOOKS/Cloud/3683-practical-fraud-prevention.pdf, pp. 2, 19–214).
