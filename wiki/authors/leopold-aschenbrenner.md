## Biography & Context
Leopold Aschenbrenner graduated as valedictorian of Columbia University at age 19, served on OpenAI's Superalignment team under Ilya Sutskever and Jan Leike until 2024, and subsequently founded the San Francisco macro-technology investment fund Situational Awareness LP.

In his landmark June 2024 monograph [[situational-awareness-the-decade-ahead]] (165 pp., dedicated to Ilya Sutskever), Aschenbrenner extrapolates compute, algorithmic, and 'unhobbling' orders of magnitude (OOMs) to argue that AGI by 2027 is strikingly plausible, that automated AI research will trigger a rapid intelligence explosion to superintelligence, and that a Manhattan Project–scale U.S. national security mobilization ('The Project') is inevitable (source: BOOKS/AI/2024 Situational_Awareness_Leopold_Aschenbrenner.pdf, pp. 1–6, 41, 81, 121–151).
