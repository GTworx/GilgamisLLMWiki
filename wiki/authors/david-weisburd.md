## Biography & Context
David Weisburd is Distinguished Professor of Criminology, Law and Society at George Mason University, Walter E. Meyer Professor Emeritus of Law and Criminal Justice at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, and recipient of the Stockholm Prize in Criminology (2010) for pioneering **Place-Randomized Controlled Trials** and formulating the **Law of Crime Concentration at Places**.

In [[handbook-of-quantitative-criminology]] (2010), co-edited with [[alex-r-piquero]], Weisburd synthesizes spatial crime mapping, experimental field trials, and statistical causal inference in criminal justice.
