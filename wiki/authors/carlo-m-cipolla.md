## Biography & Context
Carlo M. Cipolla (1922–2000) was an Italian economic historian who held professorships at the University of Pavia and UC Berkeley. Renowned for his seminal treatises on clocks, guns, and pre-industrial economics, his 1976 essay *The Basic Laws of Human Stupidity* formulated an influential quadripartite taxonomy defining stupid people as those whose actions cause losses to others while yielding no gain (or even loss) to themselves.
