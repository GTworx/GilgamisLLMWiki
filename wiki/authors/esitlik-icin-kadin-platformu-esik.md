## Biography & Context
The Women's Platform for Equality (EŞİK) is a coalition of over 340 women's and LGBTI+ organizations across Türkiye. EŞİK monitors legal compliance, human rights, and constitutional protections, producing groundbreaking feminist field reports on disaster sociology and post-earthquake gender equality.
