# Author: Martin Gardner

**Bio / Role**: American mathematics and science writer (1914–2010) whose *Scientific American* 'Mathematical Games' column (1956–1981) popularized recreational mathematics, Conway's Game of Life, Penrose tilings, and RSA cryptography for generations. Edited and introduced Henry Dudeney's *536 Puzzles & Curious Problems* (1967).
