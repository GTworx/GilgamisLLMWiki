## Biography & Context
Howard Gardner is the John H. and Elisabeth A. Hobbs Research Professor of Cognition and Education at the Harvard Graduate School of Education. Best known for his groundbreaking 1983 theory of Multiple Intelligences (*Frames of Mind*), Gardner has written over thirty books investigating creativity, leadership, moral responsibility (The Good Project), and the psychological profiles of political and scientific elites.
