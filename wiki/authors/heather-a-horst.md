## Biography & Context
Heather A. Horst is a leading digital anthropologist and Director of the Institute for Culture and Society (Western Sydney University; formerly RMIT and UC Irvine) whose ethnographic research examines mobile phones, everyday digital media, and financial technologies across the Caribbean, Pacific, and global cities.

Co-editing [[digital-anthropology]] (2012) with [[daniel-miller]], Horst co-authored the discipline's foundational six-principle prospectus challenging romanticized myths of pre-digital authenticity (source: BOOKS/Anthropology_/Digital-Anthropology-edited-by-Heather-A.-Horst-and-Daniel-Miller.pdf, pp. 4–17).
