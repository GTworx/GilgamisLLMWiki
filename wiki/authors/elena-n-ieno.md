## Biography & Context
Dr. Elena N. Ieno is a marine biologist and senior statistical consultant at Highland Statistics Ltd. (Newburgh, UK).

Co-authoring *A Beginner's Guide to R* (Springer *Use R!*, 2009, 228 pp., synthesized in [[python-and-r-scientific-computing-foundations-for-ai-and-data-science]]) with [[alain-f-zuur]] and [[erik-h-w-g-meesters]], Ieno designs empirical life-science case studies teaching scientists to transition from spreadsheet errors to scripted R workflows (source: BOOKS/AI/2009_Book_ABeginnerSGuideToR.pdf, pp. 4–8).
