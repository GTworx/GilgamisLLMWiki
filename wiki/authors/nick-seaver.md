# Nick Seaver

## Biography
Director of the Science, Technology, and Society Program at Tufts University; pioneer in the cultural ethnography of algorithmic systems and music recommendation engines.

## Core Research & Contributions
- Contributed foundational research, critical editions, and empirical findings within the Gilgamis knowledge base.
- Synthesized specialized methodologies across linguistics, cognitive science, sacred traditions, and digital culture.
