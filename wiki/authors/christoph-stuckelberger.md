## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Christoph Stückelberger (born 1951) is a Swiss ethicist, Founder and President of the **Globethics.net Foundation** in Geneva, Professor Emeritus of Ethics at the University of Basel, and visiting professor in Moscow, Enugu (Nigeria), and Beijing.

As lead editor of [[global-ethics-for-leadership-values-and-virtues-for-life]] (2016) with [[walter-fust]] and [[obiora-ike]] and co-editor of [[ethics-in-higher-education-values-driven-leaders-for-the-future]] (2017) with [[divya-singh]], Stückelberger champions a relational ethic that is simultaneously **globally responsible and contextually rooted**.
