## Biography & Context
Monica Ramona Costache is a mathematician trained at the Department of Mathematics, S.N.S. Bucharest, focusing on topological spaces and linear-operator structures underlying quantum theory.

In [[mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-physics]] (2006, with [[adrian-stan]]), she co-authored the Han-sur-Lesse Winterschool primer on Hilbert-space foundations of quantum mechanics (source: BOOKS/Physics/Adrian-Stan-2018 Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Physics.pdf, pp. 1, 3–10).
