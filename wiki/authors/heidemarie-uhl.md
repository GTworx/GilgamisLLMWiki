# Heidemarie Uhl

## Biography
Dr. Heidemarie Uhl (1956–2023) was an Austrian historian at the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW) and world-renowned scholar of collective memory, Holocaust memorial culture, and historical identity politics.
