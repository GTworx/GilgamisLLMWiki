# Abu Hamid al-Ghazali

## Biography
Abu Hamid al-Ghazali (1058–1111) was one of the most prominent theologians, jurists, and mystics in Islamic history, authoring the monumental *Ihya Ulum al-Din* (The Revival of the Religious Sciences).
