## Biography & Context
**Jonathan Haidt, PhD**, is the Thomas Cooley Professor of Ethical Leadership at the **New York University Stern School of Business** (source: [[leading-for-tomorrow]], Chapter 23 & Contributors, pp. 281–291, 330). Earning his BA in philosophy from Yale University and his PhD in social psychology from the University of Pennsylvania, Haidt taught for sixteen years at the University of Virginia before joining NYU Stern. Named one of *Foreign Policy*'s "Top 100 Global Thinkers" and *Prospect* magazine's "Top World Thinkers," he is co-founder of Heterodox Academy, the Constructive Dialogue Institute, and EthicalSystems.org.

Co-architect of **Moral Foundations Theory**, Haidt is the author of several widely influential books, including *The Happiness Hypothesis* (2006), *The Righteous Mind: Why Good People Are Divided by Politics and Religion* (2012), *The Coddling of the American Mind* (2018, with Greg Lukianoff), and the #1 *New York Times* bestseller *The Anxious Generation* (2024). In Chapter 23 of [[leading-for-tomorrow]] (2026), Haidt analyzes how the "Great Rewiring of Childhood" (the shift from a play-based to a phone-based childhood between 2010 and 2015) and hyper-polarized social media ecosystems have reshaped the incoming workforce, urging leaders to restore institutional telos (truth vs. social advocacy), foster viewpoint diversity, and rebuild resilient, high-trust workplace norms.
