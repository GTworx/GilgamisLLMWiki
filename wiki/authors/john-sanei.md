## Biography & Context
**John Sanei** is a South African-born global futures strategist, bestselling author, and leadership educator who combines future studies, consumer behavior, and applied neuroscience to help individuals and organizations navigate exponential disruption (source: [[the-adaptability-advantage]], Introduction & About the Author, pp. 13–17, 187). He serves as a faculty member at **Singularity University** in Silicon Valley and as an associate lecturer at **Duke Corporate Education (Duke CE)**, and was the first African invited to join the global Singularity University faculty.

Co-host of the award-winning *The Expansive* podcast and author of ten books (including *What's Your Moonshot?* and *Who Do We Become?*), Sanei argues that traditional strategic planning fails during civilizational transitions because leaders attempt to solve novel futures using survival-wired, past-conditioned nervous systems. In [[the-adaptability-advantage]] (2026), he synthesizes trauma release, neuroplasticity, emotional mastery, and technological foresight into a six-part blueprint—teaching leaders how to clear **Emotional Debt**, cultivate **Adaptive Intelligence (AQ)**, and balance high-tech artificial intelligence with high-touch human wisdom.
