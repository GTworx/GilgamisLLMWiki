## Biography & Context
**Vanessa Errecarte, MBA**, is a strategic marketing advisor, entrepreneur, Founder and CEO of **Marketing Simplified**, and a longtime lecturer at the **UC Davis Graduate School of Management**, where she created and teaches the school's premier MBA course on personal branding and digital strategy (source: [[valuable-and-visible]], Introduction & About the Author, pp. 14–18, 255). Over a twenty-year career in brand strategy and digital marketing, she has advised Fortune 500 executives, healthcare practitioners, academic researchers, and mission-driven founders.

Errecarte's work on personal branding was galvanized by a family medical crisis when her infant daughter Ella was diagnosed with infantile idiopathic scoliosis and spared invasive spinal surgeries thanks to another parent sharing early serial-casting information online ([[valuable-and-visible]], pp. 14–16). Recognizing that quiet, credentialed experts frequently leave the digital public square to unqualified, algorithm-chasing influencers, Errecarte developed **Service-First Personal Branding** and the **EPIC Method** (**E**stablish, **P**ackage, **I**nform, **C**ultivate). In [[valuable-and-visible]] (2026), she provides a comprehensive playbook for packaging expertise into memorable frameworks (**ABCs of Framework Creation**), transitioning from a "digital renter" to a "digital owner," and overcoming impostor syndrome by reframing professional silence as *"unintentional selfishness."*
