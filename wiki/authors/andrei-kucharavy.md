## Biography & Context
Andrei Kucharavy is a Professor and Senior Researcher at HES-SO Valais-Wallis (Sierre, Switzerland) and EPFL, collaborating with the Swiss Cyber-Defence (CYD) Campus of armasuisse Science and Technology.

As lead editor and principal contributor of [[large-language-models-in-cybersecurity-threats-exposure-and-mitigation]] (Springer Open Access, 2024, 249 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-031-54827-7`), Kucharavy maps the architecture, scaling limits, hallucination mechanics, training-data leakage, prompt injection vulnerabilities, and dual-use offensive/defensive cyber capabilities of foundation models (source: BOOKS/AI/AI LLM Cybesecurity.pdf, pp. 1–10, 35–51, 109–121, 195–201).
