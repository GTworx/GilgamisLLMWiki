# Author: Jerrold M. Post

**Bio / Role**: American psychiatrist (1934–2020), Professor of Psychiatry, Political Psychology, and International Affairs at George Washington University, and founder of the CIA's Center for the Analysis of Personality and Political Behavior. Pioneered at-a-distance political personality profiling for Camp David and Gulf Crisis diplomacy, and edited *The Psychological Assessment of Political Leaders* (2003).
