## Biography & Context
Andreas Krause is Professor of Computer Science at ETH Zürich, where he leads the Learning & Adaptive Systems Group, serves as Chair of the ETH AI Center, and is an ACM Fellow and Ellis Fellow.

In [[probabilistic-artificial-intelligence]] (ETH Zürich, February 2025, `arXiv:2502.05244v1`, 418 pp., co-authored with [[jonas-hubotter]]), Krause unifies Bayesian inference, Gaussian processes, submodular mutual information maximization, Bayesian optimization, and safe model-based reinforcement learning under a single rigorous mathematical framework where uncertainty is a first-class citizen (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 Probabilistic AI.pdf, pp. 1–11, 59–101, 161–301).
