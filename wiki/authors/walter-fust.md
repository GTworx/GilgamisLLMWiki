## Biography & Context
Ambassador Walter Fust (born 1945) is a Swiss diplomat who served for fifteen years as Director-General of the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC, 1993–2008), CEO of the Global Humanitarian Forum under Kofi Annan, and President of the Board of Globethics.net.

In [[global-ethics-for-leadership-values-and-virtues-for-life]] (2016), Fust details how embedding shared ethical values transforms institutional management and international development governance.
