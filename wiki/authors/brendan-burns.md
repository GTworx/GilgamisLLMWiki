## Biography & Context
Brendan Burns is a cofounder of the Kubernetes open-source project and a Distinguished Engineer at Microsoft Azure, where he leads teams focused on DevOps, open source, containers, and microservices. Previously at Google, he helped architect cloud-scale container management systems inspired by Borg and Omega.

In [[kubernetes-up-and-running]] (3rd Ed., 2022, co-authored with [[joe-beda]], [[kelsey-hightower]], and [[lachlan-evenson]]), Burns codifies immutable infrastructure, declarative configuration, and self-healing reconciliation loops as the universal foundation of cloud-native computing (source: BOOKS/Cloud/kubernetesup&running.pdf, pp. 2, 23–27).
