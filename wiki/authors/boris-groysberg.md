## Biography & Institutional Context
Boris Groysberg is the Richard P. Chapman Professor of Business Administration at Harvard Business School. Renowned for his scholarship on human capital, star talent management (*Chasing Stars*), and organizational culture, his crisis leadership research investigates executive decision-making and neurobiology under extreme threat states.
