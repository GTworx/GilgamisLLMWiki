## Biography & Context
Garret J. Etgen is Professor of Mathematics at the University of Houston specializing in ordinary differential equations and modernizing undergraduate calculus pedagogy.

Co-authoring [[calculus-one-and-several-variables]] (10th Ed., 2007), Etgen integrated 1,200+ visual illustrations, interdisciplinary applied projects, and Skill Mastery Review sets across 1,171 pages (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, pp. 7–12).
