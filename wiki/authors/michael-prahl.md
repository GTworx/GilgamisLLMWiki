## Biography & Context
Michael Prahl is a private equity practitioner, former Executive Director of INSEAD's Global Private Equity Initiative (GPEI), and co-founder of Asia-focused private equity advisory and investment ventures.

Co-authoring [[mastering-private-equity]] (2017) with [[claudia-zeisberger]] and [[bowen-white]], Prahl bridges academic corporate finance with real-world deal sourcing, auction pricing, governance, and operational transformation (source: BOOKS/Private Equity/2017 mastering-private-equity-transformation-via-venture-capital-minority-investments-and-buyouts.pdf, pp. 5–7, 346).
