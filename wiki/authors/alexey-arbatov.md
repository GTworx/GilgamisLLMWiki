## Biography & Context

Alexey Arbatov (born 1951) is Head of the Center for International Security at the Primakov National Research Institute of World Economy and International Relations (IMEMO) of the Russian Academy of Sciences in Moscow. A member of the Russian Academy of Sciences and former member of the Russian State Duma (where he served as Deputy Chairman of the Defense Committee), Arbatov is one of Russia's most eminent authorities on nuclear arms control, disarmament, and strategic stability.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Arbatov authored Chapter 5 ("Nuclear Deterrence: A Guarantee for or Threat to Strategic Stability?"), offering a critical Russian perspective on the erosion of the bilateral arms control architecture, the perils of counterforce precision weapons, and the rising danger of strategic instability between the US and Russia.
