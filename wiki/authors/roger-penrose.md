## Biography & Context
Sir Roger Penrose (born 1931) is Rouse Ball Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at the University of Oxford and recipient of the **2020 Nobel Prize in Physics** (for proving that black hole singularity formation is a robust prediction of Einstein's General Theory of Relativity). Renowned for Penrose tilings (quasicrystals), spin networks, twistor theory, and cosmic censorship, Penrose has also reshaped the philosophy of mind.

In [[the-emperors-new-mind-concerning-computers-minds-and-the-laws-of-physics]] (1989/1999), Penrose marshals Turing's halting problem, Gödel's incompleteness theorem, the **Weyl Curvature Hypothesis**, and quantum measurement paradoxes to argue that human mathematical insight and consciousness are fundamentally **non-algorithmic**, requiring objective state-vector reduction in a future theory of **Correct Quantum Gravity (CQG)**.
