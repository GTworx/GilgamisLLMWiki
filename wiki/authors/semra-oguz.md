## Biography & Context
Specialist Physiotherapist Semra Oğuz focuses on neurological rehabilitation and daily-mobility exercise protocols for patients living with Parkinson's disease and movement disorders.

In [[parkinson-hastaligi-hasta-ve-yakinlari-icin-el-kitabi]] (2013), she co-authored the structured physical-therapy and posture/gait training programs empowering patient independence (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Parkinson-Hastaligi-Hasta-ve-Yakinlari-icin-El-Kitabi.pdf, pp. 1, 6).
