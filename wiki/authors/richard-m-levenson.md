## Biography & Institutional Context
Richard M. Levenson is Professor and Vice Chair for Strategic Technologies in the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at UC Davis Health. Known for pioneering multispectral imaging and MUSE microscopy, he led the research training pigeons to read medical imaging.
