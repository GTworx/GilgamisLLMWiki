## Biography & Context
Christian L. Degen is Professor of Spin Physics at ETH Zurich and a pioneer in quantum sensing. His laboratory conducts foundational research into diamond nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center magnetometry, nanoscale nuclear magnetic resonance, and quantum metrology.
