## Biography & Institutional Context
Johannes Himmelreich is an assistant professor of public administration and international affairs at Syracuse University's Maxwell School. His work examines the political philosophy of emerging technologies, focusing on whether human driving constitutes a fundamental civil right in an era of automated transit.
