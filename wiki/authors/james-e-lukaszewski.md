## Biography & Context
**James E. Lukaszewski**, ABC, APR, Fellow PRSA, BEP, is one of the world's foremost crisis communication strategists, widely recognized across corporate, military, and government circles as *"America's Crisis Guru®"* (source: [[influencing-leaders]], About the Authors, p. 229). Founder and President of The Lukaszewski Group Division of Risdall Public Relations, he has spent nearly five decades coaching C-suite executives, generals, admirals, and corporate boards through existential crises, hostile litigation, regulatory investigations, and ethical reckonings.

Lukaszewski served for twenty-two years as an adjunct professor of strategic communication at New York University and has authored fourteen books on crisis communication, reputation recovery, and executive counseling. A recipient of the Public Relations Society of America (PRSA) **John W. Hill Award** and named among *Corporate Legal Times*' "28 Experts to Call When All Hell Breaks Loose," Lukaszewski focuses on the behavioral mechanics of how technical specialists transition from peripheral "doers" to trusted boardroom confidants. In [[influencing-leaders]] (2026), co-authored with [[helio-fred-garcia]], he codifies a career of executive advising into a step-by-step operating manual for reading leadership psychology, navigating the "No-Friendship Zone," and speaking the "Language of Done."
