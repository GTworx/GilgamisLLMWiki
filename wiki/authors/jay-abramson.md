## Biography & Context
Jay Abramson is Principal Lecturer in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Arizona State University with over 33 years of precalculus teaching experience and leadership in open-access STEM curricula.

As Senior Contributing Author of OpenStax's [[precalculus-openstax]] (2017, 1,156 pp.), Abramson created a free, peer-reviewed bridge from algebraic/trigonometric functions to university calculus (source: BOOKS/Math/Precalculus-OP_9wwF7YT.pdf, pp. 3–4, 13–15).
