## Biography & Context
Ethan Mollick (PhD, MIT Sloan School of Management) is Associate Professor of Management at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania and Co-Director of Wharton Generative AI Labs, where he leads empirical field experiments with Boston Consulting Group (BCG) on human–AI collaboration.

In his *New York Times* bestseller [[co-intelligence-living-and-working-with-ai]] (2024, 114 pp. analytical summary edition), Mollick formulates the **Four Rules for Co-Intelligence**, the **Jagged Technological Frontier**, **Centaurs vs. Cyborgs** workflow architectures, and the transformation of AI into Person, Creative, Coworker, Tutor (solving Bloom's Two-Sigma Problem), and Coach (source: BOOKS/AI/2024 Co-Intelligence PDF Summary_.pdf, pp. 3–11, 31, 61, 91).
