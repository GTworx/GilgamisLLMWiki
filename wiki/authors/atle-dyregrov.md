## Biography & Context
Atle Dyregrov is a Norwegian clinical psychologist, Professor Emeritus at the Center for Crisis Psychology (Faculty of Psychology, University of Bergen), and a founding member of the European Society for Traumatic Stress Studies. He is internationally recognized for his pioneering clinical work on child bereavement, post-disaster psychological trauma, and school-based crisis intervention.
