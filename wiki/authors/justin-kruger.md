## Biography & Context
Justin Kruger is a social psychologist and Professor of Marketing at New York University's Stern School of Business. Alongside David Dunning, he co-authored the foundational 1999 study demonstrating the 'Dunning-Kruger Effect'—the metacognitive dual burden whereby incompetent individuals lack the very skills needed to recognize their own incompetence, while high performers erroneously assume others possess equal facility.
