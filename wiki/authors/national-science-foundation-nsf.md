## Biography & Institutional Context
The National Science Foundation (NSF) is an independent U.S. federal agency supporting fundamental research across science and engineering. Its 'Future of Manufacturing' (FoM) initiative directs multi-hundred-million-dollar grants into cyber-manufacturing, bioprinting, and sustainable production platforms.
