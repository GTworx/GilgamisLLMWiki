## Biography & Context
Richard G. Lipsey (born 1928) is Professor Emeritus of Economics at Simon Fraser University, Founding Director of the Economic Growth and Policy Program at the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research (CIAR), and co-originator (with Kelvin Lancaster) of the celebrated *General Theory of Second Best* (1956).

In [[economic-transformations-general-purpose-technologies-and-long-term-economic-growth]] (2005), co-authored with [[kenneth-i-carlaw]] and [[clifford-t-bekar]], Lipsey formulates the **Structuralist-Evolutionary (S-E)** theory of long-term economic growth driven by 24 historical **General Purpose Technologies (GPTs)** from Neolithic agriculture and Sumerian writing to electricity and the electronic computer.
