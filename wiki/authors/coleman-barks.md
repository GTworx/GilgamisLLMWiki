# Author: Coleman Barks

**Bio / Role**: American poet and emeritus professor of English at the University of Georgia (b. 1937) whose free-verse poetic renderings of Rumi—including *The Essential Rumi* (1995) and *Rumi: The Book of Love* (2003), crafted from scholarly translations by John Moyne, A. J. Arberry, and Reynold Nicholson—made Rumi one of the most widely read poets in the English-speaking world.
