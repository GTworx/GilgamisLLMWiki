## Biography & Context
Marco Di Maggio is a Professor at Harvard Business School and Imperial College Business School, Faculty Research Fellow at the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER), and Co-Director of the HBS FinTech Lab. His scholarship spans financial intermediation, monetary policy transmission, credit networks, decentralized finance (DeFi), and Web3 tokenomics.

In [[blockchain-crypto-and-defi-bridging-finance-and-technology]], Di Maggio synthesizes the cryptographic foundations, smart-contract architectures, Layer-2 scaling, stablecoins, CBDCs, Automated Market Makers, regulatory frameworks, and AI-blockchain convergence shaping modern digital finance.
