## Biography & Institutional Context
Daniel Palanker is Professor of Ophthalmology and Director of the Hansen Experimental Physics Laboratory at Stanford University. He is the inventor of the PRIMA subretinal photovoltaic micro-implant system, restoring artificial central vision to patients blinded by geographic atrophy.
