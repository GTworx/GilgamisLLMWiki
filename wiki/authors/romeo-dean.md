## Biography & Context
Romeo Dean is a computer science researcher at Harvard University and the AI Futures Project specializing in AI hardware supply chains, wafer/packaging bottlenecks, and gigawatt datacenter buildouts.

In [[ai-2027]] (2025), Dean models the FLOP scaling trajectories, Nvidia NVL72 GB300 server architectures, and Taiwan Strait geopolitical dependencies underpinning the 2025–2027 intelligence explosion (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 Book_ai-2027.pdf, pp. 1, 4, 11, 46).
