## Biography & Context
Pui Kei Johnston Chu is a cloud data architect and Snowflake specialist focusing on scalable multi-tenant storage, workload isolation, and data-application security patterns.

Co-authoring [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]] (2021), Chu contributes architectural blueprints for balancing tenant isolation, compute concurrency, and governance in data-intensive SaaS (source: BOOKS/SaaS/OReilly-Report-Architecting-Data-Intensive-SaaS-Applications.pdf, pp. 3–4, 31–42).
