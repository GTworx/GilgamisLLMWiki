## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Ton J. Cleophas (MD, PhD) is Professor of Clinical Pharmacology and Biostatistics at the Department of Medicine, Albert Schweitzer Hospital (Dordrecht, The Netherlands) and past President of the American College of Angiology, having co-authored over 25 Springer textbooks on clinical biostatistics and medical data mining.

In [[machine-learning-in-medicine-a-complete-overview]] (Springer, 2015, 498 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-15195-3`, co-authored with [[aeilko-h-zwinderman]]), Cleophas synthesizes 80 step-by-step clinical machine learning methodologies across cluster/classification models, generalized (log)linear models, and rule/ensemble models for patient risk stratification and diagnostic discovery (source: BOOKS/AI/2015_Book_MachineLearningInMedicine-ACom.pdf, pp. 1–11, 51, 201, 401).
