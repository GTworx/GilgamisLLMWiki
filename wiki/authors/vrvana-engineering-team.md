## Biography & Institutional Context
The Vrvana engineering team designed the pioneer Totem mixed-reality headset, introducing ultra-low-latency video pass-through and inside-out positional tracking. The startup was acquired by Apple in 2017 to form the core optical and systems architecture for Apple Vision Pro.
