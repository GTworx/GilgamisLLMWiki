## Biography & Context
Héctor García (born 1981 in Spain; known as Kirai) is a former CERN software engineer and longtime Tokyo resident whose essays and books interpret Japanese culture, craftsmanship, and longevity for global audiences.

Co-authoring [[ikigai-the-japanese-secret-to-a-long-and-happy-life]] (2016/2017) with [[francesc-miralles]], García conducted field interviews in Ogimi, Okinawa to distill the lifestyle, diet, and existential purpose of the world's longest-lived communities (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/Ikigai _ the Japanese secret to a long and happy life ( PDFDrive ).pdf, pp. 3, 8–16).
