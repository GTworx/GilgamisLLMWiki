## Biography & Context
Fernando Pizarro is a SaaS revenue strategist, technology author, and Winning by Design partner who co-authored and edited the foundational *Sales Blueprints* series for scaling recurring-revenue teams.

In [[blueprints-for-a-saas-sales-organization]] (2018, with [[jacco-van-der-kooij]]) and as editor of [[the-saas-sales-method-for-customer-success-and-account-managers]] (2018), Pizarro translates post-Series A SaaS scaling into repeatable pods, content fuel, and data-driven Sales Operations Centers (source: BOOKS/SaaS/(Winning By Design) Jacco Van Der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro - Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization-Fernando Pizzaro, Jacco Van Der Kooij (2017) (2).pdf, pp. 4–7).
