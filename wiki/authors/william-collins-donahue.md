## Biography & Context
William Collins Donahue is the John J. Cavanaugh Professor of the Humanities at the University of Notre Dame (formerly at Rutgers and Duke University) and a leading scholar of 20th-century German and Austrian literature, Weimar culture, and Holocaust studies.

In [[the-end-of-modernism-elias-canettis-auto-da-fe]], Donahue overturns ahistorical readings of [[elias-canetti]]'s *Die Blendung* (*Auto-da-Fé*), demonstrating that Canetti crafted an 'analytic modernism' that indicts Weimar intellectual escapism, misogyny, anti-Semitism, and the collapse of the democratic public sphere.
