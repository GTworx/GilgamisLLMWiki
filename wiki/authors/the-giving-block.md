## Biography & Context
The Giving Block is a cryptocurrency and NFT fundraising infrastructure platform enabling charities, hospitals, universities, and social-impact foundations to accept and process digital-asset philanthropy.

In [[crypto-and-nft-philanthropy-afsp-case-study]] (2022), The Giving Block documents how the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention raised $400,000 in crypto and NFT donations during 2021 (source: BOOKS/Anthropology_/CASE STUDY-TGB-AFSP.pdf, p. 1).
