## Biography & Context
Atif Kureishy is Vice President of Global Emerging Practices (AI and Deep Learning) at Teradata / Think Big Analytics, advising Fortune 500 enterprises on production machine learning architectures.

In [[achieving-real-business-outcomes-from-artificial-intelligence-and-the-future-of-machine-intelligence]] (O'Reilly Media, 2019, 73 pp., co-authored with [[chad-meley]] and [[ben-mackenzie]]), Kureishy codifies the enterprise data, talent, governance, and custom deep-learning pipelines required to turn AI pilots into measurable financial and operational outcomes (source: BOOKS/AI/2019 Achieving-Real-Business-Outcomes-from-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf, pp. 1–11, 26, 51).
