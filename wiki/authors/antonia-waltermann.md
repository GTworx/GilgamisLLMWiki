## Biography & Context
Dr. Antonia Waltermann is Assistant Professor of Foundations and Methods of Law at Maastricht University, specializing in sovereignty, non-human legal agency, and comparative legal theory.

In [[introduction-to-law]] (2017), Waltermann co-edits the transnational curriculum bridging Civil Law, Common Law, EU Law, and legal philosophy.
