## Biography & Context
Maria Schuld is a pioneering quantum machine learning researcher at Xanadu Quantum Technologies and the University of KwaZulu-Natal. Author of foundational textbooks on QML, Schuld's research bridges statistical learning theory, kernel methods, and variational quantum circuits.
