## Biography & Context
Dr. Miroslav Kubat is Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Miami (Coral Gables, Florida), internationally recognized for his pioneering research on learning from imbalanced datasets, decision-tree induction, and radial-basis function networks.

In [[an-introduction-to-machine-learning]] (Springer, Second Edition, 2017, 348 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-63913-0`), Kubat delivers a comprehensive 17-chapter pedagogical synthesis of modern inductive learning—from Bayesian classifiers, Perceptrons, WINNOW, SVMs, and Computational Learning Theory (PAC/VC-dimension) to Bagging, Schapire's Boosting, AdaBoost, Deep Learning, and rigorous statistical performance evaluation (source: BOOKS/AI/2017_Book_AnIntroductionToMachineLearnin.pdf, pp. 1–11, 39–51, 142–151, 245–251).
