## Biography & Context
Dr. Marshall S. Rich holds a PhD in Forensic Cyberpsychology and is a cybersecurity researcher and practitioner specializing in the intersection of network forensics, machine learning anomaly detection, and behavioral profiling of cyber adversaries.

In [[advanced-network-forensics-a-cyber-behavioral-science-approach]] (2026), Rich introduces the **Cyber Forensics Behavioral Analysis (CFBA)** model and **Adversarial Behavioral Score (ABS)**, uniting Dark Triad/Big Five psychological profiling with deception networks and SCADA/IoT critical infrastructure forensics.
