## Biography & Context
Bernd Meibohm, PhD, FCP, is Professor and Chair of Pharmaceutical Sciences at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Pharmacy (Memphis), specializing in the PK/PD and immunogenicity of therapeutic proteins and monoclonal antibodies.

Co-editing [[pharmaceutical-biotechnology-fundamentals-and-applications]] (4th Ed., 2013), Meibohm integrates quantitative biopharmacology with oncology, autoimmune, and regenerative biologics (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2013_Book_PharmaceuticalBiotechnology.pdf, pp. 5–9).
