## Biography & Context
Monsignor Prof. Dr. Obiora Ike (born 1956) is a Nigerian ethicist, human rights theologian, Founder of the Catholic Institute for Development, Justice and Peace (CIDJAP) in Enugu, Professor of Ethics at Godfrey Okoye University, and Executive Director of Globethics.net in Geneva.

Across [[global-ethics-for-leadership-values-and-virtues-for-life]] (2016) and [[ethics-in-higher-education-values-driven-leaders-for-the-future]] (2017), Ike bridges African communal ethics (*Ubuntu*), the UN 2030 Sustainable Development Goals, and value-driven university teaching.
