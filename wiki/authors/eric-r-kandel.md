## Biography & Context
Eric R. Kandel (born 1929 in Vienna) is University Professor Emeritus of Biochemistry and Biophysics at Columbia University, Senior Investigator at the Howard Hughes Medical Institute, and recipient of the **2000 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine** for discovering the molecular and synaptic mechanisms of short- and long-term memory storage in neurons (using the marine snail *Aplysia*).

In [[reductionism-in-art-and-brain-science-bridging-the-two-cultures]] (2016), Kandel bridges C. P. Snow's 'Two Cultures' by showing how post-WWII New York School Abstract Expressionists (de Kooning, Pollock, Rothko, Louis, Flavin, Turrell) used artistic reductionism of form, line, color, and light in parallel with neurobiological reductionism—shifting visual perception from bottom-up figurative recognition to top-down creative imagination (the **Beholder's Share**).
