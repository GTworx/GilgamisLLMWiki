## Biography & Context
Iain Staffell is Professor of Sustainable Energy Systems at the Centre for Environmental Policy, Imperial College London, and co-creator of the *Renewables.ninja* and *Electric Insights* platforms advising governments and the IPCC on renewable power integration.

In [[monetizing-energy-storage-a-toolkit-to-assess-future-cost-and-value]] (2023), co-authored with [[oliver-schmidt]], Staffell models the system-level economics, learning rates, and arbitrage revenues of twelve grid storage technologies.
