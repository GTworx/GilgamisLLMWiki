## Biography & Context
Sergey Bravyi is an IBM Fellow and world-renowned quantum information theorist at the IBM Thomas J. Watson Research Center. Co-inventor of magic-state distillation and quantum LDPC bivariate bicycle codes, Bravyi has made foundational contributions to quantum complexity theory and topological quantum memory.
