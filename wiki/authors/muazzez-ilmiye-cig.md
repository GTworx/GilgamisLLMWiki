# Author: Muazzez İlmiye Çığ

## Biography & Contributions

Trailblazing Turkish archaeologist and Sumerologist (1914–2024) who catalogued tens of thousands of Sumerian, Akkadian, and Hittite cuneiform tablets at the Istanbul Archaeology Museums alongside Samuel Noah Kramer. Author of *Kur'an, İncil ve Tevrat'ın Sumer'deki Kökeni* (1995).
