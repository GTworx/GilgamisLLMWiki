## Biography & Context
**Chris Shipley** is a veteran Silicon Valley technology analyst, innovation strategist, and co-leader of the **Silicon Guild**, a consortium of bestselling business and leadership authors (source: [[leading-for-tomorrow]], About the Editors, p. 340). From 1996 to 2009, Shipley served as Executive Producer of the **DEMO Conference**, where she helped launch and spotlight some of the most transformative technologies of the modern internet era—including TiVo, VMware, Salesforce, PalmPilot, and E*Trade—earning recognition from the *San Jose Mercury News* as "the most powerful woman in Silicon Valley you've never heard of."

Co-founder of Guidewire Group and co-author (with Heather McGowan) of *The Adaptation Advantage* (2020) and *The Empathy Advantage* (2023), Shipley specializes in how technological inflection points reshape human work, learning, and identity. In [[leading-for-tomorrow]] (2026), co-edited with [[scott-barry-kaufman]], she curates and synthesizes twenty-six essays from world-renowned thinkers (including [[rita-mcgrath]], [[amy-c-edmondson]], and [[jonathan-haidt]]), charting how leaders can harness generative AI, navigate demographic and geopolitical turbulence, and lead with empathy and strategic foresight.
