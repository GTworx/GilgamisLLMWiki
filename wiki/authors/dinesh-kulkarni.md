## Biography & Context
Dinesh Kulkarni is a senior data-platform leader and co-author specializing in secure data sharing, cloud data marketplaces, and enterprise SaaS data monetization.

In [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]] (2021), Kulkarni examines how Sharing by Reference replaces brittle copy-based data exchanges, enabling SaaS ecosystems like Braze on the Snowflake Data Marketplace (source: BOOKS/SaaS/OReilly-Report-Architecting-Data-Intensive-SaaS-Applications.pdf, pp. 3–4, 55–63).
