## Biography & Context
Dr. Kwo Young is a principal battery materials scientist at BASF / Ovonic Battery Company who has authored numerous foundational studies on Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH) and Lithium-ion (Li-ion) electrochemistry for hybrid and battery electric vehicles.

In [[electric-vehicles-light-duty-fuel-economy-and-shared-micromobility]], Young (with Caisheng Wang, Le Yi Wang, and Kai Strunz) formulates the propulsion physics, State of Charge ($SOC$) / Depth of Discharge ($DOD$) dynamics, and cell-voltage vs. safety trade-offs of EV battery packs.
