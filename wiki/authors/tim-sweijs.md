## Biography & Context

Dr. Tim Sweijs is Director of Research at The Hague Centre for Strategic Studies (HCSS) and a Senior Research Fellow at the Netherlands Defence Academy. He holds degrees in War Studies (Ph.D., M.A. from King's College London), International Relations (M.Sc.), and Philosophy (B.A. from the University of Amsterdam). 

Sweijs is a specialist in horizon scanning, conflict early warning, strategic foresight, and capability development. He advises national governments, NATO, and international institutions on emerging technologies, cross-domain coercion, and evolving geopolitical risks. In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Sweijs co-edited the volume and authored foundational analyses on cross-domain deterrence and contemporary deterrence theory synthesis.
