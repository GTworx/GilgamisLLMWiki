## Biography & Context
Google Quantum AI is the advanced quantum computing hardware and research division of Alphabet, based in Santa Barbara, California. The group achieved historic milestones including the first demonstration of quantum computational supremacy (Sycamore, 2019) and foundational breakthroughs in superconducting quantum error correction.
