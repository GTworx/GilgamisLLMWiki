## Biography & Context
Lawrence C. Miller, CISSP, is a prolific enterprise technology author, information-security specialist, and veteran contributor to John Wiley & Sons' *For Dummies* Custom Publishing series across IoT, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and enterprise SaaS.

Across five Wiley Special Editions in this wiki—the three-volume [[iot-for-dummies-applications-solutions-and-security-compendium]] (2016–2018, for INSIDE Secure, Qorvo, and Arm), [[iaas-for-dummies-oracle-special-edition]] (2018), and [[saas-for-dummies-oracle-special-edition]] (2021)—Miller maps the full enterprise modernization stack from hardware-rooted edge security to second-generation bare-metal IaaS and AI-embedded cloud SaaS suites (source: BOOKS/IoT/Book IoT_Security_for_Dummies_VNK.pdf, pp. 3–7; BOOKS/Cloud/Book IaaS_For_Dummies_2nd_Oracle_Special_Edition.pdf, pp. 3–11; BOOKS/SaaS/saas-for-dummies-3rdedition.pdf, pp. 3–7).
