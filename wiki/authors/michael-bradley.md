## Biography & Context
Michael Bradley is the Global Program Director for Blockchain Enablement and Education at IBM, with over two decades of enterprise architecture experience across IBM Development Labs (Messaging, IoT, Emerging Technologies) and IBM Services.

As lead co-author of [[getting-started-with-enterprise-blockchain]] alongside [[david-gorman]], [[matt-lucas]], and [[matthew-golby-kirk]], Bradley codifies the governance, PKI privacy, and Hyperledger Fabric smart-contract patterns for permissioned multi-company business networks.
