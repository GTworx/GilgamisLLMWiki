## Biography & Context
Dominique Levin is a SaaS executive, Winning by Design partner, and customer-success strategist focused on engineering frictionless post-sale onboarding, recurring customer impact, and compounding Net Revenue Retention.

In [[the-saas-sales-method-for-customer-success-and-account-managers]] (2018, co-authored with [[jacco-van-der-kooij]]), Levin operationalizes Preboarding, Business Orchestration, Customer Health Scores, The Golden Sequence, and Executive Impact Reviews (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Dominique Levin  Jacco van der Kooij  Winning by Design - The SaaS Sales Method for Customer Success  Account Managers_ How to Grow Customers (Sales Blueprints Book 6)-Winning By Design (2018).pdf, pp. 7–16, 48–76).
