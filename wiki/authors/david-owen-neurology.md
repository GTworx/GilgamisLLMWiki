## Biography & Context
David Owen (Baron Owen) is a British physician, neurologist, and politician who served as UK Foreign Secretary (1977–1979) and co-founded the Social Democratic Party (SDP). Bridging medical neuroscience and statecraft, Owen co-developed the clinical framework of 'Hubris Syndrome' with psychiatrist Jonathan Davidson, identifying acquired executive personality disorders triggered by prolonged political or military power.
