## Biography & Context
DeLiang Wang is Professor of Computer Science and Engineering and Center for Cognitive and Brain Sciences at The Ohio State University, and IEEE Fellow. He is a pioneer in computational auditory scene analysis and oscillatory neural networks, formulating the Locally Excitatory Globally Inhibitory Oscillator Network (LEGION) for dynamic feature binding and visual object segmentation.
