## Biography & Context
David Baum is a technology writer and enterprise architecture analyst who collaborates with O'Reilly Media and cloud data engineering leaders to document modern analytics and cybersecurity operating models.

In [[deploying-a-modern-security-data-lake]] (2022), Baum explains how cloud-native security data lakes, the Connected Applications Model, and Detection-as-Code replace brittle, expensive legacy SIEMs (source: BOOKS/Cloud/Deploying a Modern Security Data Lake.pdf, pp. 1–14, 26–44).
