## Biography & Context
Rafael Yuste is Professor of Biological Sciences and Neuroscience at Columbia University, Director of the NeuroTechnology Center, and co-originator of the US BRAIN Initiative. A pioneer in two-photon calcium imaging and holographic optogenetics, Yuste chairs the NeuroRights Foundation, campaigning globally for constitutional neuro-rights protecting mental privacy, cognitive liberty, and psychological integrity against invasive BCIs.
