## Biography & Context
Daan J. A. Crommelin, PhD, is Professor Emeritus of Biopharmaceutics at the Utrecht Institute for Pharmaceutical Sciences (Utrecht University, The Netherlands) and a global authority on recombinant protein formulation, liposomes, and pharmaceutical biotechnology.

Co-editing [[pharmaceutical-biotechnology-fundamentals-and-applications]] (4th Ed., 2013) with [[robert-d-sindelar]] and [[bernd-meibohm]], Crommelin anchors the foundational science of biologics formulation and delivery (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/2013_Book_PharmaceuticalBiotechnology.pdf, pp. 5–9).
