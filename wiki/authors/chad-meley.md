## Biography & Context
Chad Meley is Vice President of Analytics and AI Products and Solutions at Teradata.

Co-authoring [[achieving-real-business-outcomes-from-artificial-intelligence-and-the-future-of-machine-intelligence]] (O'Reilly Media, 2019), Meley examines production AI case studies across financial fraud detection, autonomous driving telemetry, manufacturing yield optimization, and healthcare (source: BOOKS/AI/2019 Achieving-Real-Business-Outcomes-from-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf, pp. 1–26).
