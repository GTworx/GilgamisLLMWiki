## Biography & Context
Anil Seth is Professor of Cognitive and Computational Neuroscience at the University of Sussex, Co-Director of the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research (CIFAR) Program on Brain, Mind, and Consciousness, and author of *Being You: A New Science of Consciousness*. His research pioneers the predictive processing model of perception, viewing conscious experience as a 'controlled hallucination' calibrated by sensory prediction errors.
