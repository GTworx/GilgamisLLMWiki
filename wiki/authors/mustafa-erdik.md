## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Mustafa Erdik is Emeritus Professor of Earthquake Engineering at Boğaziçi University Kandilli Observatory, President of the Turkish Earthquake Foundation, and recipient of the Bruce Bolt Medal. He is the preeminent scholar of seismic hazard assessment and risk modeling in Istanbul.
