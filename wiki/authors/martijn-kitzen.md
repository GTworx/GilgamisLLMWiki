## Biography & Context

Prof. Dr. Martijn Kitzen holds the Chair of Irregular Warfare and Special Operations at the Faculty of Military Sciences, Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA). A former Royal Netherlands Army infantry officer with operational deployments, Kitzen specializes in local conflict dynamics, tribal networks, and counterinsurgency.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Kitzen (with Christina van Kuijck) authored Chapter 15 ("All Deterrence Is Local: The Utility and Application of Localised Deterrence in Counterinsurgency"), demonstrating how micro-level social dynamics govern deterrence in irregular conflicts.
