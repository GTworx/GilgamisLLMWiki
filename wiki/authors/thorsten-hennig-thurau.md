## Biography & Context
Thorsten Hennig-Thurau is Professor of Marketing and Media Research at the University of Münster (Germany) and Research Professor at City, University of London, renowned for his pioneering econometric studies on motion picture box office, streaming cascades, electronic word-of-mouth, and social media.

In [[entertainment-science-data-analytics-and-practical-theory]] (2019), co-authored with [[mark-b-houston]], Hennig-Thurau replaces Hollywood's *'Nobody-Knows-Anything'* myth with data analytics and introduces the **Pinball Framework** of digital entertainment communication.
