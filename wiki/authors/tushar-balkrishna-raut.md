## Biography & Context
CMA Dr. Tushar Balkrishna Raut is an Assistant Professor at St. Gonsalo Garcia College of Arts & Commerce (Vasai) and course author for the University of Mumbai's Institute of Distance and Open Learning (IDOL) Master in Management Studies (MMS) finance program.

In [[venture-capital-and-private-equity-mms-curriculum]] (2023), Raut integrates global VC/PE valuation, term-sheet structuring, and exit strategies with SEBI AIF regulations, Indian Income Tax Act provisions, and developing-market ethical governance (source: BOOKS/Private Equity/Private Equity & Venture Capital MS Thesis.pdf, pp. 2–6, 118).
