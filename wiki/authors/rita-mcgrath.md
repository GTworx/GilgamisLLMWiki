## Biography & Context
**Rita Gunther McGrath, PhD**, is a longtime professor of management at **Columbia Business School**, founder of the advisory firm Valize, and one of the world's most cited authorities on strategy and innovation in uncertain environments (source: [[leading-for-tomorrow]], Foreword & Contributors, pp. 11–14, 333). Consistently ranked in the **Top 10 of the Thinkers50** global management awards and winner of the Thinkers50 #1 Strategy Award, McGrath received the Strategic Management Society's CK Prahalad Award for scholarly impact on practice.

She is the author of several landmark strategy texts, including *Discovery-Driven Growth* (with Ian MacMillan), *The End of Competitive Advantage: How to Keep Your Strategy Moving as Fast as Your Business* (2013), and *Seeing Around Corners: How to Spot Inflection Points in Business Before They Happen* (2019). In her Foreword to [[leading-for-tomorrow]] (2026), McGrath argues that the 20th-century industrial leadership playbook—built around optimizing stable competitive advantages and hierarchical control—has reached a structural inflection point, requiring leaders to govern through distributed sensing, permissionless innovation at the edges, and psychological safety.
