# Grégoire Courtine

## Biography
Grégoire Courtine is Professor of Neuroscience at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Lausanne (EPFL) and co-director of the Defitech Center for Interventional Neurotherapies (NeuroRestore), pioneering digital bridges that restore walking after spinal cord injury.
