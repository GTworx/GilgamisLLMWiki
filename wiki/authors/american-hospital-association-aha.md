## Biography & Institutional Context
The American Hospital Association (AHA) is the national organization that represents and serves nearly 5,000 hospitals, healthcare networks, and patients across the United States, providing definitive annual statistical censuses on hospital bed capacity, operational expenses, and uncompensated care burdens.
