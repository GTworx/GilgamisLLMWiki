## Biography & Context
Henry P. Stapp is a senior theoretical physicist at the University of California's Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory who worked with Wolfgang Pauli and Werner Heisenberg and pioneered research on Bell nonlocality, S-matrix theory, and the role of conscious observation in von Neumann quantum mechanics.

In [[mind-matter-and-quantum-mechanics]] (3rd Ed., 2009), Stapp formulates the Heisenberg/James model linking intentional human attention, Process 1 collapse, and the Quantum Zeno Effect to neuroplasticity (source: BOOKS/Physics/Henry Stapp - Mind Matter and Quantum mechanics.pdf, pp. 5, 7–11).
