## Biography & Context
Mike Amundsen is an internationally recognized API architect, speaker, and O'Reilly author who advises enterprises worldwide on RESTful architecture, distributed web design, and API-first digital transformation.

In [[api-strategy-for-decision-makers]] (2022, co-authored with [[derric-gilling]]), Amundsen translates Jobs-to-Be-Done product thinking, non-breaking API evolution, and customer observability into an executive lifecycle strategy (source: BOOKS/Cloud/OReilly-API-Strategy-for-Decision-Makers.pdf, pp. 5–17, 35–47).
