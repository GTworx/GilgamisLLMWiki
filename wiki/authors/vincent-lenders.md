## Biography & Context
Dr. Vincent Lenders is the founding Director of the Cyber-Defence (CYD) Campus at armasuisse Science and Technology (Thun/Lausanne/Zurich, Switzerland) and co-founder of the OpenSky Network and Electrosense.

Co-editing [[large-language-models-in-cybersecurity-threats-exposure-and-mitigation]] (2024), Lenders oversees research uniting EPFL, ETH Zurich, IBM Research, and Sophos AI on securing critical systems against adversarial LLM exploitation (source: BOOKS/AI/AI LLM Cybesecurity.pdf, pp. 4–16).
