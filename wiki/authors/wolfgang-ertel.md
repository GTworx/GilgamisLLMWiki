## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Wolfgang Ertel is Professor of Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science at Ravensburg-Weingarten University of Applied Sciences (Germany) and Head of the Institute for Artificial Intelligence, renowned for his research on parallel automated theorem proving (SETHEO), maximum-entropy probabilistic logic (LEXMED), and service robotics.

In [[introduction-to-artificial-intelligence]] (Springer *Undergraduate Topics in Computer Science*, Second Edition, 2017, 365 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-58487-4`), Ertel provides a unified mathematical textbook spanning propositional and first-order predicate logic, heuristic search, maximum-entropy and Bayesian reasoning under uncertainty, machine learning, deep convolutional networks, and reinforcement learning (source: BOOKS/AI/2017_Book_IntroductionToArtificialIntell.pdf, pp. 1–11, 51, 151, 251).
