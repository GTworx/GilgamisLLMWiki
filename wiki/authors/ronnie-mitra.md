## Biography & Context
Ronnie Mitra is a software architect, API strategist, and consultant (Mitra Pandey Consulting, Ltd.) specializing in microservices operating models, Domain-Driven Design, and large-scale digital transformation.

In [[microservices-up-and-running]] (2021, co-authored with [[irakli-nadareishvili]]), Mitra introduces the SEED(S) API-first design method, Team Topologies alignment, and GitOps Kubernetes delivery (source: BOOKS/Cloud/Microservices-Up-and-Running_complete.pdf, pp. 4, 31–72).
