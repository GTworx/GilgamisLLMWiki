## Biography & Context
CB Insights Research is a data-driven market-intelligence platform analyzing venture capital, emerging technology strategy, M&A, and Big Tech expansion across global industries.

In [[amazon-in-healthcare-strategy-for-a-3-trillion-market]] (2018), CB Insights dissects Amazon's PillPack acquisition, employer healthcare venture, PBM disruption, and Alexa/Prime aging-in-place strategy (source: BOOKS/Health Pharma/CB-Insights_Amazon-Strategy-Healthcare.pdf, pp. 1–4).
