## Biography & Context
Daniel Kokotajlo is Executive Director of the AI Futures Project and a former governance researcher at OpenAI (2022–2024), widely recognized for his remarkably accurate 2021 forecast *'What 2026 Looks Like'* and for publicly resigning from OpenAI's non-disparagement equity agreement to warn of AGI safety risks.

In [[ai-2027]] (published April 2025, 71 pp., co-authored with [[scott-alexander]], [[thomas-larsen]], [[eli-lifland]], and [[romeo-dean]]), Kokotajlo leads a month-by-month, quantitatively grounded scenario forecast charting the emergence of recursive AI R&D automation, model weight espionage, misaligned superintelligence (`Agent-1` to `Agent-5`), and the geopolitical race between the U.S. ('OpenBrain') and China ('DeepCent') (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 Book_ai-2027.pdf, pp. 1–36, 46–71).
