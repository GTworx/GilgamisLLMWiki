## Biography & Context
Karen Hao is an award-winning investigative journalist and former senior AI editor at *MIT Technology Review*, foreign correspondent for *The Wall Street Journal*, and contributor to *The Atlantic*.

Drawing on seven years of reporting and over 300 interviews with more than 260 insiders—including 90+ current and former OpenAI executives, researchers, and contractors alongside field investigations across Kenya, Chile, and Uruguay—Hao authored [[empire-of-ai-dreams-and-nightmares-in-sam-altmans-openai]] (Penguin Press, 2025, 575 pp.), dissecting how OpenAI transformed from a non-profit safety lab into an imperial corporate apparatus (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 empire-of-ai-dreams-and-nightmares-in-sam-altmans-openai.pdf, pp. 3–11, 25–26, 251).
