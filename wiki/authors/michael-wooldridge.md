## Biography & Context
Michael Wooldridge (born 1966) is Professor of Computer Science and former Head of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford, Director of Foundational AI Research at the Alan Turing Institute, and Fellow of the AAAI, EurAI, and ACM, having published over 400 scientific papers on multi-agent systems and rational agency.

In [[a-brief-history-of-artificial-intelligence-what-it-is-where-we-are-and-where-we-are-going]] (Flatiron Books / Macmillan, 2020, ISBN 978-1-250-77073-8), Wooldridge chronicles the intellectual history of AI from Alan Turing's 1950 imitation game and the 1956 Dartmouth Workshop through the symbolic 'Golden Age,' the 1973 Lighthill Report AI Winter, expert systems, behavioral robotics, rational agent theory, and the post-2012 deep learning revolution, while rigorously distinguishing narrow statistical mastery from conscious general intelligence (source: BOOKS/AI/a-brief-history-of-artificial-intelligence-what-it-is-where-we-are-and-where-we-are-going.epub, ch. 1–9).
