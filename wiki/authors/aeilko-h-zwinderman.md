## Biography & Context
Prof. Dr. Aeilko H. Zwinderman is Professor of Biostatistics and Chair of the Department of Clinical Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Bioinformatics at the Academic Medical Center (AMC), University of Amsterdam, and past President of the International Society for Clinical Biostatistics.

Co-authoring [[machine-learning-in-medicine-a-complete-overview]] (Springer, 2015, 498 pp.) with [[ton-j-cleophas]], Zwinderman bridges classical clinical epidemiology (propensity scores, Cox survival regression, mixed models) with 80 machine learning methodologies in SPSS and SPSS Modeler (source: BOOKS/AI/2015_Book_MachineLearningInMedicine-ACom.pdf, pp. 4–11, 51, 201, 401).
