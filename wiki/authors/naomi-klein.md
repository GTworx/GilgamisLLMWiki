## Biography & Context
Naomi Klein (born 1970) is a Canadian author, social theorist, and Professor of Climate Justice at the University of British Columbia, known for *No Logo* (1999), *The Shock Doctrine* (2007), *This Changes Everything* (2014), and *Doppelganger: A Trip into the Mirror World* (2023).

In [[doppelganger-israel-palestine-and-the-doppelganger-effect]] (2023), Klein uses the psychoanalytic and literary figure of the **doppelgänger** to analyze how Nazi fascism mirrored European settler-colonial genocides (*'The Nazi in the Mirror'*), how Christian anti-Semitism projected capitalism's abstract domination onto Jews, recovery of the Jewish Labor Bund's principle of ***doikayt* ('hereness')**, and the tragic twin dynamics of Israel and Palestine.
