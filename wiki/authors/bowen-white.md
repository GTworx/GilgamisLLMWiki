## Biography & Context
Bowen White is an alternative-investments practitioner (formerly with Permal and INSEAD's Global Private Equity Initiative) specializing in institutional GP–LP dynamics, portfolio construction, and private-market evolution.

As co-author of [[mastering-private-equity]] (2017), White brings LP-centric analytical rigor to fundraising, performance reporting, co-investing, and PE secondaries (source: BOOKS/Private Equity/2017 mastering-private-equity-transformation-via-venture-capital-minority-investments-and-buyouts.pdf, pp. 5–7, 346).
