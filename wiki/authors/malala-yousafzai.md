# Author: Malala Yousafzai

**Bio / Role**: Pakistani education activist, co-founder of the Malala Fund, and youngest-ever recipient of the Nobel Peace Prize (2014). Co-author (with Christina Lamb) of *I Am Malala* (2013), chronicling her defiance of Taliban school bans in the Swat Valley and advocacy for universal girls' education.
