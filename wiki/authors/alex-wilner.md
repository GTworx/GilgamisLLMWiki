## Biography & Context

Dr. Alex Wilner is Associate Professor at the Norman Paterson School of International Affairs (NPSIA) at Carleton University, Ottawa, Canada. He is an internationally recognized expert on deterrence theory, non-state actors, counter-terrorism, and the strategic impact of emerging technologies.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Wilner (with Casey Babb) authored Chapter 21 ("New Technologies and Deterrence: Artificial Intelligence and Adversarial Behaviour"), introducing the concept of hyper-coercion driven by artificial intelligence.
