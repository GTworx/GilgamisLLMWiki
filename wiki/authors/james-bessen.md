## Biography & Context
James Bessen is Executive Director of the Technology & Policy Research Initiative at Boston University School of Law. Before becoming an economist, Bessen founded Bestinfo in 1983, developing the first commercial WYSIWYG desktop-publishing software for PCs.

In [[learning-by-doing-the-real-connection-between-innovation-wages-and-wealth]] (2015), Bessen proves through two centuries of economic history (from 19th-century Lowell power-loom weavers to modern bank ATMs and software workers) that major inventions only raise mass wages after decades of uncodified **'learning by doing'** and standardized skill diffusion.
