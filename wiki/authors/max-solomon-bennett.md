## Biography & Context
Max Solomon Bennett is an AI researcher, cognitive neuroscience writer, and entrepreneur (co-founder of Alby and former VP of Product at Bluecore) whose work bridges evolutionary neurobiology and artificial intelligence.

In [[a-brief-history-of-intelligence-evolution-ai-and-the-five-breakthroughs-that-made-our-brains]] (2023/2025, 103 pp. synthesis edition), Bennett reconstructs the four-billion-year evolutionary lineage of the human brain through five canonical architectural breakthroughs—*Steering*, *Reinforcing*, *Simulating*, *Mentalizing*, and *Speaking*—and maps each directly onto modern AI paradigms on the threshold of 'Breakthrough #6' (silicon superintelligence) (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 A Brief History of Intelligence.pdf, pp. 3–11, 31, 91).
