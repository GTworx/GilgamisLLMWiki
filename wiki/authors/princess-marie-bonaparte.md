## Biography & Context
Princess Marie Bonaparte (1882–1962) was a French psychoanalyst, great-granddaughter of Lucien Bonaparte (Napoleon's brother), and close disciple of Sigmund Freud, whom she famously rescued from Nazi-occupied Vienna. She was a founding member of the Paris Psychoanalytic Society and contributed seminal psychoanalytic studies on the psychosexual perception of time, female sexuality, and psychobiography.
