## Biography & Context
Dr. Oliver Schmidt is an energy storage economist, Honorary Research Fellow at Imperial College London, and clean-energy industry executive whose research in *Nature Energy* and *Joule* pioneered the application of experience curves and Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS) across stationary and mobile energy storage.

In [[monetizing-energy-storage-a-toolkit-to-assess-future-cost-and-value]] (2023), co-authored with [[iain-staffell]], Schmidt provides a rigorous techno-economic toolkit for evaluating future costs, revenue stacking, and Long-Duration Energy Storage (LDES) across decarbonizing power grids.
