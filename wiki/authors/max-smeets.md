## Biography & Context

Dr. Max Smeets is Director of the European Cyber Conflict Research Initiative (ECCRI) and Senior Researcher at the Center for Security Studies (CSS) at ETH Zurich. He is an Affiliate at Stanford University's CISAC and author of *No Shortcuts: Why States Struggle to Develop a Military Cyber-Force*.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Smeets (with Stefan Soesanto) authored Chapter 20 ("Cyber Deterrence: The Past, Present, and Future"), evaluating how the unique technical properties of cyberspace reshape deterrence theory.
