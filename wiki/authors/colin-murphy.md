## Biography & Context
Dr. Colin Murphy is Research and Consulting Director at the Shared-Use Mobility Center (SUMC) in Chicago, specializing in public transit integration, multimodal data standards, and clean urban mobility.

As lead author of *TCRP Research Report 230: Transit and Micromobility* in [[electric-vehicles-light-duty-fuel-economy-and-shared-micromobility]], Murphy analyzes first-and-last-mile bikeshare/e-scooter integration, fare subsidies, and the Mobility Data Specification (MDS).
