# Yusuf Halaçoğlu

## Biography
Prof. Dr. Yusuf Halaçoğlu is a prominent Turkish historian, former president of the Turkish Historical Society (Türk Tarih Kurumu, 1993–2008), and leading authority on Ottoman demographic records.
