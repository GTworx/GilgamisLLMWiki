## Biography & Context
Adrian Stan is a theoretical physicist affiliated with the Zernike Institute for Advanced Materials (University of Groningen, The Netherlands) specializing in many-body quantum physics and operator formalism.

Co-authoring [[mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-physics]] (2006) with [[monica-ramona-costache]], Stan connects Planck/Einstein/Dirac/von Neumann quantum history to Hilbert-space topology (source: BOOKS/Physics/Adrian-Stan-2018 Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Physics.pdf, pp. 1, 5–6).
