## Biography & Context
Marc Benioff is the cofounder, Chair, and CEO of Salesforce (founded in 1999 after a 13-year executive career at Oracle), widely credited as the chief evangelist of Software-as-a-Service (SaaS), multi-tenant cloud CRM, the V2MOM management framework, and the 1-1-1 integrated corporate philanthropy model.

In [[behind-the-cloud]] (2009, co-authored with [[carlye-adler]]), Benioff shares 111 operational plays detailing how Salesforce's 'End of Software' campaign scaled from a San Francisco apartment into a global cloud platform (source: BOOKS/SaaS/Behind the Cloud_ The Untold Story of How Salesforce.com Went from Idea to Billion-Dollar Company-and Revolutionized an Industry   ( PDFDrive ).pdf, pp. 2–6, 10–15).
