## Biography & Context
Bernard F. Rogers Jr. was an American traveler and photographer for the *National Geographic* archive during the 1930s whose visual documentation of Mediterranean and Near Eastern ports preserved rare linguistic and infrastructural details of interwar urban life.

In [[gormedigimiz-turkiye-123-yillik-national-geographic-arsivi]], Rogers's 1937 photographs of Istanbul's Galata Bridge ferry terminals document maritime transit across Moda–Kalamış and Adalar–Pendik as well as early Republican Turkish signage (*'Girilir'* and *'Çıkılır'*).
