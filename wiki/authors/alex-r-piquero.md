## Biography & Context
Alex R. Piquero is a world-renowned criminologist (Professor at the University of Miami, formerly Florida State University, and former Director of the U.S. Bureau of Justice Statistics) whose research focuses on criminal careers, developmental trajectory modeling, and quantitative criminological methods.

As co-editor of [[handbook-of-quantitative-criminology]] (2010) with [[david-weisburd]], Piquero codified the statistical, longitudinal, and experimental toolkit of modern empirical criminology.
