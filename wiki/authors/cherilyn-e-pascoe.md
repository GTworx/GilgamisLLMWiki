# Cherilyn E. Pascoe

## Biography
Cherilyn E. Pascoe is the Director of the National Cybersecurity Center of Excellence (NCCoE) at NIST, leading the multi-year modernization and interagency drafting of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0.
