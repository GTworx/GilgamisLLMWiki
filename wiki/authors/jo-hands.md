## Biography & Context
Jo Hands is Co-Founder and Director of Whiteark (Melbourne, Australia), a management consulting and operational-transformation firm partnering with private equity sponsors and portfolio executives from acquisition due diligence through exit readiness.

In [[the-ultimate-private-equity-playbook]] (2021, co-authored with [[james-ciuffetelli]]), Hands codifies the dual-lever framework separating Strategic Multiple-Expansion levers from Operational EBITDA-Margin levers (source: BOOKS/Private Equity/Private+Equity+Playbook+-+by+Whiteark.pdf, pp. 2–6, 44).
