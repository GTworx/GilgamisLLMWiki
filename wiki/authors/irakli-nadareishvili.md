## Biography & Context
Irakli Nadareishvili is an enterprise software architect, technology executive, and adjunct faculty contributor at Temple University whose work focuses on microservices architecture, Event Sourcing, CQRS, and cloud-native engineering organizations.

Co-authoring [[microservices-up-and-running]] (2021) with [[ronnie-mitra]], Nadareishvili details how bounded-context data ownership, Terraform IaC pipelines, and Argo CD GitOps reduce inter-team coordination costs (source: BOOKS/Cloud/Microservices-Up-and-Running_complete.pdf, pp. 4–5, 91–194).
