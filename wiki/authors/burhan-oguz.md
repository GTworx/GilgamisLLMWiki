## Biography & Contributions

Turkish mechanical and electrical engineer (İTÜ, 1942), early labor unionist, industrialist, university lecturer, and self-taught polymath historian of Anatolian culture (1919–2009). Winner of the Sedat Simavi Social Sciences Award, he authored the encyclopedic *Türkiye Halkının Kültür Kökenleri* and the three-volume *Türk Halk Düşüncesi ve Hareketlerinin İdeolojik Kökenleri* (1997).
