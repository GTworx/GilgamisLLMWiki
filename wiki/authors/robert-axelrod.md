## Biography & Context

Robert Axelrod (born 1943) is an American political scientist, game theorist, and Professor Emeritus of Political Science and Public Policy at the Gerald R. Ford School of Public Policy at the University of Michigan. He is renowned internationally for his interdisciplinary research on the evolution of cooperation, conflict resolution, complex adaptive systems, and decision-making in international security.

Axelrod's landmark 1984 book *The Evolution of Cooperation* transformed game theory and social science by providing rigorous empirical and mathematical proof that cooperative behavior can emerge and stabilize among self-interested actors without an overarching sovereign authority. His work bridges political science, evolutionary biology (working closely with William D. Hamilton), economics, psychology, and artificial intelligence. He was awarded a MacArthur "Genius" Fellowship in 1987 and the National Medal of Science in 2014.
