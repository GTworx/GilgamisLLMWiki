## Biography & Context
Patrick Radden Keefe is an American investigative journalist, staff writer at *The New Yorker*, and author of *Say Nothing*, *Empire of Pain*, and *Rogues*. In addition to his investigations into institutional secrecy and pharmaceutical corruption, Keefe has written extensively on psychedelic therapy, altered states of consciousness, and mystical cognition in clinical trials.
