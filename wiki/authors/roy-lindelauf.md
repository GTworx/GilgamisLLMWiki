## Biography & Context

Prof. Dr. Roy Lindelauf is Professor of Data Science and Military Operations at the Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA) and Associate Professor of Operations Research at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft). He specializes in game-theoretic modeling, network analysis, algorithmic decision systems, and intelligence analytics.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Lindelauf authored Chapter 22 ("Nuclear Deterrence in the Algorithmic Age: Game Theory Revisited"), examining the mathematical foundations of classical strategic games (Prisoner's Dilemma, Chicken) and their interaction with automated, AI-driven command and control architectures.
