## Biography & Context
J. Michael Spector is Professor of Learning Technologies at the University of North Texas, former President of the Association for Educational Communications and Technology (AECT), and Editor of the *Handbook of Research on Educational Communications and Technology*.

In [[educational-technology-a-primer-for-the-21st-century]] (2019), Spector synthesizes cognitive learning science, ADDIE instructional design, and Design-Based Research (DBR).
