# Daniel J. Bernstein

## Biography
Daniel J. Bernstein (djb) is an internationally renowned cryptographer, mathematician, and research professor at the University of Illinois Chicago and Ruhr University Bochum, pioneering modern public-key cryptography (Curve25519, ChaCha20) and lattice-based post-quantum algorithms (NTRU Prime, SPHINCS+).
