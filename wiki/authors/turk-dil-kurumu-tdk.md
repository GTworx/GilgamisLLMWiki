# Türk Dil Kurumu (TDK)

## Biography
The Turkish Language Association (Türk Dil Kurumu, TDK) is the regulatory body of the Turkish language, founded in 1932 by Mustafa Kemal Atatürk to conduct philological research and lexicography.
