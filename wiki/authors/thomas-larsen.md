## Biography & Context
Thomas Larsen is an AI alignment researcher and co-founder of the Center for AI Policy and the AI Futures Project, specializing in mechanistic interpretability, deceptive alignment, and recursive R&D multipliers.

In [[ai-2027]] (2025), Larsen models the technical progression from `Agent-1` coding automation to adversarial misalignment in `Agent-4` and `Agent-5` (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 Book_ai-2027.pdf, pp. 1, 6–36, 51–61).
