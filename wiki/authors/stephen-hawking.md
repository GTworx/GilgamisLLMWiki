## Biography & Context
Professor Stephen W. Hawking, CH, CBE, FRS (1942–2018) was the Lucasian Professor of Mathematics at the University of Cambridge (1979–2009) and one of the preeminent theoretical physicists of the modern era, celebrated for the Penrose–Hawking singularity theorems, the discovery of thermal Hawking radiation from black holes, the Hartle–Hawking No-Boundary cosmological proposal, and global science communication.

Across four landmark works in this wiki—[[a-brief-history-of-time]] (1988), [[the-theory-of-everything-origin-and-fate-of-the-universe]] (2005), [[the-grand-design]] (2010, with [[leonard-mlodinow]]), and [[brief-answers-to-the-big-questions]] (2018)—Hawking bridges spacetime singularity physics, M-theory, model-dependent realism, space colonization, and existential AI risk (source: BOOKS/Physics/A Brief History of Time ( PDFDrive ).pdf, pp. 4–7; BOOKS/Physics/Brief Answers to the Big Questions ( PDFDrive ).pdf, pp. 5–18).
