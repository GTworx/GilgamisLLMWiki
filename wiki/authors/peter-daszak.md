## Biography & Institutional Context
Peter Daszak is a British-American zoologist and President of EcoHealth Alliance. A world authority on disease ecology, his research focuses on identifying novel zoonotic viruses in wildlife populations (bats) at tropical deforestation frontiers before they jump to humans.
