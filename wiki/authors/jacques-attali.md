## Biography & Context
Jacques Attali (born 1943) is a French economist, social theorist, polymath author of over eighty books, Special Adviser to President François Mitterrand (1981–1991), and founding President of the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD, 1991–1993). Educated at École Polytechnique, Sciences Po, and ENA, Attali has long investigated how cultural forms, information networks, and technological shifts prefigure macroeconomic transformations.

In his landmark treatise [[noise-the-political-economy-of-music]] (*Bruits*, 1977 / 1985), Attali argues that music is not a passive reflection of the economic base, but a prophetic herald of future social orders across four historical networks: *Sacrificing*, *Representing*, *Repeating*, and *Composing*.
