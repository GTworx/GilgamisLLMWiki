## Biography & Context
The Niels Bohr Institute at the University of Copenhagen, founded by quantum pioneer Niels Bohr in 1921, is a world-leading center for quantum physics, condensed matter theory, and solid-state quantum photonics under the leadership of Peter Lodahl.
