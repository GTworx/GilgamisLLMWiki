# Ömer Lütfi Taşcıoğlu

## Biography
Dr. Ömer Lütfi Taşcıoğlu (Brig. Gen. Ret.) is a Turkish military historian and author of foundational multilingual treatises on late Ottoman archival records, Caucasian campaigns, and Turkish-Armenian diplomatic relations.
