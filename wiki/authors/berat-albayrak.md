## Biography & Context
Dr. Berat Albayrak (born 1978) is a Turkish businessman, finance PhD (specializing in renewable energy investment financing), and politician who served as Türkiye's Minister of Energy and Natural Resources (2015–2018) and Minister of Treasury and Finance (2018–2020).

In [[burasi-cok-onemli-enerjiden-ekonomiye-tam-bagimsiz-turkiye]] (2022), Albayrak documents the formulation of Türkiye's **National Energy and Mining Policy**—including FSRU/underground gas storage expansion, the **YEKA** renewable technology-transfer auctions, and the acquisition of Türkiye's sovereign deep-sea drilling fleet (*Fatih*, *Yavuz*, *Kanuni*) that discovered the Sakarya Black Sea gas field.
