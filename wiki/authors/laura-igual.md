## Biography & Context
Dr. Laura Igual is Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at the Universitat de Barcelona (Spain) and researcher at the Computer Vision Center (CVC).

In [[introduction-to-data-science-a-python-approach-to-concepts-techniques-and-applications]] (Springer *Undergraduate Topics in Computer Science*, 2017, 227 pp., `DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-50017-1`, co-authored with [[santi-segui]]), Igual synthesizes the complete Python data-science workflow—from Pandas/NumPy exploratory data analysis, statistical estimation, and LASSO sparse regression to network analysis, recommender systems, A/B testing, and MapReduce big-data pipelines (source: BOOKS/AI/2017_Book_IntroductionToDataScience.pdf, pp. 1–11, 51, 121, 181).
