## Biography & Context
David Eagleman is a neuroscientist at Stanford University, CEO of Neosensory, and best-selling author of *Incognito* and *Livewired*. His laboratory studies time perception, sensory substitution, synesthesia, and neural plasticity, formulating the 'Defensive Activation Theory' which posits REM sleep as a defensive mechanism preventing visual cortex takeover during circadian darkness.
