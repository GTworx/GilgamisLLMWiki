## Biography & Context
Gilit Saporta is Director of Fraud Analytics at DoubleVerify with over two decades of hands-on leadership in online fraud prevention, risk analytics, and financial-crime intelligence at PayPal, Forter, and leading fintech organizations.

In [[practical-fraud-prevention]] (2022, co-authored with [[shoshana-maraney]]), Saporta codifies adversarial fraudster archetypes, dynamic risk-vs.-friction calibration, story-based ML modeling, and SQL/Python defense frameworks across eCommerce, consumer banking, marketplaces, and AML (source: BOOKS/Cloud/3683-practical-fraud-prevention.pdf, pp. 2, 19–133).
