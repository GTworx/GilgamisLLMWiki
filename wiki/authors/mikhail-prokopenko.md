## Biography & Context
**Mikhail Prokopenko, PhD**, is Professor of Complex Systems in the School of Computer Science and **Director of the Centre for Complex Systems** at the **University of Sydney**, Australia, having previously served as Principal Research Scientist at CSIRO (Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation) (source: [[the-non-orientable-topology-of-condorcets-paradox]], Title Page & References, pp. 1, 18–20). Author of hundreds of peer-reviewed publications and multiple books on guided self-organization, information dynamics, epidemic modeling, and collective intelligence, Prokopenko is internationally recognized for applying information theory, statistical mechanics, and algebraic topology to multi-agent systems and societal decision-making.

In [[the-non-orientable-topology-of-condorcets-paradox]] (2026), co-authored with [[ori-livson]] and [[siddharth-pritam]], Prokopenko investigates the topological foundations of collective rationality. By demonstrating that Condorcet cycles under transitivity induce non-orientable manifolds (the Klein bottle $K$ and real projective plane $\mathbb{RP}^2$) and proving that Arrow's Unrestricted Domain and Non-Dictatorship axioms correspond respectively to orientable and non-orientable social choice spaces (Theorems 6 & 7, pp. 15–16), Prokopenko and his co-authors unify topological social choice with the broader mathematics of self-reference, Gödelian incompleteness, and multi-agent collective intelligence.
