## Biography & Context
**Kerri O'Neill** is Chief People Officer for **Ipsos UK & Ireland**, Global Lead for AI Workplace Transformation at Ipsos, and a Trustee of **Oxfam GB** (source: [[rapid-reculturing]], About the Authors, pp. 295–296). Named to the **CIPD HR30** list of the most influential human resources leaders and recipient of multiple industry awards for workplace culture and inclusion, O'Neill has led global people transformations across media, market research, retail, and international humanitarian organizations.

Previously serving as Senior Partner at Bailey & French alongside [[alex-bailey]], O'Neill bridges theoretical organizational psychology with frontline executive execution across hybrid workforces and AI-disrupted industries. In [[rapid-reculturing]] (2026), co-authored with Bailey, she documents how organizations can dismantle bureaucratic friction ("process debt"), deploy the **5-5-5 Coaching Conversation**, and integrate generative AI as an empowering human co-pilot rather than an instrument of surveillance.
