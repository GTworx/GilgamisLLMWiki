## Biography & Context
Dr. Alain Mermoud is Head of Technology Monitoring and Intelligence at the Cyber-Defence (CYD) Campus of armasuisse Science and Technology in Thun, Switzerland.

As co-editor of [[large-language-models-in-cybersecurity-threats-exposure-and-mitigation]] (2024), Mermoud directs strategic horizon-scanning on generative AI risks for the Swiss Federal Department of Defence, Civil Protection and Sport (source: BOOKS/AI/AI LLM Cybesecurity.pdf, pp. 4–6).
