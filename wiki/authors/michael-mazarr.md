## Biography & Context

Michael J. Mazarr is a Senior Political Scientist at the RAND Corporation and former Associate Dean and Professor of Strategy at the US National War College. Mazarr has authored numerous influential studies for the US Department of Defense and Joint Staff on grand strategy, grey-zone conflict, deterrence dynamics, and international order.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Mazarr authored Chapter 2 ("Understanding Deterrence"), providing a comprehensive foundation of core deterrence principles: the distinction between deterrence by denial and deterrence by punishment, the essential role of assurances, and the cognitive requirements for credible signaling.
