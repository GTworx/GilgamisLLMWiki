# Turkish Disaster and Emergency Management Authority (AFAD)

## Biography
The Disaster and Emergency Management Authority (Afet ve Acil Durum Yönetimi Başkanlığı - AFAD) is Turkey's central governmental institution coordinating national disaster risk reduction, emergency response, and international humanitarian operations.
