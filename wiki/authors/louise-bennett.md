## Biography & Context
Louise Simone Bennett-Coverley, OM, OJ, MBE (1919–2006), affectionately known as **'Miss Lou,'** was a pioneering Jamaican poet, folklorist, educator, and BBC radio host (* Caribbean Carnival*, 1945–1953) who elevated Jamaican Patois ('Nation Language') from colonial stigmatization to a celebrated literary art form.

As analyzed by [[ashley-dawson]] in [[mongrel-nation-colonization-in-reverse]], Bennett's famous dramatic monologue *'Colonization in Reverse'* satirized and celebrated the post-1948 Windrush exodus to Britain as a world-historical inversion of the British Empire.
