## Biography & Context
Lorwen C. Nagle is a cognitive and behavioral neuroscientist whose research dissects the neurocircuitry of chronic procrastination, frontoparietal-limbic competition, hyperbolic delay discounting, and executive fatigue, developing empirical interventions for volitional control failure.
