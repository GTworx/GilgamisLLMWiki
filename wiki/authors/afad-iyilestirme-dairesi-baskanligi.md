## Biography & Context
The Department of Recovery of AFAD (İyileştirme Dairesi Başkanlığı) is responsible for planning, executing, and monitoring post-catastrophe rehabilitation, temporary-to-permanent shelter transitions, rights-holder housing allocations, and community restitution across disaster-affected zones in Türkiye.
