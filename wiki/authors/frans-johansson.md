## Biography & Context
Frans Johansson (born 1968) is a Swedish-American writer, entrepreneur, and CEO of The Medici Group (educated at Brown University and Harvard Business School) who pioneered the study of cross-disciplinary innovation.

In [[the-medici-effect-what-elephants-and-epidemics-can-teach-us-about-innovation]] (2004/2006), Johansson coined the **Medici Effect** to explain how stepping into the **Intersection** of diverse disciplines, cultures, and domains (such as Renaissance Florence or Mick Pearce's termite-mound biomimetic architecture) ignites exponential creative breakthroughs.
