## Biography & Context
Stephen Marsh is Professor of Computer Science at Ontario Tech University and a pioneer in computational trust, having authored the first doctoral dissertation formalizing computational trust for artificial intelligence in 1994. His recent work extends into Computational Wisdom, designing ethical decision architectures and self-regulating cognitive agents capable of moral deliberation.
