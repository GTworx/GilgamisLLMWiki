# Author: H. M. Batson

**Bio / Role**: British literary commentator (Henrietta M. Batson, 1859–1943) whose 1900 Methuen edition of *The Ruba'iyat of Omar Khayyam* supplied an influential stanza-by-stanza philosophical commentary decoding the metaphysical tensions in FitzGerald's fifth edition.
