## Biography & Context
Eli Lifland is a top-ranked international superforecaster (#1 on the RAND Forecasting Initiative and INFER leaderboards), co-founder of Samotsvety Forecasting, and researcher at the AI Futures Project.

In [[ai-2027]] (2025), Lifland leads the quantitative appendices forecasting compute scaling ($10^{27}$ to $10^{28}$ FLOP), superhuman coding milestones, and algorithmic progress multipliers (source: BOOKS/AI/2025 Book_ai-2027.pdf, pp. 1, 4–6, 51).
