## Biography & Contributions

English polymath, solicitor, marine zoologist, and Persian philologist (1861–1943) whose 1898 Bodleian manuscript facsimile, literal translation, and source-tracking analysis of FitzGerald's *Rubaiyat* established modern textual criticism of Omar Khayyam.
