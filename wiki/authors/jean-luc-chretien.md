## Biography & Institutional Context
Jean-Loup Chrétien is a historic French astronaut (CNES/NASA) and former fighter pilot who became the first Western European in space (Salyut 7 in 1982) and the first non-American/non-Soviet to perform a spacewalk (Mir in 1988), serving as an advisor on international space cooperation.
