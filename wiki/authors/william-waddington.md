## Biography & Context
William Waddington is a cloud data solutions architect and lead co-author collaborating with Snowflake and O'Reilly Media on scalable SaaS data-platform design.

In [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]] (2021), Waddington and co-authors codify cloud-first separation of storage and compute, multi-tenant isolation, ELT pipelines, and zero-copy Secure Data Sharing for modern data applications (source: BOOKS/SaaS/OReilly-Report-Architecting-Data-Intensive-SaaS-Applications.pdf, pp. 3–4, 7–65).
