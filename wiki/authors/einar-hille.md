## Biography & Context
Einar Hille (1894–1980) was a towering American mathematician (Yale University, President of the AMS, co-author of the Hille–Yosida theorem in functional analysis) whose analytical rigor shaped 20th-century operator theory and university calculus education.

Honored in [[calculus-one-and-several-variables]] (10th Ed., 2007, with [[saturnino-l-salas]] and [[garret-j-etgen]]), Hille's legacy unites foundational calculus with modern functional analysis (source: BOOKS/Math/C A L C U L U S_ONE AND SEVERAL VARIABLES Artun.pdf, pp. 7–8).
