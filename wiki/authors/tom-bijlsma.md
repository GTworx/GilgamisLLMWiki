## Biography & Context

Lt. Col. Dr. Tom Bijlsma is Assistant Professor of War Studies and Operational Analysis at the Faculty of Military Sciences, Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA). He specializes in behavioral decision theory, cognitive biases, heuristics in military leadership, and crisis decision-making.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Bijlsma authored Chapter 23 ("What's on the Human Mind? Decision Theory and Deterrence"), applying Kahneman and Tversky's Prospect Theory and cognitive heuristics (anchoring, confirmation, availability, affect) to explain why deterrence attempts systematically fail.
