## Biography & Context
**Scott Barry Kaufman, PhD**, is a cognitive scientist, humanistic psychologist, and Founder and Director of the **Center for Human Potential** (source: [[leading-for-tomorrow]], About the Editors, p. 339). Earning his PhD in cognitive psychology from **Yale University** and an MPhil in experimental psychology from the **University of Cambridge** under a Gates Cambridge Scholarship, Kaufman has taught at Columbia University, the University of Pennsylvania, and New York University, and hosts *The Psychology Podcast*, which has garnered over 30 million downloads.

Named by *Business Insider* as one of "50 Groundbreaking Scientists Who Are Changing the Way We See the World," Kaufman is the author or editor of more than ten books—including *Ungifted: Intelligence Redefined*, *Wired to Create*, *Transcend: The New Science of Self-Actualization* (which reimagined Abraham Maslow's hierarchy of needs as a dynamic sailboat metaphor), and *Rise Above* (2025). In [[leading-for-tomorrow]] (2026), co-edited with [[chris-shipley]], Kaufman synthesizes contemporary humanistic psychology with executive strategy, arguing that tomorrow's leaders must move from defensive ego-protection ("security needs") toward growth-oriented self-transcendence, psychological safety, and post-traumatic growth.
