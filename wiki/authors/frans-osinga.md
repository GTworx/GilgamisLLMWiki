## Biography & Context

Air Commodore Prof. Dr. Frans Osinga is Professor of Military Operational Art and Sciences and Chair of the War Studies Department at the Faculty of Military Sciences, Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA), as well as Special Professor in War Studies at Leiden University. A former Royal Netherlands Air Force F-16 pilot and graduate of the Netherlands Defence College, Osinga earned his Ph.D. at Leiden University in 2005 with his seminal work *Science, Strategy and War: The Strategic Theory of John Boyd*.

Osinga is one of Europe's leading military theorists, specializing in command and control, operational art, strategic culture, air power theory, and contemporary deterrence. In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, co-edited with Tim Sweijs, Osinga investigates the conceptual and operational adaptations required by Western defense establishments facing renewed great power competition, hybrid threats, and emerging disruptive military technologies.
