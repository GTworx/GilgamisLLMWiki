# Author: Tevfik Fikret

**Bio / Role**: Late-Ottoman Turkish poet, educator, painter, and chief architect of the *Servet-i Fünun* (Edebiyat-ı Cedîde) literary movement (1867–1915). Transformed *aruz* prosody into a modern vehicle of lyrical melancholy and fierce secular civic conscience in *Rübab-ı Şikeste*, *Haluk'un Defteri*, and *Şermin*.
