# Comparative Turkic Folklore and Mythology Consortium

## Biography
Consortium of folklorists and cultural anthropologists studying Dede Korkut formulaic oral poetry, tree of life archetypes (Yggdrasil vs. Bayterek), ethnographic rites of Tahtacı Turkmens, and modern holiday inventions (Nardugan).
