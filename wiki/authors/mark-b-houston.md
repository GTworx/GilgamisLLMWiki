## Biography & Context
Mark B. Houston is the Eunice and James L. West Chair in Marketing at the Neeley School of Business, Texas Christian University, and former Editor-in-Chief of the *Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science*.

In [[entertainment-science-data-analytics-and-practical-theory]] (2019), co-authored with [[thorsten-hennig-thurau]], Houston synthesizes 35 years of empirical research across movies, games, books, and music.
