# Author: Jalal al-Din Rumi

**Bio / Role**: Thirteenth-century Persian-language Sufi mystic, Islamic jurist, theologian, and poet (Mawlana Jalal al-Din Muhammad Balkhi / Rumi, 1207–1273), born in Balkh and settled in Seljuk Konya. Author of the *Masnavi-ye Ma'navi* and *Divan-i Shams-i Tabrizi*, whose spiritual legacy inspired the Mevlevi Order and global mystical literature.

## Biography & Contributions

Thirteenth-century Persian-language Sufi mystic, Islamic jurist, theologian, and poet (Mawlana Jalal al-Din Muhammad Balkhi / Rumi, 1207–1273), born in Balkh and settled in Seljuk Konya. Author of the *Masnavi-ye Ma'navi* and *Divan-i Shams-i Tabrizi*, whose spiritual legacy inspired the Mevlevi Order and global mystical literature.
