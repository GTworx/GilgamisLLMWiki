## Biography & Context

Dr. Samuel Zilincik is a strategic analyst and researcher at the Netherlands Defence Academy and The Hague Centre for Strategic Studies (HCSS). He specializes in strategic theory, cross-domain deterrence, and the role of human emotions in international security.

In *Deterrence in the 21st Century*, Zilincik co-authored Chapter 8 ("The Essence of Cross-Domain Deterrence" with Tim Sweijs) and Chapter 24 ("Deterrence: A Continuation of Emotional Life with the Admixture of Violent Means" with Isabelle Duyvesteyn), pioneering the integration of emotion science and multi-domain theory into strategic studies.
