# Author: Nassim Nicholas Taleb

**Bio / Role**: Lebanese-American essayist, risk-engineering scholar, former derivatives trader, and Distinguished Professor of Risk Engineering at NYU Tandon (b. 1960). Author of the five-book *Incerto* series (*Fooled by Randomness*, *The Black Swan*, *The Bed of Procrustes*, *Antifragile*, and *Skin in the Game*), pioneering modern tail-risk epistemology, convexity, optionality, and symmetric ethical accountability.

## Biography & Contributions

Lebanese-American essayist, risk-engineering scholar, former derivatives trader, and Distinguished Professor of Risk Engineering at NYU Tandon (b. 1960). Author of the five-book *Incerto* series (*Fooled by Randomness*, *The Black Swan*, *The Bed of Procrustes*, *Antifragile*, and *Skin in the Game*), pioneering modern tail-risk epistemology, convexity, optionality, and symmetric ethical accountability.
