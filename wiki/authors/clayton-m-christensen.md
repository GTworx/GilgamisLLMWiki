## Biography & Context
Clayton M. Christensen (1952–2020) was the Kim B. Clark Professor of Business Administration at Harvard Business School and co-founder of Innosight and the Clayton Christensen Institute for Disruptive Innovation. Widely regarded as one of the most influential management theorists of the modern era (*The Innovator's Dilemma*, 1997), Christensen extended disruptive innovation and **Jobs-to-Be-Done** theory to healthcare and public education.

In [[disrupting-class-how-disruptive-innovation-will-change-the-way-the-world-learns]] (2008) and his Foreword to [[blended-using-disruptive-innovation-to-improve-schools]] (2015) with [[michael-b-horn]], [[curtis-w-johnson]], and [[heather-staker]], Christensen demonstrated how modular, student-centric online and blended learning will transform K–12 education from a factory-batch system into a mastery-based architecture.
