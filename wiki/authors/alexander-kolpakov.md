## Biography & Context
Dr. Alexander Kolpakov is a senior researcher at the Center for Urban Transportation Research (CUTR) at the University of South Florida, Tampa, specializing in alternative fuel fleets, transit policy, and shared micromobility regulation.

As lead author of *NCHRP Synthesis 597: Micromobility Policies, Permits, and Practices* in [[electric-vehicles-light-duty-fuel-economy-and-shared-micromobility]], Kolpakov synthesizes state DOT statutory definitions, municipal permitting, and safety frameworks for e-bikes and e-scooters.
