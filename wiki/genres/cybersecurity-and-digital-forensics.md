## Overview & Scope
**Cybersecurity, Incident Response, and Digital Forensics** unites federal security standards, network telemetry analysis, cryptographic chain-of-custody preservation, machine learning anomaly detection, and forensic cyberpsychology to defend critical infrastructure and attribute adversarial intrusions.

In the wiki, [[paul-cichonski]] et al.'s [[computer-security-incident-handling-guide-nist-sp-800-61r2]] establishes the canonical four-phase CSIRT lifecycle, while [[marshall-s-rich]]'s [[advanced-network-forensics-a-cyber-behavioral-science-approach]] advances proactive **Cyber Forensics Behavioral Analysis (CFBA)**.
