## Overview & Scope
**Psychoanalysis, Depth Psychology, and Trauma Healing** explores the hidden architecture of unconscious desire, bodily containment, archetypal myth, and therapeutic transformation.

Our shelf unites [[sigmund-freud]]'s foundational pair ([[bilimsel-bir-psikoloji-projesi-1895]] and [[ruyalari-yorumu-interpretation-of-dreams]]), [[jacques-lacan]]'s Seminars II and VIII ([[seminar-2-the-ego-in-freuds-theory]], [[seminar-8-transference]]) plus the international Lacanian clinical review [[heterite-11-les-paradoxes-du-desir]], [[didier-anzieu]]'s [[deri-ben-the-skin-ego]], [[russell-grigg]] et al.'s [[female-sexuality-early-psychoanalytic-controversies]], [[joseph-campbell]]'s [[the-hero-with-a-thousand-faces]], [[michael-a-singer]]'s [[the-untethered-soul]], [[phil-stutz]] & [[barry-michels]]'s [[the-tools-stutz-michels]], and [[peter-a-levine]] & [[maggie-kline]]'s somatic trauma guide [[cocuklarda-travmanin-yaralarini-sarmak]].
