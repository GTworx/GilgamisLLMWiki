## Overview & Scope
**Theoretical Physics, Quantum Mechanics, and Cosmology** spans humanity's deepest mathematical models of physical reality—from subatomic Hilbert-space operators and wavefunction branching to black-hole evaporation, M-theory, and the origin and fate of spacetime.

In this wiki, the genre encompasses [[stephen-hawking]]'s cosmological quartet ([[a-brief-history-of-time]], [[the-theory-of-everything-origin-and-fate-of-the-universe]], [[the-grand-design]] with [[leonard-mlodinow]], and [[brief-answers-to-the-big-questions]]), [[hugh-everett-iii]]'s original 1957 Many-Worlds dissertation [[on-the-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-relative-state-formulation]], [[henry-p-stapp]]'s quantum-mind treatise [[mind-matter-and-quantum-mechanics]], [[jochen-pade]]'s two-volume [[quantum-mechanics-for-pedestrians]], rigorous operator-theory notes by [[stephan-fackler]] ([[mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-ulm-lecture-notes]]) and [[adrian-stan]] & [[monica-ramona-costache]] ([[mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-physics]]), and [[igor-yevgenyevich-irodov]]'s classic [[problems-in-general-physics]].
