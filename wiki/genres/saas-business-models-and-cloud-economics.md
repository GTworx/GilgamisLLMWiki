# Genre: SaaS Business Models and Cloud Economics

**Category**: Enterprise Software, Cloud Strategy & Unit Economics
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Explores the commercial, operational, and architectural principles governing Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) and cloud software businesses. Encompasses B2B subscription pricing strategies, value metrics, unit economics (CAC payback, LTV/CAC, Rule of 40), Net Revenue Retention (NRR), product-led growth (PLG), customer success frameworks, multi-tenant database architectures, and post-ZIRP capital efficiency.
