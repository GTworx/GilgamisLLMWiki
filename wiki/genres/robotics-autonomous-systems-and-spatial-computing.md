## Overview & Scope
Robotics, Autonomous Systems & Spatial Computing investigates the engineering, sensory perception, and software architectures enabling machines to interact autonomously with physical reality and human perception. This encompasses general-purpose humanoid robots, heterogeneous multi-agent swarms, hyper-redundant snake mechanisms, reinforcement learning sim-to-real transfer, industrial cobots, video pass-through mixed reality, and spatial human-machine interfaces.

### Strategic Significance
The convergence of embodied foundation models, high-torque actuators, and spatial optical waveguides marks the transition of artificial intelligence from digital screens into physical manipulation. As robots master dexterous physical labor and spatial computing blends digital telemetry into direct human sight, the boundary between physical and cybernetic systems dissolves.
