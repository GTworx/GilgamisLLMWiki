## Overview & Scope
**Financial History, Banking Crises, and Market Psychology** investigates the human emotions, dynastic networks, and governance failures that drive markets from boom to meltdown.

Within this wiki, the genre unites [[edwin-lefevre]]'s Wall Street classic [[reminiscences-of-a-stock-operator]], [[tristan-gaston-breton]]'s 250-year family chronicle [[la-saga-des-rothschild]], [[duncan-mavin]]'s forensic post-mortem of Credit Suisse ([[meltdown-credit-suisse]]), and [[georg-simmel]]'s sociological foundation ([[the-philosophy-of-money]]).
