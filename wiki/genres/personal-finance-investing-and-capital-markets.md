## Overview & Scope
**Personal Finance, Investing, and Capital Markets** spans the complete financial stack—from everyday household balance sheets to multi-billion-dollar Wall Street underwriting.

In this wiki, institutional capital markets are anchored by [[david-p-stowell]]'s [[investment-banks-hedge-funds-and-private-equity]] and technical spreadsheet engineering by [[conrad-carlberg]] ([[excel-for-accountants]]) and [[vj-books-editorial]] ([[financial-analysis-using-excel]]), while retail wealth building is covered across U.S. and UK household encyclopedias ([[managing-your-money-all-in-one-for-dummies]], [[personal-finance-and-investing-all-in-one-for-dummies]]), diversified ownership investing ([[investing-for-dummies]]), fundamental equity picking ([[stock-investing-for-dummies]]), real-estate leverage ([[financing-real-estate-investments-for-dummies]]), active trading psychology ([[stock-trading-strategy-3-book-bundle]]), and dealmaking memoir ([[trump-the-art-of-the-deal]]).
