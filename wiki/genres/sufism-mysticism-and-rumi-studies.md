## Overview & Scope
**Sufism, Mysticism, and Rumi Studies** explores the interior contemplative tradition of Islam—uniting ecstatic poetry, narrative pedagogy, spiritual psychology, and philological scholarship.

In this wiki, the genre revolves around an extraordinary seven-work [[jalal-al-din-rumi]] and [[shams-i-tabrizi]] library: [[jawid-mojaddedi]]'s rhyming Oxford verse translation ([[the-masnavi-books-one-and-two-mojaddedi]]), [[edward-henry-whinfield]]'s classic six-book abridgment ([[the-masnavi-whinfield]]), [[coleman-barks]]'s modern poetic anthologies ([[the-essential-rumi]], [[rumi-the-book-of-love]]), [[william-c-chittick]]'s reconstruction of Shams's *Maqalat* ([[me-and-rumi-shams-i-tabrizi]]), [[afzal-iqbal]]'s historical biography ([[the-life-and-work-of-jalal-ud-din-rumi]]), and [[leonard-lewisohn]]'s scholarly anthology ([[the-philosophy-of-ecstasy-rumi-and-the-sufi-tradition]]).
