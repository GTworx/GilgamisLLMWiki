# Genre: Biography, Memoir, and Intellectual History

**Description**: Lives of transformative moral reformers, political revolutionaries, conquerors, humorists, and dealmakers—illuminating how personal character collides with historical structures.
**Key Themes**: [[moral-courage-nonviolent-resistance-and-transformative-biography]], [[revolutionary-ambition-power-and-historical-legacy]], [[universal-love-and-human-dignity]]
**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

## Overview & Scope
**Biography, Memoir, and Intellectual History** anchors abstract political, philosophical, and economic ideas in lived human experience.

Across this wiki, the genre spans nonviolent moral witnesses—[[mahatma-gandhi]]'s [[gandhi-my-experiments-with-truth]], [[troy-jackson]]'s [[becoming-king-martin-luther-king-jr]], [[malala-yousafzai]] & [[christina-lamb]]'s [[i-am-malala]], [[trevor-noah]]'s [[born-a-crime]], and [[firoozeh-dumas]]'s [[funny-in-farsi]]—alongside studies of ambition, revolution, and power including [[frank-mclynn]]'s [[napoleon-a-biography]], [[david-mclellan]]'s [[karl-marx-a-biography]], [[richard-l-harris]]'s [[che-guevara-a-biography]], and [[donald-j-trump]]'s [[trump-the-art-of-the-deal]], framed by life-curatorship anthologies from [[larry-anderson]] ([[inspiration-to-live-your-magic]]) and [[unity-stoakes]] ([[50-plus-books-that-changed-my-life]]).
