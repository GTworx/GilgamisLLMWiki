## Overview & Scope
**Product Design and Behavioral Engineering** focuses on the craft of discovering, prototyping, and iterating technology products that solve urgent customer jobs and earn durable daily engagement.

Across our knowledge base, this genre bridges [[alexander-osterwalder]] et al.'s [[value-proposition-design]], [[nir-eyal]] & [[ryan-hoover]]'s [[hooked-how-to-build-habit-forming-products]], [[eric-ries]]'s [[the-lean-startup]], [[brant-cooper]] & [[patrick-vlaskovits]]'s [[the-lean-entrepreneur]], and [[pragmatic-coders]]'s [[how-to-start-a-startup-pragmatic-coders]], cross-linked directly with [[marty-cagan]]'s [[inspired-how-to-create-tech-products-customers-love]].
