## Overview & Scope
**Cognitive, Moral, and Social Psychology** investigates how minds perceive reality, form moral tribes, coordinate collective rituals, and respond to media, technology, and conflict.

Key pillars include [[jonathan-haidt]]'s [[the-righteous-mind]], [[michael-lewis]]'s chronicle of Kahneman and Tversky in [[the-undoing-project]], [[donald-d-hoffman]]'s [[the-case-against-reality]], [[simon-grondin]]'s [[psychology-of-perception]], [[steven-pinker]]'s [[the-better-angels-of-our-nature]], [[norbert-wiener]]'s [[the-human-use-of-human-beings]], [[neil-postman]]'s [[amusing-ourselves-to-death]], [[paul-connerton]]'s [[how-societies-remember]], [[priya-parker]]'s [[the-art-of-gathering]], and empirical threat studies [[psychology-of-terrorism-ncjrs]], [[the-devil-made-me-do-it-malevolent-creativity]], and [[of-heroes-and-villains-pre-revolutionary-russia]].
