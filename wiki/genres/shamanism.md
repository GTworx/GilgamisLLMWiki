# Genre: Shamanism

**Summary**: An ancient spiritual and healing tradition practiced worldwide in which specialized practitioners (shamans) enter altered states of consciousness to perceive and interact with non-physical dimensions, spirits, and ancestors.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Anthropological and archaeological research reveals shamanism as humanity's archaic spiritual foundation, dating back to the Upper Paleolithic. Through fasting, drumming, sensory deprivation, and entheogenic plant medicines (such as Ayahuasca, peyote, and psilocybin), shamans functioned as healers, navigators of the unconscious, and intermediaries between human communities and the natural ecosystem.
