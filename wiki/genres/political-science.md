## Overview & Scope

Political Science investigates how individuals and groups allocate power, resolve collective action dilemmas, build institutions, aggregate democratic preferences, and manage conflict. It incorporates rational choice and social choice theory, behavioral analysis, institutional design, and comparative politics.

In the wiki, political science provides the analytical framework for institutional mechanisms that promote cooperation, regulate interstate and intrastate conflict, and confront the mathematical limits of majority voting and collective rationality (such as Condorcet's voting paradox and Arrow's Impossibility Theorem).
