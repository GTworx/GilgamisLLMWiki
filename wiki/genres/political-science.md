## Overview & Scope

Political Science investigates how individuals and groups allocate power, resolve collective action dilemmas, build institutions, and manage conflict. It incorporates rational choice theory, behavioral analysis, institutional design, and comparative politics.

In the wiki, political science provides the analytical framework for institutional mechanisms that promote cooperation and regulate interstate and intrastate conflict.
