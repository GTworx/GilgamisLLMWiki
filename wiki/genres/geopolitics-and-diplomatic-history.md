## Overview & Scope
**Geopolitics, Grand Strategy, and Diplomatic History** examines how sovereign states, empires, and civilizational blocs negotiate legitimacy, balance power, and manage systemic transitions across historical epochs.

Within this wiki, the genre anchors a rich grand-strategy dialogue uniting [[henry-kissinger]]'s comparative diplomatic synthesis [[world-order]], [[francis-fukuyama]]'s Hegelian thesis [[the-end-of-history-and-the-last-man]], [[john-j-mearsheimer]] & [[stephen-m-walt]]'s realist critique [[the-israel-lobby-and-us-foreign-policy]], [[john-bolton]]'s White House insider chronicle [[the-room-where-it-happened]], [[stephen-fortescue]] et al.'s century-long study [[russian-politics-from-lenin-to-putin]], and [[turkic-world-policy-analysts]]'s contemporary legislative scorecard [[turkic-world-senate-analysis-2026]].
