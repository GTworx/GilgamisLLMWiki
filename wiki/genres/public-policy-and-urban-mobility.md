## Overview & Scope
**Public Policy, Transportation Systems, and Urban Mobility** examines how federal agencies, state Departments of Transportation, and municipal transit authorities regulate vehicle emissions, street-space allocation, and multimodal first/last-mile transit integration.

Featured in [[electric-vehicles-light-duty-fuel-economy-and-shared-micromobility]] through the National Academies studies led by [[gary-marchant]], [[colin-murphy]] (*TCRP Report 230*), and [[alexander-kolpakov]] (*NCHRP Synthesis 597*).
