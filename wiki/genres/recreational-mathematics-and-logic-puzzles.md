## Overview & Scope
**Recreational Mathematics, Logic Puzzles, and Brainteasers** celebrates playful intellectual rigor—the fertile borderland where riddles, paradoxes, and games spark serious mathematical breakthroughs in graph theory, combinatorics, probability, and game theory.

This genre brings together golden-age and historical anthologies—[[henry-ernest-dudeney]] & [[martin-gardner]]'s [[536-puzzles-and-curious-problems]] and [[david-wells]]'s [[the-penguin-book-of-curious-and-interesting-puzzles]]—with modern mathematical-circle and decision-theory collections by [[presh-talwalkar]] ([[math-puzzles-classic-riddles]]), [[zbigniew-romanowicz]] ([[100-math-brainteasers]]), [[gary-r-gruber]] ([[the-worlds-200-hardest-brain-teasers]]), [[ian-livingstone]] & [[jamie-thomson]] ([[brain-teasers-211-logic-puzzles]]), [[joseph-leeming]] ([[riddles-riddles-riddles]]), and [[paul-carson]]'s mental-fluency manual ([[the-math-hacker-book]]).
