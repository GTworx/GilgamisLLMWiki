# Genre: Quantum Information Science and Computing

**Category**: Science, Technology & Computing
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Explores the theoretical foundations, physical hardware implementations, algorithmic complexity, error correction protocols, and commercial development of quantum computing and quantum information science. Encompasses superconducting transmons, trapped ions, neutral atom arrays, photonic circuits, silicon spin qubits, topological matter, quantum supremacy benchmarks, quantum low-density parity-check (qLDPC) codes, quantum sensing, quantum cryptography, and hybrid quantum-classical computing frameworks.
