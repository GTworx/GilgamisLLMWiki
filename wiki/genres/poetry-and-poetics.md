## Overview & Scope

Poetry and Poetics examines verse forms, oral bards, musical meters, and metaphorical expression across cultures. In folk and oral literature, poetry functions not merely as aesthetic contemplation, but as an active mode of knowledge transmission, ethical pedagogy, and social critique.

Through formal metrics (such as the Turkish syllabic *hece vezni* or free verse *serbest nazım*), poetry encodes cultural memory, preserves linguistic subtleties, and articulates intuitive truths that often elude propositional prose.
