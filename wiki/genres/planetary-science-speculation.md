# Genre: Planetary Science Speculation

**Summary**: Scientific and investigative speculation exploring planetary catastrophism, interplanetary impacts, celestial geomorphology, and the vulnerability of terrestrial civilizations to cosmic hazards.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Bridging planetary astronomy, space exploration, and catastrophism, this genre investigates the violent history of the solar system and its implications for Earth. By examining geological features on neighboring bodies like Mars alongside Earth's cratering record, researchers analyze cosmic impact hazards, comet fragment swarms, and potential anomalous surface features (such as Cydonia), urging the human species to prioritize planetary defense and space colonization.
