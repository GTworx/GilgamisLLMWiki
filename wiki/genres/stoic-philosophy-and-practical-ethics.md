# Genre: Stoic Philosophy and Practical Ethics

**Description**: Classical Roman and Hellenistic Stoic primary texts, modern translations, scholarly studies, and aphoristic guides to equanimity, moral volition, and resilience under adversity.
**Key Themes**: [[stoic-equanimity-dichotomy-of-control-and-self-mastery]], [[universal-love-and-human-dignity]], [[antifragility-black-swans-and-skin-in-the-game]]
**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

---

## Overview & Scope
**Stoic Philosophy and Practical Ethics** preserves antiquity's most battle-tested operating system for human character, emotional resilience, and civic responsibility.

In this wiki, the genre gathers the complete Roman Stoic triad—[[seneca]]'s [[letters-from-a-stoic]] (paired with [[paul-veyne]]'s [[seneca-the-life-of-a-stoic]]), [[marcus-aurelius]]'s [[meditations-marcus-aurelius]], and [[a-a-long]]'s definitive study [[epictetus-stoic-and-socratic-guide]]—synthesized in [[frances-hazlitt]] & [[henry-hazlitt]]'s [[the-wisdom-of-the-stoics]] and echoed in modern aphoristic form by [[nassim-nicholas-taleb]]'s [[the-bed-of-procrustes]].
