# Genre: Executive Coaching & Personal Mastery

**Description**: Practitioner playbooks, cognitive operating systems, habit architectures, and executive coaching methodologies bridging individual self-governance and high-trust organizational performance.

## Overview & Scope
Unites Silicon Valley executive coaching (*Trillion Dollar Coach*, *The Great CEO Within*, *Coaching for Leadership*, *Starting and Running a Coaching Business*), focus and habit science (*Deep Work*, *So Good They Can't Ignore You*, *The Power of Habit*, *Intentional Attention*, *The 7 Habits*), modern career strategy (*The LinkedIn Playbook*, *The Mission Generation*, *Tools of Titans*, *How to Build a Career in Tech*), and contemplative self-mastery (*The Power of Now*, *The Daily Stoic*, *Ego Is the Enemy*).
