## Overview & Scope
**Neuroscience, Neuroaesthetics, and Philosophy of Mind** explores how hundreds of billions of neurons and trillions of synapses generate perception, memory, emotion, aesthetic creativity, and conscious awareness—and whether mind can be reduced to classical computation.

In the wiki, this genre unites [[sandra-ackerman]]'s *Decade of the Brain* synthesis [[discovering-the-brain]], Nobel laureate [[eric-r-kandel]]'s [[reductionism-in-art-and-brain-science-bridging-the-two-cultures]], and Nobel laureate [[roger-penrose]]'s [[the-emperors-new-mind-concerning-computers-minds-and-the-laws-of-physics]].
