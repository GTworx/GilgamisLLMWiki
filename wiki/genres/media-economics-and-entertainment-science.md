# Genre: Media Economics, Digital Management, and Entertainment Science

**Description**: Econometric, managerial, and industrial-organization analysis of movies, music, games, publishing, and digital streaming platforms.

## Overview & Scope
**Media Economics, Digital Management, and Entertainment Science** applies data analytics, consumer psychology, and platform economics to cultural and information goods characterized by high first-copy fixed costs, near-zero reproduction marginal costs, and extreme power-law demand skewness.

Anchored in the wiki by [[thorsten-hennig-thurau]] and [[mark-b-houston]]'s [[entertainment-science-data-analytics-and-practical-theory]] and [[eli-m-noam]]'s [[media-and-digital-management]].
