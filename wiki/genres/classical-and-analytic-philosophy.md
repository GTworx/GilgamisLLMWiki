## Overview & Scope
**Classical, Formal, and Political Philosophy** anchors our knowledge base in 2,400 years of rigorous philosophical inquiry—from Athenian dialectics to contemporary formal logic and political epistemology.

Spanning [[plato]]'s complete Hackett corpus ([[plato-complete-works]]) and [[saint-augustine]]'s [[confessions-of-saint-augustine]] through [[karl-popper]]'s [[the-open-society-and-its-enemies-vol-1]], [[joseph-a-schumpeter]]'s [[capitalism-socialism-and-democracy]], [[christoph-schuringa]]'s [[karl-marx-and-the-actualization-of-philosophy]], [[sven-ove-hansson]] & [[vincent-f-hendricks]]'s [[introduction-to-formal-philosophy]], [[quassim-cassam]]'s [[vices-of-the-mind]], [[william-b-irvine]]'s [[a-guide-to-the-good-life-stoic-joy]], [[sam-harris]]'s [[lying-sam-harris]], and accessible surveys [[sophies-world]] and [[philosophy-100-great-thinkers]], the genre forms the conceptual backbone connecting political science, economics, and cognitive science.
