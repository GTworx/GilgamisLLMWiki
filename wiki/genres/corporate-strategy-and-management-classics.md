# Genre: Corporate Strategy, Product Management, and Management Classics

**Description**: Empirical studies of enduring corporate greatness, Harvard Business Review centennial canons, empowered tech product discovery, open-source collaboration, and adaptive career strategy.
**Key Themes**: [[enduring-greatness-empowered-product-teams-and-open-source-ecosystems]], [[platform-power-data-governance-and-career-adaptability]], [[adaptive-intelligence-and-organizational-resilience]]
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

## Overview & Scope
**Corporate Strategy, Product Management, and Management Classics** distills a century of organizational science into actionable playbooks for leaders, builders, and teams.

Anchored by [[jim-collins]] & [[jerry-i-porras]]'s [[built-to-last]] and [[jim-collins]]'s [[good-to-great]], the genre spans four flagship [[harvard-business-review-editorial]] volumes ([[hbr-at-100]], [[hbr-10-must-reads-on-teams]], [[hbr-insights-future-of-business-boxed-set]], [[hbr-women-at-work-collection]]), [[karen-berman]] & [[joe-knight]]'s [[financial-intelligence-for-managers]], modern software & product engineering in [[marty-cagan]]'s [[inspired-how-to-create-tech-products-customers-love]] and [[eric-steven-raymond]]'s [[the-cathedral-and-the-bazaar]], empirical marketing in [[erik-mooi]] et al.'s [[market-research-process-data-methods-stata]], VC operations in [[venture-capital-a-practical-guide]], and career reinvention in [[reid-hoffman]] & [[ben-casnocha]]'s [[the-start-up-of-you]].
