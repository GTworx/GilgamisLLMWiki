## Overview & Scope
**Cultural, Nomadic, and Digital Anthropology** investigates humanity's diverse 'communal experiments in living' through sustained participant-observation—refusing artificial boundaries between traditional ethnography and 21st-century technological worlds.

Within the Gilgamis LLM Wiki, this genre unites [[tim-ingold]]'s philosophical defense [[anthropology-why-it-matters]], [[heather-a-horst]] & [[daniel-miller]]'s foundational manifesto [[digital-anthropology]], [[lane-denicola]]'s design-ethnography essay [[the-digital-as-para-world]], [[mehmet-eroz]]'s classic Anatolian pastoral ethnography [[yorukler]], and [[the-giving-block]]'s Web3 philanthropy study [[crypto-and-nft-philanthropy-afsp-case-study]].
