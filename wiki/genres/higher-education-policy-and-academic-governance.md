# Higher Education Policy and Academic Governance

## Overview
Studies the structural architecture, legislative frameworks, administrative models, and socioeconomic impact of higher education institutions. Examines academic freedom, institutional neutrality doctrines (the Kalven Report), university board governance (mütevelli heyetleri), technology transfer and regional innovation ecosystems, centralized university entrance examinations (YKS), and historical modernization paradigms (Köy Enstitüleri).
