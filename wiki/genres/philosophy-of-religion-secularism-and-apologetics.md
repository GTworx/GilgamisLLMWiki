## Overview & Scope
**Philosophy of Religion, Secularism, and Apologetics** captures both sides of the modern debate over God, Darwinian naturalism, religious cognition, and moral foundations.

Within this wiki, the secular-evolutionary wing is represented by [[richard-dawkins]]'s polemical manifesto [[the-god-delusion]], [[daniel-c-dennett]]'s evolutionary-cognitive framework [[breaking-the-spell]], and [[dylan-evans]]'s concise philosophical primer [[atheism-all-that-matters]]—counterbalanced directly by [[pawel-bloch]]'s point-by-point theistic rebuttal [[richard-dawkins-god-delusion-bloch]] and [[todd-burpo]] & [[lynn-vincent]]'s popular experiential memoir [[heaven-is-for-real]].
