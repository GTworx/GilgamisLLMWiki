## Overview & Scope
Evolutionary Biology, Population Genomics & Biological Anthropology investigates macro-evolutionary dynamics, self-organized criticality, primate mirror systems, archaic hominin admixture (Neanderthal/Denisovan introgression), human population genomic structures, paleoneurology, and molecular geroscience.

### Strategic Significance
Mapping the evolutionary lineage of human biology—from ancient non-coding neural enhancers to archaic genetic variants in modern clinical populations—provides the fundamental bedrock for understanding human nature, cognition, vulnerability to disease, and longevity.
