# Genre: Disaster Risk Reduction and Emergency Management

**Category**: Disaster Risk Science, Emergency Logistics & Crisis Preparedness
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Examines institutional, logistical, and computational frameworks for mitigating natural hazards and managing sudden-onset crises. Encompasses integrated disaster response plans (TAMP), community resilience metrics, humanitarian supply chain management, urban search and rescue coordination, temporary shelter engineering, early warning systems, and post-disaster recovery lifecycle planning.
