# Genre: Catastrophism

**Summary**: The scientific and philosophical paradigm asserting that Earth's geological features, biological extinctions, and human history have been shaped primarily by sudden, violent, short-lived catastrophic events rather than solely by slow uniformitarian processes.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Initially founded in 19th-century geology by Georges Cuvier and later marginalized by Charles Lyell’s uniformitarianism, catastrophism was dramatically revived in the late 20th century with the discovery of the Chicxulub asteroid impact that wiped out the dinosaurs. In contemporary archaeology and planetary science, catastrophism encompasses cometary impacts (the Younger Dryas event), solar storms, and glacial outburst floods.
