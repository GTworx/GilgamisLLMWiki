## Overview & Scope
**Ethics, Value Philosophy, and Virtue Leadership** explores the foundational nature of value (*Arete* / Quality) and how ethical norms and character virtues guide individual craftsmanship, university scholarship, and global institutional leadership.

In the wiki, this genre unites [[robert-m-pirsig]]'s [[zen-and-the-art-of-motorcycle-maintenance]], [[christoph-stuckelberger]], [[walter-fust]], and [[obiora-ike]]'s [[global-ethics-for-leadership-values-and-virtues-for-life]], and [[divya-singh]] & [[christoph-stuckelberger]]'s [[ethics-in-higher-education-values-driven-leaders-for-the-future]].
