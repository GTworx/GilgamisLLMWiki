## Overview & Scope
**Energy Policy, Storage Economics, and Clean-Tech Transition** investigates the twin imperatives of 21st-century energy systems: guaranteeing sovereign supply security while decarbonizing power grids and transportation through renewable generation, Long-Duration Energy Storage (LDES), and vehicle electrification.

In the wiki, this genre unites [[berat-albayrak]]'s [[burasi-cok-onemli-enerjiden-ekonomiye-tam-bagimsiz-turkiye]], [[oliver-schmidt]] and [[iain-staffell]]'s [[monetizing-energy-storage-a-toolkit-to-assess-future-cost-and-value]], and [[kwo-young]] & [[gary-marchant]]'s [[electric-vehicles-light-duty-fuel-economy-and-shared-micromobility]].
