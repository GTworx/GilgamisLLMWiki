# Genre: Occult Studies

**Summary**: The scholarly and historical examination of hidden, esoteric, and magical traditions—including alchemy, astrology, Kabbalah, and sacred geometry—and their role in intellectual and cultural history.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Occult studies explore traditions historically labeled "occult" (hidden) that deeply influenced the Renaissance, the birth of modern science, and political revolutions. Rather than dismissing occultism as irrational superstition, modern historians (such as Frances Yates) and researchers demonstrate how esoteric philosophies animated figures from Isaac Newton and Giordano Bruno to revolutionary state planners.
