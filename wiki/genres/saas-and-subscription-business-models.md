## Overview & Scope
**SaaS, Subscription Economics, and Customer-Led Growth** chronicles how multi-tenant cloud delivery overturned perpetual boxed-software licensing, shifting enterprise value creation from one-time initial transactions to continuous customer impact, Net Revenue Retention (NRR > 130%), and compounding lifetime value.

This genre spans the origin story of cloud CRM in [[marc-benioff]] & [[carlye-adler]]'s [[behind-the-cloud]], scientific revenue architecture in [[jacco-van-der-kooij]], [[fernando-pizarro]], & [[dominique-levin]]'s [[blueprints-for-a-saas-sales-organization]] and [[the-saas-sales-method-for-customer-success-and-account-managers]], customer-driven product squads in [[david-cancel]]'s [[hypergrowth-how-the-customer-driven-model-is-revolutionizing-business]], retention engineering in [[robert-skrob]]'s [[retention-point-membership-and-subscription-growth]], data-intensive SaaS architecture in [[william-waddington]] et al.'s [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]], API monetization in [[api-strategy-for-decision-makers]], and enterprise SaaS adoption/governance in [[saas-for-dummies-oracle-special-edition]], [[snowflake-the-definitive-guide]], and [[state-of-it-asset-management-2022]].
