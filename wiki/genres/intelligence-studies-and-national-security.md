## Overview & Scope
**Intelligence Studies, Covert Action, and National Security** investigates the hidden machinery of statecraft—from human intelligence recruitment and psychological leader assessment to illicit-finance active measures and National Security Council coordination.

In this wiki, the genre bridges [[victor-ostrovsky]] & [[claire-hoy]]'s Mossad accountability exposé [[by-way-of-deception]], [[jerrold-m-post]], [[margaret-g-hermann]], [[stephen-g-walker]], & [[david-g-winter]]'s CIA/academic handbook [[the-psychological-assessment-of-political-leaders]], [[craig-unger]]'s investigation of Russian hybrid warfare and organized crime in [[house-of-trump-house-of-putin]], and [[john-bolton]]'s first-hand NSC memoir [[the-room-where-it-happened]].
