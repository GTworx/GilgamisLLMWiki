# Genre: Security Studies

**Description**: The academic subdiscipline of international relations focusing on national, international, and human security, threat perception, conflict prevention, and defense policy.

## Overview & Scope

Security Studies analyzes threats to the survival and stability of states, societies, and individuals. While traditionally focused on interstate war and military balancing, contemporary security studies encompasses non-traditional security challenges including cyber warfare, hybrid interference, terrorism, economic coercion, resilience, and emerging disruptive technologies (AI, autonomous systems).
