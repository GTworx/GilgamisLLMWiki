## Overview & Scope

International Relations (IR) explores the structural conditions of global anarchy, state behavior, balance of power, international regimes, and conflict dynamics. Key theoretical paradigms include Realism (anarchy, power distribution, deterrence), Neoliberal Institutionalism (cooperation under anarchy via repeated interaction and institutions), Constructivism (norms, strategic culture, identity), and Behavioral Decision Models.

In the wiki, IR provides the macro-political context for both Axelrod's institutional cooperation theorems and the deterrence architectures analyzed in the NL ARMS volume.
