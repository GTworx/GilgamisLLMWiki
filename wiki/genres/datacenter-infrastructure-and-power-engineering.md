# Genre: Datacenter Infrastructure and Power Engineering

**Category**: Technology Infrastructure & Energy Systems
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Covers the mechanical, electrical, thermodynamic, and civil engineering systems required to build and operate hyperscale and enterprise digital infrastructure. Spans high-voltage utility grid interconnections, substation engineering, power distribution units, backup generation, uninterruptible power supplies, direct-to-chip liquid cooling, immersion cooling, thermal heat reuse, colocation market dynamics, and space-based orbital compute constellations.
