## Overview & Scope
**Literary Criticism and Modernist Studies** analyzes how 20th-century novelists, poets, and critics responded to the fragmentation of traditional realism, urbanization, mass media, and totalitarian ideologies. It distinguishes between purely formalist 'aesthetic modernism' and politically engaged 'analytic modernism.'

Within the wiki, [[william-collins-donahue]]'s [[the-end-of-modernism-elias-canettis-auto-da-fe]] examines [[elias-canetti]]'s *Die Blendung* as a devastating critique of Weimar intellectual inwardness, connecting with [[robert-m-pirsig]]'s [[zen-and-the-art-of-motorcycle-maintenance]] and [[ashley-dawson]]'s [[mongrel-nation-colonization-in-reverse]].
