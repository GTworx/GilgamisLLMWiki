# Genre: Turkish Republican History & Biographical Memoirs

**Description**: Primary archival documents, eyewitness memoirs, historical biographies, investigative studies, and humanist essays illuminating Ottoman-Turkish continuity, Atatürk and the Republican founding, and modern Turkish cultural life.
**Key Themes**: [[ataturk-republican-founding-and-ottoman-turkish-memory]], [[ottoman-turkish-modernization-poetics-and-temporal-satire]], [[revolutionary-ambition-power-and-historical-legacy]]
**Last updated**: 2026-11-23

---

## Overview & Scope
Anchors a rich Turkish-language and comparative historical cluster spanning tenth-century Eurasian travel (*İbni Fadlan Seyahatnamesi*) and Renaissance Ottoman iconography (*Sultan Cem'in Portreleri*) through the founding generation's primary records (Lord Kinross, Afet İnan, Ali Mithat İnan, Cemal Granda/Turhan Gürkan, Kâzım Karabekir, Uğur Mumcu, Sezai Karakoç) and contemporary cultural, literary, and industrial reflections (Cemal Süreya, İlber Ortaylı, Ali Çimen, Üzeyir Garih, Burak Dalgın).
