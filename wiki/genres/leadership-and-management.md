## Overview & Scope
**Leadership and Management** within the Gilgamis LLM Wiki encompasses the empirical, strategic, and behavioral frameworks through which individuals and executive teams guide institutions through technological, cultural, and geopolitical disruption. Moving decisively beyond 20th-century Taylorist command-and-control hierarchies, the contemporary works in this genre converge on a central insight: in an era of exponential artificial intelligence, polycrisis, and institutional distrust, sustainable performance depends on **adaptive intelligence**, **psychological safety**, **authentic human connection**, and **trusted strategic counsel**.

Across the five 2026 works anchored in this genre, leadership is examined from complementary vantage points:
1. **Boardroom Counsel & Executive Influence**: How technical subject-matter experts earn a seat at the table, decode executive anxiety, and speak the "Language of Done" ([[influencing-leaders]]).
2. **Neuroscience of Personal & Strategic Adaptability**: How leaders clear accumulated "Emotional Debt" and rewire survival-conditioned nervous systems to build **Adaptive Intelligence (AQ)** ([[the-adaptability-advantage]]).
3. **Cultural Rewilding & Systemic Empowerment**: How organizations replace bureaucratic "process debt" with positive-psychology-driven **Organizational Rewilding** around the pillars of *Human*, *Connect*, *Simplicity*, and *Empowerment* ([[rapid-reculturing]]).
4. **Human-Centered AI & Self-Transcendent Governance**: How twenty-six leading scholars and practitioners synthesize generative AI co-piloting, "failing well" in novel territory, and humanistic self-transcendence ([[leading-for-tomorrow]]).
5. **Service-First Visibility & Ethical Authority**: How leaders and domain experts package their intellectual capital and lead with measurable public impact over performative image ([[valuable-and-visible]]).
