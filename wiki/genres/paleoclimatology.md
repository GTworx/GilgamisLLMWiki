# Genre: Paleoclimatology

**Summary**: The scientific study of past climates throughout Earth's geological history, reconstructing temperatures, ice sheet dynamics, sea levels, and catastrophic weather shifts using ice cores, ocean sediments, and speleothems.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Paleoclimatology is indispensable for understanding human prehistory. The transition out of the Last Glacial Maximum (between 20,000 and 10,000 years ago) witnessed dramatic climate swings—including the abrupt Bølling-Allerød warming, the sudden Younger Dryas freeze (12,800–11,600 BP), and violent meltwater pulses that raised global oceans by 120 meters. Paleoclimatological models directly provide the geological timeline for alternative archaeology.
