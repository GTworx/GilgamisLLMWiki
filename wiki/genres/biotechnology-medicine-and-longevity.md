## Overview & Scope
**Pharmaceutical Biotechnology, Clinical Medicine, and Longevity Science** spans the complete arc of human health—from recombinant protein engineering and 2026 in-vivo CRISPR/CAR-T cures to bedside neurological rehabilitation, healthcare market transformation, and preventive centenarian living.

This genre brings together [[stat-news-editorial]]'s cutting-edge 2026 report [[beyond-cancer-the-expanding-reach-of-cell-and-gene-therapies]], [[daan-j-a-crommelin]], [[robert-d-sindelar]], & [[bernd-meibohm]]'s graduate textbook [[pharmaceutical-biotechnology-fundamentals-and-applications]], [[cb-insights-research]]'s [[amazon-in-healthcare-strategy-for-a-3-trillion-market]], [[hulya-apaydin]] et al.'s clinical Turkish handbook [[parkinson-hastaligi-hasta-ve-yakinlari-icin-el-kitabi]], [[hector-garcia]] & [[francesc-miralles]]'s global longevity study [[ikigai-the-japanese-secret-to-a-long-and-happy-life]], and [[the-giving-block]]'s mental-health philanthropy case [[crypto-and-nft-philanthropy-afsp-case-study]].
