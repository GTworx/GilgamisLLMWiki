# Genre: Esoteric History

**Summary**: The historical investigation of secret societies, hermetic schools, occult traditions, sacred geography, and their hidden influence on major political, religious, and philosophical revolutions throughout world history.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Esoteric history traces subterranean intellectual and spiritual currents—such as Gnosticism, Hermeticism, alchemy, the Knights Templar, and Freemasonry—that operated beneath the surface of orthodox religious and political structures. In *The Master Game*, Hancock and Bauval reveal how sacred astronomical geometries, Egyptian talismanic magic, and Hermetic philosophies were encoded into the architecture and urban layout of world capitals such as Paris, Rome, and Washington, D.C.
