## Overview & Scope
**Postcolonial Studies, Diasporic Culture, and Critical Social Theory** investigates the enduring legacies of European imperialism, the post-1945 'colonization in reverse' of metropolitan centers by Caribbean, Asian, and African diasporas, and the psychological/political projections of ethno-nationalism.

Featured in [[ashley-dawson]]'s [[mongrel-nation-colonization-in-reverse]] (with [[louise-bennett]]) and [[naomi-klein]]'s [[doppelganger-israel-palestine-and-the-doppelganger-effect]].
