## Overview & Scope
**Jurisprudence, Comparative Law, and Quantitative Criminology** bridges the normative philosophy and comparative doctrine of positive law with rigorous statistical and randomized experimental evaluation of crime and policing.

In the wiki, [[jaap-hage]], [[antonia-waltermann]], and [[bram-akkermans]]'s [[introduction-to-law]] provides the comparative legal grammar across Civil, Common, EU, and International Law, while [[alex-r-piquero]] and [[david-weisburd]]'s [[handbook-of-quantitative-criminology]] codifies spatial crime mapping, trajectory modeling, and place-randomized trials.
