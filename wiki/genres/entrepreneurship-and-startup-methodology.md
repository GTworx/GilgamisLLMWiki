# Genre: Entrepreneurship and Startup Methodology

**Description**: Foundational playbooks and empirical frameworks for launching, validating, scaling, financing, and surviving high-uncertainty ventures—from Customer Development and Lean Startup to Blitzscaling, Zero to One, and startup failure forensics.
**Key Themes**: [[lean-startup-customer-development-and-product-market-fit]], [[disruptive-innovation-blitzscaling-and-founder-resilience]], [[venture-capital-term-sheets-and-fundraising-mechanics]]

## Overview & Scope
**Entrepreneurship and Startup Methodology** captures the modern operating canon of venture creation—uniting scientific customer discovery with contrarian monopoly strategy, hypergrowth management, and unvarnished founder survival.

In this wiki, the genre anchors the complete Silicon Valley intellectual arc: [[steve-blank]] & [[bob-dorf]]'s [[the-startup-owners-manual]], [[eric-ries]]'s [[the-lean-startup]], [[geoffrey-a-moore]]'s [[crossing-the-chasm]], [[clayton-m-christensen]]'s [[the-innovators-dilemma]] and [[the-prosperity-paradox]], [[peter-thiel]] & [[blake-masters]]'s [[zero-to-one]], [[reid-hoffman]] & [[chris-yeh]]'s [[blitzscaling]], [[ben-horowitz]]'s [[the-hard-thing-about-hard-things]], [[tom-eisenmann]]'s [[why-startups-fail]], and iconic founder narratives including [[phil-knight]]'s [[shoe-dog]] and [[eric-jorgenson]] & [[naval-ravikant]]'s [[the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant]].
