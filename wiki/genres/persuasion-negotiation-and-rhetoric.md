## Overview & Scope
**Persuasion, Negotiation, and Classical Rhetoric** unites 2,400 years of communicative art with empirical social psychology and crisis negotiation.

Within this wiki, the genre links [[robert-b-cialdini]]'s [[influence-the-psychology-of-persuasion]], [[chris-voss]] & [[tahl-raz]]'s [[never-split-the-difference]], [[dale-carnegie]]'s [[how-to-win-friends-and-influence-people]], [[jay-heinrichs]]'s [[thank-you-for-arguing]], [[madsen-pirie]]'s [[how-to-win-every-argument]], [[r-b-sparkman]]'s [[the-art-of-manipulation]], [[henry-cloud]] & [[john-townsend]]'s [[boundaries-cloud-townsend]], and [[robert-greene]]'s [[the-laws-of-human-nature]] and [[the-33-strategies-of-war]].
