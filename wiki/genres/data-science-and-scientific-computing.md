# Genre: Data Science, Statistical Modeling, and Scientific Computing

**Description**: Methodologies, programming architectures (Python and R), exploratory statistics, and computational pipelines for extracting actionable knowledge and predictive models from complex empirical datasets.
**Key Themes**: [[probabilistic-inference-and-uncertainty-quantification]], [[rationality-and-decision-making]], [[evolution-of-strategies]]

## Overview & Scope
**Data Science, Statistical Modeling, and Scientific Computing** bridges mathematical statistics, numerical optimization, clinical biostatistics, and software engineering to turn raw high-dimensional telemetry into calibrated scientific and enterprise decisions.

In the wiki, this genre is anchored by [[laura-igual]] and [[santi-segui]]'s [[introduction-to-data-science-a-python-approach-to-concepts-techniques-and-applications]], [[ton-j-cleophas]] and [[aeilko-h-zwinderman]]'s 80-chapter clinical treatise [[machine-learning-in-medicine-a-complete-overview]], [[andreas-krause]] and [[jonas-hubotter]]'s [[probabilistic-artificial-intelligence]], [[judith-hurwitz]] and [[daniel-kirsch]]'s [[machine-learning-for-dummies-ibm-limited-edition]], and the four-volume Springer programming foundation [[python-and-r-scientific-computing-foundations-for-ai-and-data-science]] by [[john-hunt]], [[kent-d-lee]], and [[alain-f-zuur]], [[elena-n-ieno]], and [[erik-h-w-g-meesters]].
