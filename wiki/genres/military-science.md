# Genre: Military Science

**Description**: The systematic study of military processes, operational doctrine, weapon systems, command and control (C2), and the conduct of warfare.

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23  

---

## Overview & Scope

Military Science encompasses the technical, operational, and tactical dimensions of armed forces. It examines doctrine, force posture, air/land/sea/cyber/space domains, counterinsurgency, joint operations, artificial intelligence applications, and military law.

Within this knowledge base, military science grounds theoretical concepts of deterrence and conflict in concrete military architectures—such as NATO's enhanced Forward Presence (eFP), Russian anti-access/area-denial (A2/AD) networks, and algorithmic command and control.
