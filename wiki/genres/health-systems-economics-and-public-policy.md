## Overview & Scope
Health Systems, Economics, Reimbursement & Public Policy examines institutional architecture, provider reimbursement models (RVUs, DRGs, MIPS), regulatory maturity benchmarking (WHO GBT), global cancer epidemiology (GLOBOCAN), health technology assessment (QALY, ICER, cost-effectiveness), and macro-level hospital infrastructure.

### Strategic Significance
Healthcare delivery is governed by intricate economic incentives, fee schedule regulations, capital allocation dynamics, and public health policies. Analyzing how regulatory frameworks shape medical technology adoption, physician behavior, drug pricing, and global pandemic preparedness is essential for understanding systemic resilience and sustainable health equity.
