# Genre: Anthropology of Consciousness

**Summary**: The study of human consciousness across cultures and prehistory, focusing on shamanic states, entheogenic plants, altered states of consciousness, visionary experiences, and their foundational role in the genesis of human art and religion.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

This discipline explores the interface between neurobiology, ethnobotany, and archaeology. Investigating the Upper Paleolithic "Creative Explosion" (~40,000 BP), researchers such as David Lewis-Williams and [[graham-hancock]] argue that rock and cave art (Chauvet, Lascaux) were not mundane hunting records or "art for art's sake," but precise depictions of visionary experiences induced by altered states of consciousness. Comparative studies with modern Amazonian Ayahuasca shamanism reveal recurring transcultural archetypes, therianthropes, and visionary encounters.
