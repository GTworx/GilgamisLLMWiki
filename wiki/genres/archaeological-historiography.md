# Genre: Archaeological Historiography

**Summary**: The critical examination of the history, institutional politics, methodologies, and ideological contexts surrounding archaeological discoveries and the formation of academic consensus.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Archaeological historiography investigates how archaeological research is commissioned, interpreted, and utilized by states and institutions to construct national identity, civic pride, and historical narratives. It tracks the evolution of archaeological science from early imperial treasure-hunting to modern scientific methodology. A prime case study is early Republican Turkey, where Mustafa Kemal Atatürk mobilized archaeology as a foundation of the "Scientific Republic" (*Bilim Cumhuriyeti*), sending scholars like [[ekrem-akurgal]] abroad to construct an inclusive, secular Anatolian heritage.
