## Overview & Scope
World History, Macro-Anthropology, and Ancient Civilizations encompasses broad-scale investigations of human collective development across millennia. Drawing together Big History, comparative archaeology, cognitive anthropology, and global historiography, this genre deconstructs linear teleologies and Eurocentric narratives of progress. It analyzes the systemic dynamics of early urbanization, state formation, collapse events, religious transformations, and technological revolutions from the Paleolithic to the contemporary era.
