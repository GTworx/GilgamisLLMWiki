# Genre: Internet of Things and Industrial Automation

**Category**: Edge Computing, Hardware Systems & Industrial Infrastructure
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Covers the hardware, software, protocols, and operational frameworks connecting physical assets, industrial machinery, smart buildings, and human biometrics to digital intelligence networks. Spans edge computing runtimes, time-series telemetry ingestion, digital twin architectures, IT/OT convergence, SCADA/ICS security, wearable biosensing devices, cellular IoT (NB-IoT / LTE-M), and smart facility energy management.
