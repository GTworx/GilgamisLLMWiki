## Overview & Scope
**Marketing Strategy, Consumer Decision, and Brand Equity** examines how modern organizations identify, understand, reach, and retain customers in complex, digitized, and highly competitive global markets. Moving far beyond 20th-century mass-media broadcasting and linear purchase funnels, the discipline synthesizes econometric scanner data, cognitive neuroscience, social network telemetry, and machine-learning-driven personalization.

Key dimensions of the genre include:
1. **The Circular Consumer Decision Loop**: Overturning the linear AIDA purchase funnel in favor of dynamic evaluation loops and postpurchase brand loyalty flywheels.
2. **Quantitative Marketing Science & Econometrics**: Utilizing massive retail scanner datasets (e.g. NielsenIQ, Chicago Booth Kilts Center) to rigorously model price elasticities, promotion lift, and brand cannibalization.
3. **Social Listening & Telemetric Brand Equity**: Harnessing natural language processing and real-time sentiment analysis across social networks to track brand equity, manage PR crises, and measure share of voice.
4. **Omnichannel & Direct-to-Consumer Dynamics**: Navigating the convergence of physical retail, quick commerce, live shopping, and digital customer journeys.
5. **CMO Mobility & Marketing Leadership**: Analyzing executive tenure, board-level marketing accountability, and the strategic alignment of marketing investments with enterprise shareholder value.
