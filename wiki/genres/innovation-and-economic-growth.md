# Genre: Innovation Theory, General Purpose Technologies, and Economic Growth

**Description**: Evolutionary, historical, and empirical analysis of technological revolutions, General Purpose Technologies (GPTs), learning-by-doing skill diffusion, and cross-disciplinary creativity.

## Overview & Scope
**Innovation Theory, General Purpose Technologies, and Economic Growth** examines how radical inventions emerge at disciplinary intersections, diffuse through decades of complementary institutional redesign and worker 'learning by doing,' and drive long-run intensive economic growth.

Anchored by [[richard-g-lipsey]], [[kenneth-i-carlaw]], and [[clifford-t-bekar]]'s [[economic-transformations-general-purpose-technologies-and-long-term-economic-growth]], [[james-bessen]]'s [[learning-by-doing-the-real-connection-between-innovation-wages-and-wealth]], and [[frans-johansson]]'s [[the-medici-effect-what-elephants-and-epidemics-can-teach-us-about-innovation]].
