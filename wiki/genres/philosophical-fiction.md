# Genre: Philosophical Fiction and the Novel of Ideas

**Description**: Narrative fiction in which philosophical inquiry, epistemology, ethics, and the critique of civilization form the central dramatic engine.
**Key Themes**: [[critique-of-academic-orthodoxy]], [[social-critique-and-moral-conscience]], [[fanaticism-and-human-sacrifice]], [[metaphysics-of-quality-and-global-ethics]], [[rationality-and-decision-making]]

## Overview & Scope
**Philosophical Fiction and the Novel of Ideas** utilizes narrative journey, character collision, and satire to investigate foundational questions of epistemology, axiology (value theory), alienation, and mass psychology.

In the wiki, this genre encompasses [[robert-m-pirsig]]'s Chautauqua on the Metaphysics of Quality in [[zen-and-the-art-of-motorcycle-maintenance]], [[william-collins-donahue]]'s study of [[elias-canetti]]'s *Auto-da-Fé* in [[the-end-of-modernism-elias-canettis-auto-da-fe]], and [[graham-hancock]]'s [[war-god-nights-of-the-witch]].
