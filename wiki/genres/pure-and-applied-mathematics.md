## Overview & Scope
**Pure, Applied, and Pedagogical Mathematics** provides the formal symbolic grammar underlying physics, computer science, machine learning, and quantitative decision-making—from middle-school algebraic word translation to infinite series, vector calculus, and unbounded Hilbert-space operators.

Within this wiki, the genre links [[learningexpress-editors]] & [[alex-oconnor]]'s [[501-math-word-problems-and-middle-school-math-challenges]], [[jay-abramson]]'s OpenStax [[precalculus-openstax]], [[saturnino-l-salas]], [[einar-hille]], & [[garret-j-etgen]]'s classic [[calculus-one-and-several-variables]], and advanced mathematical physics texts including [[stephan-fackler]]'s [[mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-ulm-lecture-notes]], [[adrian-stan]] & [[monica-ramona-costache]]'s [[mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-physics]], [[jochen-pade]]'s [[quantum-mechanics-for-pedestrians]], [[igor-yevgenyevich-irodov]]'s [[problems-in-general-physics]], and [[hugh-everett-iii]]'s [[on-the-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-relative-state-formulation]].
