## Overview & Scope
**Behavioral Science, Tail Risk, and Antifragility** explores how human intuition, social networks, and institutions perform under uncertainty, complexity, and extreme outliers.

This genre is anchored by two complete five-book canons: [[nassim-nicholas-taleb]]'s *Incerto* ([[fooled-by-randomness]], [[the-black-swan]], [[the-bed-of-procrustes]], [[antifragile]], [[skin-in-the-game]]) and [[malcolm-gladwell]]'s behavioral-journalism quintet ([[the-tipping-point]], [[blink-power-of-thinking-without-thinking]], [[outliers-story-of-success]], [[what-the-dog-saw]], [[david-and-goliath]]), alongside [[robert-b-cialdini]] and [[robert-greene]].
