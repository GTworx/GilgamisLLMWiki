## Overview & Scope
**Blockchain, Cryptoeconomics, and Decentralized Finance (DeFi)** encompasses the computer science, cryptography, and game-theoretic mechanism design of shared, append-only ledgers that coordinate economic exchange and multi-party workflows without a single centralized custodian.

In the wiki, this genre spans both public, permissionless Web3 and DeFi ecosystems in [[marco-di-maggio]]'s [[blockchain-crypto-and-defi-bridging-finance-and-technology]] and private, permissioned corporate consortia built on Hyperledger Fabric in [[michael-bradley]] et al.'s [[getting-started-with-enterprise-blockchain]].
