# Genre: Hermeticism

**Summary**: A philosophical, religious, and esoteric tradition based primarily upon writings attributed to Hermes Trismegistus, uniting Egyptian theology, Greek philosophy, alchemy, and sacred geometry under the maxim "As above, so below."  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Hermeticism emerged in Hellenistic Egypt, synthesizing pharaonic wisdom with Platonism. Its central texts—the *Corpus Hermeticum* and the *Emerald Tablet*—proclaim the divine nature of the human intellect, the interconnectedness of all cosmic levels, and the spiritual duty of humanity to become co-creators of harmony. Translated into Latin in Florence in 1460 by Marsilio Ficino, Hermeticism ignited the Renaissance and provided the intellectual framework for early scientific experimentation and Masonic philosophy.
