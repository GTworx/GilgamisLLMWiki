# Genre: Anatolian Civilizations

**Summary**: The multidisciplinary study of the prehistoric, Bronze Age, Iron Age, and classical cultures that inhabited Anatolia (Asia Minor), emphasizing its role as the bridge and wellspring of world civilization.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Anatolia represents one of the dense epicenters of human civilization, from the world's oldest megalithic temple complex at Göbekli Tepe (10th millennium BC) through the Hattian, Hittite, Hurrian, Urartian, Phrygian, Lydian, Ionian, and Byzantine eras. Turkish classical archaeologist [[ekrem-akurgal]] pioneered the thesis of the "Anatolian Civilizations Synthesis," proving that Greek archaic art, philosophy, and sciences were fundamentally nourished and cross-pollinated by earlier Anatolian and Near Eastern civilizations.
