# Genre: Global Supply Chains and Logistics Systems

**Category**: Global Logistics, Trade Policy & Industrial Resilience
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Investigates the physical, geopolitical, and digital architecture of global supply chains, freight corridors, and raw material flows. Encompasses maritime trade chokepoints, container shipping economics, cold-chain pharmaceutical logistics, active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) sourcing, nearshoring and friend-shoring transitions, the European Green Deal (CBAM / SKDM), supply chain decarbonization, and multi-tier supplier risk management.
