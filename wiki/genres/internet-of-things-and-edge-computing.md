## Overview & Scope
**Internet of Things, Fog Computing, and Cyber-Physical Systems** explores the Fourth Industrial Revolution convergence of Operational Technology (OT) with Information Technology (IT)—where billions of constrained sensors, actuators, wearables, vehicles, and industrial machines stream telemetry across LPWAN/mesh protocols into distributed fog nodes and cloud analytics platforms.

In this wiki, the genre pairs Cisco engineers [[ammar-rayes]] and [[samer-salam]]'s graduate-level textbook [[internet-of-things-from-hype-to-reality]] with [[lawrence-c-miller]]'s three-volume executive and hardware-security trilogy [[iot-for-dummies-applications-solutions-and-security-compendium]], bridging directly into time-series data platforms in [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]].
