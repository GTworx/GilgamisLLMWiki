## Overview & Scope
**Consultative Sales and Commercial Teaching** treats high-stakes enterprise selling as an evidence-based behavioral discipline rather than high-pressure persuasion. It traces how buyers form explicit needs and navigate internal stakeholder consensus.

In the wiki, [[neil-rackham]]'s [[spin-selling]] (35,000 sales calls) and [[matthew-dixon]] & [[brent-adamson]]'s [[the-challenger-sale-challenge-yourself-action-book]] connect directly with [[james-e-lukaszewski]]'s [[influencing-leaders]] and [[vanessa-errecarte]]'s [[valuable-and-visible]].
