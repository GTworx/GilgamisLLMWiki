## Overview & Scope
**Political Psychology, Leadership Profiling, and Ideology** explores how human cognition, intuition, personality traits, motives, and moral narratives shape both mass electorates and elite heads of state.

This genre links [[j-eric-oliver]] & [[thomas-j-wood]]'s empirical study of America's epistemic divide in [[enchanted-america]] with [[jerrold-m-post]], [[margaret-g-hermann]], [[stephen-g-walker]], & [[david-g-winter]]'s multi-method leader-profiling canon [[the-psychological-assessment-of-political-leaders]], connecting deeply with [[thomas-sowell]]'s [[a-conflict-of-visions-ideological-origins-of-political-struggles]] across the wider wiki.
