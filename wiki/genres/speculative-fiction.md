# Genre: Speculative Fiction

**Summary**: An expansive literary genre encompassing works that explore "what if" scenarios, incorporating elements of supernatural, mythic, historical revisionism, and alternate realities into narrative form.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Speculative fiction allows authors to dramatize theories, metaphysical worldviews, and historical what-ifs that transcend the strict documentary boundaries of non-fiction. In *War God*, Graham Hancock uses speculative elements—such as direct interventions by metaphysical entities and demonic forces—to convey the psychological and spiritual reality experienced by 16th-century Mesoamericans and Spanish invaders.
