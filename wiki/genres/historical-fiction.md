# Genre: Historical Fiction

**Summary**: Narrative literary fiction set in a accurately researched historical period that dramatizes historical events, cultural clashes, and psychological atmospheres through both real historical personages and imaginative characters.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

## Synthesis

Historical fiction dramatizes transformative civilizational encounters. In [[war-god-nights-of-the-witch]], Graham Hancock leverages deep archival knowledge of 16th-century Mesoamerica to recreate the brutal clash between Hernán Cortés's Spanish conquistadors and Moctezuma II's Aztec empire, infusing historical chronicle with the metaphysical and demonic beliefs held by the historical participants themselves.
