# Genre: Evolutionary Psychology and Behavioral Ecology

**Category**: Evolutionary Psychology, Human Ethology & Adaptationist Behavioral Science
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Studies human cognitive architectures, emotional systems, social behaviors, and cultural patterns through the lens of evolutionary adaptation and natural selection. Encompasses benign violation theories of humor, sexual selection and signaling (humor as fitness indicator), transgenerational chosen trauma, parental investment strategies, cognitive bias evolution, moral intuitions, and survival heuristics shaped during the Environment of Evolutionary Adaptedness (EEA).
