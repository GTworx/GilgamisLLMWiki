## Overview & Scope
**Organizational Psychology** bridges cognitive and humanistic psychology, social neuroscience, and institutional sociology to understand how human beings flourish, collaborate, and innovate inside organizations. Within the Gilgamis LLM Wiki, this genre investigates why conventional top-down corporate change initiatives fail—and how aligning organizational architecture with evolved human psychological needs unlocks extraordinary resilience and creativity.

Key empirical threads across the wiki's organizational psychology corpus include:
- **Psychological Safety and "Failing Well"**: [[amy-c-edmondson]]'s research in [[leading-for-tomorrow]] (Chapter 20, pp. 247–256) demonstrating that high-performing teams distinguish preventable errors from **intelligent failures** in novel territory by cultivating interpersonal safety.
- **Organizational Rewilding and Positive Psychology at Work**: [[alex-bailey]] and [[kerri-oneill]]'s [[rapid-reculturing]], which applies Fredrickson's broaden-and-build theory, emotional contagion, and the five-step **HUMAN Model** to restore natural human agency in over-engineered bureaucracies.
- **Humanistic Self-Transcendence and Post-Traumatic Growth**: [[scott-barry-kaufman]]'s integration of Maslow's late self-transcendence theory into executive leadership in [[leading-for-tomorrow]] (Chapters 4 & 24), alongside [[jonathan-haidt]]'s moral-psychological diagnosis of generational anxiety and institutional trust (Chapter 23).
- **Applied Neuroscience of Adaptability**: [[john-sanei]]'s analysis in [[the-adaptability-advantage]] of how chronic amygdala hijack and unresolved **Emotional Debt** lock executives into defensive repetition, and how neuroplastic practices restore cognitive flexibility.
