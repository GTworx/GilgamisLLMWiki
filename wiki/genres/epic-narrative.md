# Genre: Epic Narrative

**Summary**: A grand narrative format focusing on heroic trials, monumental civilizational collisions, and epochal transformations of whole peoples and empires.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Following in the tradition of ancient literary epics (such as the *Epic of Gilgamesh* and the *Iliad*), epic narratives depict watershed moments of civilizational collapse and rebirth. Characterized by expansive temporal and geographic scope, multi-layered character arcs, and thematic confrontation with destiny, this genre captures the titanic struggles that reshape historical trajectories.
