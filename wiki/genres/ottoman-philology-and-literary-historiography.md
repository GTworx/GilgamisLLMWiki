## Overview & Scope
**Ottoman Philology and Literary Historiography** preserves and interprets the textual heritage connecting classical Ottoman chronicles, Divan verse, provincial minstrel notebooks (*cönk* / *mecmua*), and fin-de-siècle modernization.

In this wiki, the field spans primary Ottoman-script print ([[tevfik-fikret]]'s 1910 [[rubab-i-sikeste]]), structured paleographic instruction ([[recep-ahishali]] et al.'s [[osmanli-turkcesi-metinleri-1]]), exhaustive rhetorical stylistics ([[ayse-cevirici-cagin]]'s [[tevfik-fikretin-siirlerindeki-edebi-sanatlar]]), encyclopedic poet biography ([[sadettin-nuzhet-ergun]]'s [[turk-sairleri-cilt-2]]), 19th-century Kastamonu manuscript edition ([[kastamonulu-ishak-zade-hasan-fevzi]] & [[eyup-akman]]'s [[fusul-i-asikan]]), and comparative Persian manuscript philology ([[edward-heron-allen]]'s [[the-rubaiyat-of-omar-khayyam-bodleian-facsimile]]).
