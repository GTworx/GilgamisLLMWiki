## Overview & Scope
**Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Autonomous Agents** encompasses the full historical and technical trajectory from Alan Turing's 1950 imitation game and McCulloch-Pitts logical neurons to modern Transformer architectures, Bayesian deep learning, reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), and superhuman agentic workflows.

Within the Gilgamis LLM Wiki, this genre unites foundational university textbooks—[[wolfgang-ertel]]'s [[introduction-to-artificial-intelligence]], [[miroslav-kubat]]'s [[an-introduction-to-machine-learning]], [[sandro-skansi]]'s [[introduction-to-deep-learning-from-logical-calculus-to-artificial-intelligence]], and [[andreas-krause]] & [[jonas-hubotter]]'s [[probabilistic-artificial-intelligence]]—with frontier strategic, neuro-evolutionary, and investigative analyses including [[leopold-aschenbrenner]]'s [[situational-awareness-the-decade-ahead]], [[daniel-kokotajlo]] et al.'s [[ai-2027]], [[karen-hao]]'s [[empire-of-ai-dreams-and-nightmares-in-sam-altmans-openai]], [[mustafa-suleyman]] & [[michael-bhaskar]]'s [[the-coming-wave-ai-power-and-our-future]], [[max-solomon-bennett]]'s [[a-brief-history-of-intelligence-evolution-ai-and-the-five-breakthroughs-that-made-our-brains]], [[michael-wooldridge]]'s [[a-brief-history-of-artificial-intelligence-what-it-is-where-we-are-and-where-we-are-going]], [[ethan-mollick]]'s [[co-intelligence-living-and-working-with-ai]], [[brian-solis]] & [[dave-wright]]'s [[infinite-how-visionary-leaders-transform-todays-businesses-into-ai-forward-companies]], and [[andrei-kucharavy]] et al.'s [[large-language-models-in-cybersecurity-threats-exposure-and-mitigation]].
