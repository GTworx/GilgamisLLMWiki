# Genre: Alternative Archaeology

**Summary**: A field of speculative and revisionist inquiry that challenges mainstream archaeological timelines, proposing that advanced civilizations existed prior to the conventionally accepted rise of agriculture and urbanism (~4000–3000 BC).  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Alternative archaeology questions the gradualist, linear model of human progress (from primitive hunter-gatherer to agrarian state). Researchers within this genre emphasize anomalous megalithic engineering, sophisticated astronomical alignments, out-of-place artifacts, paleoclimatological indicators, and global flood mythology. Key figures such as [[graham-hancock]], [[robert-bauval]], [[john-anthony-west]], and [[robert-schoch]] argue that a cataclysmic event at the end of the Pleistocene destroyed a sophisticated precursor civilization, leaving megalithic markers and mythological traditions that seeded early historical cultures.
