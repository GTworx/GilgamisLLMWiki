**Category**: Geophysics, Fault Mechanics & Seismic Hazard Mapping

## Overview
Covers the physical dynamics of the solid Earth, tectonic plate boundaries, active fault rupture mechanics, paleoseismology, and probabilistic seismic hazard assessment (PSHA). Encompasses fault slip rates, ground motion prediction equations (GMPEs), soil liquefaction risk, geological GIS mapping, and the spatial integration of earth scientific data into municipal urban master plans.
