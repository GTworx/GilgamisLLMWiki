## Overview & Scope
Clinical Medicine, Cardiology & Medical Imaging spans diagnostic cardiology, multimodality cardiovascular imaging (stress cardiac CT, CMR, myocardial perfusion, coronary CTA), clinical oncology, interventional therapeutics, bionic vision, and organ repair. Works in this genre emphasize rigorous clinical trials, dosimetry, diagnostic error taxonomy, and evidence-based clinical practice guidelines.

### Strategic Significance
Cardiovascular disease and oncology remain the leading global causes of mortality and morbidity. The shift toward non-invasive, high-resolution anatomical and functional imaging (such as dynamic CT perfusion, FFR-CT, and subretinal neuroprosthetics) represents a profound paradigm shift from reactive symptomatic intervention to early, personalized microstructural diagnosis and targeted clinical intervention.
