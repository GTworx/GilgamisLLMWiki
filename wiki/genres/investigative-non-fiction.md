# Genre: Investigative Non-Fiction

**Summary**: A journalistic and literary discipline that applies rigorous investigative methods—fieldwork, archival research, whistleblower interviews, and forensic analysis—to uncover hidden historical, political, or scientific realities.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Investigative non-fiction brings the probing tools of investigative journalism into academic and historical realms. Pioneered in alternative history by authors like [[graham-hancock]] (formerly of *The Economist*), this genre challenges institutional gatekeeping, follows anomalous data leads, and communicates complex scientific debates to a broad global audience.
