## Overview & Scope
**Cloud Computing, Distributed Systems, and Platform Architecture** examines how elastic compute, immutable containers, decoupled cloud storage, API-first interfaces, and Infrastructure as Code replaced static hardware datacenters with globally scalable, programmable platforms.

Within the Gilgamis LLM Wiki, this genre unites canonical O'Reilly systems manuals—[[brendan-burns]] et al.'s [[kubernetes-up-and-running]], [[ronnie-mitra]] & [[irakli-nadareishvili]]'s [[microservices-up-and-running]], [[joyce-kay-avila]]'s [[snowflake-the-definitive-guide]], [[william-waddington]] et al.'s [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]], [[mike-amundsen]] & [[derric-gilling]]'s [[api-strategy-for-decision-makers]], [[david-baum]]'s [[deploying-a-modern-security-data-lake]], and [[gilit-saporta]] & [[shoshana-maraney]]'s [[practical-fraud-prevention]]—with enterprise cloud strategy and governance benchmarks including [[lawrence-c-miller]]'s [[iaas-for-dummies-oracle-special-edition]] and [[saas-for-dummies-oracle-special-edition]], [[scott-d-lowe]]'s [[enterprise-cloud-for-dummies-nutanix-special-edition]], [[flexera-research]]'s [[state-of-it-asset-management-2022]], and [[ammar-rayes]] & [[samer-salam]]'s [[internet-of-things-from-hype-to-reality]].
