## Overview & Scope
**Private Equity, Venture Capital, and Leveraged Buyouts** examines both the institutional mechanics and political-economic consequences of private-market ownership—ranging from early-stage venture capital financing and minority growth equity to multi-billion-dollar leveraged buyouts, secondary markets, and emerging-market AIF regulation.

Within the Gilgamis LLM Wiki, this genre stages a rigorous multi-perspective synthesis uniting INSEAD's comprehensive academic-practitioner treatise [[mastering-private-equity]] ([[claudia-zeisberger]], [[michael-prahl]], [[bowen-white]]), Whiteark's operational multiple-expansion guide [[the-ultimate-private-equity-playbook]] ([[jo-hands]], [[james-ciuffetelli]]), University of Mumbai's Indian regulatory and developing-market curriculum [[venture-capital-and-private-equity-mms-curriculum]] ([[tushar-balkrishna-raut]]), and investigative journalist [[josh-kosman]]'s hard-hitting critique of LBO debt loading and dividend recapitalizations in [[the-buyout-of-america]].
