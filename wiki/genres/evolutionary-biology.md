# Genre: Evolutionary Biology

**Description**: The subfield of biology that studies the evolutionary processes—natural selection, adaptation, speciation, and population genetics—that produced the diversity of life.

## Overview & Scope

Evolutionary Biology investigates how physical and behavioral traits propagate across generations. The application of evolutionary game theory (John Maynard Smith, George R. Price) and sociobiology (W.D. Hamilton, E.O. Wilson) revealed how cooperative behaviors, mutualism, and reciprocal altruism evolve naturally without conscious intention or centralised control.

In this wiki, evolutionary biology provides the ecological foundation for strategy dynamics, demonstrating how reciprocal strategies invade, outcompete selfish phenotypes, and achieve collective stability.
