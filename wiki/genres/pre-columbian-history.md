# Genre: Pre-Columbian History

**Summary**: The historical and archaeological study of the indigenous cultures, civilizations, technologies, and settlements of the Americas prior to the arrival of Christopher Columbus in 1492.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Pre-Columbian history has undergone a profound revolution over the past three decades. Long constrained by European colonialist narratives portraying the Americas as a sparsely populated wilderness, modern LiDAR scanning, paleogenomics, and geo-archaeology have revealed complex urban civilizations in the Amazon, monumental mound-building astronomies across the Mississippi Valley, and continuous human habitation extending far deeper into the Pleistocene than previously acknowledged.
