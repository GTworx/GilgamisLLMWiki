## Overview & Scope
Agricultural Sciences, AgriTech & Global Food Systems spans crop domestication archeogenomics, precision agriculture telemetry, controlled environment vertical farming, sustainable value-chain certification, sensory discrimination methodology, and global food production rebalancing.

### Strategic Significance
Agriculture accounts for 70% of planetary freshwater withdrawals and a third of global greenhouse gas emissions. Precision smart farming, crop genomic resilience, and controlled environment agriculture offer the vital tools required to nourish 10 billion people while restoring degraded ecosystems and conserving biodiversity.
