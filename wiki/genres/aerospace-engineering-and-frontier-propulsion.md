### Strategic Significance
Rapid launch cost deflation, commercial space industrialization, and urban air mobility represent an unprecedented expansion of human civilization's operational theater. Simultaneously, theoretical advances in metric engineering within General Relativity transition interstellar propulsion concepts from speculative science fiction into rigorous mathematical physics.
