## Overview
AgriTech, Controlled Environment Farming & Crop Genomics focuses on technological and genetic innovations modernizing food production. This includes IoT soil sensor telemetry, automated vertical greenhouse systems, archeogenomic mapping of crop domestications (maize, grapevine), circadian botany, and pollinator biodiversity.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
By optimizing crop microclimates and unlocking ancestral genetic resilience traits, modern agritech decouples food security from erratic climate patterns while drastically reducing agricultural environmental footprints.
