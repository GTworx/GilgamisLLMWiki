## Overview
Robotics, Humanoid Automation & Physical Embodiment investigates general-purpose humanoid bill-of-materials economics, actuator torque density, tactile sensory limitations, sim-to-real reinforcement learning, supervisory human bandwidth over robot swarms, and the labor economics of automating physical occupations.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Physical embodiment is the ultimate frontier of artificial intelligence. When the operational cost of dexterous humanoid robots falls below human hourly wages, global manufacturing, supply chain logistics, and service labor will experience profound structural realignment.
