# Theme: Seismic Hazard Engineering and Building Codes

**Category**: Structural Engineering, Seismic Design & Construction Regulation
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Explores performance-based earthquake engineering, building inspection governance, structural retrofitting, and legal liability in seismic zones. Analyzes design response spectra, non-ductile reinforced concrete vulnerability, soft-story collapse mechanisms, building inspection reforms (e.g., Law No. 4708), and seismic code compliance (e.g., TBDY 2018).
