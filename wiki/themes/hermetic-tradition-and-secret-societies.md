# Theme: Hermetic Tradition and Secret Societies

**Summary**: The historical survival, clandestine transmission, and political influence of Hermetic philosophy, Gnosticism, and sacred geometry through esoteric brotherhoods (Templars, Rosicrucians, Freemasons).  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Throughout medieval and early modern European history, orthodox ecclesiastical authorities brutally suppressed non-conformist spiritual philosophies. The Hermetic tradition—holding that humanity is endowed with divine creative potential and that nature operates through reciprocal harmonies ("As above, so below")—found refuge in clandestine fraternities and secret societies. In *The Master Game*, Hancock and Bauval reveal how these brotherhoods preserved ancient architectural geometry and solar talismans, eventually emerging during the Enlightenment to guide the founding architectures of Paris and Washington, D.C.
