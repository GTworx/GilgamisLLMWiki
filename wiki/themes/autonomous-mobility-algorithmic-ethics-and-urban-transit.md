## Overview
Autonomous Mobility, Algorithmic Ethics & Urban Transit explores multi-modal sensor fusion (LiDAR, radar, cameras), simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), V2X communication, the philosophy of moral crumple zones, statistical risk allocation versus trolley fallacies, and the political philosophy of whether human driving constitutes a fundamental civil right.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Autonomous transit dramatically reduces highway fatalities and optimizes urban throughput, but forces societies to resolve profound legal and ethical questions regarding algorithmic liability, privacy, and the phase-out of manual human driving.
