## Overview
Spatial Computing, Augmented Reality & Human-Machine Interfaces examines the evolution of human-computer interaction beyond planar 2D screens. It encompasses video pass-through mixed reality headsets, holographic optical waveguides, spatial audio, 6DoF inside-out tracking, enterprise frontline wearable deployments, and historical proto-speculative visions of optical telepresence.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
By anchoring contextually relevant digital intelligence directly onto physical environments, spatial computing transforms human perceptual bandwidth, accelerating worker learning curves and establishing the spatial web.
