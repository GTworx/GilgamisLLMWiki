# Theme: Metaphysics of Quality (Arete), Virtue Ethics, and Global-Contextual Values

**Summary**: Healing the Romantic–Classical schism through pre-intellectual Quality (Arete) while balancing universal ethical standards with contextual cultural virtues.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-01

---

## Synthesis
**Metaphysics of Quality (Arete), Virtue Ethics, and Global-Contextual Values** bridges ontological axiology with practical leadership ethics across cultures.

In [[zen-and-the-art-of-motorcycle-maintenance]], [[robert-m-pirsig]] demonstrates that pre-intellectual **Quality (*Arete*)** precedes the division of Romantic art and Classical technology, demanding inner peace of mind and loving craftsmanship. In [[global-ethics-for-leadership-values-and-virtues-for-life]] and [[ethics-in-higher-education-values-driven-leaders-for-the-future]], [[christoph-stuckelberger]], [[walter-fust]], [[obiora-ike]], and [[divya-singh]] operationalize twelve global values and twelve leadership virtues in dialogue with [[asik-borani]]'s Anatolian humanism in [[asik-borani-soz-insanin-aynasi]].
