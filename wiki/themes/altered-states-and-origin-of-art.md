# Theme: Altered States and Origin of Art

**Summary**: The thesis that human symbolic culture, representational art, spiritual cosmologies, and religious impulses originated from shamanic altered states of consciousness, dreams, and entheogenic visionary experiences.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

In *Supernatural*, Graham Hancock explores the "Great Leap Forward" of the Upper Paleolithic (~40,000–35,000 BP), when human beings suddenly began painting complex cave art (Chauvet, Lascaux) featuring therianthropes (part-human, part-animal beings), geometric entoptic signs, and multidimensional realms. Adopting David Lewis-Williams's neuropsychological model of shamanic rock art and comparing it directly to modern Ayahuasca and DMT experiences, Hancock contends that encounters with non-human intelligences in altered states were the fundamental catalyst for human spiritual culture, language, and symbolic abstraction.
