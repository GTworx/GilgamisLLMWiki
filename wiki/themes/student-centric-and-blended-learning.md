# Theme: Student-Centric Disruption, Blended Learning, and Smart Pedagogy

**Summary**: Replacing standardized factory-batch schooling with modular, competency-based blended learning models (Rotation, Flex, A La Carte, Enriched Virtual) and smart classrooms.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-01

---

## Synthesis
**Student-Centric Disruption, Blended Learning, and Smart Pedagogy** synthesizes how disruptive innovation theory, Jobs-to-Be-Done student motivation, and smart instructional design transform education from time-based batch lecturing to mastery-based personalized learning.

Anchored by [[clayton-m-christensen]], [[michael-b-horn]], and [[curtis-w-johnson]]'s [[disrupting-class-how-disruptive-innovation-will-change-the-way-the-world-learns]], [[michael-b-horn]] and [[heather-staker]]'s [[blended-using-disruptive-innovation-to-improve-schools]], and [[ronghuai-huang]], [[j-michael-spector]], and [[junfeng-yang]]'s [[educational-technology-a-primer-for-the-21st-century]], and linked with [[divya-singh]] & [[christoph-stuckelberger]]'s [[ethics-in-higher-education-values-driven-leaders-for-the-future]].
