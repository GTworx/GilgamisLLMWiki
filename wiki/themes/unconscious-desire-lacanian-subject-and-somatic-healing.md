# Theme: Unconscious Desire, the Lacanian Subject, and Somatic Healing

**Summary**: Charting the psyche from Freud's 1895 neural project and dream-work through Lacan's Symbolic/Real subject, Anzieu's Skin-Ego, Campbell's Monomyth, Joyce's dream-language, and somatic/action therapies.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Synthesis
**Unconscious Desire, the Lacanian Subject, and Somatic Healing** unifies a full century of depth psychology across clinical psychoanalysis, modernist literature, mythology, and embodied trauma care.

Anchored by [[sigmund-freud]] ([[bilimsel-bir-psikoloji-projesi-1895]], [[ruyalari-yorumu-interpretation-of-dreams]]), [[jacques-lacan]] ([[seminar-2-the-ego-in-freuds-theory]], [[seminar-8-transference]]), [[heterite-11-les-paradoxes-du-desir]], [[didier-anzieu]]'s [[deri-ben-the-skin-ego]], and [[female-sexuality-early-psychoanalytic-controversies]], the theme expands outward into [[finnegans-wake]] and [[the-hero-with-a-thousand-faces]] and into direct therapeutic practice via [[the-untethered-soul]], [[the-tools-stutz-michels]], and [[cocuklarda-travmanin-yaralarini-sarmak]].
