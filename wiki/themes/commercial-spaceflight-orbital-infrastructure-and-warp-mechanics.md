## Overview
Commercial Spaceflight, Orbital Infrastructure & Relativistic Propulsion investigates reusable launch economics, LEO satellite constellations, orbital debris mitigation protocols, space diplomacy (Artemis Accords), urban air mobility (eVTOL), and theoretical positive-energy warp drives derived from classical General Relativity.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
The economic threshold for orbital access has dropped by orders of magnitude, turning low-Earth orbit into a vibrant commercial and geopolitical domain, while theoretical metric engineering provides a rigorous physical roadmap toward interstellar exploration.
