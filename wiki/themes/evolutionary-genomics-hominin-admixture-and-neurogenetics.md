## Overview
Evolutionary Genomics, Hominin Admixture & Neurogenetics explores the genetic architecture of human cognitive and morphological evolution. It investigates introgressed Neanderthal polymorphisms in neurodevelopmental conditions, human-accelerated neural enhancers, hominid hand dexterity, language origins in primate mirror circuits, and ancestral population admixture.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Demonstrates that modern human biology and cognition are deep evolutionary composites shaped by archaic hybridization and ancient environmental selective pressures, illuminating the roots of human diversity and neurodevelopment.
