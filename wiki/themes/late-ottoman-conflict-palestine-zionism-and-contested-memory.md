# Theme: Late Ottoman Conflict, Palestine, Zionism, and Contested Memory

**Description**: The disintegration of the Ottoman imperial order, World War I fronts, Armenian massacres, the Mandate partition, Zionist settlement, and the 1948 Nakba.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview & Core Concepts
The dissolution of the Ottoman Empire during the late 19th and early 20th centuries unleashed geopolitical reverberations that continue to shape the contemporary Middle East. This theme examines the demise of Ottoman pluralism, the rise of nationalist movements, wartime violence and population expulsions on the Caucasian and Palestinian fronts, the ideological foundations and archival controversies surrounding Zionism, Palestinian dispossession, and the enduring struggle over historical memory, colonial mandates, and state legitimacy.
