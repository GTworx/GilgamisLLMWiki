# Theme: Catastrophism and Earth History

**Summary**: The scientific and historical perspective that Earth's geological, biological, and human history has been punctuated by sudden, violent, planetary-scale convulsions, overturning rigid uniformitarian assumptions.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

For over a century, orthodox geology and archaeology were dominated by uniformitarianism ("the present is the key to the past," assuming all changes happen gradually over millions of years). Modern planetary science, paleoclimatology, and alternative archaeology have revived catastrophism: Earth's past has witnessed abrupt global thermal spikes, massive glacial meltwater floods, crustal displacement, and asteroid/comet bombardments. This theme highlights how civilizations are fragile fragile entities existing at the mercy of unpredictable cosmic and terrestrial equilibria.
