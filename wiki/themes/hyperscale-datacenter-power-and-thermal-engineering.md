# Theme: Hyperscale Datacenter Power and Thermal Engineering

**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Conceptual Overview
Examines the severe physical, electrical, and thermodynamic constraints governing the rapid buildout of generative AI datacenter campuses. Analyzes gigawatt-scale electricity demand inflections, utility transmission interconnection queues, behind-the-meter generation (nuclear, gas, SMRs), power transformer and switchgear supply-chain bottlenecks, liquid cooling cold-plate retrofits, water consumption, and space-based orbital compute clusters.
