## Synthesis

The weaponization of social media networks, algorithmic outrage, psychological manipulation, and state-sponsored computational propaganda to destabilize societal cohesion and shape strategic decisions.

### Architectural Foundations & Strategic Dynamics
Modern technological security requires a fundamental departure from perimeter defense. Whether applied to silicon hardware (combating untrusted offshore foundry overproduction and Trojan insertion via logic locking and split manufacturing) or software networks (enforcing continuous cryptographic authentication, micro-segmentation, and phishing-resistant FIDO2 tokens), trust cannot be assumed by physical proximity or network boundaries.

Similarly, cognitive warfare and algorithmic disinformation exploit digital social network topology and human emotional arousal to bypass rational verification, making societal resilience, institutional trust, and cryptographic provenance verification essential components of comprehensive national defense.
