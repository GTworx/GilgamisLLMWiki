# Theme: General Purpose Technologies (GPTs), Learning by Doing, and Intersectional Innovation

**Summary**: How radical inventions born at disciplinary intersections require decades of structural redesign and worker 'learning by doing' before generating broad-based productivity and wage growth.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-01

---

## Synthesis
**General Purpose Technologies (GPTs), Learning by Doing, and Intersectional Innovation** unites three complementary pillars of innovation theory: (1) [[frans-johansson]]'s **Medici Effect** in [[the-medici-effect-what-elephants-and-epidemics-can-teach-us-about-innovation]], showing how breakthrough ideas erupt at the **Intersection** of diverse disciplines and cultures; (2) [[richard-g-lipsey]], [[kenneth-i-carlaw]], and [[clifford-t-bekar]]'s **Structuralist-Evolutionary GPT Theory** in [[economic-transformations-general-purpose-technologies-and-long-term-economic-growth]], tracing 24 historical GPTs over 10,000 years and proving why new GPTs require rebuilding the entire *Facilitating Structure*; and (3) [[james-bessen]]'s empirical history in [[learning-by-doing-the-real-connection-between-innovation-wages-and-wealth]], proving that mass wages rise only when uncodified **learning-by-doing** skills are standardized and diffused across ordinary workers.
