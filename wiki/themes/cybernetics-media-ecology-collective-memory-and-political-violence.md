# Theme: Cybernetics, Media Ecology, Collective Memory, and Political Violence

**Summary**: Examining how information feedback, entertainment media, embodied ritual memory, purposeful gathering, and situational climates govern social cohesion—or fracture into extremism and political violence.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Synthesis
**Cybernetics, Media Ecology, Collective Memory, and Political Violence** traces the informational and ritual glue holding human societies together—and what happens when it snaps.

[[norbert-wiener]]'s [[the-human-use-of-human-beings]] and [[neil-postman]]'s [[amusing-ourselves-to-death]] diagnose how communication technologies rewire social epistemology; [[paul-connerton]]'s [[how-societies-remember]] and [[priya-parker]]'s [[the-art-of-gathering]] illuminate the constructive power of embodied memory and purposeful ritual; and [[psychology-of-terrorism-ncjrs]], [[of-heroes-and-villains-pre-revolutionary-russia]], and [[the-devil-made-me-do-it-malevolent-creativity]] dissect the psychological pathways, narratives, and situational pressures behind political violence.
