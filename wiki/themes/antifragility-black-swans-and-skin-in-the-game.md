## Synthesis
**Antifragility, Black Swans, and Skin in the Game** encapsulates [[nassim-nicholas-taleb]]'s complete five-volume *Incerto* architecture across our knowledge graph.

Beginning with survivorship bias and stochastic illusions in [[fooled-by-randomness]], advancing into non-Gaussian outlier shocks in [[the-black-swan]] and aphoristic critiques of rigid modernity in [[the-bed-of-procrustes]], the philosophy constructs its constructive blueprint in [[antifragile]] (systems that gain from volatility via convexity and barbell optionality) and grounds it in ancient legal-ethical symmetry in [[skin-in-the-game]].
