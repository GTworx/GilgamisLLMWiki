# Theme: Banking Dynasties, Institutional Meltdowns, and Speculative Psychology

**Summary**: Lessons across three centuries of finance: why disciplined family trust and trend patience endure, whereas unchecked leverage, hubris, and cultural breakdown destroy even 167-year-old banks.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

---

## Synthesis
**Banking Dynasties, Institutional Meltdowns, and Speculative Psychology** pairs triumph and collapse across financial history.

[[tristan-gaston-breton]]'s [[la-saga-des-rothschild]] traces how courier intelligence, pan-European family cohesion, and sober risk management sustained a banking house across 250 years; [[edwin-lefevre]]'s [[reminiscences-of-a-stock-operator]] distills Jesse Livermore's enduring laws of trend discipline alongside the personal perils of speculation; and [[duncan-mavin]]'s [[meltdown-credit-suisse]] reveals how four decades of unchecked Wall Street hubris, Greensill, and Archegos doomed Credit Suisse in March 2023.
