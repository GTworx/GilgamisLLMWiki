# Theme: Bronze Age Collapse, Big History, and Civilizational Cycles

**Description**: Systemic complexity, multi-causal ecological and economic shocks, civilizational rhythms, and deep-time macro-historical transformations across global societies.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview & Core Concepts
Civilizational cycles and catastrophic collapse represent central inquiries in deep-time historiography. From the simultaneous disintegration of Late Bronze Age Mediterranean civilizations circa 1177 BC—triggered by interlocking climate shifts, sea migrations, drought, and supply chain ruptures—to the macro-historical trajectories charted by Big History and cyclical historians, this theme investigates how societies grow fragile through hyper-specialization, how institutional inertia blinds elites to non-linear risks, and how collapse events pave the way for civilizational regeneration and institutional reorganization.
