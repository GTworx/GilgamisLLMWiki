# Theme: Catastrophe Risk Financing and Disaster Resilience

**Category**: Risk Transfer, Disaster Insurance & Humanitarian Economics
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Analyzes financial mechanisms for hedging systemic catastrophe risk, disaster microinsurance, and capital mobilization. Encompasses parametric insurance pools (e.g., DASK), catastrophe bonds, corporate disaster philanthropy, tax policy in emergency relief, anti-money laundering (AML) controls on disaster charities, and blockchain transparency in aid tracking.
