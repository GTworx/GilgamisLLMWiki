# Theme: Pre-Clovis and American Antiquity

**Summary**: The revolution in American archaeology demonstrating human occupation of the Americas tens of thousands of years before the "Clovis-First" paradigm (13,000 BP), revealing sophisticated prehistoric indigenous civilizations.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

For half a century, American archaeology enforced the dogmatic "Clovis-First" model, maintaining that humans first entered North America across the Bering Strait around 13,000 years ago. Discoveries at Monte Verde (Chile), Meadowcroft (Pennsylvania), Bluefish Caves (Yukon), and the Cerutti Mastodon site (California, dated to 130,000 BP) demolished this orthodoxy. In *America Before*, Graham Hancock integrates this revolution with newly uncovered geometric earthworks in the deforested Amazon, evidence of anthropogenic fertile soils (*terra preta*), and genetic anomalies linking indigenous Amazonians to Australasians, arguing that the Americas were a thriving theater of deep human antiquity.
