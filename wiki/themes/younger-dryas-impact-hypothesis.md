# Theme: Younger Dryas Impact Hypothesis

**Summary**: The scientific hypothesis that fragments of a disintegrating giant comet struck Earth approximately 12,800 years ago, initiating the 1,200-year Younger Dryas cold snap, widespread wildfires, megafaunal extinction, and catastrophic flooding.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

First formalized in 2007 by a team of over two dozen scientists led by Richard Firestone and Allen West, the Younger Dryas Impact Hypothesis (YDIH) identified widespread geochemical markers across the globe dating to ~10,800 BC: nanodiamonds, magnetic microspheres, iridium spikes, high-temperature melt glass, and platinum anomalies. This cosmic catastrophe triggered the abrupt freezing of the Northern Hemisphere, followed around 11,600 years ago (9600 BC) by an equally sudden global warming spike and torrential melting (Meltwater Pulse 1B). In [[magicians-of-the-gods]], Graham Hancock argues that Göbekli Tepe was built immediately following this cataclysm as an intentional time capsule and civilizational rescue project.
