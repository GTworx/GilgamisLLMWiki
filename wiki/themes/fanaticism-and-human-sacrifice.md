# Theme: Fanaticism and Human Sacrifice

**Summary**: The philosophical and psychological critique of religious and ideological absolutism that demands the violent sacrifice of human lives in service to abstract dogmas, deities, or state terror.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Across diverse cultures and eras, ideological fanaticism has justified extreme dehumanization. In *War God*, Graham Hancock examines the symbiotic darkness of two extreme theological doctrines: the Aztec state religion, which slaughtered tens of thousands of captive victims atop the Templo Mayor to nourish the sun-god Huitzilopochtli, and the Spanish Inquisitorial zeal, which burned heretics and slaughtered indigenous populations in the name of Christ. This theme champions the universal sanctity of human life against sacrificial and extremist ideologies.
