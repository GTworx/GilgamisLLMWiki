# Theme: Stoic Equanimity, Dichotomy of Control, and Self-Mastery

**Summary**: Building an unshakeable inner citadel by separating what lies within our moral volition from external fortune, status, and adversity.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

---

## Synthesis
**Stoic Equanimity, Dichotomy of Control, and Self-Mastery** unites Rome's three great Stoic teachers with modern scholarly interpretation.

[[a-a-long]]'s [[epictetus-stoic-and-socratic-guide]] clarifies the freed slave's razor-sharp distinction between internal choice (*prohairesis*) and external circumstance; [[marcus-aurelius]]'s [[meditations-marcus-aurelius]] shows an emperor practicing that discipline nightly on the war frontier; [[seneca]]'s [[letters-from-a-stoic]] and [[paul-veyne]]'s [[seneca-the-life-of-a-stoic]] confront the moral tensions of court power; and [[frances-hazlitt]] & [[henry-hazlitt]]'s [[the-wisdom-of-the-stoics]] distills all three into a practical companion—reverberating into modern times through [[nassim-nicholas-taleb]]'s [[the-bed-of-procrustes]].
