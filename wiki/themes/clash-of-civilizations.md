# Theme: Clash of Civilizations

**Summary**: The catastrophic confrontation between fundamentally incompatible cultural, theological, and ideological systems, resulting in the annihilation or subjugation of one civilizational order by another.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

The collision between Hernán Cortés’s Spanish expedition and Moctezuma II’s Aztec Empire in 1519 stands as one of the starkest civilizational clashes in human history. In *War God*, Graham Hancock explores how differences in technology, theology, and military philosophy produced total incomprehension and apocalyptic violence between two expansionist imperial systems—one driven by Catholic crusading zeal and gunpowder, the other by ritual human sacrifice and cosmic fatalism.
