# Theme: Orion Correlation and Sacred Astronomy

**Summary**: The principle that ancient megalithic sites were designed as terrestrial mirrors of specific constellations (notably Orion, Cygnus, and Draco) and calibrated to astronomical dates determined by the precession of the equinoxes.  
**Last updated**: 2026-09-12

---

## Synthesis

Originated by [[robert-bauval]] and expanded alongside [[graham-hancock]], the Orion Correlation Theory (OCT) contends that the three pyramids of the Giza necropolis represent the belt stars of the constellation Orion, while the Nile represents the Milky Way. Furthermore, the Great Sphinx—facing due east—functions as an equinoctial marker intended to face its celestial counterpart, the constellation Leo, at the spring equinox during the astronomical epoch of 10,500 BC (*Zep Tepi*). This theme demonstrates how ancient architects embedded astronomical clocks into stone to communicate epochal time across millennia.
