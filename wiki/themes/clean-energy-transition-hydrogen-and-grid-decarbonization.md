## Overview
Clean Energy Transition, Hydrogen & Grid Decarbonization analyzes the technical and economic pathways for replacing fossil fuel combustion with zero-carbon electricity, green hydrogen, and long-duration energy storage. It explores hourly marginal emissions modeling, utility rate design, Virtual Power Plants, space-based solar power, and industrial waste heat recovery.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
True decarbonization requires matching clean energy generation with consumption on an hourly, locational basis rather than relying on annual paper offsets, driving capital into firm clean baseload, grid battery storage, and transmission corridors.
