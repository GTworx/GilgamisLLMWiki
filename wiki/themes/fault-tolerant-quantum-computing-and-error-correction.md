# Theme: Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing and Error Correction

**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Conceptual Overview
Investigates the theoretical thresholds, algebraic code constructions, physical hardware implementations, and real-time decoding architectures required to protect quantum information from environmental decoherence and gate errors. Tracing developments from the surface code to quantum low-density parity-check (qLDPC) bivariate bicycle codes, this theme explores threshold theorems, transversal Clifford gates, magic-state distillation, belief propagation decoding, and the modular scaling of cryogenic and photonic quantum hardware.
