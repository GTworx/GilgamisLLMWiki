# Theme: AGI Trajectories, Intelligence Explosions, and Superalignment

**Summary**: Quantitative scaling forecasts (Orders of Magnitude of compute, algorithmic efficiency, and agentic unhobbling), recursive AI R&D automation, and the technical and geopolitical challenges of aligning superhuman intelligence.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-03

---

## Synthesis
**AGI Trajectories, Intelligence Explosions, and Superalignment** examines the transition from narrow generative models to Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Superintelligence (ASI), together with the technical alignment, national security, and political-economic dilemmas triggered by recursive self-improvement.

Across the Gilgamis LLM Wiki, [[leopold-aschenbrenner]]'s [[situational-awareness-the-decade-ahead]] (2024) and [[daniel-kokotajlo]], [[scott-alexander]], [[thomas-larsen]], [[eli-lifland]], and [[romeo-dean]]'s [[ai-2027]] (2025) model how ~0.5 OOMs/year of physical compute scaling, ~0.5 OOMs/year of algorithmic efficiency, and 'unhobbling' (chain-of-thought, RL, tool use, and long-horizon agency) project a transition from GPT-4 to superhuman coding and AI R&D agents (`Agent-1` through `Agent-5`) by 2027–2028. While [[max-solomon-bennett]]'s [[a-brief-history-of-intelligence-evolution-ai-and-the-five-breakthroughs-that-made-our-brains]] frames silicon superintelligence as 'Breakthrough #6' following five billion-year vertebrate/primate brain milestones, [[karen-hao]]'s [[empire-of-ai-dreams-and-nightmares-in-sam-altmans-openai]] exposes the corporate governance fractures, data/energy extraction, and internal safety wars behind OpenAI's quest for AGI, and [[michael-wooldridge]]'s [[a-brief-history-of-artificial-intelligence-what-it-is-where-we-are-and-where-we-are-going]] and [[roger-penrose]]'s [[the-emperors-new-mind-concerning-computers-minds-and-the-laws-of-physics]] critically interrogate the gulf between statistical pattern matching and genuine conscious comprehension.
