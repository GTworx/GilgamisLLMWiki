# Theme: Energy Transition, Sovereign Supply Security, and Storage Economics

**Summary**: Techno-economic and geopolitical strategies for decarbonizing power grids and transport via renewable energy, Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS), EV batteries, and sovereign supply security.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-01

---

## Synthesis
**Energy Transition, Sovereign Supply Security, and Storage Economics** synthesizes the engineering, financial, and geopolitical dimensions of the global shift from fossil-fuel dependency to electrified, renewable energy systems.

In the wiki, [[berat-albayrak]]'s [[burasi-cok-onemli-enerjiden-ekonomiye-tam-bagimsiz-turkiye]] examines national supply security, YEKA renewable localization, and deep-sea gas exploration; [[oliver-schmidt]] and [[iain-staffell]]'s [[monetizing-energy-storage-a-toolkit-to-assess-future-cost-and-value]] models Wright's Law experience curves, LCOS, and Long-Duration Energy Storage (LDES); and [[kwo-young]], [[gary-marchant]], [[colin-murphy]], and [[alexander-kolpakov]]'s [[electric-vehicles-light-duty-fuel-economy-and-shared-micromobility]] maps EV battery electrochemistry, 2025–2035 vehicle fuel economy, and shared urban micromobility.
