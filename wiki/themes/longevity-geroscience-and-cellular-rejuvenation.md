## Overview
Longevity, Geroscience & Cellular Rejuvenation focuses on the biological hallmarks of aging, multi-omic temporal dysregulation, cellular reprogramming, and lifestyle longevity architectures. It explores non-linear aging transitions (e.g., at ages 44 and 60), transient Yamanaka-factor-mediated epigenetic rejuvenation, hair follicle stem cell niche reactivation via adipocyte lipolysis, and sociological adaptations in age-friendly urban environments.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Aging is increasingly conceptualized not as an inevitable chronological decay, but as a modifiable biological process governed by epigenetic drift, metabolic exhaustion, and proteostatic stress. Interventions targeting systemic geroscience offer the potential to compress morbidity and dramatically expand human healthspan.
