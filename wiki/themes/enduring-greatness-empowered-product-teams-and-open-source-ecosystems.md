**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

## Synthesis
**Enduring Greatness, Empowered Product Teams, and Open-Source Ecosystems** bridges classic corporate longevity studies with modern software-native operating models.

[[jim-collins]] & [[jerry-i-porras]] ([[built-to-last]]) and [[jim-collins]] ([[good-to-great]]) establish the organizational spine—core ideology, Level 5 humility, confronting brutal facts, and the Flywheel—complemented by a century of management research in [[hbr-at-100]] and [[hbr-10-must-reads-on-teams]]. In technology-powered industries, [[marty-cagan]]'s [[inspired-how-to-create-tech-products-customers-love]] translates those principles into empowered product-discovery teams, while [[eric-steven-raymond]]'s [[the-cathedral-and-the-bazaar]] reveals how open peer communities harness Linus's Law to out-iterate closed hierarchies.
