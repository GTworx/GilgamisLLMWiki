# Theme: Revolutionary Ambition, Power, and Historical Legacy

**Summary**: Comparative biographies of figures who sought to remake empires, economies, or political orders through military genius, ideological theory, guerrilla insurrection, or leveraged commercial branding.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

---

## Synthesis
**Revolutionary Ambition, Power, and Historical Legacy** examines four radically different archetypes of disruptive willpower across modern history.

[[frank-mclynn]]'s [[napoleon-a-biography]] dissects the quintessential Enlightenment-era conqueror-modernizer whose tactical brilliance collided with imperial overreach; [[david-mclellan]]'s [[karl-marx-a-biography]] chronicles the exiled philosopher whose critique of industrial capitalism reshaped twentieth-century geopolitics; [[richard-l-harris]]'s [[che-guevara-a-biography]] follows the guerrilla theorist who attempted to accelerate Marxist revolution through armed *foco* voluntarism; and [[donald-j-trump]]'s 1987 memoir [[trump-the-art-of-the-deal]] captures the late-twentieth-century media-age dealmaker leveraging publicity, debt, and audacity—a worldview whose later presidential consequences ripple through [[the-room-where-it-happened]], [[enchanted-america]], and [[house-of-trump-house-of-putin]].
