## Overview
Translational Medicine, Biomarkers & Clinical Diagnostics investigates the translation of molecular discoveries and bioengineering innovations into clinical practice. This includes non-invasive multimodality cardiac imaging, subretinal photovoltaic neuroprosthetics, structured RNA immuno-oncology aggregates, 3D bioprinting of functional extracellular matrices, diagnostic error attribution, and biomarker-driven screening regimens.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Bridging the chasm between benchtop molecular biology and bedside clinical efficacy requires rigorous diagnostic accuracy, physiological validation, and standardized clinical metrics. Advanced diagnostic technologies reduce diagnostic error harm burdens, enable minimally invasive restorative interventions, and unlock targeted biological therapies.
