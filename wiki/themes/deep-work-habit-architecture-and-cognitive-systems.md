# Theme: Deep Work, Habit Architecture & Cognitive Systems

**Summary**: How dual-system cognition, basal-ganglia habit loops, distraction-free concentration, attentional training, and unified perception govern elite creative output and psychological clarity.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Synthesis
Connects Daniel Kahneman's dual-system architecture (*Thinking, Fast and Slow*) and Charles Duhigg's cue-routine-reward loops (*The Power of Habit*) with Cal Newport's skill-first craftsmanship (*Deep Work*, *So Good They Can't Ignore You*), Justin Anderson's pressure-tested attentional control (*Intentional Attention*), Stephen Covey's principle-centered habits, Timothy Ferriss's deconstructed routines (*Tools of Titans*), Eckhart Tolle's present-moment awareness, and David Bohm's proprioception of thought.
