# Theme: Evolutionary Naturalism, New Atheism, and Theistic Apologetics

**Summary**: The twenty-first-century clash between Darwinian/memetic explanations of religion, secular moral philosophy, Thomistic-historical Christian apologetics, and personal afterlife narratives.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

---

## Synthesis
**Evolutionary Naturalism, New Atheism, and Theistic Apologetics** captures a complete multi-perspective debate over belief, reason, and transcendence.

On the naturalistic side, [[richard-dawkins]]'s [[the-god-delusion]] treats deity claims as improbable scientific hypotheses, [[daniel-c-dennett]]'s [[breaking-the-spell]] explains religion through hyperactive agency detection, memetics, and 'belief in belief,' and [[dylan-evans]]'s [[atheism-all-that-matters]] distills secular ethics and existential meaning. On the theistic side, [[pawel-bloch]]'s [[richard-dawkins-god-delusion-bloch]] contests Dawkins's metaphysics, moral Darwinism, and Gospel historiography point by point, while [[todd-burpo]] & [[lynn-vincent]]'s [[heaven-is-for-real]] exemplifies the enduring pull of personal mystical and near-death testimony.
