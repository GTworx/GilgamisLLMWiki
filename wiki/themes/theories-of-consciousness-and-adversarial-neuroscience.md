# Theme: Theories of Consciousness and Adversarial Neuroscience

**Category**: Philosophy of Mind, Neurobiology of Awareness & Empirical Consciousness Research
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview
Investigates competing empirical and philosophical accounts of subjective conscious experience (qualia) and their rigorous testing via multi-lab adversarial collaboration (e.g., COGITATE). Encompasses Integrated Information Theory, Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT), Higher-Order Theories (HOT), Predictive Processing, Orch-OR quantum microtubule dynamics, conscious realism/interface theory of perception, and defensive activation models of sensory plasticity in sleep.
