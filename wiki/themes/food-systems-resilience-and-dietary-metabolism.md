**Category**: Clean Energy Systems, Agriculture & Evolutionary Genomics

## Overview
Food Systems Resilience, Culinary History & Dietary Metabolism examines the intersection of agricultural production outputs, human metabolic health, culinary technology (cooking, food processing, fermentation, distilling), and planetary dietary sustainability.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Realigning agricultural production with human nutritional guidelines is the most powerful dual intervention for preventing chronic metabolic disease while halting deforestation and biodiversity loss.
