# Theme: Neurobiology of Consciousness, Neuroaesthetics, and the Non-Algorithmic Mind

**Summary**: From synaptic memory (LTP) and bottom-up/top-down visual processing of abstract art ('The Beholder's Share') to Gödelian and quantum-gravitational arguments against algorithmic Strong AI.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-01

---

## Synthesis
**Neurobiology of Consciousness, Neuroaesthetics, and the Non-Algorithmic Mind** explores the biological and physical foundations of human thought, perception, and creativity.

In [[discovering-the-brain]], [[sandra-ackerman]] maps the *Decade of the Brain* breakthroughs in PET/MRI neuroimaging, psychiatric neurochemistry, and hippocampal Long-Term Potentiation (LTP). In [[reductionism-in-art-and-brain-science-bridging-the-two-cultures]], Nobel laureate [[eric-r-kandel]] shows how abstract art's reductionism of form, line, color, and light shifts neural processing from bottom-up V1–IT recognition to top-down creative imagination (**The Beholder's Share**). In [[the-emperors-new-mind-concerning-computers-minds-and-the-laws-of-physics]], Nobel laureate [[roger-penrose]] proves via Gödel's theorem and quantum gravity that conscious human insight transcends algorithmic Turing computation—reinforcing [[john-sanei]]'s [[the-adaptability-advantage]].
