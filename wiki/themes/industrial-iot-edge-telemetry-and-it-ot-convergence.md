# Theme: Industrial IoT, Edge Telemetry, and IT/OT Convergence

**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Conceptual Overview
Analyzes the technological and cultural bridging of enterprise Information Technology (IT) with plant-floor Operational Technology (OT). Explores digital twin simulations, real-time edge processing, SCADA/ICS security perimeters (Purdue model / ISA-95), wearable biomedical telemetry, and cellular IoT network slicing.
