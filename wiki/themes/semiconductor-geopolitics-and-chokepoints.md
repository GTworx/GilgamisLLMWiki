# Theme: Semiconductor Geopolitics and Chokepoints

**Summary**: The strategic competition over integrated circuit design, extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography, and advanced foundry fabrication—where ultra-concentrated technological chokepoints in Silicon Valley, the Netherlands (ASML), and Taiwan (TSMC) underpin 21st-century military deterrence, AI supremacy, and US–China grand strategy.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-01

---

## Synthesis
As synthesized in [[chris-miller]]'s [[chip-war-the-fight-for-the-worlds-most-critical-technology]] (2022) and cross-referenced with [[deterrence-in-the-21st-century]] (2021) and the 2026 AI leadership corpus, the global semiconductor supply chain represents the most complex engineering artifact and the tightest geopolitical chokepoint in human history:

1. **The Pentagon's Offset Strategies and Silicon Warfare**: From guiding Apollo spacecraft and Minuteman II ICBMs in the 1960s to Paveway laser-guided bombs in Vietnam, the **Second Offset Strategy** in the 1970s–1991 Gulf War, and today's AI-driven **Third Offset**, American military overmatch has rested on Gordon Moore's exponential doubling of transistors rather than numerical troop mass ([[chip-war-the-fight-for-the-worlds-most-critical-technology]], Parts I, II, IV, VIII).
2. **Morris Chang's Pure-Play Foundry and the Taiwan Strait Dilemma**: By founding **TSMC** in 1987 as a pure-play foundry that never competed with its fabless customers (Apple, Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm), [[morris-chang]] enabled the fabless revolution while concentrating 37% of global logic capacity and over **90% of the world's most advanced processors** in Taiwan—creating both Taiwan's "Silicon Shield" and the acute geopolitical vulnerability analyzed in Chapter 54 (*"The Taiwan Dilemma"*, pp. 327–345).
3. **Weaponized Interdependence and Technological Chokepoints**: Because advanced chipmaking requires monopolistic nodes controlled by the U.S. and its allies—California EDA software (Cadence, Synopsys), British Arm architectures, American fab equipment (Applied Materials, Lam Research, KLA), and Dutch **ASML EUV lithography** ($13.5\text{ nm}$ tin-plasma lasers and Carl Zeiss mirrors)—Washington has weaponized supply-chain chokepoints via targeted export controls (connecting directly to [[francesco-giumelli]] on sanctions, [[dean-cheng]] on Chinese *weishe*, and [[alex-wilner]] on AI deterrence in [[deterrence-in-the-21st-century]]).
