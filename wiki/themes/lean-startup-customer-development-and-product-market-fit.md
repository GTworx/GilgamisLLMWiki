# Theme: Lean Startup, Customer Development, and Product-Market Fit

**Summary**: Treating early-stage ventures as hypothesis-testing engines—using Customer Discovery, MVPs, Value Proposition Canvases, habit loops, and beachhead crossing to achieve repeatable product-market fit before premature scaling.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Synthesis
**Lean Startup, Customer Development, and Product-Market Fit** traces the scientific revolution in modern venture building.

Originating in [[geoffrey-a-moore]]'s [[crossing-the-chasm]] and codified exhaustively by [[steve-blank]] & [[bob-dorf]] in [[the-startup-owners-manual]], the search-before-execution paradigm reached global scale via [[eric-ries]]'s [[the-lean-startup]], [[alexander-osterwalder]] et al.'s [[value-proposition-design]], [[brant-cooper]] & [[patrick-vlaskovits]]'s [[the-lean-entrepreneur]], [[nir-eyal]] & [[ryan-hoover]]'s [[hooked-how-to-build-habit-forming-products]], and [[pragmatic-coders]]'s [[how-to-start-a-startup-pragmatic-coders]]—while [[tom-eisenmann]]'s [[why-startups-fail]] provides the vital empirical check against Lean 'False Positives.'
