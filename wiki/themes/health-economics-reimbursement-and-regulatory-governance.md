## Overview
Health Economics, Reimbursement & Regulatory Governance explores provider payment systems, Medicare Physician Fee Schedule conversion factors, Relative Value Units (RVUs), health technology assessment (HTA), incremental cost-effectiveness ratios (ICER), quality-adjusted life years (QALYs), WHO regulatory authority benchmarking (GBT), and hospital market consolidation.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Healthcare allocation decisions are mediated by regulatory approvals and fee schedule reimbursement formulas. Understanding how national regulatory authorities achieve maturity level 4, and how Medicare payment modifications drive specialty physician migration and private equity consolidation, provides critical insights into modern healthcare infrastructure.
