# Theme: Sumerology, Cuneiform Epigraphy, and Mesopotamian Mythology

**Description**: The decipherment of third-millennium BCE Sumerian cuneiform clay tablets and their foundational influence on ancient Near Eastern law, epic literature, science, and world religious traditions.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Overview & Core Concepts
Representing the dawn of recorded human history, this theme investigates the urban, legal, literary, and scientific achievements of ancient Sumerian city-states (Uruk, Ur, Nippur, Lagash, Shuruppak). Anchored by the lifelong cataloging and translation work of Muazzez İlmiye Çığ and Samuel Noah Kramer, it explores the Epic of Gilgamesh, the Sacred Marriage rites of Inanna and Dumuzi, school tablets from the *Edubba*, base-60 sexagesimal mathematics, and the profound textual parallels connecting Sumerian flood and creation myths to subsequent biblical and Islamic traditions.
