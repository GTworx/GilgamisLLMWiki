## Overview
Transnational Organized Crime, Illicit Narcotics & Forensic Science investigates the geopolitical, economic, and biochemical mechanics of illicit trade networks. Encompassing cartel state capture, chemical precursor tracking, wastewater-based epidemiology (WBE) of drug consumption, transnational money laundering, forensic ballistics electrodeposition, and quantitative homicide epidemiology.

---

## Core Implications & Analysis
Transnational organized crime operates as an illicit parallel state capitalizing on governance vacuums. Combating illicit networks requires rigorous scientific telemetry—from mass spectrometry of municipal wastewater to advanced forensic ballistics—paired with anti-corruption integrity frameworks to dismantle financial impunity.
