# Theme: Supply Chain Decarbonization and Geopolitical Resilience

**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

## Conceptual Overview
Investigates the strategic restructuring of global trade corridors and production networks in response to geopolitical fragmentation and climate policy. Focuses on Scope 3 carbon reduction, the Just Transition (Adil Geçiş), the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), nearshoring, critical energy transition minerals, and pharmaceutical supply chain resilience.
