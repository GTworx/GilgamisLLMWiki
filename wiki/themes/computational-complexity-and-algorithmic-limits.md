# Computational Complexity and Algorithmic Limits

## Thematic Synthesis
Investigates the theoretical boundaries of computational tractability, algorithmic efficiency, and mathematical hardness. Explores Karp's 21 NP-complete problems, the P versus NP millennium problem, polynomial-time reductions, sub-logarithmic graph algorithms breaking sorting barriers, superpermutation bounds, and the fundamental physical limits of computation.
