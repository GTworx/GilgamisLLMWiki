# Theme: IoT Cyber-Physical Convergence, Fog Computing, and Hardware-Rooted Edge Security

**Summary**: Extending internet protocols, distributed fog/edge intelligence, and silicon-rooted cryptographic trust into physical sensors, actuators, smart homes, and industrial machines where breaches carry lethal real-world consequences.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-22

---

## Synthesis
**IoT Cyber-Physical Convergence, Fog Computing, and Hardware-Rooted Edge Security** explores what happens when software, wireless networking, and machine learning escape the screen and embed directly into physical infrastructure—vehicles, medical implants, power grids, factories, and homes.

[[ammar-rayes]] and [[samer-salam]]'s [[internet-of-things-from-hype-to-reality]] demonstrates how IT/OT convergence demands constrained deterministic protocols (`IEEE 802.15.4e TSCH`, `LoRaWAN`, `6LoWPAN`, `Time-Sensitive Networking`), distributed **Fog Computing**, blockchain-based machine identity, and a 'second thin waist' in the IP hourglass for data semantics. Complementing this protocol architecture, [[lawrence-c-miller]]'s [[iot-for-dummies-applications-solutions-and-security-compendium]] underscores that IoT is fundamentally about recurring services ('Smart Home as a Service') and actionable 'Small Data'—while warning that hacked pacemakers, vehicles, or grids turn cybersecurity into a life-or-death safety discipline requiring silicon-level root keys, secure execution enclaves, and OTA patching. At cloud ingestion scale, [[william-waddington]] et al.'s [[architecting-data-intensive-saas-applications]] shows how multi-tenant cloud platforms turn massive IoT time-series streams into real-time predictive intelligence.
